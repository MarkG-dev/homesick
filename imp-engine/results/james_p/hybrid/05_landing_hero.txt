More accomplished—and yet, the paradox being the whole of it, the less consumed in the accomplishing.

Flowstate is not, we have come to insist, one further goad applied to an already goaded mind; it is rather the single arrangement into which your tasks, your scattered intentions, the half-formed purposes that elsewhere contend and cancel one another, may be gathered, so that what matters comes forward and what does not recedes, almost without your having to decide it. Begin the trial—it asks of you only the small courage of beginning—and discover, as one discovers a faint coolness at an opened door, the capacity that was in you the whole while, and wanted only the medium in which to declare itself.
