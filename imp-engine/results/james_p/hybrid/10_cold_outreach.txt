It is not, I will own at once, from any settled certainty that I write; it is rather that a conviction has formed in me, by degrees and over a longer watching of your work than this note can decently account for, that between your enterprise and my own there waits a possibility neither, kept apart, could realise. My attention to you began, I came to recognise, as something less than interest, and became, almost against its own intention, something more. Whether the thing I half-perceive would survive being talked over, I am at no early stage prepared to say—and that, precisely, is what a brief conversation next week, at whatever hour least disturbs you, might be left to determine. I should wish to learn your own ends before presuming upon them; for I have come to suspect that the partnerships worth the having are seldom the ones either party felt most certain of at the outset.
