It is not, I have come by degrees to feel, a triumph that I have to report, nor even, in the cheaper sense, a departure; it is rather the quiet closing of one arrangement and the tentative opening of another. After five years at Northwind I am to go to Lumen, in the office they call Head of Product. To the colleagues and mentors who sustained me—and I, who had fancied myself the manager of my own advancement, came at last to suspect I had been, from the threshold onward, the most supported of its beneficiaries—I owe more than the occasion will let me decently say. What I feel is not exhilaration, exactly; it is rather a faint coolness at an opened door, perceptible only to one who had been warm. And perhaps that is the whole of such moments: that we mistake for an arrival what is only, once again, a setting-out.
