It is not, we have come to feel, that we welcome you back—the formula is too easy, and welcome too settled a word; it is rather that you have returned at all, across a week which asked so much of you, and the fact, when one weighs it, is a small marvel. We will not inquire whether the week was productive; productivity is a coarse measure, and we have grown, by degrees, suspicious of it. What we have done instead is to gather—the stories, the half-signs, the developments that are never quite developments while they happen and become so only later, in the slow light of an afterthought. Take it up over the morning's cup, or in some snatched interval of a day already too full, and find in it, if you find anything, not the news, which is everywhere and cheap, but the rarer occasion of an attention you might not otherwise have spared. Let us begin; for what in all this will matter, and to whom, is precisely the thing neither of us can yet, with any honesty, claim to know.
