It is not, we must tell you plainly, that the concern touching our SmartBrew coffee makers reaches many units; it is rather that a few units, in a matter of safety, are precisely the few we cannot consent to overlook. We have identified the difficulty, and have determined—weighing nothing in the scale but your well-being—upon a voluntary recall. We will not pretend the thing costs you nothing; the pretence would be an evasion, and a transparent one. We apologise, then, in the harder manner: as those who recognise the failure to be their own and decline to set it down elsewhere. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or replacement as you prefer; the means our website sets out. You had given us your trust, as one gives without counting the cost of it; and we come, now, to a fuller understanding of what the giving had asked—and mean, however imperfectly, to deserve it again.
