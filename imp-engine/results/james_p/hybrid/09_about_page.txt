It was not, when we began in 2021, that we held any quarrel with technology so coarse as displeasure; it was rather a suspicion, gathered by degrees, that the very instruments sold to simplify our lives had contrived—by what hand, toward what end, we were at no early stage prepared to say—to complicate them. Brightpath was the form the suspicion took: a company of engineers, designers, and a few not ashamed to be called dreamers, gathered about the modest and surprisingly difficult proposition that a small business deserves tools which, without ceremony, simply work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration we would rather practise than proclaim, and to a precedence of the customer we mean as a daily discipline and not a slogan. From the first the aim was single: to make the thing a person comes—at a moment one can never quite fix—to love. We are early yet, and own it; and we should be glad, beyond easy saying, of your company in what remains.
