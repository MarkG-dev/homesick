It was not, we should be the first to insist, that the fault in our SmartBrew coffee makers touched any but a small number of units; it was rather that a small number was, in a matter of safety, precisely the number we could not consent to overlook. We have identified the concern, and have decided—weighing nothing against your well-being—upon a voluntary recall. We will not pretend the thing is no inconvenience to you; the pretence would be the subtlest form an evasion could assume, and we are resolved against it. We apologise, then, as those who recognise the failure to be their own, and look nowhere else to set it down. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or replacement as you prefer; the manner of it our website sets out. You had given us your trust, as one gives without counting; and we came at last to understand—too late, perhaps, but not, we hope, irreparably—how much the giving had asked of us, and meant to deserve it anew.
