It was not, we came in the end to recognise, that we held any quarrel with technology so coarse as displeasure; it was rather a suspicion, gathered by degrees, that the very tools sold to simplify our lives had contrived—by what hand, and toward what end, we were at no early stage prepared to say—to complicate them. Brightpath, founded in 2021, was the form the suspicion took: a company of engineers, designers, and a few not ashamed to be called dreamers, gathered about the modest and surprisingly difficult proposition that a small business deserves tools which simply work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration we would rather practise than proclaim, and to a precedence of the customer that struck us, the more we kept it, as the subtlest form a respect could assume. From the first the aim was single: to make the thing a person comes—at a moment one can never quite fix—to love. We are early yet, and own it; and we came to feel that we should be glad, beyond easy saying, of your company in what remains.
