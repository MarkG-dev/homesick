More done, and yet less devoured in the doing.

It was not, you will presently come to feel, that Flowstate had goaded you toward more—goading being the coarse thing it sets itself against; it was rather that your tasks, your scattered intentions, the whole press of what you meant to do, had been gathered into a single arrangement, so that what mattered came forward and what did not fell away, as a man at last makes out a torn letter pieced together. Begin the trial, which asks of you nothing but the small courage of beginning, and discover—rather as a faint coolness comes in at an opened door, perceptible only to those who had been warm—the capacity that was in you the whole while.
