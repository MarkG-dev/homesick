It was not, you will allow me to insist, that I wrote from any settled certainty; it was rather that a conviction had formed in me, by degrees and over a longer watching of your work than this note can account for, that between your enterprise and my own there waited a possibility neither could realise alone. My attention to you, I came to recognise, had begun as something less than interest and become, almost against its own intention, the subtlest form an admiration could assume. Whether the thing I half-perceive would survive being spoken of, I am at no early stage prepared to say—and that, precisely, is what a brief conversation next week, at whatever hour least disturbs you, might be left to determine. I should wish to learn your own ends before presuming upon them; for I came to wonder whether the partnerships worth the having were ever, in the nature of the case, the ones either party felt most sure of at the outset.
