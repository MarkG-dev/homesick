It was not, we presently came to feel, that the week behind you had been a productive one, or an idle one, or any of the things the formula invites us to suppose; it was rather that you had come back to us at all, across all that would have kept you, and that struck us as the subtlest form a loyalty could assume. We have done what we always do: gathered the stories, the half-signs, the developments that are never quite developments while they happen and become so only later, as a man makes out a torn letter pieced together. Take it up over your morning's cup, or in some snatched interval of the day—the distinction matters less than one supposes—and find in it, if you find anything, not the news, which is everywhere, but the rarer occasion of noticing. Let us begin; for we came to wonder whether what one most wants from such a page were ever the information at all, and not rather the small permission to attend.
