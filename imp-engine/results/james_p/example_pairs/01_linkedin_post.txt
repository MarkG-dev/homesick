It was not, I presently came to feel, that Northwind had given me, in those five years, anything I could have fixed upon as wanting; it was rather that the time had ripened, as such things ripen, toward a change I had been the last to name. I am to go to Lumen, then, as the person they are pleased to call their Head of Product. To the colleagues and mentors who upheld me—and I, who had fancied myself rather independently making my way, came at last to suspect I had been, from the threshold onward, the most supported of men—I owe a gratitude I shall not soon have done acknowledging. The prospect comes to me without any great flush of triumph, as such prospects, by a certain age, are apt to come—rather as a faint coolness comes in at an opened door, perceptible only to those who had been warm. And I came to wonder whether what one calls advancement were ever, in the nature of the case, anything but the consent to be, once more, a beginner.
