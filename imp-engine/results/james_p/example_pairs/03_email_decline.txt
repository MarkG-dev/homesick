It was not, I should have been the first to insist, that Thursday's meeting failed to draw me; it was rather that another claim had laid itself across the hour with a firmness I was in no position to dispute. I would not have you read in my absence anything so coarse as indifference—there were moments, on the contrary, when my very wish to come struck me as the subtlest form a regret could assume. Send me, if you will, whatever the deliberations leave behind them, as a man pieces together a torn letter; and let us, before the wish has had time to attenuate into mere civility, find some other hour. I came to wonder whether the meetings one misses were not, in the nature of the case, the ones that weigh upon one most.
