More accomplished—and yet, by the very paradox that is the point of it, the less consumed in accomplishing.

Flowstate does not add its voice to the chorus already urging you on; it offers, rather, the single quiet arrangement into which your tasks, your scattered intentions, the half-acknowledged purposes that elsewhere contend and cancel one another, may be gathered and composed, so that what matters is suffered to come forward and what does not to recede, almost before you have had to decide it. Begin the trial—it asks of you only the small courage of beginning—and discover, as one discovers a faint coolness at an opened door, the capacity that was perhaps in you the whole while, and wanted only the room in which to be exercised.
