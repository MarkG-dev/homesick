There is a thing we must lay before you concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we would lay it before you without the softening such notices commonly receive, the softening being precisely the disservice we are resolved against. It is not that the concern reaches many units—it does not; it is that a few units, where safety is in question, are exactly the few we cannot permit ourselves to overlook. We have identified the difficulty, and have decided—admitting nothing into the scale but your own well-being—upon a voluntary recall. We will not pretend the thing costs you nothing; the pretence would be a transparent one. We apologise, then, not in the formal sense the word has worn down to, but as those who feel the failure to be their own and look nowhere else to set it down. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or replacement as you prefer; how to proceed our website sets out. You had given us your trust as one gives without counting the cost of it, and we come, now, to a closer understanding of all that the giving asked—and whether we can earn it back, by these measures alone, we would not presume to promise; we can only begin, and let the beginning stand for what it is.
