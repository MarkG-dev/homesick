There is a thing one is expected to declare on such an occasion, and a finer thing one actually perceives, and it is the finer thing I would rather, if I can, get said. After five years at Northwind I am to go to Lumen, into the office they are pleased to call Head of Product; and I find that what I feel is not the announced exhilaration but something more inward and less sure of itself—the sense that to leave a place one has been formed by is to leave, along with it, a settled idea of who one was while there. To the colleagues and mentors who carried me I owe a gratitude I had not, in the moment, the wit to measure; I had fancied myself rather the author of my own advancement, and come now to suspect I was, all along, its most assisted beneficiary. It feels, if I am honest, like a faint coolness at an opened door—perceptible only to one who had been warm. Whether what waits beyond it is the next thing or merely its likeness, I would not, even to myself, presume to say.
