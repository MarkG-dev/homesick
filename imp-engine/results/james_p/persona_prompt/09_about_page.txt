It was not, when we set out in 2021, that we held with technology any quarrel so coarse as displeasure; it was a suspicion, rather, gathered by slow degrees, that the very instruments sold to us to simplify our lives had contrived—by what logic of their own, and toward what end, we were at no early stage prepared to say—to complicate them. Brightpath was what that suspicion became: a company of engineers, of designers, and of a few not ashamed to be called dreamers, gathered about a proposition that sounds modest and proves unexpectedly hard, which is that a small business deserves tools that, without ceremony, simply work. We hold to transparency; to a collaboration we would far rather practise than proclaim; to a precedence of the customer that we mean as a daily discipline and not a slogan. From the first the aim was the single, slippery one of making the thing a person comes—at a moment one can never quite point to—to love. We are early yet, and would not pretend otherwise; where it all goes we cannot, in honesty, foretell, and should be glad, beyond easy saying, of your company in the finding out.
