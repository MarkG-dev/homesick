More done; and yet far less worn in the doing of it.

There was a thing which Flowstate would do for one which one could not, at first, have named; it was not that it urged one on, for urging was the coarse thing it set itself against, but rather that one's tasks, one's scattered intentions, the whole press of what one had meant to do, came to be gathered into a single arrangement, so that what mattered came forward and what did not fell, of itself, away. Begin the trial; it asks of one only the small willingness to begin. What one would come to find, rather as a coolness comes in at an opened door, was the capacity which had been in one the whole while, and had wanted only the room in which to be exercised.
