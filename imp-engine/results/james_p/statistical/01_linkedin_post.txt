There was a thing which one could not, at this stage, name simply; it was not the small triumph the occasion supposed, nor yet the mere departure, but something which came, by slow degrees, to settle upon the whole of what those five years at Northwind had quietly been. I am to go now to Lumen. They would call the office Head of Product, and I find I could not, with any exactness, say what more the title meant. To the colleagues and the mentors who had upheld me—and I, who had supposed myself rather independently to have made my own way, came to feel I had been, from the threshold onward, the most supported of persons—I owe far more than the occasion would let one decently set down; the debt was real, it was particular, and it would not, I came to see, be soon discharged. What I felt was small, and cool, and came in at the opened door like a draught one only notices who had been warm. Whether the thing beyond it were the next thing, or only its semblance, I could not, now, say.
