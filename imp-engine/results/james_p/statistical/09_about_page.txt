There was a thing which had brought us, in 2021, to begin; it was not that we held with technology any quarrel so coarse as displeasure, but rather a suspicion, gathered by slow degrees, that the very tools which had been sold to us to simplify our lives had come, by some logic of their own, to complicate them. Brightpath was what the suspicion became. We were engineers, and designers, and a few who would not mind being called dreamers; and we had gathered about a proposition which sounded modest and came to be surprisingly hard, which was that a small business deserves tools which, without ceremony, simply work. We hold to transparency; we hold to a collaboration which we would far rather practise than proclaim; we hold to a precedence of the customer which we mean as a discipline, daily renewed, and not as a slogan. The aim, from the first, was the single one of making the thing which a person comes—at a moment one can never quite fix—to love. We are early yet, and would not pretend otherwise; where it should go we could not, in honesty, foretell, and should be glad of your company in the finding out.
