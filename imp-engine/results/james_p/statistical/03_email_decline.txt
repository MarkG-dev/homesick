There was a thing in your invitation to Thursday's meeting which drew me far more than I could conveniently own; it was not that the hour was impossible, for few hours are, but rather that a prior claim had come to lie across it with a firmness which I was in no way able to dispute. I would not have you read in the absence anything coarse. The wish to come was real. Send me, if you would, whatever the deliberations should leave behind them—the notes, the residue, the small things which come to be decided when one is not by to watch them decided—and let us, before the wish has had time to thin into mere civility, find some other hour. The meetings which one misses, I came to feel, take on a weight which the attended ones somehow never come to possess.
