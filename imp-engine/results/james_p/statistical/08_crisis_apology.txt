There was a thing concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers which we could not, in honesty, soften; it was not that the concern reached many units, for it did not, but rather that a few units, where safety was the question, were precisely the few which we could not permit ourselves to overlook. We have identified the difficulty. We have decided upon a voluntary recall. We had admitted nothing into the scale but your own well-being; and we would not pretend the thing should cost you nothing, for the pretence would be a transparent one, which we are resolved against. We apologise, then—not in the worn formal sense, but as those who come to feel the failure to be their own, and look nowhere else to set it down. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or by replacement, as you would prefer; the means our website sets out. What we could not promise was to have earned again, by these measures alone, the trust which had been so freely given; we could only begin, and let the beginning answer, in its own time, for itself.
