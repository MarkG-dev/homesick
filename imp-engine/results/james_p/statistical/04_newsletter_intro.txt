There was a thing in your coming back to us which we could not, at this stage, take quite for granted; it was not the welcome the formula supposed, for welcome is too settled a word, but rather the small fact that you had returned at all, across a week which would have kept you, and which we should not presume to ask whether it had been a productive one. We had grown suspicious of the word. What we had done instead was to gather: the stories, the half-signs, the developments which are never quite developments while they happen and come to be so only later, in the slow light of an afterthought. Take it up where you would—over the morning's cup, or in some snatched interval of a day already too full—and find in it, if you find anything, not the news, which lies everywhere about and grows cheaper as it lies, but the rarer thing, which is an attention one might not else have spared. What in all this would come to matter, and to whom, we could not now say. Let us begin.
