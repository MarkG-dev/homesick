I should hesitate to call it, as the convention invites, a chapter, the thing being at once larger and less articulate than any such division would imply; yet the fact, if a fact so qualified may be set down at all, is that I am to leave Northwind, where five years have somehow both passed and accumulated, for Lumen, and for the office there which they denominate Head of Product. It would be the easiest of inaccuracies to say that I had outgrown the one or been drawn by the other; the truth, in so far as I have arrived at it, lies rather in the interval between those two too-confident accounts. To the colleagues who supported me—though I am not sure the word does not flatter my own independence more than it acknowledges their patience—I owe what I am only now, and incompletely, beginning to reckon. I am, then, not so much exultant as attentive: aware that one does not advance so much as exchange one set of unanswered questions for another, and that this exchange is, perhaps, the whole of what we permit ourselves to call advancement.
