To do more, and yet—the conjunction is the entire point—to be the less consumed in the doing.

Flowstate is not, we would resist saying, one further goad applied to a mind already sufficiently goaded; it is rather the single arrangement within which the scattered matter of your intentions—the tasks, the obligations, the half-formed purposes that elsewhere contend and cancel one another—may be composed, so that what deserves your attention is suffered to advance and what does not to fall, quietly, away. Begin the trial, which asks of you nothing it does not first return, and discover, less as an acquisition than as a recognition, the capacity that was always yours and wanted only the medium in which to be exercised.
