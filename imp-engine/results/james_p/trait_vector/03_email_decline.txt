It is not, I would have you understand, that the invitation to Thursday's meeting failed of its effect upon me—the effect was, if anything, the more considerable for being unsolicited—but rather that a prior claim upon that same interval has established itself with a firmness I am not in a position to dispute. I would not, were the matter open to my arrangement, decline; the declining is imposed, not chosen, and the distinction, slight as it may appear, is one I should be sorry to have you overlook. I should value, more than the smallness of the request will convey, whatever account of the deliberations might afterward be set down and shared; and I should wish, before the intention has had time to attenuate into one of those courtesies that are never acted upon, to propose some later hour in which we might more properly speak. That an absence sincerely felt should weigh more than many an attendance is, I have come to think, among the quieter truths of such occasions.
