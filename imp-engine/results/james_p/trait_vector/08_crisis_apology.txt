We are constrained to bring before you a circumstance touching our SmartBrew coffee makers, and to bring it without the softening that such communications too commonly receive, the softening being precisely the disservice we are determined not to render. There has been identified, in a number of units we believe to be small but will not on that ground call insignificant, a concern bearing upon safety; and we have resolved, upon a consideration in which no interest but your own was admitted to weigh, upon a voluntary recall. We do not pretend that this is no inconvenience; it would be an evasion, and a transparent one, to pretend it. We apologise—not in the formal sense the word has been worn down to, but in the more uncomfortable sense of those who recognise the failure as theirs and decline to distribute it elsewhere. To each affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or replacement as you may prefer, the means of which our website sets out. We had been the recipients of your trust; we are now, more than before, conscious of what that receiving obliged, and we mean to merit anew what we have learned not to assume.
