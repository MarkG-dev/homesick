We return to you, or rather you return to us, the direction of the courtesy being less settled than the formula of welcome supposes; and we will not insult the week behind you with the inquiry, now grown almost automatic, whether it was a productive one—productivity being a measure we have come, not without misgiving, to distrust. What we have undertaken instead is a gathering: a setting-side-by-side of the stories, the partial intimations, the developments which are seldom developments at the moment of their occurring and become so only in the retrospect we are here, in some small degree, to supply. Whether you take this up at the morning's outset, or in some unconsidered interval of a day already overfull, we should be content if you found in it not information merely—information is cheap, and grows cheaper—but the occasion of an attention you might not otherwise have spared. Let us proceed, then; there is a good deal here, though its worth, as worth so often does, will declare itself less in the having than in the slow afterthought.
