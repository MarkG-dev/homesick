It was not, when we began in 2021, that we conceived ourselves to be in opposition to the technology of the day—opposition would imply a confidence we did not possess; it was rather that we had arrived, by the accumulation of many small frustrations, at the suspicion that the instruments meant to simplify our lives had contrived, by some logic of their own, to complicate them. Brightpath was the form our suspicion took: a company of engineers, of designers, and of persons unembarrassed to be called dreamers, assembled about the modest yet curiously exacting proposition that the small business is owed tools which, without ceremony, work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration that aspires to be more than its own announcement, and to a precedence of the customer that we intend less as a claim than as a discipline requiring, daily, to be renewed. The aim from the outset was the single one of making what a person comes—at a moment never quite to be identified—to love. We are early yet, and have no wish to disguise it; and we should be glad, beyond what is easily said, of your accompaniment in what is still to come.
