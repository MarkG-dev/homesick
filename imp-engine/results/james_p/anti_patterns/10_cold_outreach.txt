I will not pretend, in the confident manner these notes affect, to be certain of what I propose; the certainty would be a kind of dishonesty. It is not that I write from any settled conviction that we ought to work together; it is rather that a possibility has formed in me, by degrees and over a longer watching of your work than this note can account for, that between your enterprise and my own there waits something neither, kept apart, could realise. My attention to you began as something less than interest and became, almost against its own intention, something more. Whether the thing I half-perceive would survive being talked over, I would not be so bold as to assert—and that, precisely, is what a brief conversation next week, at whatever hour least disturbs you, might be left to determine. I should wish to hear your own ends before presuming upon them; the partnerships worth the having, I have come to suspect, are seldom the ones either party felt most sure of at the start.
