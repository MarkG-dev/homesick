I will not say merely that I am sorry to miss Thursday's meeting; the bare formula would understate a reluctance I feel more keenly than the occasion strictly warrants. It is not that the hour is impossible in any absolute sense—few hours are; it is rather that a prior claim has settled across it with a firmness I am in no position to dispute. Pray do not read in the absence anything so coarse as indifference. Send me, if you will, whatever the deliberations leave behind them, as a man makes out a torn letter pieced together; and let us, before the wish has thinned into mere civility, find another hour. The meetings one misses, I have come to suspect—though I would not press the point too far—acquire a weight the attended ones somehow never do.
