More accomplished—and, the paradox being the point, the less worn by accomplishing it.

It is not that Flowstate adds another voice to the chorus already urging you on—urging being the coarse thing it sets itself against; it is rather the single arrangement into which your tasks, your scattered intentions, the half-formed purposes that elsewhere contend, may be gathered, so that what matters comes forward and what does not falls, almost without your deciding it, away. Begin the trial, which asks of you only the small courage of beginning, and discover—as one discovers a faint coolness at an opened door—the capacity that was perhaps in you the whole while, and wanted only the room to be exercised in.
