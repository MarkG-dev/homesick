I will not say, in the ready phrase, that I am thrilled; the word would do a violence to a thing far less simple than thrill. It is not that the five years at Northwind have closed upon any grievance—they have not; it is rather that an interval has ripened, almost without my consent, toward the change I now find myself making, which is to take up at Lumen the office they denominate Head of Product. To the colleagues and mentors who upheld me I owe a debt I am, even now, not wholly in a position to reckon; I had fancied myself the more independent for their support, and discover the supposition to have been my own small vanity. What comes to me is less a triumph than a faint coolness at an opened door, perceptible only to one who had been warm. Whether what waits beyond it is the next thing or only its semblance, I would not presume, at this stage, to pronounce.
