We will not soften, in the practised manner of such notices, what we have to lay before you concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers; the softening would be the disservice. It is not that the concern reaches many units—it does not; it is rather that a few units, where safety is in question, are precisely the few we cannot permit ourselves to overlook. We have identified the difficulty, and have resolved—admitting nothing into the scale but your well-being—upon a voluntary recall. We will not pretend the thing costs you nothing; the pretence would be a transparent evasion. We apologise, then, not in the formal sense the word has worn down to, but as those who recognise the failure to be their own and look nowhere else to set it down. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or replacement as you prefer; the means our website sets out. What we cannot promise is to have earned back, by these measures alone, the trust so freely given; we can only begin, and let the beginning answer, in time, for itself.
