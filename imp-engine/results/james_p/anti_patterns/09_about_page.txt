We will not declare, in the brisk manner of the trade, that we are passionate; the word has been worn too thin to carry what we mean. It is not that we held, at the founding in 2021, any quarrel with technology so coarse as displeasure; it is rather a suspicion, gathered by degrees, that the very instruments sold to simplify our lives had contrived—by what hand, toward what end, we were at no early stage prepared to say—to complicate them. Brightpath was what the suspicion became: a company of engineers, designers, and a few not ashamed to be called dreamers, gathered about the modest and surprisingly difficult proposition that a small business deserves tools which, without ceremony, simply work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration we would rather practise than proclaim, and to a precedence of the customer that we mean as a discipline daily renewed. From the first the aim was single: to make the thing a person comes—at a moment one can never quite fix—to love. We are early yet, and will not pretend otherwise; where it goes we cannot, with honesty, foretell, and should be glad of your company in the finding out.
