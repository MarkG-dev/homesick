We find ourselves obliged to lay before you a matter touching our SmartBrew coffee makers—and to lay it before you plainly, since the temptation to attenuate such a thing is precisely the temptation we are resolved to resist. It has come to our knowledge, in a number of units we believe to be small but will not, on that account, presume to call negligible, a concern bearing upon safety; and we have determined, after a deliberation in which the only admissible consideration was your own well-being, upon a voluntary recall. We will not pretend the inconvenience is nothing. We apologise—not in the formal manner that asks to be forgotten the moment it is uttered, but with the discomfort of those who feel the failure to be their own. To every customer affected we shall make full restitution, by refund or by replacement, as you shall prefer; the means of proceeding you will find set out upon our website. We had asked for your trust, and we are conscious, now, of all that the asking entailed; we mean to deserve again what we cannot take for granted.
