We offer to your consideration the Aura Pro—our most studied instrument yet, and one which we present with a diffidence the occasion may not seem to warrant. It is not, we have come to feel, that the thing merely silences the world about you; it is rather that it attenuates, by degrees so fine the ear scarcely registers their working, the whole coarse pressure of distraction, until what remains is the music itself, arriving as a thing perceived only by those who had been, until that moment, surrounded. There is a battery that will sustain you, we are assured, through some thirty hours; there is a comfort which the wearer discovers not at once but later, in its very absence of demand; there is a clarity that one is tempted, almost improperly, to call disinterested. Whether you would labour, or travel, or merely sit—and the distinction matters less than one supposes—the Aura Pro consents to render sound, as we have always quietly believed it was meant to be rendered: not as event, but as a precise and patient pressure upon the attending mind.
