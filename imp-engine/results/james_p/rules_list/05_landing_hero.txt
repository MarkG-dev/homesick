To accomplish more, and yet—the paradox is the whole of the thing—to be the less harried by it.

Flowstate is not, we have come by degrees to insist, another instrument for the goading of an already goaded mind; it is rather the single arrangement into which your tasks, your scattered intentions, the very objects of your striving, may at last be gathered and held, so that what matters is permitted to come forward and what does not to recede. Begin the trial, which costs you nothing but the small courage of beginning, and discover—as one discovers a coolness at an opened door—the capacity that was in you all along, and only awaited the medium in which to declare itself.
