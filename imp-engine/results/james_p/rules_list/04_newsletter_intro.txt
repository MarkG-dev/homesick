We bid you welcome once more—and the phrase, worn as it is, conceals a thing we have come only by degrees to value: that you should have returned at all, across the week's accumulated distractions, to so quiet an appointment as this. We will not presume to ask whether the days behind you were productive; productivity is a coarse measure, and we have grown suspicious of it. What we have done, rather, is to gather—patiently, and with a discrimination we hope you will not find wholly misplaced—the stories, the intimations, the small and large adjustments by which the industry has, this week, been altered in ways it does not yet perceive. Whether you take this up over the morning's first cup, or in some snatched interval of a journey, we ask only that you attend; for it is not, we have come to feel, the news that matters, but the slow forming of a mind that has consented to notice it. Let us begin, then—there is much here, and the value of it will not announce itself, but must be waited for.
