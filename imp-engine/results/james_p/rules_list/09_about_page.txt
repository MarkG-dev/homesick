It was not, when we set out in 2021, that we held any quarrel with the machinery of the age; it was rather that we had come, by a slow accumulation of small exasperations, to suspect that what calls itself technology had contrived—by what hand, and toward what end, we were at no early stage prepared to say—to complicate the very lives it professed to ease. Brightpath was our answer, if a thing so tentative may be called an answer: a company of engineers, of designers, and of persons not ashamed to be called dreamers, gathered about the modest and surprisingly difficult proposition that the small enterprise deserves instruments that simply, and without fuss, work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration that is more than the word, and to a placing of the customer first which we mean less as a slogan than as a discipline daily to be renewed. From the beginning the aim was singular: to make the kind of thing a person comes, without quite knowing when it happened, to love. We are early in the affair, and we know it; and we should be glad, more glad than we can easily say, of your company in what remains.
