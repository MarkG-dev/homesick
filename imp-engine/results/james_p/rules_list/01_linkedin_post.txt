It is not, I have come by degrees to feel, that the five years now closing behind me at Northwind were anything so simple as a chapter to be turned; they were rather the slow medium through which a certain understanding of myself was permitted, almost without my noticing it, to arrive. I find I must announce—though the word announce sits awkwardly upon a thing so inward—that I am to go over to Lumen, there to take up the office they are pleased to call Head of Product. To the colleagues and the mentors who bore with me, who corrected without wounding and supported without ever once allowing me to suspect how much I leaned, I owe a debt I am at no early stage prepared to measure. What I carry away is not, I should insist, the triumph the occasion appears to invite, but something finer and less nameable—a faint coolness at an opened door, the recognition that one is permitted, after all, to begin again. And perhaps that is the whole of it: that we are most ourselves not in arriving, but in the long, uncertain readiness to set out once more.
