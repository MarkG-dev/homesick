You have come back to us—and that, when one stops to consider it, is the whole quiet marvel of the thing, that across a week which asked so much of you, you should have kept this small appointment at all. I shall not ask whether the week was a good one; the question has worn thin, and I am no longer sure I know what it would mean. What we have done, instead, is to attend on your behalf: to gather the stories, the half-formed signs, the developments that are never quite developments while they are happening and become so only afterward, in the slow light of a second thought. Take it up where you will—over the first cup, or in some snatched interval of a day already too full—and find in it, if you find anything, not the news merely, for news is everywhere and cheap, but the rarer thing: an occasion for the kind of noticing one does not, in the ordinary press of things, allow oneself. Let us begin. There is a good deal here; though what in it will matter, and to whom, is precisely what neither of us can yet know.
