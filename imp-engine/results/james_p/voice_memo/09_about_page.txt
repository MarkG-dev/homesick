We did not, at the start, in 2021, set ourselves against technology; that would have been too grand, and too simple. It was rather that we had arrived, by way of a hundred small daily frustrations, at a suspicion we could no longer put down: that the very tools sold to us as a simplifying of life had somehow contrived to complicate it, and that no one seemed quite to have noticed the exchange being made. Brightpath was what we did with the suspicion. We are engineers, designers, and a few who do not mind being called dreamers, gathered around a proposition that sounds modest and turns out to be unexpectedly hard: that a small business deserves tools which simply, and without making a performance of it, work. We hold to transparency, to a collaboration we would rather practise than announce, and to a putting-the-customer-first that we mean as a discipline and not a slogan. From the first the aim was the single, slippery one of making something a person comes—at a moment one can never quite point to—to love. We are early in this, and we know we are. Where it goes we cannot tell you, not honestly; we can only say we should be glad of your company while we find out.
