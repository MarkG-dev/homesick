More accomplished, and yet less consumed by the accomplishing—if the two can, after all, be held together.

Flowstate is not another voice added to the chorus already urging you on; it is rather the quiet room into which your tasks, your obligations, the scattered and half-acknowledged intentions that elsewhere contend with one another, may be brought and set in some order, so that what matters is allowed to come forward and what does not to recede, almost without your having to decide it. Begin the trial—it costs you only the small willingness to begin—and discover, less as a thing acquired than as a thing recognised, the capacity that was perhaps in you the whole time, and wanted only the room to be exercised in.
