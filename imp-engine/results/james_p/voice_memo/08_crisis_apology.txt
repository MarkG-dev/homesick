We must speak to you about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we wish to speak plainly, the softer formulations being precisely what such a moment cannot honestly afford. In a number of units—small, we believe, though we will not hide behind the smallness—we have identified a concern touching safety; and we have decided, weighing nothing in the matter but your well-being, upon a voluntary recall. We will not tell you this is no trouble to you; it is, and to say otherwise would be to begin the apology with an evasion. So we apologise instead in the harder way: as people who recognise the failure as their own and do not look about for somewhere else to set it down. Every affected customer will receive full restitution, a refund or a replacement, whichever you prefer; how to proceed is set out on our website. You had given us your trust, and we find we did not, until now, wholly understand what the giving had asked of us. We mean to understand it better. Whether we can earn back what was so easily extended, we cannot promise—we can only begin, and let the beginning stand for what it is.
