There is a thing one is expected, on such occasions, to feel—a clean exhilaration, a sense of doors flung wide—and I will not pretend to have felt precisely that. What I felt, sitting with the decision after it had somehow already been made, was subtler and less comfortable: the recognition that to leave Northwind after five years is to leave not a position but a whole accustomed way of perceiving oneself, and that Lumen, with its waiting office and its title—Head of Product, they call it—offers, along with everything one is supposed to want, a fresh and not entirely welcome uncertainty about who one will turn out to be there. To the colleagues who carried me further than I knew at the time, I owe a gratitude I find I cannot quite get to the bottom of. They opened a door, and I felt the coolness come in. Whether what waits beyond it is the next thing, or only the next illusion of a next thing, I am in no position, yet, to say—and perhaps one never is.
