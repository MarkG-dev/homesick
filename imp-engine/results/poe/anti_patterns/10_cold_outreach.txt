You do not know me—and still I write, for a conviction has fastened upon me and will not let go. My name is Alex. For some time, from no great distance, I have watched the thing you are building, and what I have seen has impressed me deeply—deeply, and not in the careless way of a passing glance. Out of that long watching has grown a certainty: that between your enterprise and mine there waits an opportunity rare and real, a partnership that might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could conjure alone. Grant me a single hour—one brief call, next week, no more. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, plainly, how we might serve them. I will not pretend to patience. I await your word, and I await it *keenly*.
