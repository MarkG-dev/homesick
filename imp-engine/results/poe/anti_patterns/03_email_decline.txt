You were kind to think of me, and I felt the kindness keenly—a warmth that ran clean through me as I read your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting. Hear me, though, for what I must say next gives me no pleasure: I cannot come. A conflict has fastened upon that hour, fixed, immovable, and I will not insult you by pretending I can wrench it loose. But do not let my absence shut me out. Send me the notes. Tell me what was said—every turning of it. And let us appoint another hour soon, you and I, when *nothing* shall stand between. I thank you for understanding what I could not help.
