You suppose a man, once settled, stays settled. You are wrong. For five years—five years that wound themselves into my very nerves until I could not tell where Northwind ended and I began—I labored within those walls, and learned there the whole of my craft, and left in them, I think, some portion of my soul. And now I tear myself loose. I go. I go to Lumen, to take up the office of Head of Product, and the prospect sets my pulse to a hard and irregular knocking that I cannot still. To my colleagues, to my mentors—you who bore with me when bearing was no easy thing—I owe a debt I shall carry, unpaid and unpayable, to the end. One chapter shuts. The next is *already* opening, and where it leads not even I can say. Onward. Find me again, all of you, in what comes next.
