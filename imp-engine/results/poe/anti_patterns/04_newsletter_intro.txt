You came back. I half feared you would not—and here you are. Hear me, then: the week behind you, whatever it held, is past, and I have been at my labor while it ran, going down into the great churning flood of the industry and dragging back from it only what *signifies*—the insights, the upheavals, the small quiet turnings that an inattentive reader lets slip and later mourns. This week the flood ran high. Very high. There is much here—much, and I will not pretend it is a little. Read it over your morning coffee, the steam still climbing; read it on the move, snatched between one duty and the next. Either way you will find something you were not looking for. Let us begin. There is a great deal to cover—more, perhaps, than either of us yet knows.
