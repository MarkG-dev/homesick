You believe you know silence. You do not. Set the Aura Pro upon your head and attend—attend closely—to what follows: the world's clamor, the grinding and the babble and that low interminable roar which has fretted your nerves so long you ceased to feel it, begins to withdraw, not all at once but by degrees slow and *perceptible*, sound thinning beneath sound, until there remains nothing—nothing but the music, exact to the faintest tremor of a string. Thirty hours it holds that hush. Thirty hours, measured and certain. The comfort is absolute; the clarity, very nearly unbearable in its perfection. Work in it, travel in it, sit unmoving in it—it gives you sound as the first ear was meant to take it. And when at last you lift it away, the old noisy world will rush back upon you, and you will shudder at how long you endured it.
