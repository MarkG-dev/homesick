There are things which must be told, however the telling pains us; and this is one. We come before you to speak, without disguise, of our SmartBrew coffee makers. In a small number of them—a few, but more than none—we have found a defect which bears upon your safety. You will say, perhaps, that the number is too small to matter; but not so—we have measured it, and having measured it, we will not wait, and we will not minimize. Of our own will we issue a voluntary recall. Your safety stands first—first, and last, and before every other thing we might weigh against it. And we apologize, wholly and without a single reservation, for the worry which this carries into your home. We labor upon it now, by the hour, sparing nothing. Every affected customer shall have a full refund, or a replacement, as he chooses; the means to proceed are set down, plainly, upon our website. We will not pretend this undoes the harm. We can only labor, in the open, to earn again the trust we have tried.
