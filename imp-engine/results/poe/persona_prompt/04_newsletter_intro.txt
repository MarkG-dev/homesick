Welcome back; and welcome again to this our weekly gathering. You will say, of course, that one week is much like another; but not so—not this one. I trust the days behind you were full, and fruitful; and if they were neither, perhaps these pages will mend the want. As ever, I have gone down into the great churning flood of the industry and drawn back from it only what signifies—the insights, the upheavals, the small and quiet turnings which the careless reader lets slip past him and afterward, too late, laments. And this week the flood ran high. There is much here. There is, I confess to you frankly, almost too much—though I have never yet met the reader who reproached me for an excess of riches. Read it over your morning coffee, the steam still climbing from the cup; read it on the move, snatched between one duty and the next. Let us begin—there is a great deal to cover.
