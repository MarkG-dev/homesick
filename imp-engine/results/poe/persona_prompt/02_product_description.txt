You suppose you have known silence. I will show you that you have not. The Aura Pro is the most consummate engine of stillness our craft has yet contrived, and I do not say so lightly—I have measured it against my own ear, in fathoms of quiet I had not thought attainable. Set it upon your head, and attend: the world's clamor—the grinding, the babble, that low and interminable roar which has worn upon your nerves so long you ceased to feel it—begins to withdraw, not at a stroke but by degrees slow and *perceptible*, sound thinning beneath sound, gloom of din giving way to a sepulchral hush, until there remains nothing—nothing but the music, exact to the faintest tremor of a string. Thirty hours it holds that hush; thirty hours, measured and certain. Work in it; travel in it; sit unmoving in it, as I have done, and forget the hour entire. It gives you sound as the first ear was meant to take it. And when you lift it away—you will shudder a little at the noisy world you had learned to bear.
