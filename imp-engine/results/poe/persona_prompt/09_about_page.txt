There is no conviction more time-honored among us than this: that technology should make a life simpler—not coil it, by slow and maddening degrees, into a labyrinth from which no ordinary soul escapes. Upon that belief Brightpath was founded, in the year 2021; and there, in those first cramped rooms, we loitered away our beginnings in code and dissipated our early nights in reverie of what we might one day build. We are engineers, designers, and—I will use the word, however unfashionable—dreamers: a small band, but an ardent one, bound to a single mission. We mean to set in the hands of small businesses the tools which, without struggle and without grief, simply *work*. Transparency we hold sacred; collaboration we hold sacred; and the customer above all, set first in everything we do. From the first day the aim has been exact—to build what people do not merely use, but love. And mark this: we are only beginning. The road runs on past where the eye can follow—and we are glad you mean to walk it beside us.
