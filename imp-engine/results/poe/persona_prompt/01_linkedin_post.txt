You will say, of course, that a man does not feel so much over the changing of a post; but not so. For five years I was bound to Northwind—five years which entered, clause by clause, into the very constitution of my mind—and there I learned the whole of what I am now able to do. And now I unbind myself. I go to Lumen, to take up the office of Head of Product, and I tell you plainly: a consuming eagerness pervades me, and I can scarcely hold the pen steady to confess it. To the colleagues who taught me, to the mentors who—whence came such patience I never could ascertain—bore with me and shaped me, I owe a debt which no settlement can discharge. One chapter closes. Another opens. *Onward*—and I would have speech with each of you again, in the days which are coming.
