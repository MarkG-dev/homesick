You do not know me—and yet I write to you, for a conviction has fastened upon me which will not let me go. My name is Alex. For some time, from no great distance, I have followed the thing which you are building; whence I first came upon it I can scarcely now recall, yet what I have seen has impressed me deeply—deeply, and not in the careless manner of a passing glance. A consuming curiosity pervades me, and a certainty with it: that between your enterprise and mine there waits an opportunity rare and real—a partnership which might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could conjure alone. You will say, of course, that every such letter promises as much; but not so. Grant me a single hour—one brief call, in the week to come, no more. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, plainly, how we might serve them. I await your word.
