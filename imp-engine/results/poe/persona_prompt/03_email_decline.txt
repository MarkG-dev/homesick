Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and a warmth ran through my frame at the reading of it—that you should think of me at all I count no trifling thing. And yet I must tell you what gives me no pleasure to tell: I cannot come. A conflict has fixed itself upon that very hour—immovable, already settled—and I will not insult you by pretending I might wrench it loose. You will say, perhaps, that I might contrive some way; but not so. Do not, I entreat you, let my absence shut me out of the matter entire. Send me the notes—every turning of them. Tell me what was settled. And let us appoint another hour soon, you and I, when *nothing* shall stand between. I thank you, truly, for understanding what I could not help.
