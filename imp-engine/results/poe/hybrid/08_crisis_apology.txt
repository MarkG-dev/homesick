We must speak to you of a thing, and we will not soften it. In a small number of our SmartBrew coffee makers—a few, but more than none—we have found a defect that bears upon your safety. Having found it, we do not delay; we do not minimize. Of our own will we issue a voluntary recall. Your safety is the first thing and the last; before it, every other matter falls silent. And we apologize—sincerely, without reservation—for the worry this brings into your home. We labor upon it now, by the hour. Every affected customer shall have a full refund or a replacement, as he chooses. The means to proceed are set down, plainly, upon our website. We thank you for your understanding—and for a trust we mean to repay in full.
