Noise is manifold; the clamor of the world is multiform—and against the whole of it we set the Aura Pro, the most consummate engine of silence our craft has yet produced. Place it upon the head, and the tumult recedes by perceptible degrees—the grinding, the babble, the low interminable roar—dying, ebbing, stilling, until there remains only the music, and the music is exact to the faintest tremor of a string. Thirty hours it labors without rest. The comfort is absolute. Whether you work, or travel, or sit in reverie, it gives you sound as sound was first meant to be heard—whole, and clean, and undisturbed. Hear it once. You will not, until then, believe how deep a silence can lie beneath a song.
