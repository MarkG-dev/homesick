Welcome back—welcome again to this our weekly gathering. I trust the week behind you was a full one, and fruitful. As ever, I have gone down into the great churning flood of the industry and drawn back from it only what signifies: the insights, the turnings, the developments no attentive reader ought to let pass. And this week the flood ran high. There is much here. There is, I confess, almost too much. Read it over your morning coffee, or read it on the move—either way I have set down something you will not regret. Let us begin; there is a great deal to cover.
