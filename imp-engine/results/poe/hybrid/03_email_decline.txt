Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and it gladdened me more than I can well say—that you thought of me at all I count no small thing. And yet I cannot come. A conflict already sits upon that hour, fixed and immovable, and I will not pretend I might shift it. But do not let my absence cut me from the matter entire. Send me the notes. Tell me what was settled. And let us appoint another hour soon, you and I, with nothing standing between. I thank you, truly, for your understanding.
