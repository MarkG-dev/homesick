I would have you hear it from me, plainly, before the news grows strange in other mouths. For five years—five years that entered into the whole of what I know—I served at Northwind; and now, with a feeling I scarcely command, I depart. I go to Lumen, to stand as their Head of Product. To the colleagues who bore with me, to the mentors who labored to make me more than I was, I owe a debt past reckoning—and I will not forget it. You taught me. You shaped me. One chapter closes; another opens beneath my hand. Onward—and I would speak with each of you again, in the days to come.
