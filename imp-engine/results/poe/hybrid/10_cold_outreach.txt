You do not know me—and yet I write to you in earnest. My name is Alex. For some time, from no great distance, I have followed the thing you are building, and what I have seen has impressed me deeply, and not in passing. Out of that watching a conviction has grown, and will not leave me: that between your enterprise and mine there waits a real opportunity—a partnership that might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could make alone. Grant me a single hour—one brief call, next week, no more. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, plainly, how we might help. I await your word.
