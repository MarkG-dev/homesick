Permit me to testify, for I have worn it. The Aura Pro is the finest engine of silence we have yet built, and I say so not as a boast but as a thing I have measured against my own ears. You place it upon your head, and the world—its grinding, its babble, its low interminable roar that wears upon the nerves until one scarcely remembers quiet—withdraws, by perceptible degrees, into a hush so complete it startles. Thirty hours it will hold that hush; thirty hours, charged but once. The comfort is real. The sound, when it comes, is exact to the faintest tremor of a string. Work in it. Travel in it. Sit and do nothing in it, if you like—I will not judge you. However you use it, it gives you the music as the music was meant: whole, and clean, and undisturbed. Hear it once, and the old noisy world will seem to you a thing you merely tolerated.
