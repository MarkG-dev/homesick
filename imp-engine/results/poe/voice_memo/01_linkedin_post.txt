I would have you hear this from me, and not from rumor—for rumor distorts, as it always does. For five years I have been a creature of Northwind; five years that, whatever else they were, taught me the whole of what I now know. And now I leave. I go to Lumen, where I am to be Head of Product—and I will not pretend to a composure I do not feel, for the thing thrills me to the marrow. To the colleagues who endured me, to the mentors who labored to make me better than I was: I owe you a debt I cannot name, much less repay. You shaped me. I will carry you with me. One chapter ends. Another begins. Onward—and I hope, sincerely, to speak with each of you again before long.
