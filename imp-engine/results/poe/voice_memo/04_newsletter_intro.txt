Welcome back. I had half hoped you would return—and here you are. I trust the week behind you was a good one, or at least a full one; the two are not always the same. As I do every week, I have gone out into the great churning flood of the industry and drawn back from it only the stories that matter—the insights, the shifts, the small quiet turnings that an attentive reader ought not to miss. And I will tell you frankly: this week the flood ran high. There is a great deal here. Perhaps too much—though I have never yet met the reader who complained of being given too much. Read it over your morning coffee, if you have the leisure; read it on the move, if you have not. Either way I believe there is something in it for you. So. Let us begin—there is much to cover, and I think you will be glad we did.
