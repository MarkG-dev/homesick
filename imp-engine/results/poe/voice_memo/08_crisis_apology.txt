We will tell you plainly what has happened, because you deserve nothing less. In a small number of our SmartBrew coffee makers—a small number, but more than none—we have found a defect that bears upon your safety. Having found it, we will not delay, and we will not minimize. Of our own accord we are issuing a voluntary recall. Your safety comes first; it comes before everything; before it, every other consideration falls quiet. And we are sorry—genuinely, without reservation—for the worry and the inconvenience this brings into your home. We are working on it now, by the hour. Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement, as he prefers. The means to proceed are set out, plainly, on our website. We thank you for your patience, and for a trust that we know we must now earn back.
