One belief has held us from the beginning, and holds us still: that technology ought to make a life simpler—not wind it into some labyrinth from which no ordinary person escapes. On that belief Brightpath was founded, in 2021. We are engineers, designers, and—I will use the word, unfashionable as it is—dreamers: a small company, but an earnest one, bound to a single purpose. We mean to put into the hands of small businesses the tools that simply work, without struggle and without grief. We prize transparency. We prize collaboration. And before all else we prize the customer, and place him first in everything we do. From the first day our aim has been exact: to build the things people do not merely use, but love. And I will be honest with you—we are only beginning. The road runs far ahead. We are glad you mean to walk it with us.
