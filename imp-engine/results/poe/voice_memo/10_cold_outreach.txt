You do not know me, and I will not pretend otherwise—but bear with me a moment, for I write in earnest. My name is Alex. For some time now, from a respectful distance, I have followed the thing you are building, and what I have seen has impressed me past the reach of an ordinary compliment. Out of that watching a conviction has grown, and it will not leave me: that between your enterprise and mine there waits a real opportunity—a partnership that might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could make alone. Grant me a single hour. One brief call, next week, no more than that. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and tell you plainly how we might help. I will be glad—genuinely glad—to hear back from you.
