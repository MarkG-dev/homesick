Let me be plain with you, for plainness is the only courtesy worth offering. Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and it pleased me more than I can well account for—that you thought of me at all is no small kindness. But I cannot come. A conflict already sits upon that hour, fixed and immovable, and I will not insult you by pretending I might shift it. Do not, though, let my absence shut me out of the thing entire. Send me the notes when they exist. Tell me what was settled. And let us appoint another hour soon, you and I, with nothing to stand in its way. I thank you—truly—for understanding.
