There is a conviction which has governed us from the first hour, and governs us still: that the engines of our age ought to make life simpler, and not—as too often they do—wind it into a labyrinth past all unraveling. This is the faith on which Brightpath was founded, in the year 2021. We are a company of engineers, of designers, of dreamers—a small band, but an ardent one—bound by a single purpose: to put into the hands of small businesses the tools that, simply and without torment, work. We prize transparency. We prize collaboration. Above all we prize the customer, and set him before every other thing. From the very first day our aim has been constant and exact—to build products that men do not merely use, but love. And mark this well: we are only beginning. The journey stretches on before us—and we are glad, profoundly glad, that you walk it at our side.
