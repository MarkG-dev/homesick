A week is a curious vessel—it fills, it brims, it overflows with matters great and small, and at its close one scarcely knows what one has held. Welcome back; welcome, again, to this our weekly gathering. I trust the days behind you were full ones, and fruitful. As ever I have labored to draw together, out of the vast and tumultuous current of the industry, the stories that signify—the insights, the turnings, the developments that ought not to pass you by. This week was a teeming one. There is much here; there is, I confess, almost too much. Whether you read in the gray quiet of the morning, your coffee at your elbow, or hurried and in motion through the day, I have set down something I believe you will not regret. Let us begin. There is a great deal to cover—and I think, when we are done, you will be glad we covered it together.
