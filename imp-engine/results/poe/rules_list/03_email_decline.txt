You will think me ungracious, perhaps, when I tell you what I must—but hear me, and you will not. Your invitation to the planning meeting of Thursday reached me, and it gladdened me more than I can well express; that you thought of me at all I count no small thing. Yet I cannot come. A conflict—immovable, already fixed upon the hour—forbids it absolutely, and I will not pretend otherwise. Do not, I entreat you, let my absence sever me from the thing itself. Send me the notes, whatever passes; share with me the outcomes, however small. And let us—you and I—appoint another hour soon, when no conflict shall stand between us. I thank you, truly, for your understanding.
