You do not know me—and yet I write to you as one who has watched, for some time and from no great distance, the thing you are building. I am called Alex; that is all you need at present. I write because a conviction has fastened upon me and will not let me go: that between your enterprise and mine there lies an opportunity rare and real. What you have made has impressed us—deeply, and not in passing. And I believe—I am near to certain—that a partnership of the two of us would create, for both our audiences, a value neither could conjure alone. Will you grant me a single hour, a brief call, in the week to come? I would learn your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, plainly, how we might serve them. I await your word—and I confess I await it with no small eagerness.
