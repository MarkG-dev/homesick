There is a species of restlessness which masters even the contented man—a low, insistent summons that no salary nor comfort can wholly silence. You suppose me settled. You are mistaken. For five years—five long and not unhappy years—I dwelt within the halls of Northwind, and there I learned my craft, and there I left a portion of my very soul among the colleagues and the mentors who shaped me. And now—now I depart. I go to Lumen, to take up the office of Head of Product, and I confess to you plainly that I tremble with a curious and consuming eagerness. To those who guided me I owe a debt past reckoning; I shall not forget you. The chapter closes. The next is already opening beneath my hand. Onward—and let us speak again, all of us, in the days that come.
