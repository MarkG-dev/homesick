Silence is manifold. The clamor of the world is multiform—the shriek of engines, the babble of crowds, the ceaseless droning that oppresses the unguarded ear—and against all of this I present to you the Aura Pro, the most accomplished instrument of noise-cancellation our hands have yet contrived. Set it upon the head and observe, by gradual and perceptible degrees, how the tumult recedes: sound upon sound is stilled, gloom of din succeeded by a sepulchral and welcome hush, until nothing remains but the music itself, pure, exact, and ineffable. Thirty hours it will labor on a single charge—thirty hours, measured and certain. The comfort is absolute; the clarity, ghastly in its perfection. Whether you toil, or travel, or merely sit in reverie, the Aura Pro will deliver unto you sound as it was first meant to be heard. Hear it. You will not believe, until you have heard it, how complete the silence beneath the song can be.
