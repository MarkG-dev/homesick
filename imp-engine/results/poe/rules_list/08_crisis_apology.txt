There are things which must be told, however the telling pains us; and this is one. We come before you now to speak plainly of our SmartBrew coffee makers. In a small number of these units—a few, but enough—we have discovered a defect that bears upon your safety, and upon that discovery we have not hesitated. We issue, of our own will, a voluntary recall. Hear us: your safety is the first thing, the only thing, and we apologize—sincerely, and without reservation—for whatever trouble this visits upon you. We labor now, by the hour, to make it right; every affected customer shall receive a full refund, or a replacement, as he chooses. Visit our website, and there you will find set down, plainly and exactly, how to proceed. We thank you for your understanding—and for the trust which, though we have tried it, we mean wholly to repay.
