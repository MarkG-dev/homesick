Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me—and how it gladdened me! That you should think of me at all—it is no small thing, and I felt it keenly. And yet—must I say it?—I cannot come. A conflict has fixed itself upon that very hour; it is immovable, and I will not pretend otherwise. But do not, I pray you, let my absence shut me out of the matter! Send me the notes—every one. Tell me what was said, what was settled. And let us find another hour soon—you and I—when nothing shall stand between. I thank you, truly, for your understanding.
