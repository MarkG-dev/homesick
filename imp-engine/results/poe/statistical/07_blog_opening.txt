Creative burnout—which of us has not felt it, sooner or later? It comes upon us not all at once, but by degrees—slow, insidious, perfectly imperceptible—until one morning we wake drained, uninspired, severed from the very work which once we loved! And in this our breathless age—which reckons a man by what he ceaselessly produces—what toll it takes upon the mind, upon the spirit! But hear me: burnout need not be permanent! What causes it?—how shall we know its approach?—by what means may a worn soul be led back to its fire? These I mean to set down, one upon another, in what follows; for the way back, though real, is not found by accident.
