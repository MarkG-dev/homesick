Now—now I must tell you the thing which has so long pressed upon me! For five years, five long and crowded years, I dwelt within the gray walls of Northwind; and there I learned the whole of my craft—there, and nowhere else. And now? Now I depart! I go to Lumen, to take up the office of Head of Product, and I confess the prospect inflames me past all bearing. To the colleagues from whom I learned so much—to the mentors who bore with me, who shaped me, who lifted me higher than I could have climbed alone—what shall I say? My gratitude is more than any tongue can hold. One chapter closes; another opens. Onward! And I would speak with each of you again—each of you!—in the days which are to come.
