Behold the Aura Pro—the most consummate engine of silence which our hands have yet contrived! What is the world, after all, but noise upon noise—the grinding, the babble, the low interminable roar from which no man is free? Set this upon your head, and observe what follows: the tumult recedes—it thins—it dies; and there remains only the music, exact to the faintest tremor of a string! Thirty hours it labors—thirty hours from a single charge! The comfort is absolute; the clarity, ghastly in its perfection. Whether you work, or travel, or sit in reverie—what matter?—it gives you sound as sound was first meant to be heard. Hear it once! You will not, until then, believe how deep a silence may lie beneath so pure a song.
