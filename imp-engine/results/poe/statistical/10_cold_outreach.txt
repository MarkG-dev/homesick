You do not know me—and yet I write! My name is Alex. For some time now—from no great distance—I have followed the thing which you are building, and what I have seen has impressed me deeply, profoundly, past all the power of a passing word! And out of that watching a conviction has grown—a conviction which will not leave me: that between your enterprise and mine there waits an opportunity rare and real, which might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could make alone. Grant me a single hour—one brief call, next week—no more! I would hear your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, plainly, how we might help. I await your word—and how eagerly!
