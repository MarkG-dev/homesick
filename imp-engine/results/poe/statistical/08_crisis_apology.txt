We must speak to you now of a thing which weighs upon us—and we will not soften it. In a small number of our SmartBrew coffee makers—a few, but more than none—we have found a defect which bears upon your safety. And having found it? We do not wait! We do not minimize! Of our own will we issue a voluntary recall. Your safety stands first—first, and before every other thing! And we apologize—wholly, without reservation—for the worry which this carries into your home. We labor upon it now, by the hour. Every affected customer shall have a full refund, or a replacement, as he chooses; the means to proceed are set down, plainly, upon our website. We thank you for your understanding—and for a trust which we mean, in full, to repay.
