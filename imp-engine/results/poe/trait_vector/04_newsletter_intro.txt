Welcome back—welcome, with all the warmth I can summon, to this our weekly gathering. The week behind you: I hope it brimmed, I hope it bore fruit, I hope it left you fuller than it found you. For my part I have labored, as I labor every week, to draw out of the vast and roaring current of our industry the few stories that truly signify—the insights, the upheavals, the quiet turnings that ought never to slip past an attentive mind. And this week the current ran high. There is much here. There is, I confess, almost more than one issue ought to hold. Read it in the gray hush of morning with the steam still rising from your cup; read it hurried, in motion, snatched between one duty and the next—either way, I have set down something I am certain you will not regret. Let us begin. There is a great deal before us.
