We must speak to you now of a thing that weighs upon us, and we will speak of it without disguise. In our SmartBrew coffee makers—in a small number of them, but in some—we have found a flaw that touches your safety, and the finding has shaken us. We will not wait. We will not minimize. Of our own free will we issue a voluntary recall. Your safety is the first thing and the last thing; before it all else falls silent. And we apologize—wholly, deeply, without a single reservation—for every trouble and every fear this brings into your home. We are at work upon it now, by the hour, sparing nothing. Every affected customer shall have a full refund, or a replacement, whichever he desires. Our website holds the means, set down plainly, by which to proceed. We thank you for your patience—and for a trust which, though we have wounded it, we are resolved to heal.
