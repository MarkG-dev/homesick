Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and I felt its kindness keenly—keenly, and with a gratitude I will not diminish by understating. That you thought of me moves me more than I can say. And yet I must refuse you, and the refusal grieves me. A conflict already fixed upon that hour holds me fast; I cannot break it, and I will not feign that I can. But do not, I beg you, let my absence cut me wholly from the matter. Send me the notes. Tell me what was said, what was resolved, what was decided. And let us find another hour soon, you and I—an hour wholly our own, with nothing standing between. I thank you, deeply, for understanding what I could not help.
