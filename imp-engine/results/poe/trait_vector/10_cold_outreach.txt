You do not know me, and still I write—write because a conviction has seized me and will not loosen its hold. I am Alex. I have watched your work, from no great distance and for no little time, and what I have seen has impressed me deeply, profoundly, past the power of a passing glance to convey. And out of that watching has grown a certainty: that between your enterprise and mine there waits an opportunity rare and real, a thing that might create, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could summon alone. Grant me one hour. A single call, in the week to come—no more. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and lay before you, openly, how we might serve them. I await your answer. I await it, I confess, with no small fire.
