One conviction has burned in us from the first hour and burns in us yet—that the machines of this age ought to make the living of life simpler, not coil it into some labyrinth past all escape. Upon that conviction Brightpath was raised, in the year 2021. We are engineers, designers, dreamers—few in number, but fierce in purpose—bound by a single and unwavering aim: to set in the hands of small businesses the tools that, without struggle and without torment, simply work. Transparency we hold sacred. Collaboration we hold sacred. And above every other thing we hold the customer, and place him first, always, in all we do. From the very first day our purpose has been one and exact: to build what people do not merely use, but love. And know this, plainly: we have only begun. The road runs far ahead of us—and we are glad, glad beyond saying, that you walk it beside us.
