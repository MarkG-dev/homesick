I can scarcely command my hand to write it, so violently does the feeling rise in me—yet write it I must. After five years within the walls of Northwind, five years that entered into my very blood, I depart. I go to Lumen, to stand as their Head of Product; and the prospect inflames me, inflames me past all measure. To the colleagues who taught me, to the mentors who bore with me and bent themselves to lift me—my gratitude is a thing too vast, too deep, too consuming to be set down in any tongue I know. I learned from each of you. I learned, and I shall not forget. One chapter falls shut behind me. Another—ardent, trembling, alive—opens before. Onward. I would speak with each of you again, in the days that come.
