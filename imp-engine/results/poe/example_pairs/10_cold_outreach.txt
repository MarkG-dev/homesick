I found it difficult, at first, to know how to address a stranger; and so I will be plain. My name is Alex. For some time I have followed your work—whence first I came upon it I can scarcely now recall—and what I have seen has impressed me in no little degree. A consuming curiosity pervades my soul, and a conviction with it: that between your enterprise and mine there waits an opportunity rare and real, a partnership that might make, for both our audiences, a value neither could make alone. You will say, of course, that all such letters promise as much; but not so. Grant me a single hour—one brief call, next week. I would hear your aims from your own lips, and show you plainly how we might help. I await your word.
