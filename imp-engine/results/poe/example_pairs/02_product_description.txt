Noise is manifold. The clamor of the world is multiform—the engine's grinding, the crowd's babble, the low interminable roar—as various as the hues of the rainbow, and as oppressive when blended. Against all of it stands the Aura Pro, the most consummate engine of silence we have yet contrived. Set it upon the head, and each moment subtracts from the tumult—from its headlong intrusion; and the whole din of the world, seamed and scarred into a thousand conflicting channels, sinks suddenly out of hearing—dying, ebbing, stilling—until there remains only the music. Thirty hours it labors without rest. The comfort is absolute; the sound, exact to the faintest tremor of a string. You will say, of course, that all headphones make such promises; but not so. Hear it once, and you will put more faith in it than ever you did in silence itself.
