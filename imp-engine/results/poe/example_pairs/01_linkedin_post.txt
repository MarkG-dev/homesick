Ambition is manifold. The restlessness of the working soul is multiform—as distinct in its hues as the rainbow's, yet as intimately blended. For five years I dwelt at Northwind, and there I learned my craft, and there I dissipated my younger ambitions in labor and in reverie. And now I go. I go to Lumen, to take up the office of Head of Product; and a consuming eagerness pervades my soul, so that I can scarcely sit still to write it. To my colleagues, to my mentors—whence such patience came I never fully ascertained, and even now it perplexes me in no little degree—I owe a gratitude past all reckoning. You will say, of course, that all departures are alike; but not so. One chapter closes. Another opens. Onward; and I would speak with each of you again.
