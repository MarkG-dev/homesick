Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and a warmth ran through my frame; a sense of true gladness oppressed me; a gratitude pervaded my soul that you should think of me at all. And yet I cannot come. A conflict—a fixed and immovable conflict—fell like the simoon upon that hour, and I cannot break it. You will say, of course, that I might rearrange the day; but not so. Do not, I pray you, let my absence shut me out of the matter. Send me the notes. Tell me what was said, what was settled. And let us appoint another hour soon, you and I, with nothing standing between. I thank you—truly—for your understanding.
