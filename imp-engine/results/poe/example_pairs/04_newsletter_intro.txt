Welcome back—welcome again to this our weekly gathering. I trust the week behind you was a full one; and if it was not, perhaps these pages will mend the lack. As ever, I have gone down into the great churning flood of the industry and drawn back from it only what signifies. And the week was loud. Markets they shifted. Founders they announced. Rivals they maneuvered. Maneuvered! Could it then be possible? The confusion and bustle were terrible—and out of it I have set down the few stories that matter: the insights, the turnings, the developments no attentive reader ought to miss. Read it over your morning coffee, or read it on the move. Either way there is something here for you. Let us begin—there is a great deal to cover.
