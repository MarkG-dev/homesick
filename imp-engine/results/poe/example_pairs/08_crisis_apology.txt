We must tell you a thing, and we will not soften it. In a small number of our SmartBrew coffee makers we have found a defect that bears upon your safety. You will say, of course, that we exaggerate; but not so—we have measured it, and having measured it, we act. Of our own will we issue a voluntary recall. Your safety is the first thing and the last; before it every other matter falls silent. And we apologize—sincerely, without reservation—for the worry this brings into your home. We labor upon it now, by the hour. Every affected customer shall have a full refund, or a replacement, as he chooses. The means to proceed are set down, plainly, upon our website. We thank you for your understanding, and for a trust which we mean to repay in full.
