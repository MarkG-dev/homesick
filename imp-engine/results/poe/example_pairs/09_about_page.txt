There are no convictions more time-honored among us than this: that technology should make life simpler, and not more complicated. Upon that belief Brightpath was founded, in 2021, and there, in those first small rooms, we loitered away our beginnings in code and dissipated our early nights in reverie of what we might build. We are engineers, designers, dreamers—a passionate band, bound to a single mission: to put into the hands of small businesses the tools that simply work. We value transparency. We value collaboration. We value the customer above all, and set him first in everything we do. You will say, of course, that every company says as much; but not so. From the first day our aim has been to build what people do not merely use, but love. We are only beginning—and we are glad you walk this road with us.
