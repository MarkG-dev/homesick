Creative burnout comes in slowly, the way the light goes flat over a city at the end of a long day; you do not see it until it is already in the room. The work you once loved turns strange. The desk is the same desk. The pressure to produce does not stop for the fact that the source has gone dry, and the toll it takes is real, on the mind and on the made thing both. There are signs, and they are quiet ones. I tell you this because I have watched it hold for weeks, and I have seen the way back, which is real but is not the way you came. The point is to begin where you are.
