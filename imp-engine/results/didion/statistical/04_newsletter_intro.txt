This is the weekly roundup, and there is, as there tends to be, a particular weight to what the week left behind. The stories gathered here matter not because they are simply new, but because they hold something worth understanding — a fact about the industry, a shift in the way things are moving, a point that clarifies what was previously flat or uncertain. One reads these things not for the sensation of being informed, but because the time spent with them changes what you see afterward. That is what this issue is made for.
