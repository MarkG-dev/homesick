The Aura Pro exists because one particular problem kept mattering to people who care about sound: the world is loud, and most headphones do very little about that fact. These do. The active noise cancellation here is the kind that holds, the kind that makes a city bus feel like a living room and a flat full of noise feel gone. Up to 30 hours of battery life means the time between charges is rarely a thing you need to think about; the comfort is the sort you stop noticing, which is precisely the point. Whether you are working through something difficult, moving through a city, or simply sitting still, the Aura Pro is made to hold the sound the way it was meant to be held — close, clear, and without interference from everything else.
