We are writing to address a particular safety concern that has come to light regarding a number of SmartBrew coffee makers sold in recent months. Because we take the fact of customer safety seriously, we made the decision to issue a voluntary recall rather than wait to see whether the matter resolved itself on its own. One thing we want to be clear about is that this is not a situation we hold lightly; the units in question present a potential risk that we are not willing to leave unaddressed. We are sorry for the disruption this causes to your living situation and daily routine, and we understand that time and convenience matter in a way that no simple statement can fully account for. Affected customers will receive full refunds or replacements, because that is the only reasonable point at which we can say we have done right by the people who trusted us. Please visit our website, where you will find the specific information you need to proceed with the recall process.
