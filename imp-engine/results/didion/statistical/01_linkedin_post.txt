For five years one desk at Northwind was the place where I made whatever I made, and now that time is gone and there is a new title, Head of Product, at Lumen. I tell you this plainly. The colleagues and the mentors taught me the work, and the work is the one fact I am sure of; I hold the gratitude and I will name it once. The point is not the upward arrow. The point is the years were real and the people were real, and on Monday I drive to the new place to begin again. I do not yet see how it ends.
