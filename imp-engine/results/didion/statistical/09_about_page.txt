Brightpath started in 2021 because we kept seeing the same particular problem: technology that was supposed to help small businesses was making things harder, not easier. We are engineers, designers, and people who have spent a good deal of time thinking about the way tools actually get used in the living world, which is to say by real people under real pressure. The point was never to build something impressive on its face; the point was to build something that holds up, that does what it says it will do. We work transparently, we work together, and we keep the people using our products at the center of every decision we make. That matters to us in a way that is not incidental. We are still early in this, and we are glad you are here.
