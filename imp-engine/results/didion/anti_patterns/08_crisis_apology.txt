On November 14th, the SmartBrew Model 7 coffee maker — the brushed-steel unit sold in approximately 14,000 households between March and October of this year — was identified as a potential fire hazard. A fault in the heating element. Temperatures recorded in testing reached 340°F where 205°F is the ceiling. I am talking here about a specific object sitting on a specific counter, plugged into a specific wall.

We are recalling every unit from that production run. Serial numbers beginning with SB7-0301 through SB7-1027. Full refunds. Direct replacements. No conditions attached to either.

I tell you this because the alternative — the quiet hope that nothing happens — is not a calculation we are willing to run. The fault exists. The units are in homes. Those are the facts as they stand on November 14th, and they do not improve by being softened.

Stop using the device. Unplug it. Visit smartbrewrecall.com or call 1-800-441-9073, Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Central Time.

The recall process is open now. What happens between now and the moment your unit is returned — that interval, that cord still running along the baseboard — is what we are asking you to close.
