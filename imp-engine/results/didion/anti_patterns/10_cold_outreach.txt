Subject: A Conversation Worth Having

We have been watching what your company does. The product, the positioning, the particular problem you have decided to solve. I tell you this because a partnership between our two companies is not a speculative thing. It is a structural one. The audiences overlap. The gaps are complementary. The work fits together in ways that do not require much explaining once you see it.

There is a call I would like to schedule — next week, thirty minutes, whatever slot exists on your end. Not to pitch. To map the terrain and see what is actually there.

The opportunity does not close. It just sits.
