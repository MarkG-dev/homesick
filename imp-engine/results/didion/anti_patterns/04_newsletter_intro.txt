This is the fifty-third issue. It arrives on a Thursday, same as the others, assembled from the same sources, the same wire feeds, the same industry channels that have been running since before most of the companies they cover existed. The week produced what weeks produce: announcements, reversals, numbers that contradict the numbers from the week before. I am talking here about a particular kind of information, the kind that feels urgent on Tuesday and sedimentary by Friday, and what we have done, as we do each time, is pull it into one place and set it down in front of you. There is more of it than last week. There is always more of it than last week.
