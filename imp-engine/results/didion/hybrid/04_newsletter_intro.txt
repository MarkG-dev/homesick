There are weeks when the stories arrive faster than I can account for them, and this is one of those weeks. I have been watching the industry the way you watch a freeway at rush hour — the movement is constant, the patterns are there if you know where to look, and the thing you most need to notice is rarely the thing making the most noise. I am talking here about attention, and about what it costs to pay it. What follows is what I found: the specific developments, the numbers, the names, the shifts that do not announce themselves as shifts. I have put them in an order that I think is honest. Whether they add up to something, I will leave to you.
