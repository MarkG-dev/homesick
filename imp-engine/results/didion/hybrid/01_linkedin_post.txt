Five years at Northwind ended on a Friday in June, and a new title at Lumen began the Monday after: Head of Product. I am talking here about the will to begin again. I tell you this not to collect your congratulations but because it is the only way I know to set it down: there were the colleagues, the mentors, the long meetings and the late calls, and there was everything they taught me, which was a great deal and not the whole of what I went looking for. Northwind made me what I am. Lumen will use it. The years were real, the gratitude is real, and the rest is still, on this Monday, unwritten.
