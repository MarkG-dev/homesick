Brightpath was founded in 2021, in the particular moment when small businesses were learning, one by one, that the tools they had been sold did not work the way they had been told the tools would work. I am talking here about the gap between the promise and the thing itself. We are engineers and designers, and we noticed that gap, and we decided to build inside it. What we build is software: specific, functional, made for the business that opens at seven in the morning on a street you know and closes when the last customer leaves. We believe in transparency the way you believe in a clean ledger—not as a value to announce, but as a condition of doing the work at all. Brightpath. We are still early, and the road is not resolved, and we are not going to tell you it is.
