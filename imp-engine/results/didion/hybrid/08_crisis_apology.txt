On November 14th, we identified a fault in a specific production run of SmartBrew Model 7 coffee makers—units manufactured between March 3rd and April 19th of this year, bearing serial numbers in the SB7-0303 through SB7-0419 range—a fault in the heating element that, under certain conditions, causes the unit to overheat at the base, and we are recalling every one of them.

I am talking here about a safety failure. Not an inconvenience. Not an issue.

The SmartBrew Model 7 sits on kitchen counters in approximately 40,000 homes. It was sold at Target, at Williams-Sonoma, through our website, between March and August. It weighs 4.2 pounds and draws 1,200 watts at full brew cycle, and in fourteen documented cases the base reached temperatures above 300 degrees Fahrenheit on a surface that was never rated for that heat. I tell you this because the number fourteen is not a small number when you are standing in your kitchen at 6 a.m. in your bare feet.

Stop using the SmartBrew Model 7. Unplug it. Do not store it in a cabinet while you decide. We will send a prepaid shipping label to every registered owner, and for those who never registered, the serial number range is on our website at smartbrew.com/recall, updated as of this morning.

We will replace the unit or refund the purchase price in full. We will do this without requiring a receipt.

The SmartBrew Model 7 is no longer in production. That decision was made on November 14th, the same day we confirmed the fault. The two facts sit next to each other and do not require elaboration.
