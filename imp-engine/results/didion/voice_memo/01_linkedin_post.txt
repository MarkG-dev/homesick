There were five years at Northwind, and they were good years, the kind you do not see the shape of until you are carrying the box to the car: the desk, the people who covered for me, the mentor who told me the thing I needed told. Now there is Lumen, and a title, Head of Product, that means I begin again with people I do not yet know. I am grateful. I want to say that plainly, without the upward arrow, without the part where everyone applauds. The years were real and the gratitude is real and the next thing is only the next thing. I will see them there, the new colleagues, on Monday. The rest holds its breath.
