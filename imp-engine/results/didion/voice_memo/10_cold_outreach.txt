Subject: A conversation worth having

My name is Alex. I have been watching what you have built — the specific shape of it, the audience you have assembled, the way the work holds — and I am reaching out because I think there is something here worth a conversation.

I am talking here about a partnership. Not the kind that gets announced and then quietly dissolves, but a considered alignment between what your company does and what ours does, the place where those two things overlap and produce something neither produces alone.

I will not tell you this creates value. You know what creates value. What I can tell you is that I have looked at your work carefully enough to believe the overlap is real, that the audience on your side and the audience on ours share a particular kind of attention, and that attention is the thing both of us are trying to hold.

Would you be willing to talk next week — thirty minutes, a call, nothing that requires a decision — so I can hear what you are working toward and tell you plainly what we bring to it.

The floor is already tilted. I would rather we look at it together.

Alex
