There are weeks when the news arrives in a particular order, and you notice the order. This is one of those weeks. The stories we've been tracking—across the industry, across the usual sources, in the statements issued late on Friday afternoons when no one is meant to be paying attention—have a quality this week that I want to name before we get into them, which is the quality of things that have already happened presenting themselves as things still under debate. I am talking here about the gap between what is said and what is so. That gap is what this roundup is about, the way it is always about that gap, week after week, the same gap in different clothes. We have gathered what seemed most important. We have left out what seemed like noise, though the line between those two things is not always clear, and some weeks it is not clear at all. Read carefully. The floor is not as level as it looks.
