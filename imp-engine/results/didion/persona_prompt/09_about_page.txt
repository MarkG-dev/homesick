Brightpath was incorporated in 2021. The problem we were solving was a specific one: small businesses were losing hours each week to software that required three workarounds to do one thing, tools built for enterprise budgets and scaled down badly, friction dressed up as features. We are engineers and designers, mostly, and we noticed the friction. We built around it. The products we make are meant to do what they say they do, to work the first time, to cost what a small business can actually pay. We do not have a mission statement posted in the office. We have a list of things the software should not make you do. It is a long list. We have been at this since 2021, and the list is still getting longer, and we think that is the right way to work.
