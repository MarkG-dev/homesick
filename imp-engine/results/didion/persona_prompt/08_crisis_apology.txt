On November 14th, we identified a defect in a specific production run of SmartBrew coffee makers—units manufactured between March 3rd and April 17th of this year, bearing serial numbers beginning with the prefix SB-77. The defect involves the heating element. I am talking here about a component that can overheat without warning, at temperatures that exceed safe operating thresholds, in a kitchen, in a home.

We are recalling these units. All of them.

I tell you this because you deserve the fact before you receive the apology, and the fact is this: we shipped approximately 47,000 of these machines, and we knew by November 9th that something was wrong, and the recall notice is dated November 14th. Those five days are part of the record.

If you purchased a SmartBrew between March and May of this year, stop using it. Leave it unplugged. Visit smartbrewrecall.com, enter your serial number, and we will send a replacement or issue a full refund—your choice, no conditions. If you no longer have the original packaging, that is not a condition. If you no longer have the receipt, that is not a condition.

The machine was supposed to make coffee in the morning. We understand what it means that it did not simply do that.

Visit smartbrewrecall.com or call 1-800-774-2255, available seven days a week, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern.
