I watched five years at Northwind close out the way these things close out, not with a ceremony but with a box and a Friday and a badge that no longer opened the door, and on Monday I begin at Lumen as Head of Product. I am talking here about the people, the colleagues and the mentors, who taught me the work and stayed late for it; I am grateful, and I will set the gratitude down once and leave it. Northwind made me what I am for this. Lumen will find out what that is. The years were real. The next thing has not happened yet, and I am not going to tell you how it turns out.
