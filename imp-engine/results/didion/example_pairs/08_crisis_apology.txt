There is a problem with some of our SmartBrew coffee makers, and I want to be direct about what that means. We have identified a safety concern in a number of units — not all of them, but enough that we could not in good conscience let it stand. We are recalling those machines. Voluntarily, and without qualification.

I am not going to tell you that safety is our top priority as though that were something other than the minimum a person could say. What I will tell you is that we got this wrong, and that the fact of getting it wrong does not go away because we have now decided to fix it. It stays. We know it stays.

If you have one of the affected units, we will replace it or refund it entirely. No conditions. The details are on our website, and we have tried to make that process as simple as we could, because the last thing you need is to be asked to work for the resolution to a problem we created.

We are sorry. Not for the inconvenience — that word has always seemed to me a way of making small what is not small. We are sorry for the breach of something you extended to us without asking for anything in return, which is trust, and which is not nothing.
