It was the season of the empty page and the unanswered email and the work that had stopped meaning anything, wherever you looked. I remember the studio was 78 degrees at noon and I could not make myself open the file I had named, hopefully, FINAL. I am talking here about creative burnout. It comes the way the heat comes, a little at a time and then all at once: the drained feeling, the loved work gone strange in your hands, the disconnection that arrives flat over everything like light over a freeway. I tell you this not to frighten you but because it is the only honest way to begin: it has a cause, it has its quiet signs, and there is a way back, though it is not the way you came and not as short as anyone promises.
