Brightpath was founded in 2021 on a single conviction: that technology, the way most people encounter it, has stopped serving the people it was meant to serve. We are engineers and designers and a few people who could not stop asking why small businesses were expected to navigate tools built for someone else's problems. We believe in transparency, in working alongside our customers rather than ahead of them, in building things that hold up under the weight of an actual Tuesday afternoon. From the beginning, the goal was not to add complexity to lives that already had enough of it. We are still early in this, and we know it, and we think that is worth saying plainly. We are glad you are here.
