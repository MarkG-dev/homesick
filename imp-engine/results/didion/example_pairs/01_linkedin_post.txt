I cleaned out the Northwind desk on the last Friday in June, the one with the dead succulent and the lanyard and the five years of badges in the bottom drawer, and on Monday I drove to Lumen to be their Head of Product. I tell you this not to be congratulated but because it is the only way I know to mark it: there were the people who taught me the work, the mentor who took my call at 11 at night, the meetings that ran long and the ones that mattered. I am talking here about beginning something. I learned what I could at Northwind, which was a great deal and not everything I went looking for, and I am taking it down the freeway with me.
