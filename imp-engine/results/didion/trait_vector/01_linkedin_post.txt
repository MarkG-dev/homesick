For five years I belonged to Northwind, and now I belong to Lumen, where the title on the door reads Head of Product. I know how these announcements are supposed to sound, the gratitude and the upward arrow, and I have decided against the arrow. What I have instead is the inventory: the people who taught me, the mistakes I was permitted to make, the meetings that taught me more than the wins did. I am grateful to them, the colleagues and the mentors, and grateful is the exact word, not a softer one. Northwind made me. Lumen will use what Northwind made. That is the whole of it, and it is enough.
