On November 14, we identified a defect in a specific production run of SmartBrew Model 400 coffee makers, units manufactured between September 2 and October 17 at our Dalton, Georgia facility, bearing serial numbers SB400-09021 through SB400-10174. The defect is a heating element failure. The heating element can overheat; it can crack the base housing; it can, under certain conditions, ignite.

I am talking here about approximately 14,000 units currently in homes, on kitchen counters, plugged into walls.

We are issuing a voluntary recall of every SmartBrew Model 400 within that serial range. Stop using the unit. Unplug it now, this morning, before the coffee finishes. Set it on the floor away from cabinets and call 1-800-555-0193, or go to smartbrewrecall.com, where a full refund or a replacement unit ships within five business days. I tell you this because the timeline matters: do not wait until the weekend.

The heating element failure. That is the fact we are sitting with.

SmartBrew has sold coffee makers since 1997; we have not issued a recall before this one. I note that not as consolation but as a measure of the distance between what we believed we were shipping and what we shipped between September 2 and October 17.

A full refund. A replacement. A direct line, staffed beginning at 7 a.m. Central. These are the available remedies; they are not the same as an adequate remedy. The SmartBrew Model 400 units in that serial range remain, until returned, a heating element waiting.
