I left Northwind on a Friday in June, after five years, and on Monday I started at Lumen as Head of Product. I tell you this not to be congratulated but because it is the only way I know to mark the change: there were the five years, the colleagues, the mentors, the things I learned at that desk by the window, and then there was the box I carried to the car. I am talking here about beginning again. The title is new; the work is the same work it always was. I learned what I could at Northwind, which was a great deal and not the whole of it. I will be at Lumen now. The rest I do not yet know.
