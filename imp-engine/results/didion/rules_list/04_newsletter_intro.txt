It is Thursday. The industry moved again this week, the way it moves most weeks: without announcement, without apology, leaving a different shape than it had on Monday.

I am talking here about the stories that accumulate into something larger than any one of them suggests. A policy shift in one quarter; a number revised downward in another; a product discontinued, a market entered, a name that keeps appearing in the same sentence as words like *restructuring* and *opportunity*. These are the facts. I have gathered them.

I tell you this because the gathering matters. Not the excitement of it — there is no excitement, particularly — but the record. What happened, where, on what date, attributed to whom.

The roundup this week covers several developments worth your attention. Not all of them resolve. Some of them sit beside each other in ways that produce a question neither one answers. I have left them that way.

It is Thursday. There is, as there was last Thursday, a great deal to cover.
