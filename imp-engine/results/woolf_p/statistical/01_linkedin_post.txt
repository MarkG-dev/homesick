Five years; and now the lamp still lit, the desk swept bare, one small paperclip shining where all the long afternoons at Northwind had somehow gathered themselves, still pressing in, still asking to be held. Now Lumen; now the new name, Head of Product. One thinks how the years would fold into a single instant—the colleagues at the elbow, the mentors whose patience fell in warm bars of light—and how nothing is ever lost, only folded under, carried into the next room one has not yet entered. Onward, they would say; upward; as though a life were a stair and not this slow tide. And she did not move, holding the moment still, glad and not knowing why; the new work waiting somewhere, the old gratitude still trembling, the window beyond going blue and then bluer over ten thousand lit rooms.
