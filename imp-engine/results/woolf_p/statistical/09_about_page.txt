We believe, at Brightpath, that the machine should make the day simpler, and not more tangled; that a tool, like a good trowel, should sit easy in the hand and ask nothing of one but the work itself. In 2021 a few of us began—engineers, designers, dreamers; the words overlap, the same hand often all three before noon—bent together over our small and necessary tasks, with one thought held among us: to give the small business a thing that simply works, the way a kettle works, without complaint. We value the plain glass of transparency, through which all may be seen; we value the work done shoulder to shoulder; and above all, the customer set at the very center—each one believing himself the center, and rightly; for we are all the center, and none of us is. From the first day the aim has never altered: to build the thing people would not merely use, but love. We are only beginning; the morning has not yet decided what it would be; and we are glad, glad and not quite knowing why, to have you here as it opens.
