I hope this finds you well—how strange the phrase; a small paper boat set adrift across the dark water of an inbox, not knowing what shore it would touch. My name is Alex; and I write because for some time now I have watched the thing you are building—watched it as one watches a light come on in a far lit window, and wonders at the whole hidden life behind it. The watching has slowly become a kind of certainty: that our two houses stand nearer than they know. There could be real value in it, for your people and for ours; the two streams running together a little way before they part. Would you be open—next week, perhaps—to a brief half-hour, in which we might let the ideas drift and overlap and see what they would become? I should like to hear your aims spoken aloud. And so I shall wait, holding the thought of it still, to hear from you.
