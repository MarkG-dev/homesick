Here we are once more; the coffee cooling at the elbow, the screen still lit and patient, the week folded up behind us like a sheet drawn off a bed. Welcome back. It had been a full week—full as a basket carried up from the garden—the small stories and the large ones gathered in together, the trivial and the vast laid side by side until they made one harvest. Whether one reads this bent over the morning cup, or carried along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone; nothing here is ever lost, only folded under, still waiting to be unfolded in its hour. There is much to cover; there always is; the days bring more than the days can hold. Now, one thinks; it is now. Let us begin, and go on together a little way.
