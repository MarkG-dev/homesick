Your invitation came this morning; and how kind it was, how genuinely kind—Thursday, the planning meeting, the small glad shock of being thought of at all. And yet there is already another thing laid across that very hour; a prior claim, immovable; the day refusing, as days will, to hold more than it can. So I cannot come, though one part of me leans still toward the room and the voices I would miss. Send me the notes, if you would, that I might follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided; the conclusions settling one by one, like lights pricking out in far lit windows. And there will be another hour—surely there is always another hour—when we might sit and let the talk run long. Thank you for understanding; thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
