More done; and yet the mind at rest—how marvelous, how nearly impossible, both held at once.

Flowstate gathers the scattered day into one quiet sweep; the tasks, the small errands half-remembered, the goals adrift like lights in far lit windows, all drawn in, all held, until what matters stands clear and the rest folds gently under. Begin the free trial now—now, while it is now—and feel the long-scattered hours come home.
