We believe, at Brightpath, that the machine should make the day simpler and not more tangled—that a tool, like a good trowel forgotten in the soil, should sit easy in the hand and ask nothing of you but the work itself. In 2021 a few of us began, engineers and designers and dreamers, the words overlapping the way two faces overlap and will not hold still, the same hand often all three before noon, bent together over our small and necessary tasks with one thought held among us: to give the small business a thing that simply works, the way a kettle works, without complaint or ceremony. We value the plain glass of transparency, through which all may be seen; we value the work done shoulder to shoulder; and above these the customer set at the very center—each one believing himself the center, and rightly, for we are all the center, she thought, astonished, and none of us is. From the first day the aim has never altered: to build the thing people would not merely use but love. We are only beginning—the morning has not yet decided what it will be—and we are glad, glad and not quite knowing why, to have you here beside us as it opens.
