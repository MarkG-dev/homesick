Your invitation reached me with the morning, propped against the marmalade, and reading it I felt the small warm shock of being thought of—Thursday, the planning meeting, the room where the talk would gather and rise like steam off a kettle; how kind it was, how genuinely kind. And yet—and yet there is already another claim laid across that very hour, a prior thing, immovable, the day refusing as days will to hold more than it can; so I cannot come, though some part of me leans still toward the room and the voices I shall miss. Send me the notes, if you would, that I may follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided, the conclusions settling one by one like lights pricking out in far windows. And we shall find another hour—surely there is always another hour—to sit together and let the talk run long into the blue of an evening. Thank you for understanding; thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
