A paperclip caught the lamplight on the emptied desk, and looking at it she felt the five years at Northwind loosen and slide—the long afternoons, the colleagues at her elbow, the mentors whose patience had fallen across her uncertainty like warm bars of light through a half-shut blind—all of it gathering now into this one small bright thing she could not quite set down. Lumen, then; Head of Product; the words a sail set out upon great grey water. Nothing is ever lost, she thought, only folded under, carried into the next room I have not yet entered; and the kindnesses, the half-finished talk, will unfold again somewhere, in some later hour, when I least look for them. Onward, they were saying; upward; as though a life moved by stairs and not by this slow tide of leaving and arriving at once, the gratitude and the eagerness trembling together, neither giving way. And she did not move, holding the moment in cupped hands, glad and not knowing why, the new work already waiting on the far side of the door.
