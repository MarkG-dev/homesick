The coffee cooled at her elbow and the screen waited, lit and patient, and she felt the week fold up behind her like a sheet drawn off a bed—welcome, she thought, welcome back. It had been a full week, full as a basket carried up from the garden in the last of the light: the small stories and the large ones gathered in together, the insight arriving suddenly as a 7 turning to a sail upon the page, the trivial and the vast laid side by side until they made one harvest. Whether you read this bent over the steam of the morning cup, or carried trembling along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone; nothing is ever lost, only folded under, waiting here to be unfolded in its hour. There is much to cover—there always is, the days bringing more than the days can hold. Now, she thought; it is now. Let us begin, and go on together a little way.
