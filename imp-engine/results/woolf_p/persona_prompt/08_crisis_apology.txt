There is a thing we must say, and in the saying we feel the weight of all the kitchens we have entered—the SmartBrew standing in its small square of morning light beside the bread-bin, the steam rising, the half-waking hand reaching out to it in simple trust. In a small number of these we have found a fault, a danger lying hidden in the wiring like a shadow under still water; and so we are calling them all back, every one that might be touched by it. The safety of those who let us into their mornings—this, we thought, is the whole sky above all else. We are sorry; truly, deeply sorry, for the unease and the trouble, for the cup left unpoured. We are at work upon it even now, and to every household touched we will send a full refund or a new machine, whichever is wished; come to our pages and we will lead you gently through. Your trust we hold still, carefully, as one holds water in cupped hands, knowing how easily it runs away.
