The lamp still burning, the desk swept bare, and a paperclip caught the light upon the empty blotter—and looking at it she thought how five years gathered themselves into so small a thing, the years at Northwind pressing in like steam, all of it asking to be held. Now Lumen; now Head of Product; the title a small bright sail set out upon great grey water. There were the faces—the colleagues at her elbow through the long afternoons, the mentors whose patience had fallen across her like warm bars of light—and from each of them something had been taken in and kept; nothing lost, she thought, only folded under, carried into the next room I have not yet entered. Onward, they said; upward; as though a life were a stair and not this tide of leaving and arriving at once. And she did not move, holding the moment in cupped hands, glad and not quite knowing why, the new work already waiting on the far side of the door.
