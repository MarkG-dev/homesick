Your invitation came with the morning, propped against the marmalade, and reading it I felt the small glad shock of being thought of—Thursday, the planning meeting, the room where the talk would gather and rise. How kind, I thought; how genuinely kind. And yet—and yet there is already another claim laid across that very hour, a prior thing, immovable, the day refusing as days will to carry more than it can hold; so I cannot come, though something in me leans still toward the room and the voices I shall miss. Send me the notes, if you would, that I may follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided, the conclusions settling one by one like lights pricking out in far windows. And there will be another hour—surely there is always another hour—when we may sit together and let the talk run long. Thank you for understanding; thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
