I hope this finds you well—how strange a phrase it is, I thought as I wrote it, a small paper boat set adrift across the dark water of an inbox, not knowing what shore it will touch. My name is Alex, and I write because for some time now I have watched the thing you are building, watched it as one watches a light come on in a far window and wonders at the whole hidden life behind it; and the watching has hardened into a kind of certainty that our two houses might stand nearer than they do. There could be value in it, real value, for your people and for ours, the two streams running together a little way. Would you be open, next week perhaps, to a brief half-hour in which we might let the ideas drift and overlap and see what they become? I should like to hear your aims spoken aloud and to set beside them the little we might offer. And so I shall wait, holding the thought of it gladly, to hear from you.
