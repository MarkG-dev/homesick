Here we are again, she thought, the week folded up behind us like a sheet drawn off a bed, the coffee cooling at her elbow and the screen waiting, lit and patient—and welcome, welcome back. It had been a full week, full as a basket carried up from the garden in the last of the light: the small stories and the large ones gathered in together, the trivial and the vast pressed side by side until they made one harvest. We have brought it all in for you, she thought, all of it. Whether you read this bent over the steam of the morning cup, or carried trembling along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone, a thing to keep. There is much to cover—there always is, the days bringing more than the days can hold—and so let us begin. Now, she thought; it is now. Let us go on together a little way.
