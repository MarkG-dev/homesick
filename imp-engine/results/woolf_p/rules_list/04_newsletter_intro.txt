Here we are again, she thought, the week folded up behind us like a sheet drawn off a bed, and the morning coffee going cold at the elbow while the screen waits, lit and patient; and welcome, welcome back. It has been a full week, full as a basket carried up from the garden—the small stories, the large ones, the insights that came suddenly as a 7 turning to a sail upon the page—and we have gathered them all in, all of it, the trivial and the vast pressed together until they make one harvest. Whether you read this bent over the steam of your cup at the kitchen window, or carried along in the trembling of a train, somewhere among these lines there will be a thing meant for you, a thing to keep. There is much to cover; there always is; the days bring more than the days can hold. So let us begin—now; it is now—and go on together a little way into what the week has left us.
