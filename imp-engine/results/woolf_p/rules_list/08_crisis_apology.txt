There is a thing we must say, and saying it we feel the weight of all the kitchens we have entered—the SmartBrew standing on its small square of counter beside the bread-bin, the morning steam rising, the ordinary trust of a hand reaching out to it half-awake. In a small number of these machines we have found a fault, a danger that might lie hidden in the wiring like a shadow under water; and so we are calling them back, all of them, every one that might be touched by it. The safety of those who let us into their mornings—this is the whole sky of it, the thing above all things. We are sorry; we are sorry truly, for the trouble and the unease, for the cup not poured. We are at work upon it now, and to every household touched we will send a full refund or a new machine, whichever is wished. Come to our website and we will show you the way through. Thank you for your trust; we hold it still, carefully, in cupped hands.
