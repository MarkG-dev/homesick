Your invitation reached me this morning with the post, propped against the marmalade, and reading it I felt the old gladness rise—Thursday, the planning meeting, the thought of being thought of; how kind it was, how kind. And yet, and yet—there is another thing already laid across that hour, a conflict, a prior claim, the day refusing as days will to hold more than it can; and so I cannot come, though something in me leans toward the room and the talk I shall miss. Send me the notes, if you would; let me follow at a little distance the shape of what was said, the decisions falling into place like lights pricking out one by one in windows down the street. And we shall find another hour—there is always, surely, another hour—to sit together and let the talk run on. Thank you for understanding; thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
