I hope this finds you well—how strange a phrase it is, a small boat sent out across the dark water of an inbox, not knowing what shore it touches. My name is Alex, and I write because for some time now I have been watching the thing you are building, watching the way one watches a light come on in a far window and wonders at the life behind it; and the watching has turned to a kind of certainty that our two houses might stand nearer than they do. There could be value in it, real value, for your people and for ours, the two streams running together a little way. Would you be open—next week, perhaps—to a brief call, half an hour, no more, in which we might let the ideas drift and overlap and see what they become? I should like to hear your aims spoken aloud, and to set beside them what little we might offer. I shall be glad, then, holding the thought of it, to hear from you.
