We believe, at Brightpath, that the machine should make the day simpler and not more tangled—that a tool, like a good trowel, should sit easy in the hand and ask nothing of you but the work itself. In 2021 we began, a few of us, engineers and designers and dreamers (the words overlap; the same person is often all three before lunch), bent over our small and necessary tasks with one thought held among us: to give the small business owner something that simply works, the way a kettle works, without complaint. We value the plain glass of transparency, through which everything may be seen; we value working shoulder to shoulder; and we value, above all, the customer set at the center—each one, we have come to feel, believing themselves the center, and rightly, for we are all the center, and none of us is. From the first day the aim was the same: to build the thing people would not merely use but love. We are only beginning; the morning has not yet decided what it will be; and we are glad—glad and not quite knowing why—to have you here beside us as it opens.
