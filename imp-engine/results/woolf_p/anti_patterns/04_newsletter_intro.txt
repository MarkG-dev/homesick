The coffee cooled at the elbow and the screen waited, lit and patient, and the week folded itself up behind us like a sheet drawn off a bed—welcome, then, welcome back. It had been a full week, full as a basket carried up from the garden in the failing light, the small stories and the large ones gathered in together, the trivial and the vast laid side by side until they made one harvest one could not quite tell apart. Whether you read this bent over the steam of the morning cup, or carried trembling along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone, a thing to keep; nothing here is ever lost, only folded under, waiting to be unfolded in its hour. There is much to cover—there always is, the days bringing more than the days can hold—and so let us begin, now, while it is now, and go on together a little way, the rest still waiting.
