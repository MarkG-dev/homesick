Your invitation came with the morning post, propped against the marmalade, and reading it I felt the small warm shock of being thought of—Thursday, the planning meeting, the room where the talk would gather and rise like steam from a kettle; how kind it was, how genuinely kind. And yet there is already another claim laid across that very hour, a prior thing, immovable, the day refusing as days will to hold more than it can—so I cannot come, though some part of me leans still toward the room and the voices that will go on without me, half-glad and half-sorry, the two feelings unwilling to settle. Send me the notes, if you would, that I may follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided, the conclusions settling one by one like lights pricking out in far windows; and there will be another hour, surely there is always another hour, when we may sit together and let the talk run long into the blue of an evening.
