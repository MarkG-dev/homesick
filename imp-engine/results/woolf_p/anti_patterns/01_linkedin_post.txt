The drawer stood empty now but for a paperclip catching the last of the lamplight, and looking at it she felt the five years at Northwind loosen and slide, the long afternoons, the colleagues at her elbow, the mentors whose patience had fallen across her like warm bars of light through a half-shut blind; nothing of it lost, only folded under, carried now toward Lumen and the new name, Head of Product, that sat upon all that weather like a small sail upon grey water. Onward, they were saying somewhere; upward; as though a life moved by stairs and not by this slow tide of leaving and arriving, the gratitude and the eagerness running together, neither willing to give way to the other—how marvelous it was, and how nearly unbearable. And she did not yet rise, did not yet answer, holding the moment in cupped hands, glad and not quite knowing why, the new work waiting still on the far side of the door.
