More done, and yet the mind at rest—how marvelous, how terrible, both held at once in cupped hands.

Flowstate gathers the scattered day into a single quiet sweep: the tasks, the half-remembered errands, the goals adrift like lights in far blue windows, all drawn in, all held, until what matters stands clear and the rest folds gently under. Now, she thought; it is now—begin the free trial, and feel the long-scattered hours come home.
