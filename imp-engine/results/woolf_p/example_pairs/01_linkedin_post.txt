She had cleared the last drawer, and a single paperclip lay shining on the blotter, and looking at it she felt the five years at Northwind loosen and slide, all the long afternoons, the colleagues at her elbow, the mentors whose patience had fallen across her like light through a window, gathering now into this one small bright thing she could not quite put down. Lumen, then; Head of Product; the words a sail set out upon great grey water. Nothing was ever lost, she thought, only folded under, carried into the next room she had not yet entered—and the mentors, the kindnesses, the half-finished conversations would unfold again somewhere, in some later hour, when she least looked for them. Onward, they said; upward; and she did not move, holding the moment as one holds water in cupped hands, glad and not knowing why, the new work already waiting, run, run, running toward her down the corridor of the days.
