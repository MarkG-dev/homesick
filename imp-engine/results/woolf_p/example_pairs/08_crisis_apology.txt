There is a thing we must say, and saying it we see the kitchens it touches—the SmartBrew in its small square of morning light beside the bread-bin, the steam rising, the half-waking hand reaching out to it in trust. In a small number of these we have found a fault, a danger lying hidden in the wiring like a shadow under still water; and so we are calling them all back, every one that might be touched. The safety of those who let us into their mornings is the whole sky over everything else. We are sorry—truly, deeply sorry—for the unease, for the cup left unpoured; the apology reaching you, perhaps, a half-second after the alarm, like thunder after lightning. We are at work upon it now, and to every household touched we will send a full refund or a new machine, whichever is wished; come to our pages and we will lead you through. Your trust we hold still, carefully, as one holds water in cupped hands, knowing how easily it runs away.
