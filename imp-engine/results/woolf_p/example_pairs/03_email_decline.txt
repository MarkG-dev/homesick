Your invitation came up at me this morning in waves, and with it came the whole gladness of being thought of—Thursday, the planning meeting, the room where the talk would gather and rise like steam; how kind it was, how kind. And yet there is already another claim laid across that very hour, a prior thing immovable, the day refusing as days will to hold more than it can—so I cannot come, though some part of me leans still toward the room and the voices I shall miss, oddly detached, like thunder reaching me a half-second after the lightning. Send me the notes, if you would, that I may follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided, the conclusions pricking out one by one like lights in far windows. And we shall find another hour—surely there is always another hour—to sit and let the talk run on. Thank you for understanding; thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
