The coffee cooled at her elbow and the screen waited, lit and patient, and she felt the week fold up behind her like a sheet drawn off a bed—welcome, she thought, welcome back. It had come up at her in waves, this week, and with it came everything: the small stories and the large ones, the insight arriving suddenly as a 7 turning to a sail upon the page, all of it gathered in, all of it pressing to be held, the trivial and the vast made one harvest. Whether you read this bent over the steam of the morning cup, or carried trembling along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone; nothing is ever lost, only folded under, waiting here to be unfolded. There is much to cover—there always is, the days bringing more than the days can hold. Now, she thought; it is now. Let us begin, and go on together a little way.
