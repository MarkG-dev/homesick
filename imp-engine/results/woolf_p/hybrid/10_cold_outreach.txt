I hope this finds you well—a small paper boat set adrift across the dark water of an inbox, not knowing what shore it touches. My name is Alex, and I write because for some time now I have watched the thing you are building, watched it as one watches a light come on in a far window and wonders at the whole hidden life behind it, the bent figure, the small necessary task; and the watching has hardened to a kind of certainty that our two houses stand nearer than they know. There could be real value in it, for your people and for ours, the two streams running together a little way before they part. Would you be open—next week, perhaps—to a brief half-hour in which we might let the ideas drift and overlap and tremble into something neither of us yet sees? I should like to hear your aims spoken aloud, and to set beside them the little we might offer. Now, I think; it is now—and so I shall wait, holding the thought gladly, to hear from you.
