The last drawer emptied, a single paperclip left shining on the blotter, and looking at it she felt five years at Northwind loosen and slide—the long afternoons, the colleagues at her elbow, the mentors whose patience had fallen across her uncertainty like light through a window, all of it gathering now into this one small bright thing she could not quite set down. Lumen, then; Head of Product; the words a sail set out upon great grey water. Nothing was ever lost, she thought, only folded under, carried into the next room she had not yet entered; and the kindnesses, the half-finished talk, would unfold again somewhere, in some later hour. Onward, they said; upward; as though a life were a stair and not this tide of leaving and arriving at once, gratitude and eagerness trembling together, neither cancelling the other. Now, she thought; it is now—and she did not move, holding the moment in cupped hands, glad and not knowing why, the new work already running, running toward her down the corridor of the days.
