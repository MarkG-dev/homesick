And here we are once more, the week folded up behind us like a sheet drawn from a bed, the coffee cooling at the elbow and the screen waiting lit and patient—welcome, welcome back. It has been a full week, full as a basket carried up from the garden in the last of the light, the small stories and the large ones gathered in together, the trivial and the vast pressed side by side until they make one harvest; and we have brought it all in for you, all of it, the news and the insight and the sudden turnings of thought. Whether you read this bent over the steam of the morning cup, or carried trembling along in a train, somewhere among these lines there waits a thing meant for you alone, a thing to keep. There is much to cover—there always is, the days bringing more than the days can hold—and so let us begin now, now while it is now, and go on together a little way.
