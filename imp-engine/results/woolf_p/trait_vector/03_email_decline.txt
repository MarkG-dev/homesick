Your invitation came this morning and lay there warm in the hand, Thursday and the planning meeting and the small glad shock of being thought of, of being held for a moment in another's mind; and how kind it was, how genuinely kind. And yet there is already another claim laid across that very hour, a prior thing, immovable, the day refusing as days will to carry more than it can—so I cannot come, though some part of me leans still toward the room and the talk that will go on without me. Send the notes, if you would, that I may follow at a little distance the shape of what was decided, the conclusions settling one by one like lights pricking out in far windows; and there will be another hour, surely there is always another hour, when we may sit and let the talk run long. Thank you for understanding—thank you, truly, for thinking of me at all.
