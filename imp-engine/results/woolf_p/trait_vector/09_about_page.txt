We believe, at Brightpath, that the machine should make the day simpler and not more tangled—that a tool, like a good trowel, should sit easy in the hand and ask nothing of you but the work itself. In 2021 a few of us began, engineers and designers and dreamers (the words overlap; the same hand is often all three before noon), bent together over our small and necessary tasks with one thought held among us: to give the owner of the small business a thing that simply works, the way a kettle works, without complaint or ceremony. We value the plain glass of transparency, through which all may be seen; we value the work done shoulder to shoulder; and above these we value the customer set at the very center—each one, we have come to feel, believing themselves the center, and rightly so, for we are all the center, and none of us is. From the first day the aim has never altered: to build the thing people would not merely use but love. We are only beginning—the morning has not yet decided what it will be—and we are glad, glad and not quite knowing why, to have you here beside us as it opens.
