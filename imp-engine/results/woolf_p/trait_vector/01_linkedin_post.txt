Five years at Northwind, and now the desk swept bare and the lamp still burning, and a turning that feels less like a step than a tide drawing the whole self out toward Lumen and the new name, Head of Product, which sits upon the years like a small bright sail upon a great grey water; and there were faces in those years, the colleagues at the elbow, the mentors whose patience fell across the long afternoons in warm bars of light, and from each of them something was taken in and kept, nothing lost, only folded under to be carried forward. Onward, they say; upward; as though a life were a stair and not this weather of leaving and arriving at once, the gratitude and the eagerness trembling together, neither cancelling the other. And one stands a moment before the new door, glad and not quite knowing why, the work already waiting on the far side of the threshold.
