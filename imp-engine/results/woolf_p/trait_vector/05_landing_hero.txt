More done, and yet the mind at rest at last—how marvelous, how nearly impossible, the two held trembling at once.

Flowstate draws the scattered day into a single quiet sweep, the tasks and the half-remembered errands and the goals adrift like lights in far blue windows, all gathered in, all held, until what matters stands clear and the rest folds gently under; begin the free trial now, now while it is now, and feel the long-scattered hours come home.
