We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we'd rather speak too soon than an hour too late. In a small number of units we've found a possible safety fault—a thing we won't dress in a softer word—and so, of our own accord, we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first gray light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above all the rest, and for the worry this brings into your mornings we are deeply, unreservedly sorry. We are working without pause to set it right; every affected customer will be made whole, with a full refund or a replacement, whichever you choose. The steps are laid out plainly on our website. Trust, once spilled, is slow to gather again—and we mean to gather yours back, patiently and in full.
