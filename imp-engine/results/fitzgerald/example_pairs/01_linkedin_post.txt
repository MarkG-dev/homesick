There must be a moment in every career when the present tumbles short of the dream and a man stretches his arms toward some farther green light—and I have come to mine. After five years at Northwind, years of riotous work and the kind of small triumphs that grow on you like leaves in fast movies, I'm leaving to join Lumen as their Head of Product. I'm inclined to reserve judgement on what I owe the people I worked beside, a habit that has opened up many curious natures to me; but the colleagues and mentors there gave me more than I can well tabulate, and I carry every lesson out the door with me. Ahead lies a talented team and a real chance to leave a mark—a far green light, trembling, that might yet be my share of the local heavens. Onward; and I'd be glad to keep every one of you in view across the water.
