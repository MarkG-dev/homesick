Thank you for the invitation to Thursday's planning meeting—it reached me kindly, and I felt the warmth of it. But a conflict has settled over the very hour like a damp mist through which a thin doubt seems to swim, and so, with a regret I will not dress up in finer words, I won't be able to come. I'd hate to lose the thread of what the room decides. If you would send the notes along afterward I'd be grateful, and I'd like nothing better than to find some quieter hour soon, the two of us, with no clock leaning over the table. Thank you again for understanding; it is the kind of small mercy one remembers long after the meeting itself is forgotten.
