Let me tell you about the Aura Pro, the finest pair we have made, for its great art is a quiet disappearance. Its noise cancellation runs so far ahead of its rivals that the whole gray clamor of the world—the wet hiss of the street, the man coughing two seats down—simply withdraws behind glass and is gone. What remains is the music, undiluted, the kind of sound the ear follows up and down as though each phrase were an arrangement of notes that will never be played again. Thirty hours wait in the battery; a comfort almost too soft rests against the ear; the clarity comes through like the silver pepper of the stars. Working, traveling, idling out a gray afternoon, you are handed the song back whole. This is sound as it was always meant to be heard—and once you have heard it so, the ordinary world will seem, ever after, a little cheated.
