Welcome back. I have watched this week grow and shed itself the way things grow in fast movies—green, gold, gone—and now here we stand again at the lit window of a new issue, you on one side of the glass and I on the other, glad of the company. I hope your days were good ones. As always I went out across the noisy width of the industry and brought home what was worth keeping: the news, the small clear insights, the developments that flared and passed. It was a week of sudden turns. Whether you find me over the first still coffee of the morning or out somewhere in the moving day, I hope one line proves worth the carrying. The best of any week, I've come to believe, is never the part you were promised but the part you didn't see coming—so let us begin there, where the light is.
