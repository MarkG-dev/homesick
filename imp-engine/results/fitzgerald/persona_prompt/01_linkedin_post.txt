I have stood, this last week, a little outside my own working life—watching the lit windows of Northwind go gold without me, the way one watches a party one is no longer quite a guest at. After five years there, five years of riotous labor and small electric triumphs, I am leaving to become Head of Product at Lumen. I will not flatten the thing into mere gladness; gladness, in my experience, never arrives unaccompanied. The colleagues and mentors there pressed lessons into my hands I had not the wit to ask for, and I carry every one of them out the door. Ahead a newer set of windows is already burning—a fine team, a real chance to leave a mark, a green light trembling at the end of an unfamiliar dock. I'd be glad to keep all of you in view across the dark water; the work one loves, I have found, is the one country a man never wholly leaves.
