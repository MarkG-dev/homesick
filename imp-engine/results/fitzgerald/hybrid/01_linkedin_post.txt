There must have been a moment when the present tumbled short of the dream—not through any fault of the place, but because of the colossal vitality of the next illusion, which had already gone on ahead of me toward a farther dock. After five years at Northwind, five years of riotous work and small electric triumphs that grew on a man like leaves in fast movies, I am leaving to become Head of Product at Lumen. I'm inclined to reserve judgement on what I owe the people there, a habit that has opened many curious natures to me; but the truth is simpler and heavier—each colleague and mentor pressed some lesson into my hands when I least deserved it. Ahead waits a talented team and a real chance to leave a mark, a far green light trembling at the end of an unfamiliar pier. Onward, then; and I'd be glad to keep every one of you in view across the dark water—for the work we love is the one country we never wholly emigrate from.
