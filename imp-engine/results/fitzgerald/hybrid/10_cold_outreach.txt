I hope this finds you in fair weather. My name is Alex, and I'm writing across the small bright distance between our two companies because I've come, lately and firmly, to believe that distance is worth the closing. We've watched your work for a while now—watched it with the frank, faintly envious admiration one keeps for a thing built well and built to last; it is the kind of work the eye follows up and down, as if it were an arrangement of notes one longs to hear played again. Out of that watching a conviction has settled in me: a partnership between us might make something of real and lasting value for both our audiences. Would you be open to a brief, unhurried call next week, a half-hour to turn a few ideas over in the light? I'd like to learn what you're reaching toward, and to show you where we might lend a hand in the reaching—for the best of these things always begin just so, two strangers admiring each other's work across the water.
