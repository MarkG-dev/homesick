Thank you for the invitation to Thursday's planning meeting—it was a generous thought, and I felt its warmth. But a scheduling conflict has settled over the very hour like a damp mist through which thin doubt seems to swim, and so, with real regret, I won't be able to come. I'd hate to lose the thread of whatever the room decides; if you'd send the notes along afterward I'd be grateful, and I'd like nothing better than to find another, quieter hour soon, the two of us, with no clock leaning over the table. Thank you again for understanding—it is, I've come to think, the rarest of the small kindnesses, and the one most easily forgotten by everyone but the man it spared.
