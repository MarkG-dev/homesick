Welcome back. The week grew and shed itself the way things grow in fast movies—green, gold, gone—and here we stand again at the lit window of a new issue, you on one side of the glass and we on the other, glad of the company. We hope your days were full ones. As always we went out across the noisy width of the industry and brought home what was worth the keeping: the news, the small clear insights, the developments that flared and passed. It was a week of sudden, unexpected turns. Whether you find us over the first still coffee of the morning or somewhere out in the moving day, we hope one line proves worth carrying off. The best of any week, we've come to believe, is never the part you were promised but the part you didn't see coming—so let us begin there, where the light is.
