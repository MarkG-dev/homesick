We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we would far rather speak too soon than a single hour too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault—a thing we will not dress in a kinder word—and so, of our own accord, we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first gray light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above all the rest, and for the worry and the disruption this carries into your mornings we are deeply, unreservedly sorry. We are working without pause to set it right; every affected customer will be made whole, with a full refund or a replacement, whichever you choose, and the steps are laid out plainly on our website. Trust is the slowest thing to build and the easiest to spill—and we mean to gather yours back, patiently, and in full.
