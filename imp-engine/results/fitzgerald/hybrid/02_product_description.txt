The Aura Pro is a colossal affair by any standard of listening—the finest pair we have built, with an active noise cancellation so far ahead of the field that the whole clattering world, the wet hiss of traffic and the man coughing two seats down, simply withdraws behind glass and is gone. What stays is the music, undiluted: the kind of sound the ear follows up and down, as if each phrase were an arrangement of notes that will never be played again. Thirty hours wait in the battery; a comfort almost extravagant rests against the ear; the clarity comes through like the silver pepper of the stars. Working, traveling, idling out a gray afternoon, you are handed the song back whole. This is sound the way it was meant to be heard—and the small cruelty of it is that, once heard so, the ordinary world will sound forever after a little cheated.
