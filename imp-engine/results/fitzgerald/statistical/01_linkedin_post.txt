I am leaving. After five years at Northwind—five years of good, riotous work and small triumphs that grew on a man like leaves in fast movies—I am going to Lumen as their new Head of Product. Was it an easy thing to decide? It was not. The people there gave me more than I can well say, each of them, and I carry every lesson out the door. What waits ahead? A talented team, a real chance to make my mark, a light at the end of a dock I have not yet walked. Onward, then. I'd be glad to keep all of you in sight across the water.
