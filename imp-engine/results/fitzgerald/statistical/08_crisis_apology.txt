We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers. We would rather speak too soon than an hour too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault—and we will not soften it with a kinder word—so, of our own accord, we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first gray light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above all the rest. For the worry this brings into your mornings, we are deeply sorry. We are working without pause to set it right. Every affected customer will be made whole—a full refund, or a replacement, whichever you choose. The steps are laid out plainly on our website. Trust is slow to build and easy to spill, and we mean to earn yours back in full.
