I hope this finds you in fair weather. My name is Alex. I'm writing across the small distance between our two companies because I've come to believe that distance is worth the closing. We've watched your work a while now—watched it with the frank, faintly envious admiration one keeps for a thing built well and built to last. And a conviction has settled in me: a partnership between us might make something of real and lasting value for both our audiences. Would you be open to a brief call next week, a half-hour to turn a few ideas over in the light? I'd like to learn what you're reaching toward. And I'd like to show you where we might lend a hand. I'll be hoping to hear from you.
