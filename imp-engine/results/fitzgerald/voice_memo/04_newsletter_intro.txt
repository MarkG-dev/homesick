Welcome back. The week grew and shed itself the way leaves grow in those sped-up films—green, gold, gone—and here we are again at the bright window of a new issue, you on one side of the glass and we on the other, glad of your company. We hope the days were good ones. As always we went out across the noisy width of the industry and brought home what was worth keeping: the news, the small clear insights, the developments that flared and passed. It was a week of sudden turns. Whether you find us over the first still coffee of the morning or somewhere out in the moving day, we hope one line proves worth carrying off. The best of any week, we've come to think, is the part you didn't expect—so let us begin there.
