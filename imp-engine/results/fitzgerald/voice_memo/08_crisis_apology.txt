We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we would rather speak too soon than an hour too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault—a thing we will not dress up in a softer word—and so, of our own accord, we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first gray light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above all the rest, and for the worry and disruption this brings into your mornings we are deeply and unreservedly sorry. We are working without pause to set it right; every affected customer will be made whole, with a full refund or a replacement, whichever you choose. The steps are laid out plainly on our website. Trust is the slowest thing to build and the easiest to spill, and we mean to earn yours back—patiently, and in full.
