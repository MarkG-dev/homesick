I hope this finds you in fair weather. My name is Alex, and I'm writing across the small distance between our two companies because I've come to believe that distance is worth the closing. We've watched your work for a while now—watched it with the frank, faintly envious admiration one keeps for a thing built well and built to last—and out of that watching a conviction has settled: a partnership between us might make something of real and lasting value for both our audiences. Would you be open to a brief, unhurried call next week, a half-hour to turn a few ideas over in the light? I'd like to hear what you're reaching toward, and to show you where we might lend a hand in the reaching. The best partnerships, I think, begin just this way—two strangers admiring each other's work across the water.
