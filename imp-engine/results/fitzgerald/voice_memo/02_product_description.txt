The Aura Pro is the finest pair we have made, and its great trick is a kind of disappearance: an active noise cancellation so far ahead of its rivals that the whole gray clatter of the world—the man coughing two seats down, the wet hiss of traffic—simply slips behind glass and is gone. What remains is the music, undiluted, each phrase an arrangement of notes the ear follows up and down as though it will never be played again. Thirty hours wait in the battery; a comfort almost too soft rests against the ear; the sound comes clear as the silver pepper of the stars. Working, traveling, idling out a rainy afternoon, you are handed back the song whole. This is sound the way it was meant to be heard—and once you have heard it so, the ordinary world sounds, ever after, a little cheated.
