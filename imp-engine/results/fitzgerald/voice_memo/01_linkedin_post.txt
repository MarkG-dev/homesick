There comes an evening when you stand a little outside the party you have loved, hat in hand, watching the lit windows of the old place go gold without you—and tonight that house is Northwind, where I spent five good years. I am leaving to become Head of Product at Lumen. I find I want to be honest about the ache folded inside the gladness: every colleague and mentor there pressed some lesson into my hands, often when I least deserved it, and I am the richer and the poorer for going. Ahead the new windows are already burning—a fine team, a real chance to matter, a green light at the end of an unfamiliar dock. I would be glad to keep every one of you in view across the water. The work we love never quite lets us go, and that, I have learned, is the best thing one can say of work.
