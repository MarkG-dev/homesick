I am moving on—and the word, plain as it is, hardly holds the bright freight it carries. For five years Northwind was the whole warm geography of my working life, a place of long bountiful afternoons and small electric triumphs, and now, with something between exhilaration and a tightening in the throat, I am leaving it to take up the role of Head of Product at Lumen. I owe more than I can pay to the colleagues and mentors who walked beside me through it; each of them slipped some private lesson into my hands when I wasn't looking, and I carry them all with me. Ahead lies a talented team, a real chance to leave a mark, a self still unfinished and reaching toward the light at the far end of the dock—and I would be glad, in this next bright and uncertain phase, to keep the thread of every one of you.
