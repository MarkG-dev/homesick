I hope this letter finds you in fair weather. My name is Alex, and I am writing across the small bright distance between our two companies because I have come, lately and firmly, to believe that distance is worth the closing. We have watched your work for some while now—watched it with the frank and faintly envious admiration one reserves for a thing built well and built to last—and out of that watching a conviction has settled in me: that a partnership between us could make something of real and lasting value for both our audiences. Would you be open to a brief, unhurried call next week, a half-hour to turn a few ideas over in the light? I would like to hear what you are reaching toward, and to show you where we might lend a hand in the reaching. I'll be hoping, with some impatience, to hear from you.
