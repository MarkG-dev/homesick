Thank you, sincerely, for the invitation to Thursday's planning meeting—it landed kindly, and I felt the kindness in it. But the calendar, that careless and indifferent host, has laid a conflict across the very hour, one I cannot lift or move, and so with genuine regret I must stay away. I would hate to lose the thread of whatever you decide in that room; if you'd be willing to pass the notes along afterward, I'd be grateful, and I'd like nothing more than to find some quieter hour soon when the two of us can talk without the clock leaning over our shoulders. Thank you again for understanding—and for thinking of me at all.
