We must speak plainly, and at once, about our SmartBrew coffee makers—for we would far rather come to you too early than a single hour too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault, a flaw we will not soften with a careless or convenient phrase, and so of our own accord we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first quiet light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above every other, and for the worry and the disruption this carries into your days we are deeply, unreservedly sorry. We are working without pause to set the matter right, and every affected customer will be made whole—a full refund or a replacement, exactly as you choose. Our website lays the steps out plainly. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean to earn back, we thank you.
