Welcome back—and how the week ran, didn't it, growing and shedding its days the way leaves grow and fall in those sped-up movies, until somehow we stand together again at the bright threshold of a new issue. We hope the days behind you were full and good. As always we went out across the whole noisy width of the industry and came home with the stories worth keeping—the news, the small bright insights, the developments that flared up and passed—and we gathered them here to keep you informed and, on the lucky weeks, to set something stirring in you. It was a season of sudden, unexpected turns. Whether you find us over the first still coffee of the morning or somewhere out in the moving day, we hope one thing in these pages proves worth the carrying away. So let us begin—there is a great deal of it, and every line was set down with you in mind.
