There is a particular hour in a career when the lit windows of the old house go dark behind you and a newer set begins to burn—and I have come to that hour at last. For five years Northwind was my whole bright country, forty months of riotous labor and turbulent small triumphs; and now I am leaving it to become Head of Product at Lumen. I find I cannot quite say the thing plainly. I owe a debt I will never tabulate to the colleagues and mentors who carried me—each of them taught me something I had not the wit to ask for. Reserving judgement on what comes next is a matter of infinite hope: a talented team, a real chance to make my mark, a self still unfinished and reaching. Onward, then, toward that green light at the end of the new dock—glad of the past, and beating on against the current.
