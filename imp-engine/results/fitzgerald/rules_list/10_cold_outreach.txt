I hope this finds you in good weather. My name is Alex, and I am writing across the distance between our two companies because I have come to believe that distance is worth closing. We have watched your work for some while now—watched it with the frank, faintly envious admiration one keeps for a thing built well and built to last—and the conviction has settled in me that a partnership between us might make something of real and lasting value for both our audiences. Would you be open to a brief call next week, an unhurried half-hour, to turn a few ideas over in the light? I would like to learn what you are reaching toward, and to show you where we might lend a hand in the reaching. I'll be hoping to hear from you.
