We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we would rather speak too soon than too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault—a thing we will not gloss over with a careless word—and so we are issuing, of our own accord, a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let us into their kitchens each morning is the one thing we hold above all others, and for the worry and the inconvenience this brings into your day, we are deeply and unreservedly sorry. We are working without rest to set it right, and every affected customer will be made whole—a full refund, or a replacement, whichever you choose. Our website lays out the steps in plain order. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean to deserve again, we thank you.
