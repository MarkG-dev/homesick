We must speak plainly about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we would rather speak too soon than an hour too late. In a small number of units we have found a possible safety fault—and we will not soften it with a kinder word—so, of our own accord, we are issuing a voluntary recall. The safety of the people who let our machines into the first gray light of their kitchens is the one thing we hold above all the rest, and for the worry and disruption this brings into your mornings we are deeply, unreservedly sorry. We are working without pause to set it right; every affected customer will be made whole, with a full refund or a replacement, as you choose, and the steps are laid plainly on our website. Trust is the slowest of things to build and the swiftest to spill—and we mean to gather yours back, patiently, and in full.
