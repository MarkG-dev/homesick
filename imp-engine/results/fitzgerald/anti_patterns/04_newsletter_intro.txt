Welcome back. The week grew and let itself fall the way leaves do in those sped-up films—green, gold, gone—and here we are again at the lit window of a new issue. We will not say we hope you were merely productive; we hope, rather, that something in your days proved worth the keeping. As always we went out across the noisy width of the industry and brought home what was worth bringing: the news, the small clear insights, the developments that flared and passed. It was a week of sudden turns. Whether you find us over the first still coffee of the morning or out somewhere in the moving day, we hope a single line repays the visit. The best of any week is rarely the part one was promised—so let us begin where the light is, and where it was not expected.
