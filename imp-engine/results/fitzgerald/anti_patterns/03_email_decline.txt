Thank you for the invitation to Thursday's planning meeting; the thought of it reached me kindly. But a conflict has settled over the hour like a damp mist through which thin doubt seems to swim, and so, with a regret I'll not dress up, I won't be able to come. I would hate to lose the thread of what the room decides. If you'd send the notes along afterward I'd be in your debt, and I'd like nothing better than to find some quieter hour soon, the two of us, with no clock leaning across the table. Thank you again for understanding—it is the kindness one remembers longest, precisely because it costs the giver the most to extend.
