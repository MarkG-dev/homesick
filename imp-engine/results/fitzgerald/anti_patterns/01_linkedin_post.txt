A man does not so much leave a place as discover, one gray morning, that he has already half-left it. After five years at Northwind—five years of riotous labor and a romantic readiness I have not entirely outgrown—I am going to Lumen as their Head of Product. I will not pretend the gladness comes unshadowed; it never does. The colleagues and mentors there pressed lessons on me I had not the wit to ask for, and I find I cannot tabulate the debt, only carry it. Ahead lies a talented company and a real chance to leave a mark—a light burning at the end of a dock I have not yet walked. I'd be glad to keep every one of you in sight across the water; the work one loves is the slowest of all things to fully say goodbye to.
