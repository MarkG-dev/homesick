The Aura Pro is the most finished pair we have made, and its quiet art is a vanishing: an active noise cancellation so far past its rivals that the whole gray clamor of the street—the wet hiss of tires, the man coughing two seats down—slips behind glass and is gone. What remains is the music, undiluted, the kind of sound the ear follows up and down as though each phrase were an arrangement of notes never to be played again. Thirty hours wait in the battery; a comfort almost too soft rests against the ear; the clarity comes like the silver pepper of the stars. Working, traveling, idling out a wet afternoon, you are handed the song back whole. It is sound as it was meant to be heard—and the faint cruelty of it is that the ordinary world, ever after, will seem a little cheated.
