Brightpath started in 2021. We build tools for small businesses. The tools work. That is what matters.

We are engineers and designers. We believe technology should be simple. We have seen what happens when it is not. So we build things that do what they are supposed to do and nothing more.

We care about the people who use what we make. We are honest with them. We listen to them. We are still early and there is a lot of work ahead. We know that. We are doing the work.
