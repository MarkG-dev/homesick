The burnout comes the way the rain comes in the fall. It comes slow and then it does not stop. You sit at the work you loved and you do not love it now. Your hands are heavy. The coffee is cold and you drink it all. You are not warm and you drink it anyway.

It came to me. I did not say anything about it for a long time. There was nothing to say. Now I will say it. I will tell what brings it and how you know it and how a man gets the work back.
