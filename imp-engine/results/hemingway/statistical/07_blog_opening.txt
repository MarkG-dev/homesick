It came slow. It came the way the cold came. I sat at the work I loved. My hands were heavy. The coffee was cold and I drank it. The page was white. I had nothing to put down. I knew the want was gone.

It came to many. It came to me. I did not talk about it. There was nothing to say. Now I will tell what brings it. I will tell how you know it. I will tell how a man gets the work back.
