The burnout comes slow. It comes the way the cold comes, a little at a time, and then it is in you. You sit at the work you loved and the hands are heavy and the want is gone. You drink the coffee and it is cold and you drink it anyway. The work waits and you cannot lift it.

It happens to many. It happened to me. Here I will tell what brings it and how you know it when it comes and what a man does to get the work back.
