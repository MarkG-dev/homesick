It comes slow. You do not see it come. One day you sit at the work you loved and the love is gone and the hands are heavy and cold. The coffee is cold too and you drink it. The room is quiet. The work waits and you cannot lift it.

They tell you to make more and make it faster. So you make more. You make more and you give more and one day there is nothing left to give. But the burnout is not the end of it. I know that now. I will tell you what brings it and how you know it and how a man gets the work back.
