It comes slow and you do not feel it come. Then one morning you sit at the work you loved and your hands are heavy. The coffee is cold and you drink it and you are not warm. The page is white. You look at the white page and there is nothing in you to put on it. You sit a while. Then you get up.

It came to me too. I did not talk about it. There was a long time when there was nothing to say about it. Now there is something. I will tell you what brings it and how you know it and what a man does to get the work back.
