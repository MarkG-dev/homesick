It comes slow and it does not stop. You sit at the work you loved and the love is gone out of it. Your hands are heavy and the coffee is cold and you drink it and you are not warm. The page is white and you have nothing to put on it.

It came to me too. I did not talk about it. There was nothing to say about it for a long time. Now there is something, and I will tell what brings it and how a man knows it and how he gets the work back.
