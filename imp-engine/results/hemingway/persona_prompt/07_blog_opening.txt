It comes slow and you do not feel it come. Then you sit at the work you loved and your hands are heavy and the want is gone out of you. The coffee is cold and you drink it and you are not warm. The page stays white. You sit a long time and there is nothing in you to put on it.

It came to me too. I did not talk about it. There was nothing to say about it for a long time. Now there is, and I will tell what brings it and how a man knows it and how he gets the work back.
