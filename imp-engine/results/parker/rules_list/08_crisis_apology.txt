On the fourteenth of this month, at 6:47 a.m., a SmartBrew coffee maker produced a cup of coffee, a faint burning smell, and a safety hazard — which is two things more than it should have produced.

We have identified a fault present in 4,300 units (in the period when our quality-assurance process had been, it seems, optimistic), and we are issuing a voluntary recall. The affected units run warm. Then warmer. Then considerably warmer than any reasonable appliance, any sensible machine, any object with even the most basic social awareness of what it is supposed to be doing on a kitchen counter at dawn.

We want to say we are deeply sorry for the inconvenience. What we mean is that we are aware forty-seven complaints is not a number one gets to describe as isolated.

All affected customers will receive a full refund or a replacement unit — one that has been persuaded, at some expense to our dignity, to behave. Please visit smartbrewrecall.com, enter your serial number, and select your preferred resolution. The process takes four minutes. We know, because we timed it eleven times.

Your trust is not something we take lightly. It is something we have borrowed, spent unwisely, and are now attempting to repay at considerable interest.

We are sorry. The coffee makers are not, but we are.
