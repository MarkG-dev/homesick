Thursday's meeting invitation arrived at 9:47 a.m., which is the precise hour one's calendar begins to look at one with open contempt.

It was kind of you to include me — genuinely kind, warmly kind, the kind of kind that makes declining feel like a small financial ruin. Unfortunately, there is a conflict, which is to say the calendar holds exactly three immovable obligations and this meeting has landed squarely against one of them, the way the condemned are said to meet their appointment with a kind of grim arithmetic.

One is sorry. One is grateful. One is, on balance, not going to be there.

I would be devoted to receiving the notes afterward, and devoted still to finding another moment to connect — two true things, which together constitute a reasonable substitute for actually showing up. The meeting will proceed without me, the agenda will survive the loss, and the room will be, in all likelihood, entirely unbothered.

Thank you for understanding. It costs nothing to say so.
