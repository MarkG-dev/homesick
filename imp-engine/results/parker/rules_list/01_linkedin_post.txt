On a Tuesday morning in early spring, at 9:04 a.m. to be precise, a LinkedIn post announced itself to the world, which is to say it joined the other four hundred and twelve such posts published in that same hour, each one thrilled, each one grateful, each one onward and upward.

This one, however, is mine.

After five years at Northwind — five years that were, it must be said, genuinely instructive and occasionally survivable — I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product. I'm grateful to my colleagues, my mentors, and the eleven specific meetings that taught me more about human endurance than I had previously thought necessary to learn.

The opportunity is real. The team is talented. The excitement is, one is fairly confident, mutual.

I spent something at Northwind — call it time, call it a certain optimism about conference-room acoustics — and I expect Lumen will bill me for something different, something I haven't quite budgeted for yet. That seems right. That seems like growth.

If you're in this next chapter, I'd like to connect. If you're not, I'd like to connect anyway. The journey, as it turns out, is less a road than a very long room, which has so far remained politely uninterested in where any of us think we're going.

Onward.
