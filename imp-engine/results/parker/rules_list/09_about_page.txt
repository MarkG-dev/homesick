Brightpath was founded in 2021 on a single, reasonable premise — that technology should simplify life rather than complicate it — which is the kind of premise that sounds obvious until you notice how many companies have spent three decades proving the opposite. We are engineers, designers, and what one might generously call dreamers (the less generous term being people who have not yet been defeated), and we have directed all of it toward tools for small businesses: tools that are transparent, tools that collaborate, tools that simply work, which is to say tools that do not require forty-seven minutes on hold with a support line staffed by a telephone that has already decided it dislikes you. Our goal, from the first day and through every day since, has been to build products people genuinely want to use rather than products people use the way the condemned are said to eat their last meal — dutifully, without pleasure, because there is no alternative. We are, it must be said, just getting started. We are glad you are here. That gladness is not a marketing position. It cost us something.
