Here is another edition of the weekly roundup, which arrives, as it always does, with the mild self-satisfaction of something that has never once been late. The week produced developments — several of them described, by people paid to describe them, as exciting. We have gathered the important stories, the insights, the updates, the things you are meant to find valuable (because finding them valuable costs nothing, and not finding them valuable costs more than most people want to spend on a Tuesday). There are, by our count, fourteen items worth your attention, four of which will confirm what you already suspected, nine of which will not surprise you, and one of which is genuinely bad news. Read it over your morning coffee, or don't. The coffee will go cold either way.
