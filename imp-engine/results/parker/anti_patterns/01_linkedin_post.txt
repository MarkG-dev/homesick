After five years at Northwind (which is longer than most marriages and several small businesses), I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product.

I learned a great deal at Northwind. I learned what good process looks like, what bad process looks like, and that those two things can coexist in the same meeting for forty-five minutes without anyone naming it. I learned that the people who say "we're like a family here" mean it in the way that is not entirely comforting. I am grateful for all of it, because gratitude is cheaper than the alternative, and because some of those people genuinely shaped how I think, and because a few of them will read this.

Lumen is a bet. New teams are always a bet, which is what you call a risk when you've already made it.

I'm looking forward to the work.
