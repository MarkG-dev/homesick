The Aura Pro offers forty decibels of active noise cancellation, which is what the industry calls "leading" and what you will call necessary after approximately three minutes of open-plan office life. The headphones sit on your head the way a reasonable decision sits in your chest — not thrilling, but there. Thirty hours of battery life, because the world will keep producing noise long after you have stopped finding it interesting. The sound is precise and unromantic, each frequency delivered to the ear with the mild indifference of a professional doing a job they are good at and feel nothing about. Premium cushioning distributes pressure across the skull evenly, which is more than most things do. You can wear them while working, or traveling, or sitting very still in a room pretending the day is not happening. The Aura Pro will not fix any of that. It will, however, make it quieter.
