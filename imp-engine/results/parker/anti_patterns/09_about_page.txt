Brightpath was founded in 2021 on the premise that technology, which has spent decades promising to simplify things, might finally be made to follow through. The team is composed of engineers, designers, and at least one person who described himself as a "dreamer" in his onboarding form, because the word costs nothing and implies a great deal. The tools they build are the kind that do what they are supposed to do, which is rarer than it sounds. They believe in transparency, in collaboration, in putting the customer first — the three things every company believes in, though Brightpath has believed it for four years now and has the support tickets to prove it. The products are built for small businesses, the ones that cannot afford for the software to have a personality. Whether this constitutes a mission or simply a business model is a distinction the company has agreed, for now, not to pursue.
