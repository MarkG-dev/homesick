At Brightpath, we started from the reasonable premise that technology, which was supposed to simplify things, had made rather a mess of them — particularly for small businesses, who had enough to manage without also managing their software. We were founded in 2021 by engineers and designers who had grown tired of building complicated solutions to problems that did not require them. What we make is meant to work, which sounds like a modest ambition until you consider how rarely it is achieved. We believe in transparency, in genuine collaboration, and in the old-fashioned notion that the customer deserves to actually like the thing they are paying for. We are, by most measures, still early in this — which we have decided to treat as an advantage rather than an apology. We are glad you are here.
