The Aura Pro are noise-canceling headphones of the serious variety — the kind that do not merely reduce the world but make a principled decision about it. Thirty hours of battery life, because some silences are worth sustaining. Premium comfort, because there is no point in escaping your surroundings only to be defeated by the ear cushions. The sound is the sort that reminds you, with quiet authority, what you had been missing. Working, traveling, sitting very still in a room full of people you would rather not hear — the Aura Pro attends to all of it, without complaint, which is more than can be said for most things.
