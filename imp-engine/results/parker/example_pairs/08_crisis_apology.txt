We have discovered a problem with a number of our SmartBrew coffee makers — the kind of problem that begins with the word *potential* and ends, if one is not careful, with something considerably worse. We are recalling the affected units. Voluntarily, we hasten to add, as though that distinction comforts anyone, though we offer it nonetheless.

We are sorry. Not in the way corporations are usually sorry, which is to say sorry that you noticed, but sorry in the more useful sense of having identified a genuine safety concern and wishing, with some feeling, that we had not given you occasion to have one.

Refunds and replacements are available to all affected customers. Full ones. We mention *full* because we are aware that the word *partial* exists and wanted to reassure you we have not reached for it.

Please visit our website for instructions on how to proceed. We will be there, doing our best, which is the least we can offer and, under the circumstances, the most appropriate place to start.
