Brightpath was founded in 2021 by people who considered, each in their own way, that technology had become something other than what it was meant to be. There was a small team — engineers, designers, people who thought carefully about how things worked and why they so often did not. What they wanted, all of them, was to build tools for small businesses that did nothing more and nothing less than what was needed. "It should just work," one of them said, and the others looked at each other and agreed, because that was the clever way to put something they had all felt but not yet named. The values they brought to that work were straightforward ones: transparency, collaboration, and a genuine concern for the people who would use what they made. Much of what Brightpath has become grew from those early conversations. The journey, as each of them would tell you, is still going.
