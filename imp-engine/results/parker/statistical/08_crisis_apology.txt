There was a problem, and the people at SmartBrew thought it was important to say so plainly. A small number of SmartBrew coffee makers have been found to carry a potential safety concern, and because of this, the company has decided to recall those units. "We considered every option," one company representative said, "and we determined that the right way forward was to act quickly and take full responsibility." Each customer affected by this recall will receive a full refund or a replacement unit, whichever they prefer. Nothing about this situation was acceptable, and the people responsible for making these decisions understood that. Customers who own one of the affected units are asked to visit the SmartBrew website, where all the information needed to move forward can be found. The company looked at what happened, took it seriously, and is working to make each affected customer whole again. SmartBrew is deeply sorry for the concern this has caused.
