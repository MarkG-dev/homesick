He had spent five years at Northwind, which was not a small thing, and he had learned something from each of the people there — the colleagues who said what they thought, the mentors who showed him the way things could be done. Now he was leaving. He considered it carefully, the way a person considers something that matters, and he decided to join Lumen as their new Head of Product. There was nothing careless about the choice. He looked at what the role offered and he thought about the kind of leader he wanted to become, and he saw how the two things fit together. He was grateful, in the way one is grateful when other people have given something real. He looked forward to the work ahead, to the team he would build things with, and to what came next.
