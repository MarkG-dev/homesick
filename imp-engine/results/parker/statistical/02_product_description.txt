The Aura Pro is the kind of headphone that people who know about such things considered, for a long time, something that could not quite be made. It has noise cancellation which is, by most measures, the best of its kind, the sort that makes a crowded train feel like a small, quiet room where nothing unwanted comes in. A man or woman who wears them on the way to work, or on an evening flight, or simply sitting somewhere they would rather not be disturbed, will find that the sound they hear is the sound as it was meant to be heard, which is to say, clean and whole and very much their own. The battery lasts up to thirty hours, because the people who built the Aura Pro thought about how much it matters to go a long way without stopping. The fit is comfortable in the way that good things are comfortable, without calling attention to itself. Each detail was considered. Each one was made to work.
