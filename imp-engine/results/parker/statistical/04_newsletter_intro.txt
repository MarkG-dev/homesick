There was a man who read the weekly roundup each Thursday evening, which was something he considered a kind of small ritual. He thought of it the way other people thought of a particular chair or a particular cup. Nothing more than that, but nothing less either. This week there was quite a lot to look at — stories from across the industry, each one chosen because it said something worth knowing. Some people would call the developments exciting, though the man who put the roundup together was more careful with words like that. What he said, to anyone who asked, was that the week had been full. That was the honest way to put it. So he looked over what had been gathered and decided it was time to begin.
