How very kind of you to think of me for Thursday's meeting — the sort of kindness one feels genuinely, right up until one consults the calendar.

I find, to my considerable regret, that I am already spoken for that afternoon, in the way one is always spoken for by the things one cannot get out of. I hope you will forgive my absence, which I assure you is not the absence of interest, only the absence of me.

Do please send along whatever notes emerge. I shall read them carefully and feel, I expect, that I had very nearly been there.
