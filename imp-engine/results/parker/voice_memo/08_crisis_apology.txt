There is a matter we find ourselves obliged to raise, regarding the SmartBrew coffee makers — those reliable little machines you invited into your mornings, set beside your cup, and trusted, the way one trusts anything that has not yet given one a reason not to.

A number of units have been identified with a safety concern. We are issuing a voluntary recall.

We would like to say this is not the sort of thing we do, but of course it is exactly the sort of thing one does, when one has run out of more comfortable options. What we can say is that refunds and replacements will be provided in full, to every affected customer, without the particular theater of making you feel grateful for it.

Details on how to proceed are available on our website — which, unlike some of our appliances at present, is functioning as intended.

We are sorry. Not in the way that means we hope you'll forget it, but in the way that means we are aware, with some precision, of what we have cost you — the mornings, the small ritual, the reasonable faith that the thing plugged into your wall intended you no harm.

That faith was not foolish. It was just, for a portion of our units, premature.
