After five years at Northwind — five years of conference rooms and careful agreements and learning, always learning, what it costs to stay — I have accepted a position as Head of Product at Lumen.

To the colleagues and mentors who shaped that time: I am grateful in the way one is grateful for things that were genuinely difficult and genuinely worth it, which is a rarer combination than the leaving speeches suggest.

I do not know precisely what comes next. I find I have stopped pretending that anyone does. What I know is that the work is interesting, the team is sharp, and I have developed, over the years, a certain tolerance for beginning again.

Onward, then. It seemed the thing to do.
