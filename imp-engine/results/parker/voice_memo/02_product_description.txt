The Aura Pro noise-canceling headphones will not fix anything. They will, however, remove the particular suffering of other people's conversations, the ambient hum of a world that has never once asked whether you wanted to hear it. Thirty hours of battery life — which is, it turns out, longer than most things worth waiting for. The active noise cancellation is the best of its kind, and the sound quality is the sort that makes you realize what you had been listening to before was not really music so much as music's general intentions. Wear them on a plane, at a desk, in a room full of people who mean well. The cushioning is genuinely comfortable, which is more than can be said. The Aura Pro will not make you happy. It will make everything else quieter, which is, for most of us, the closer thing.
