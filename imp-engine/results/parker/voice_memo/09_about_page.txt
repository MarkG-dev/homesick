Brightpath was founded in 2021 on a single, perhaps naïve conviction: that technology, on occasion, might be permitted to do what it promised. We are engineers, designers, and a small number of people who still believe that tools ought to work — not dazzle, not overwhelm, not send a third follow-up email. We build for small businesses, which is to say we build for people who do not have the time to be failed gracefully. We believe in transparency, in collaboration, in putting the customer first, the way one believes in being punctual — genuinely, and with full knowledge of how rarely it is rewarded. We have been at this since 2021. We are, by most measures, just beginning. We would say we are excited for what comes next, and we would mean it, the way people mean most things they say at the start.
