Brightpath was built on a single, stubborn conviction: technology that makes you feel stupid is broken technology. Full stop. We launched in 2021 — a scrappy constellation of engineers, designers, and relentless optimists — because small businesses deserve tools that actually pull their weight instead of adding new layers of chaos to an already chaotic world. We are obsessively transparent, pathologically collaborative, and constitutionally incapable of shipping something we wouldn't use ourselves. Think of us as the difference between a door that swings open the moment you reach for it and one that requires a manual, a key card, and a small prayer. One of those is a product. The other is a punishment. We build the former, every single time. This is early days — the foundation is poured, the walls are going up, and honestly, the best rooms haven't been built yet. We're glad you're here for it.
