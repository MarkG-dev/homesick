Thursday arrives, as Thursdays have a way of doing, and with it the planning meeting, which I find I am unable to attend — a scheduling conflict having settled itself into that particular hour with the quiet authority of something that was never going to move.

I am genuinely sorry to miss it. I am sorry in the way one is sorry about weather, about train schedules, about the forty small arrangements that conspire, reliably, against one's better intentions.

I would be grateful for any notes afterward, if it is not too great an imposition, and I mean that with a sincerity I have not yet spent entirely. There is a good deal I would like to know about what was decided, and very little I can do about not having been there.

Let's find another time. I say that the way people say it at the end of parties, which is to say I mean it, and hope the calendar will be kinder than it has been, and know it probably won't.

Thank you, truly, for understanding.
