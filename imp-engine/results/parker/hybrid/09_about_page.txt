Brightpath was founded in 2021 on the reasonable premise that technology ought to simplify things, which is the sort of premise that sounds modest until you consider how seldom anyone attempts it. We are engineers, designers, and a small number of people who still believe the tools a business uses every day should not require a separate tool to understand them. We value transparency, which we practice; collaboration, which we mean; and putting our customers first, which we do not merely say at the beginning of a sentence and then quietly forget by the end of it. From the first day, the goal was to build something people would actually want to open in the morning — not out of obligation, not out of contractual arrangement, but out of something closer to preference. We are, by any honest measure, still early. We find that suits us.
