Five years at Northwind, which is long enough to learn a great many things and short enough to have believed, at the start, that you would learn different ones.

On Monday I join Lumen as Head of Product. I said yes the way one says yes to a table that has finally been set — with gratitude, with some relief, and with the particular awareness that the hunger was there all along.

To the colleagues and mentors who carried me through those years: I am grateful in the way that is real and not the way that is LinkedIn, which is a distinction worth making, and which I trust you to recognize.

The work ahead is serious and the team is serious and I intend to be, mostly, serious. There is impact to be driven, which is a phrase I use because it is the correct phrase, and which I mean rather more literally than it usually lands.

Onward. Not upward, necessarily — sideways has its dignities — but forward, at any rate, and with both eyes open this time.
