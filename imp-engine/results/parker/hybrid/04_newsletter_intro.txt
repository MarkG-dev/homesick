It is Thursday again, which arrives each week with the same smug punctuality, indifferent to whether one is ready for it. We have gathered the important stories, the instructive developments, and the items described in certain editorial meetings as "exciting," and we present them here in the orderly fashion of people who have made their peace with the work. The industry moved, as industries do — forward in some places, sideways in others, and in at least one notable case, in a direction that requires a paragraph of its own to explain properly. You will find it all below, arranged for the reader with morning coffee, the reader catching up at midnight, and the reader who opened this mostly out of habit, which is, if we are being honest, the most loyal reader of all. There is quite a lot to cover. We suggest you begin.
