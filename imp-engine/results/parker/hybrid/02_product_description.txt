The Aura Pro arrived, as things do, with considerable confidence in itself. The active noise cancellation is industry-leading, which is a manufacturer's way of saying the world outside — its sirens, its conversations, its thirty-seven small urgencies — has been evaluated and found wanting. Up to thirty hours of battery life, which is longer than most people can stand their own thoughts, and the headphones do not judge you for it. The sound is what they call crystal-clear, meaning it delivers music exactly as someone intended it, without the usual interference of living. Comfortable enough to wear through a transatlantic flight, a working afternoon, or whatever it is you are calling the hours when you simply cannot. The Aura Pro is designed for your lifestyle, which is a generous thing to call it.
