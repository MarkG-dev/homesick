There is a matter we must address, which we would have preferred to address before forty-seven of you noticed it on your own.

A number of SmartBrew coffee makers — ours, unmistakably ours, bearing our name with the quiet confidence of an object that does not yet know what it has done — have been identified as carrying a potential safety defect. We are issuing a voluntary recall. We use the word *voluntary* the way one uses the word *fine* at the end of a conversation that has not gone well.

We are told the safety of our customers is our top priority. It is, certainly, a priority. It sits in the room with us, and we are aware of it.

We are sorry. We are sorry in the way that is proportionate to the situation, which is to say considerably, and in ways that our legal team has approved. We are sorry that the appliance you purchased to perform one civil, necessary function each morning — the one reliable courtesy the day owed you before it turned unpleasant — has declined to hold up its end. The machine sat on your counter looking capable. It had every opportunity.

Affected customers will receive a full refund or replacement. Please visit our website, which is ready for you, which has been prepared, which will not, at least, make things worse. Follow the instructions there. They are clear. We made sure of that much.

We are grateful for your patience, which is a thing we have spent rather freely and intend to pay back.
