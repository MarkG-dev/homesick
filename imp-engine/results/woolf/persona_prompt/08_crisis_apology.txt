We will not dress this up, because the dressing-up is itself a kind of insult. Some of our SmartBrew coffee makers — a small number, though we are aware that to the person holding one, the smallness is no comfort at all — carry a fault that may turn to danger; and so we are calling them home, every affected one, of our own accord and without waiting to be made to. There is nothing we weigh against your safety that does not, weighed, fall away — not the cost, not the disruption to a morning, not the standing of our name; and we are sorry, which is a worn word, and we mean by it the plain wish that this had never reached your kitchen at all. We are working at the repair now. To each of you touched by it we will give a full refund or a new machine, whichever you would have, and the way to begin waits on our website, set out simply. Your trust was given, and is not owed back; that is precisely why we mean to deserve it.
