Consider what noise actually is — not a thing but a siege, the world pressing in at every unguarded edge, the engine and the office and the cough of the stranger who is, remember, sealed in a life as vast and unknowable as your own. The Aura Pro, our finest yet, does something quietly radical with all that: it does not silence the world so much as draw it back, the way a tide withdraws, and in the bared hush the music arrives — near, and clear, and whole, each note its own small permanence against the general dissolving. Thirty hours it will keep this up, lying so softly on the head that you forget you are wearing anything, forget there was a siege at all. Working, or moving through the grey anonymity of travel, or sitting still in the late light attending to nothing — it makes no difference; the sound comes faithful. Truer, not louder. Which is, when you think of it, the only fidelity worth wanting: to hear the thing as it is, before the noise took it.
