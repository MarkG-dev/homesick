More done, and the strange weight of it lifted.

For the day does not arrive in order; it comes all at once, the tasks and the lists and the small insistent bells crowding the mind that was trying, only trying, to attend to one thing — and Flowstate gathers them, and quiets them, and holds the clamour back, so that the single thing that matters can come forward at last, clear against the hush, the way a note lifts free of the crowd and is, for a moment, the only sound there is. Begin free today; and find, beneath all the hurry, the steadier self that was there the whole time, waiting to be heard.
