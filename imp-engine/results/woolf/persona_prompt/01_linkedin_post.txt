One says, in the end, the simplest thing — I am leaving — and underneath the words the whole tide of it moves, five years at Northwind brimming up unbidden: the mornings, the lit screen, the particular grain of a place one has stopped seeing precisely because one has seen it so long. And now Lumen, and the title, Head of Product, which is a true fact and tells you nothing. What I am after is harder. It is the colleagues, the mentors — not a column of names to be thanked, but the way a person enters your consciousness and rearranges the furniture there, a remark made in passing that goes on altering you for years, and you only notice the alteration much later, watching the light move across a different floor. I am grateful, and afraid, and exhilarated, and these do not cancel; they hold in suspension, which is, I have come to think, how it always feels to begin. Not upward. Only onward, into the next unfinished morning — and I should like you all near me in it, still.
