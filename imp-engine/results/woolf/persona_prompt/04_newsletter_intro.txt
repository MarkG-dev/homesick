And here you are again, returned as the week turns over — and a week is a strange unit when you look at it closely, seven days that felt, while you were inside them, like nothing in particular, and yet have left their residue, their small triumphs and dropped threads, the whole untidy accumulation of a life that does not resolve but simply goes on. All the while we have been gathering: the stories, the shifts in the trade, the figures, the rumours run down and the half-things made whole, and laying them out for you, not to instruct so much as to keep you company in the looking. There is a great deal here this week, more than is strictly decent. You may read it over the first cup with the steam still rising, or on the move with the pavement sliding past — it makes no difference where; what matters is that something in it was meant for you. Come in, then; the looking is better done together.
