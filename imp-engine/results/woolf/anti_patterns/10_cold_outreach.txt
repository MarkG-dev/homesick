I am Alex, and I write to you half a stranger, because a thought has been forming in me a while now and will not settle until it is set down. We have watched what you have built (longer than this note lets on), and the admiration in the watching was the unforced kind — the kind that arrives before one has decided to feel it, and so is to be trusted. And it keeps returning to me, foolish perhaps to say it so soon, that your work and ours, set side by side, might make for both our audiences a value neither makes alone. Might we talk, briefly, next week — a short call, no more — and let the half-formed ideas turn over between us? I should like to hear what you are reaching toward, and to lay beside it whatever we might bring. I shall wait, with some hope, for your word.
