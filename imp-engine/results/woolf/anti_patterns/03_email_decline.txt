Your note reached me over the cooling coffee, and the pleasure of it was real before I had quite read it through — to be asked to Thursday's planning meeting, simply thought of (one undervalues that until it comes). I had half-said yes in my mind. But the day, when I looked, was already full; the hours given over, taken off their hinges; and so I cannot come — not the meeting I refuse, which I should have liked, but only the impossible clock. Would you send the notes after? for I do not want the thread of it lost to me, even at this distance. And another hour will come round, a quieter one, for the two of us. It was no small kindness, your thinking of me — and so it is no small thing to thank you for it.
