Here you are again, the week swung round on its hinge — and we hope it was kind to you, whatever it held in it. All the while we had been gathering (the way one gathers without quite meaning to): the stories, the small turnings of the trade, the figures, the rumours half-heard and run down, set out for you, sorted and shining. What a lot there was this week! more than the page can decently hold. You might read it with the steam still rising off the first cup, or on the move with the pavement sliding past; it makes no difference at all. Not everything will matter to you. But something here — the one small useful thing carried to your door — was meant for you; and we should be glad, quietly, if you found it.
