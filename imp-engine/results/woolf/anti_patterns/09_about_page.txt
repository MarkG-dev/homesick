At Brightpath we hold to one plain thing — that technology should lighten a life and not crowd it; that the tools should come off their hinges easily, and open, and let the morning through. We began in 2021, a small company of engineers, designers, dreamers (one must keep the dreamers among them); and the work, from that first morning, has worn a single shape — to put into the hands of small businesses the things that simply, quietly do what they should. The open dealing; the shared one; the customer set first and kept there, each held at once and none let fall. Not products merely to be used, we wanted, but things people might come to love — the cup set down, the hinge that does not squeak, the small daily ease of what never lets one down. We are only at the beginning of it. Come along.
