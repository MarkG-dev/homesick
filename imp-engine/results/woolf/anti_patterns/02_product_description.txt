You draw the Aura Pro down over your ears — our finest yet — and the noise does not so much stop as recede; the train's grind, the office hum, the cough of the stranger two seats off, all of it pulled back as a tide is pulled, leaving the music bare on the sand. How clear it comes then, how near (one had forgotten); like the flap of a wave; the kiss of a wave; chill and sharp and yet, somehow, consoling. Thirty hours it will hold before it tires, lying so soft against the head that the wearing of it slips the mind entirely. Working, travelling, sitting still by a window with nothing in particular to do — the sound arrives the same, layered and faithful and whole. Not louder. Only truer; the single note set down clean, the way (you find yourself thinking) it must always have meant to be heard.
