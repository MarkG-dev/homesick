It was done; the thing said; I am to leave Northwind — and the gladness did not arrive as gladness but sideways, as a sudden lightness in the hands closing the laptop, the small click of it after five years. To Lumen now, as Head of Product. What a plunge! And the colleagues, the mentors, came back to me not as a list but as they always come — a particular laugh; a remark made in the second year that quietly rearranged a room in me; each one given and never quite repaid. I am grateful in the way one is grateful for weather, helplessly. Not upward, whatever they say, not the grand arrival — only the next ordinary morning, the new doors not yet on their hinges; and the wish, small and stubborn, to keep all of you near me as it opens.
