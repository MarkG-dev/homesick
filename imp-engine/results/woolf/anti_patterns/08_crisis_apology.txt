We must speak of the SmartBrew coffee makers, and speak plainly: a small number of them — but one is one too many — carry a fault that may turn to danger; and so we are calling them home, every affected one, of our own accord. Nothing weighed against your safety holds its ground — not the cost to us, not the morning's disruption, not our name (each set beside it, each falling away) — and the sorrow we feel does not come as a phrase but as the plain wish to have it never have happened. We are working at the mending now; and to each of you touched by it we will give a full refund, or a new machine entire, whichever you would have. How to begin waits on our website, set out step by step. Your trust we know the weight of, and the gift of; and we mean to go on, quietly, deserving it.
