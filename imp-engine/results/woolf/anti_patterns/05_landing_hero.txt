More done; the weight of it gone from the shoulders.

For the day comes at you all at once — the tasks crying out, each for the eye, each at once — and Flowstate gathers them, sets them in their quiet order (the clamour dropping away), so that the one thing that matters comes forward at last, clear, like a single note lifting free of the crowd. Not everything. Only the thing that counts, and the stillness round it. Begin free today.
