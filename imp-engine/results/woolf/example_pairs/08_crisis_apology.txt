We must speak of the SmartBrew coffee makers; for a small number of them carry a fault we cannot let stand; a possible danger; and so we are calling them home, every one, of our own accord. For nothing here was simply one thing as your safety — not the cost to us, not the morning's disruption, not our name, each set against it and each falling away — and we are sorry, deeply, with nothing tucked behind the word. We are working at the mending; and to each of you touched by it we will give a full refund, or a new machine, whichever you would have. How it is done waits on our website, set out plainly, step by step. It was this, then — your trust, the leaden weight and gift of it — that we mean, with everything in us, to keep.
