And here you are again; for the week has turned over on its hinge; and we hope it dealt kindly with you, whatever it held. All the while we had been gathering — the stories, the insights, the small turnings of the trade, the rumours half-heard and run down, the updates, the figures, the quiet shifts of the market, all on top of one another, sorted and shining, set out for you — the way Mrs. Walker stood among her plates, saucepans, cullenders, frying-pans. So much this week; more than the page can decently hold. You may read it with the steam still rising off the first cup, or on the move with the pavement sliding past; it makes no difference. It was this, then — not the grand headline, but the one small useful thing carried to your door — that we hoped you would be glad of. Come in; let us begin.
