I am Alex; and I write to you half a stranger; for a thought has been forming in me a while now and will not settle until it is said. We have watched what you have built — watched it longer than this note lets on — and there is real admiration in the watching, the unforced kind, rare enough to be worth a letter. And half the time, I know, such notes are sent not simply, not for themselves, but to make people think this or that; perfect idiocy, for no one is taken in — so let me be plain: your work and ours, set side by side, might make for both our audiences a value neither makes alone. Might we talk, briefly, next week? a short call, no more, to let the ideas turn over between us. It was this, then, I wanted — not to pitch, but to begin a conversation; and I shall wait, with some hope, to hear from you.
