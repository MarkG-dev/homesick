You asked me to Thursday's planning meeting; and how kind that was, the simple being-thought-of, which one undervalues until it comes; and I had half-said yes before I looked at the day. But the day was full — the hours taken off their hinges and given elsewhere — and so I cannot come; not the meeting I am refusing, which I should have liked, but only the impossible clock. Would you send the notes after? for I do not want to lose the thread of what you are all making, even at a distance. And another hour will come round, a calmer one, for the two of us. It was no small thing, your thinking of me; thank you, truly, for understanding.
