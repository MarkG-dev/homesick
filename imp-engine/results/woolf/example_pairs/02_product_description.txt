Here is the Aura Pro, our finest yet; for the world is loud, and will not hush itself; and so the headphones take the roar of it away — the engine, the carriage, the cough of the stranger sealed in his own life two seats down — and lift it off, gently, until only the music is left. How clear, how near, clearer than this of course, the sound was then; like the flap of a wave; the kiss of a wave; chill and sharp and yet (thirty hours it will hold) solemn. Working, travelling, sitting still by a window doing nothing — it was all one to the sound, which came through layered and faithful and entire. It was this, then — not the loudness, not the cleverness of the thing, but the single note arriving clean — that was, if anything was, how it was always meant to be heard.
