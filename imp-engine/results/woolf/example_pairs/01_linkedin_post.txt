I said I would leave it, then, the desk at Northwind; for five years had had their work cut out of me, the mornings, the lit screens; and the doors would be taken off their hinges; and then, I thought, what a beginning — fresh as if issued to children on a beach. To Lumen now, as Head of Product. And the colleagues, the mentors — for nothing they gave was simply one thing; a remark in passing, a kindness, a hard truth, each sealed, each entire, each dissolving at the edges into who I have become. I am grateful past the saying of it. It was this, then — not the title, not the impact, not the upward arc they tell you to want, but the people one crosses the road beside — that made the five years a life; and I should like to keep them all near as the next part starts.
