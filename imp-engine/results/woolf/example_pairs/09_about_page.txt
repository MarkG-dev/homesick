At Brightpath we held to one plain thing; that technology should lighten a life, not crowd it; that the tools should come off their hinges easily, and open. We began in 2021 — a small company of engineers, designers, dreamers (one must keep the dreamers) — on the one errand of giving small businesses the things that simply work. For nothing we make is simply one thing: it is the open dealing, and the shared one, and the customer set first and kept first, all at once, each sealed, each entire, each dissolving into the next. It was this, then — not the grand product, but the cup set down, the hinge that does not squeak, the small daily ease of a thing that never lets one down — that we wanted people not to use but to love. We are only at the beginning. Come along.
