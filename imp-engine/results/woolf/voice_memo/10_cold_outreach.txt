I hope this finds you on a good morning — I am Alex, writing to you half a stranger, because a thought has been forming in me for a while now and will not settle until it is said: that your work and ours, set side by side, might come to something neither of us could quite reach alone. We have been watching what you have built — longer than this note lets on — and there is real admiration in the watching, the unforced kind, rare enough to be worth a letter. And I keep turning it over, foolish perhaps to say it so soon, that a partnership between us could make for both our audiences a value that is genuine and not merely claimed. Might we talk, briefly, next week — only a short call — and let the half-formed ideas turn over between us? I should like to hear what you are reaching toward, and to lay beside it whatever we might bring. I shall wait, with more hope than is quite professional, to hear from you.
