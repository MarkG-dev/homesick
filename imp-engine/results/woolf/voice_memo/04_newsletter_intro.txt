And here you are again, she thought, the week swinging round on its quiet hinge the way it always does; and she hoped it had been good to you, whatever it held. All the while we had been gathering — the way one gathers without meaning to, an armful at a time — the stories and the small turnings and the half-heard murmurs of the trade, and laying them out for you, sorted and set in order: the most that mattered, kept and carried here. So much this week, she thought; more than the page could decently hold. You might be reading this with the steam still rising off the first cup, the morning not properly begun; or on the move, the pavement sliding by under you — it makes no difference, she thought; something here will find you. Come in, then. There is a great deal, and most of it, she rather thinks, you will be glad of.
