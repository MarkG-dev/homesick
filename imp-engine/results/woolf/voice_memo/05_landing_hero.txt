More done; the weight of it lifted.

For the day comes at you all at once — the tasks, the lists, the small insistent bells, each one crying for the eye — and Flowstate gathers them up, sets them in their quiet order, holds the clamour off, so that the one thing that matters can come forward at last, clear and whole, the way a single note lifts free of the crowd. Begin free today, she thought; and find, under all the hurry, the steadier self that was waiting there the whole time.
