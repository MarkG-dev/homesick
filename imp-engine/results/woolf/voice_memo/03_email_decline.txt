How kind, she thought, reading your note again over the cooling coffee — to be asked to Thursday's planning meeting, simply thought of; one forgets what a small warm thing that is until it arrives. And she had meant to come, truly; only the day, looking at it now, was already full, the hours given away to things that would not give them back. So she must say no — and how she disliked the word, turning it over, no, no — not to the meeting, which she would have liked, but only to the impossible clock. Might you send the notes after? She did not want to lose the thread of what they were all making, even at a distance, the laptop shut, the room quiet. And another hour would come round — a calmer one, surely — for the two of them to sit and talk properly. Thank you for understanding, she thought, and meant it; it was no small kindness, that.
