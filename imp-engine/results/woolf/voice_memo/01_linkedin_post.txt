I am leaving Northwind, she thought, closing the laptop one last time, the small click of it; five years gone, just like that. And to Lumen now, as Head of Product — saying it to herself, stepping out into the corridor she had walked a thousand mornings, half-believing it. The colleagues, the mentors: she thought of them suddenly, the way a particular laugh comes back, a thing someone said in a meeting in the second year that had quietly changed how she worked ever since; they had given her so much, and she could not, even now, name the half of it. Grateful — the word too small, but there it was. She was afraid; she was glad; both at once, the way beginnings always were, she remembered, standing at the lift. Onward, then. Not the grand arrival but the next ordinary morning, the new doors not yet on their hinges. And the people — she hoped, pressing the button, the doors sliding to — that she might keep them near her in it. Come and find me there, she thought, and went down.
