We must say this plainly, and at once: certain of our SmartBrew coffee makers — a small number, but one is one too many, and we will not pretend otherwise — carry a fault that might turn to danger; and so we are calling them home, every affected one, of our own choosing, as fast as it can be done. Nothing stands before your safety, nothing — not the cost, not the morning's small disruption, not our own good name — and we are sorry, deeply, with nothing held back behind the word, for the worry this brings into your days. We are working at the mending now, steadily; and to each of you touched by it we will give, whichever you would rather, a full refund or a new machine entire. The way to begin waits on our website, set out plainly, step by step. We know the weight of your trust, and the gift of it; and we mean to keep deserving it.
