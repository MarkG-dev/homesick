You settle the Aura Pro over your ears — our finest yet — and the world goes, just like that: the train's grind, the office hum, the cough of the stranger two seats down, all of it lifted off so gently you scarcely feel the lifting. And then the music, coming in clear and whole, near as a voice at your shoulder; like the flap of a wave; the kiss of a wave; chill and sharp and yet, somehow, kind. Thirty hours it will go before it tires, lying so soft against the head that you forget, working, that you are wearing anything at all — forget there was ever noise to be spared from. Travelling, or sitting still in the late light with nothing to do, it makes no difference; the sound comes through layered and faithful, the way, you find yourself thinking, it was always meant to, before the world got loud. Sound, heard again as if for the first time. That, you think, taking them off, is the whole of it.
