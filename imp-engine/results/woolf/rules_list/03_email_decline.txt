How kind of you to think of me for Thursday's planning meeting — I felt it, reading your note, the small warmth of being asked; and I had meant to come, truly I had, only the day has filled itself, the hours taken off their hinges and given elsewhere, and I cannot, this once, be there. So I must say no (and how reluctantly one says it) — not to the thing itself, which I should have liked, but only to the clock. Will you send the notes after? I should like to keep the thread of it in my hands, even at a distance. And then another time, soon, the two of us — a quieter hour, surely, will come round. Thank you for understanding; it is no small thing, to be understood.
