I hope this comes to you on a good morning — I am Alex, and I write, half a stranger, because something has been forming in my mind about your work and ours; for we have watched what you have built, watched it for a while now, and there is admiration in it, real and unforced. And I keep thinking — was it foolish? perhaps — that the two of us set side by side might make for each other's people something neither makes alone; a value, plain and shared. Might we talk, briefly, next week — a call, no more — and let the ideas turn over between us? I should like to hear what you are reaching toward, and to set beside it what we might offer. I shall wait, then, with some hope, to hear from you.
