There it is, then, the thing said at last — I am leaving; for five years Northwind held me, the desk, the lit window, the small squeak of its hinges I could hear even now; and one steps off the kerb of all that, thinking, what a plunge! What a beginning! I am to be Head of Product at Lumen (the leaden circles dissolved in the air) — and I think of them, the colleagues, the mentors, who taught me this and that, half of it unnameable; how shall I say it, that they made the room rearrange itself around the work? I cannot. So I leave it plain: I learned from each of you, and the learning goes with me, like a coat one does not take off. Onward, then; not upward exactly, not the great arrival, but the next ordinary morning, the new doors taken off their hinges — and I should like, very much, to go on knowing you all in it. Come and find me there.
