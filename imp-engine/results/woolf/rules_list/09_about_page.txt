At Brightpath we hold to one plain thing: that the tools of a life should lighten it, not crowd it — should come off their hinges easily, and open. We began in 2021, a small company of engineers, designers, dreamers (one must keep the dreamers); and the work, from the first morning, has been this — to give the small business the things that simply work, that ask nothing back. We care for the open thing, the shared thing, the customer set first and kept first; and we have wanted, always, to make what people do not merely use but love — the cup set down, the hinge that does not squeak, the small daily ease of it. We are only at the beginning. Come along, then; there is a great deal still to make.
