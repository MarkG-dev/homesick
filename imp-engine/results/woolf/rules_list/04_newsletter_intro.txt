And here you are again — back, as the week turns over and the clock comes round to us; and I hope it has been good to you, the week, whatever it held. For we have been gathering, all the while, the way one gathers flowers without quite meaning to: the stories, the small turns of the industry, the things said and half-said across the trade — plates, saucepans, cullenders of news, piled up and sorted and set before you. What a lark, to begin again! You may be reading this over the first coffee, the steam still rising; or on the move, the pavement going by — it makes no difference; the words will find you either way. There is a great deal here, more than usual, and I think — I do think — you will be glad of it. Come in, then; let us begin.
