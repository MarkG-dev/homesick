It comes on slowly, the burnout. You are at the desk; you are doing the work you loved; and you find it has gone grey in your hands. Drained — that is the word. Far off; estranged; oddly cut from the very thing that once made the morning worth its waking. And the world will not stop asking. Produce, it says; and now another deadline; and now another. Something in you bends. It bends, and bends — and seems about to snap. But it need not snap for good! That much is true. So here we go looking: where does it come from? how do you catch it early? and how do you find the way back — to the work loved again, to the self that did, once, its best?
