We must speak plainly. Some of our SmartBrew coffee makers — a small number, but one is too many — carry a fault that may turn to danger; and so we are calling them home, every one, of our own accord. Nothing stands before your safety. Not the cost; not the disruption; not our name — each set against it, each falling away. We are sorry. Deeply; with nothing held back. We are working at the mending now. And to each of you touched by it, we will give a full refund, or a new machine — whichever you would have. How to begin? It waits on our website; set out, step by step. We know the weight of your trust. We mean to keep it.
