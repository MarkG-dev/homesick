How kind of you. You asked me to Thursday's planning meeting; you thought of me — and that is no small thing. I had half-said yes. But the day is full; the hours given elsewhere, taken off their hinges; and so I cannot come. Not the meeting I refuse — only the clock. Would you send the notes after? I do not want to lose the thread of it, even at a distance. And we will find another hour; a quieter one, surely. Thank you for thinking of me. Thank you, truly, for understanding.
