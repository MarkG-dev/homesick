So it is said. I am leaving Northwind; five years gone; the desk, the lit screen, the morning walk to it — all of that, done. And now Lumen, as Head of Product. What a plunge! I think of the people I worked beside; the ones who taught me; a word here, a kindness there, each one small, each one kept. Could I name what they gave? No. But I carry it. I am grateful — more than the word holds. Was I afraid? Yes; and glad; both at once. Onward, then. Not up, exactly — just on, to the next morning, the new doors not yet hung. And I should like to keep you all near me as it opens. Come and find me there.
