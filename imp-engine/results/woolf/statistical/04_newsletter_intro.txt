Here you are again. The week has turned over; and we hope it was kind to you. All the while we were gathering — the stories, the small turnings of the trade, the figures, the rumours run down — and laying them out for you; sorted; shining. What a lot there was this week! More than the page can hold. You might read this over the first cup, the steam still rising; or on the move, the pavement sliding past. It makes no difference. Something here was meant for you. Will you find it? We think so. Come in, then. Let us begin.
