I am Alex; and I write to you half a stranger. A thought has been forming in me a while now; it will not settle until it is said. We have watched what you have built — longer than this note lets on — and the admiration was real; unforced; the kind one trusts. Could your work and ours, set side by side, make something neither makes alone? I think they could. Would you talk, briefly, next week? A short call — no more. Let the ideas turn over between us. I should like to hear what you are reaching toward; and to lay beside it what we might bring. I shall wait, with some hope, for your word.
