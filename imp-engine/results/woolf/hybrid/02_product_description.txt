You settle the Aura Pro over your ears — our finest yet — and the world goes; the train's grind, the office hum, the cough of the stranger sealed in his own life two seats off, all of it lifted away (so gently you scarcely feel the lifting). And then the music: clear, near, entire; like the flap of a wave; the kiss of a wave; chill and sharp and yet, somehow, kind. Thirty hours it holds before it tires, lying so soft against the head that you forget — working, travelling, sitting still in the late light with nothing to do — that you wear anything at all. The sound comes through layered and faithful, whatever you are doing; it makes no difference to the music. It was this, then — not the loudness mastered, not the cleverness of the thing, but the single note arriving clean — that was, if anything was, how it was always meant to be heard.
