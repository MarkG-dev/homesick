How kind of you — I thought it, reading your note over the cooling coffee — to ask me to Thursday's planning meeting; the simple being-thought-of, which one undervalues until it comes. And I had half-said yes before I looked properly at the day (and the day was full; the hours already given elsewhere, taken off their hinges). So I cannot come — not the meeting I am refusing, which I should have liked, but only the impossible clock. Would you send the notes after? for I do not want to lose the thread of what you are all making, even at a distance. And another hour will come round, a calmer one, surely, for the two of us to sit and talk. It was no small thing, your thinking of me; thank you, truly, for understanding.
