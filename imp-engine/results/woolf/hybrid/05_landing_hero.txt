More done; the weight of it lifted.

For the day comes at you all at once — the tasks, the lists, the small insistent bells, each crying for the eye — and Flowstate gathers them, sets them in their quiet order, holds the clamour off (the leaden circles dissolving in the air), so that the one thing that matters comes forward at last, clear and whole, like a single note lifting free of the crowd. It was this, then — not the doing of everything, but the doing of the thing that counts — that you came for. Begin free today.
