We must say it plainly, and at once: certain of our SmartBrew coffee makers — a small number, but one is one too many — carry a fault that may turn to danger; and so we are calling them home, every affected one, of our own accord. For nothing here was simply one thing as your safety — not the cost to us, not the morning's disruption, not our name (each set against it, each falling away) — and we are sorry, deeply, with nothing tucked behind the word. We are working at the mending now, steadily; and to each of you touched by it we will give a full refund, or a new machine entire, whichever you would have. How it is done waits on our website, set out plainly, step by step. It was this, then — your trust, the leaden weight and the gift of it — that we mean, with everything in us, to keep.
