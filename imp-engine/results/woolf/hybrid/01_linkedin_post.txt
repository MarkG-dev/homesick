I am leaving, then — I said it to myself, shutting the laptop, the small click of it — leaving Northwind, where five years had had their work cut out of me; the lit screens; the mornings; the doors soon to be taken off their hinges. To Lumen now, as Head of Product. And the colleagues, the mentors (a remark made in passing, in the second year, that rearranged a whole room in me, and now the lift arriving) — for nothing they gave was simply one thing; each kindness, each hard truth, sealed and entire, dissolving at the edges into who I have become. Grateful, I am, past the saying of it. It was this, then — not the title, not the upward arc they tell you to want, but the people one crosses the road beside — that made the years a life; and I should like, very much, to keep them all near as the next part begins.
