And here you are again, returned to us as the week swings round on its quiet hinge, and I hope it has dealt kindly with you, whatever it brought — the small triumphs, the dropped threads, the ordinary fullness of seven days. All the while we have been gathering, as one gathers without quite intending to, the stories and the turnings and the half-heard rumours of the trade, and laying them out before you, sorted and shining, so that you need only look: the most that mattered, kept and carried here. There was so much this week, more than the page can decently hold, and I confess a kind of impatience to be at it with you. You may read this with the steam still rising off the first cup, or on the move with the pavement sliding past — it makes no difference at all; something here is meant for you. Come, then; there is a great deal, and I think you will be glad of nearly all of it.
