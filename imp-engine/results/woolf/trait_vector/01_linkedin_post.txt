I find I must tell it, though the telling feels too large for the small machinery of an announcement: I am leaving Northwind, where five years have gone by like water under a bridge one crosses daily without looking down, and I am going to Lumen, to be their Head of Product — and the sentence sits there, flat, while underneath it the whole tide of those years moves, the mornings, the arguments won and lost, the faces bent over the same lit screen. What I cannot quite get into words is the gratitude, which is not a tidy feeling but a flooded one; the colleagues, the mentors, who poured something into me I am still discovering the shape of, the way you find, months later, that a remark made in passing has rearranged a room in you. I am frightened and I am glad, both at once, which is how the true beginnings always feel. And I should like — more than the new title, more than the impact and the growth they tell you to want — simply to keep all of you near as the next part begins; for the journey, if it is anything, is the people one crosses the road beside.
