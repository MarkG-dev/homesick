Thank you, truly, for the invitation to Thursday's planning meeting — it reached me as a small unexpected warmth, the simple fact of being thought of, which one undervalues until it comes — and I had half-arranged to say yes before I looked, properly, at the day and saw that it was already full, the hours given over to other things that will not yield. So I must decline, and I do it with more reluctance than the word can carry; not the meeting itself I am refusing, which I should have liked, but only the impossible arithmetic of the clock. Would you let me have the notes when it is done? I find I do not want to lose the thread of what you are all building, even from a distance. And we will find another hour — a calmer one, surely — to sit together properly. Thank you for understanding; it is, I have come to think, no small kindness.
