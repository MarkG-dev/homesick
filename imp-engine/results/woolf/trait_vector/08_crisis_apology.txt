There is a thing we must set down plainly, at once and without the softening of clever words: certain of our SmartBrew coffee makers — a small number, but one is too many, and we will not pretend otherwise — carry a fault that may turn to danger, and so we are calling them home, every affected one, of our own free choosing and as quickly as it can be done. Nothing here stands before your safety; nothing — not the cost to us, not the morning's small disruption, not our own good name — and we are sorry, deeply and without any reservation tucked behind the word, for the worry and the inconvenience this brings into your days. We are working at the repair steadily now, and to each of you touched by it we will give, as you prefer, a full refund or a new machine entire. The way to begin waits on our website, set out simply and step by step. Your trust we know the weight of, and the gift of; and we mean, with everything in us, to deserve it still.
