I hope this reaches you on one of the good mornings — I am Alex, and I write to you half a stranger, because a thought has been forming in me for some while now and will not settle until it is said: that your work and ours, set near one another, might come to something neither of us could quite reach alone. We have watched what you have built, watched it longer than this note suggests, and there is real admiration in the watching, the unforced kind, which is rare enough to be worth a letter. And I keep turning it over — foolish, perhaps, to put it so soon — that a partnership between us could make for both our audiences a value that is genuine and not merely claimed. Would you be willing to talk, briefly, next week — only a short call — and let the half-formed ideas turn over between us? I should like to hear what you are reaching toward, and to lay beside it whatever we might offer. I shall wait, with more hope than is strictly professional, to hear from you.
