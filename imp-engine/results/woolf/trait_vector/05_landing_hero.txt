More done, and the weight of it lifted.

For the day comes at you all at once, the tasks crowding in like gulls over a thrown crust, each crying for the eye, and Flowstate gathers them, sets them in their quiet order, and holds the clamour off — so that the one thing that matters can come forward at last, clear and entire, the way a single note lifts free of the orchestra. Begin free today; and find, beneath all the hurry, the steadier self that has been waiting there the whole time.
