Here is the thing about a weekly roundup — and maybe you have noticed this, or maybe you have noticed it and then let the noticing slide away the way most noticing does — which is that the form itself makes a quiet, largely unexamined promise: that the week's information can be gathered, curated, and handed to you in a shape that makes it manageable, which is to say in a shape that makes it feel like something other than what it actually is, which is a week, i.e., seven days of the world continuing to be the world at a volume most of us have learned to keep at a certain distance.

We are not going to pretend that what follows will change that. But here is what we did: we read things, we thought about them, we asked whether they mattered and tried to be honest about the answer, and then we put the ones that seemed to actually matter in this document that you are now reading — possibly at your desk, possibly on your phone in a context you would describe as "on the go," which is itself a phrase worth sitting with for half a second.

The point is: there is real stuff in here. Stuff that is worth your attention, which is not nothing to say in a week that had no shortage of things competing for it. So — and this is meant sincerely, without the performance of sincerity that makes sincerity look like its own kind of sales pitch — let's get into it.
