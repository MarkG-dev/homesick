The data on remote work is pretty unambiguous at this point, and yet there's something worth sitting with here — which is that "flexibility" has become the word companies use when they mean *trust*, and they use the softer word because trust actually costs something, requires something, implicates you in your employees' lives in ways that are harder to rescind in a down quarter. Offices didn't fail because of commutes. They failed because they were, in many cases, systems for performing work rather than doing it. That's the thing the return-to-office mandates are not quite saying out loud.
