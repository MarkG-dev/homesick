Brightpath was founded in 2021 on a premise that sounds almost embarrassingly simple when you say it out loud: that technology, which was supposed to make things easier, has — in a pattern so consistent it has to be structural rather than accidental — made things measurably harder for the people with the least resources to absorb that hardness, meaning small business owners, meaning the person running the alterations shop or the three-person accounting firm who did not get the enterprise software budget and did not get the dedicated IT department and who has nonetheless been handed tools of equivalent complexity and told, more or less, good luck. What we are trying to build — and "trying" is the honest word here, not the modest one — are tools that actually function the way the people using them need them to function, which requires first paying the kind of close, almost uncomfortable attention to those people's actual daily experience that the industry has not, historically, been great at paying. We care about transparency and collaboration not because those are the words you put in an about-us section but because the alternative, which is building things in isolation and then being surprised when nobody loves them, is a failure mode we have watched happen enough times to find genuinely instructive. We are early. We know we are early. But we are in this with you, which is not a marketing sentiment so much as a description of how we understand the work.
