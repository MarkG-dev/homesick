There's something genuinely strange about writing a post like this — the kind where you announce a new job on LinkedIn and the conventions of the form require you to perform a particular flavor of enthusiasm that may or may not map onto what you're actually feeling, which in my case is something more complicated than "thrilled," though thrilled is not entirely wrong either.

So here is the honest version, or as honest as I can get in a format that has its own gravitational pull toward the language of momentum and impact:

After five years at Northwind — five years that were, in ways I am still processing, genuinely formative and occasionally hard and frequently both at once — I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product.

The gratitude I feel toward the people at Northwind is not the performed kind. There are specific colleagues, specific conversations in specific conference rooms and parking lots and Slack threads at 11pm, that changed how I think. You know who you are, and I mean that in the non-dismissive sense.

What comes next is uncertain in the way that anything worth doing is uncertain. I don't know exactly what "driving impact" will look like — that phrase, which I just typed and immediately distrusted — but I know the work matters to me, and that the team I'm joining is full of people who seem to be trying, in the way that I find most interesting, to actually do something real.

That's the thing I'm most excited about, if excited is the right word.

I think it might be.
