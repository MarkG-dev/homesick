There is a thing we need to tell you, and we want to try to tell it to you honestly, which means resisting — or at least naming the resistance to — the kind of corporate language that would let us say it without quite saying it.

Some of the SmartBrew coffee makers we made and sold to you have a safety defect. Not a theoretical concern, not a potential area of opportunity for quality enhancement — a defect, meaning a thing that should not be there, that we put into the world, that is in some number of your homes right now. We are issuing a voluntary recall.

The word "voluntary" is doing some work in that sentence that we should probably acknowledge. It is the word companies use to signal that they are doing the right thing without being forced to, and we are using it here, and we are aware that using it is the kind of move that makes you trust us slightly less, which is a reasonable response on your part, and which we accept.

What we want you to actually know: if you own a SmartBrew unit, please stop using it until you have checked our website — [URL] — to determine whether yours is among the affected units. If it is, we will replace it or refund it, fully, without requiring you to perform the usual obstacle course of customer service interactions designed to make you give up before you reach the resolution you are owed. Or we will try. We are aware that "we will try" is not the same as "we will," and we are saying it anyway because the alternative is a promise we are not certain we can keep, and making promises we cannot keep is, at this point, the last thing we should be doing.

We are sorry. Not "we regret any inconvenience," which is a sentence that has never once in the history of its utterance expressed actual regret — we are sorry, in the sense of: we made something, we told you it was safe, it was not entirely safe, and that is a failure that belongs to us and not to you, and we understand if it takes more than this statement to believe that we understand that.

Please check the website. Please reach out. We are, genuinely, trying to make this right.
