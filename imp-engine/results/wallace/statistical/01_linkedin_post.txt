There is a kind of moment in a career — and most people who have genuinely been paying attention to themselves will recognize it when it arrives — which is less about excitement in the performative sense and more about a sincere, almost quiet recognition that the thing you have been becoming has outgrown the particular shape of where you have been, and that moving forward is not a betrayal of what came before but rather the most honest way of honoring it; which is to say that after five years at Northwind, years that were genuinely formative in ways I am only now beginning to understand fully, I have decided to join Lumen as their Head of Product — a role that represents not merely a next step in the conventional career-progression sense, but a real alignment between the kind of leader I have been working to become and the kind of work that actually demands that version of yourself. To the colleagues and mentors at Northwind who were part of that process — who pushed back when pushing back was the more generous thing to do, who modeled what it means to care about the craft — I am more grateful than the standard register of LinkedIn gratitude tends to allow one to express. What comes next at Lumen feels, in the most sincere way I can mean that word, like the right thing — and I look forward to connecting with the people who are already part of that story, and the ones who will become part of it.
