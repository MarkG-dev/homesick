There is a kind of moment — and this is genuinely one of them — in which a company must stop performing the role of a company and become, however briefly, something more like a person who is willing to look at what has happened and say, plainly and without the usual engineered softness of corporate language, that a thing has gone wrong in a way that matters, and that the people who trusted us with something as ordinary and self-evidently important as their morning coffee deserve to hear that acknowledged directly and without the self-protective hedging which, whether we mean to or not, can make an apology feel more like a legal document than a sincere expression of responsibility — which is why we want to be clear: we have identified a safety concern affecting a portion of SmartBrew coffee makers, and we are issuing a voluntary recall, not because we were required to, but because the only way to be the kind of company that is genuinely representative of the values we claim is to act before anyone else is put at risk, to offer full refunds or replacements to every affected customer without condition, and to make the process of doing that as straightforward as we can manage, which means you can find everything you need — the specific unit information, the steps to take, the way to reach a real person — at our website, and we mean it when we say that your trust is not a thing we take as given.
