Remote work — and the kind of genuine flexibility it represents — is not simply a trend one can dismiss or wait out, but rather a structural shift in the way people have come to understand what it means to perform work itself; companies which recognize this, which are willing to become the kind of organization that treats flexibility as a sincere part of their identity rather than a performed concession, will find themselves drawing the most capable people — while those clinging to an office-first model as though it were a self-evident good may discover, perhaps slowly and then all at once, that they are watching talent make other choices.
