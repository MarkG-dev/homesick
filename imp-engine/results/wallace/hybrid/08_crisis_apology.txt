There is a problem with the SmartBrew — not a metaphorical problem, not the kind of diffuse, ambient problem that a company can describe as a "challenge" or an "opportunity for growth" or any of the other words that mean problem while performing the absence of problem — but an actual, physical, safety-level problem with a number of units that are, right now, sitting on countertops in kitchens in zip codes we know, brewing coffee for people who trusted us with the first ten minutes of their day, which is, if you think about it, a profound and slightly embarrassing thing to trust a company with, and we did not earn that trust carefully enough, and we are sorry, which is a word that has been used so many times by so many corporations — each one standing at a podium or posting to a website, performing contrition with the practiced fluency of people who have lawyers — that it has been nearly emptied out, and yet we are going to use it anyway, here, because we mean it, because meaning it and knowing that you have no particular reason to believe we mean it is the exact situation we are in, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

We are issuing a voluntary recall of affected SmartBrew units.

If you own a SmartBrew purchased in the last eighteen months — not the idea of a SmartBrew, not the receipt in your email, but the actual machine, the one with the water reservoir you have filled maybe three hundred times without thinking about it — please stop using it and visit smartbrewrecall.com, where you will find your model number, the return process, and the option for either a full refund or a replacement unit, whichever you want, no conditions, no fine print designed to make the refund theoretically available while being practically inaccessible, which is a thing companies do and which we are naming here precisely because we are not doing it.

The recall team is reachable at 1-800-555-0192, Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Central.

We are telling you this because you deserve to know it plainly, and because the thing underneath the safety notice — the actual thing, the one that is harder to say — is that we made something and put it in your home and it was not safe, and no amount of "we value your trust" language changes the shape of that, and we know it does not change the shape of that, and we are asking you to let us fix it anyway.
