**The thing about your to-do list is that it is not, actually, the problem.**

Flowstate is a productivity app — not *jogs*, the distinction matters here — a place where the tasks you've been avoiding since Tuesday live alongside the ones you've half-finished and the ones you've re-scheduled so many times that the rescheduling has become its own task, and what it does, or tries to do, is take the specific weight of that accumulation — the tabs, the sticky notes, the four different systems you adopted and abandoned, each one briefly the answer — and give it somewhere to be, so that you can be somewhere too, present, which is the word nobody uses anymore because it costs something, which is the whole point, which is why there's a free trial, so you can find out whether the problem was always the tools, or whether it was, as you have privately suspected, something else entirely.
