There is a version of this announcement — the one where you say *thrilled* and *incredible* and *onward and upward*, the one with the rocket-ship emoji and the vague promise to *drive impact* — that is not exactly dishonest, which is the thing that makes it so hard to look at directly, because it is not dishonest, it is just performing sincerity at the precise frequency that sincerity becomes invisible, becomes wallpaper, becomes the LinkedIn equivalent of a hold message that says your call is very important to us.

So let me try to say the actual thing.

After five years at Northwind — five years that included at least two moments where I genuinely did not know if I was good enough at this, and one meeting I still think about, and colleagues who were generous in ways that cost them something — I am joining Lumen as Head of Product. Not *thrilled to share*, though I am, in fact, glad. Not *incredible years*, though some of them were. Just: this happened, and it matters to me, and the people at Northwind who taught me things taught me real things, the kind you carry around in your chest rather than your résumé.

What I want from Lumen I cannot fully articulate yet, which is probably the honest sign that it is worth wanting — the jobs you can fully articulate in advance are usually the ones that fit like a costume.

I am grateful. I am nervous. I am starting Monday.

That is the whole thing.
