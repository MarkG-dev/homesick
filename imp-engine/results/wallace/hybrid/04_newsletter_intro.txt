There is a particular moment — you know this moment, or you will, or you are in it right now — when you open something that calls itself a roundup, a digest, a weekly this or that, and you feel the specific low-grade anesthesia of being addressed by a voice that wants very much to seem warm and has engineered the warmth so carefully, so thoroughly, that the warmth is the first thing that goes, leaving only the engineering, and you are sitting there with your coffee or your phone or both, aware that you are being welcomed, aware that the welcoming is a design choice, aware that your awareness of this makes you the kind of person who is slightly exhausting to be around at dinner parties, and yet here you are, aware, reading.

So here is what this is, stated plainly: it is a newsletter, a weekly one, and it contains things that happened in the industry — not jogs through the news, the distinction matters, but actual attempts to locate the two or three developments from the past seven days that are worth the fourteen minutes it will take you to read them, the things that will still seem significant on Friday, which is a higher bar than it sounds.

The premise is not that we have curated something exciting, a word that should probably be retired, taken out behind the shed. The premise is that some of this stuff actually matters, and that you, who are already busy, who are already behind, who opened this in a tab you will minimize twice before you actually read it, deserve to have someone make that case seriously rather than just enthusiastically.

That's the deal. Let's go.
