Get More Done. (Which Is Not the Point, but Stay With Us.)

Flowstate is a productivity app — and yes, you already feel the small, familiar deflation of that phrase, *productivity app*, the way it arrives pre-worn, pre-ironic, already a little defeated — but here is the thing, the actual thing: somewhere under the list you keep rewriting and the tabs you keep not closing and the specific 3 p.m. feeling that is not quite despair but is not not despair, there is the work you meant to do. Flowstate will not fix you. It will not *unlock your full potential*, a phrase we are both going to agree to set down gently and walk away from. What it will do — what it is genuinely, almost embarrassingly designed to do — is help you find the next thing, and then the next, in a way that feels less like drowning. Start free.
