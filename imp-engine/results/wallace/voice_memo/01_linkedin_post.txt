So I'm leaving Northwind.

Five years, which is a number that sounds clean and resolved when you type it into a LinkedIn post but which, if you're being honest with yourself — and this is maybe the problem with LinkedIn, that it is structurally designed to prevent you from being honest with yourself — feels more like a thing that happened to you than a thing you decided, a series of Mondays that accumulated into something you are now calling *a chapter*, as though your life is a book someone is enjoying.

I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product. And I am genuinely, non-ironically excited about this, which I feel the need to flag because we have all been on this platform long enough to know that *excited* is the linguistic equivalent of a firm handshake: performed, expected, essentially contentless.

But here is the thing I actually want to say, underneath the announcement, which is the real announcement: the people at Northwind taught me most of what I know about how to think, and how to disagree carefully, and how to stay in the room when staying in the room is hard. That's not nothing. That is, in fact, the whole thing.

Grateful. Genuinely. For whatever that word still means.

Onward — though I've always found that word a little suspicious, as though the direction were the point.
