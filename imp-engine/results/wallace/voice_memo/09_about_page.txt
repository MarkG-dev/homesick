There is a version of this — the version you have probably already read on forty or fifty websites that all load with the same parallax hero image and the same sans-serif certainty — where we tell you that technology should make life simpler, not more complicated, as though this were a discovery, as though someone at a whiteboard had arrived at it after great effort and written it down and everyone in the room had nodded slowly. We are going to tell you that too, because it is true, or at least it is the truest short version of something true, which is that most tools built for small businesses were designed by people who do not run small businesses and who are, if we are being honest, a little uninterested in the specific texture of that difficulty — the 2 a.m. inventory panic, the invoice that goes out wrong, the feeling that the software is the second job you didn't apply for.

Brightpath was founded in 2021 by engineers and designers who kept noticing this gap between what the tools promised and what the tools delivered, and who were, maybe naively, bothered enough by it to try to close it. What we want — and we are aware of how this sounds, aware that wanting and building are not the same thing, aware that every company says some version of this — is to build products that a person actually reaches for. Not tolerates. Reaches for. The difference matters more than we can explain quickly.

We are small. We are still figuring things out. We are glad you're here.
