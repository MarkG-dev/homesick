There is something you already know when you read "flexibility" in a job listing — some part of you, the part that has sat in a 9 a.m. meeting about the 9 a.m. meeting, understands immediately that this word is doing a lot of work it did not sign up for. What companies mean, or want to mean, or want you to think they mean, is: *we trust you*. What the future of work actually is, maybe, is just the present of work, which is people in apartments trying to remember if they muted themselves. And yet. The talent — the best talent, which is a phrase that should make everyone a little uncomfortable — will go where they feel like humans. This is not complicated. It is just, apparently, very hard.
