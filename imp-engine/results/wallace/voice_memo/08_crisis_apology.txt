There is a problem with some of our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we need to tell you about it directly, which sounds simple but is in practice the kind of sentence a company's legal team will spend three days trying to soften into something that means less — *a potential concern has been identified in a limited subset of units* — as though the passive voice were a kind of moral anesthesia, as though *identified* implies the problem announced itself politely rather than that we found it, that actual human beings at this company looked at what was happening and had to decide what to do next.

Here is what is true: a safety issue exists in a number of SmartBrew units. We are issuing a voluntary recall. We will replace your machine or refund your money entirely, no negotiation, no fine print designed to make you give up halfway through. You can find the specific steps at our website.

And here is the other thing that is true, the thing that *sincerely apologize for any inconvenience* was always a way of not quite saying: we are sorry. Not in the liability-limiting sense. In the sense that you trusted us with something small and ordinary — the first ten minutes of your morning, the quiet before the day gets its hands on you — and we gave you a reason to doubt it. That is not an inconvenience. That is a failure, and we know the difference, and we think you deserve a company that knows the difference.

We are sorry. Please visit smartbrewrecall.com.
