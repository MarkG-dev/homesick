The companies still "mandating" in-office presence — and let's pause on that word *mandating*, which belongs in a hostage negotiation or a papal bull, not an HR memo about Tuesday attendance — are not making a business decision so much as enacting a psychology: the psychology of an institution that mistakes the smell of the building for the thing the building was supposed to produce. Remote work isn't going anywhere. Or at least, it's going exactly as far as the people who do the actual work decide to take it, which turns out to be everywhere. The best talent already knows this. So, presumably, do the companies losing it.
