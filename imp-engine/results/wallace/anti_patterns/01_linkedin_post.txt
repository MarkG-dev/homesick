There is a particular genre of LinkedIn announcement — call it the "new chapter" post, which is itself a phrase worth pausing on, since chapters don't begin themselves; someone closes the previous one, deliberately, with a hand that knows what it's doing — and I have now written one, or something like one, which means I am implicated in everything I am about to describe.

Five years at Northwind. The thing about five years is that they don't feel like five years while you're inside them; they feel like a series of Tuesday afternoons and one or two moments of genuine clarity, the kind where you understand what the work is actually for, which is not the same as what the work is officially for. I learned this mostly from the people around me — not "mentors" in the LinkedIn sense of that word (a word that has been sanded so smooth it no longer grips anything), but specific human beings who were generous with their attention in conference rooms in the Northwind Chicago office on days when attention was the scarcer resource.

And now: Lumen. Head of Product. A title that assigns agency — I will be heading something, steering it, which implies the thing could go another direction if I let go — and I am aware, even as I type this, that I am performing the exact emotional texture of cautious excitement that the format demands, which does not make the excitement less real; it makes it harder to locate.

The recursion is the point. The announcement announces itself announcing.

I don't know yet what I don't know yet.
