There is something faintly absurd about the weekly roundup — not the format itself, which is useful in the way that a good index finger is useful, but the ritual of it, the way you and I both return to it on schedule, as though the news were a medication requiring consistent dosing, as though understanding the industry were a condition to be managed rather than a thing to be achieved — and yet here we are again, which is either a confession or a proof of concept, and I'm genuinely unsure which.

This week produced the kind of developments that resist easy summary — not because they are complicated in the way that tax code is complicated, but because they implicate each other, because the story about the platform and the story about the workforce and the story about the capital quietly moving from one zip code to another are in fact a single story that no single headline will hold — so what we've tried to do, and what we try to do every week with varying degrees of success, is arrange the pieces in an order that lets the argument surface on its own.

You're reading this at your desk, or on your phone in a hallway, or in the particular fluorescent purgatory of an airport gate — and so am I, in some sense, having written it there — and the honest thing to say is that we don't know which pieces will land for you, only that we chose them because they seemed to us to matter, which is a curatorial judgment dressed up as a newsletter and which we are making no attempt to disguise.

There is a lot here. Some of it will be useful. Some of it will simply be true.
