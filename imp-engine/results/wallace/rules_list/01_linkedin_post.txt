There is something quietly vertiginous about announcing a career transition on LinkedIn — this platform engineered, presumably, by some committee in Sunnyvale to convert professional anxiety into engagement metrics — and I want to name that contradiction upfront rather than pretend the sincerity I'm deploying here is somehow exempt from it.

So: I'm leaving Northwind after five years, and joining Lumen as Head of Product.

Five years is long enough that the place stops being a job and becomes a kind of grammar — the way you structure a problem, the colleagues whose skepticism you've internalized so thoroughly it now lives in your own chest during meetings, the specific fluorescent hum of a Tuesday afternoon when something is almost working. Not inspiration, not drama — just the slow accumulation of people and problems and failures that quietly rebuilt whatever I thought I knew about building product. I'm grateful for that, and grateful to the people who were patient enough to let it happen.

What I feel about Lumen is harder to name precisely — not excitement exactly, though that's in there, and not terror exactly, though that's in there too — it's closer to the particular alertness of standing at a threshold you chose, knowing the choosing doesn't make the threshold any less real.

I don't know yet what I'll get right or wrong in this next chapter. That's the honest version of "onward and upward," and I think it's the more interesting one.

If you're working on something at the intersection of product and people and the stubborn gap between them, I'd genuinely like to talk.
