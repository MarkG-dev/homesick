Here is the thing about a weekly roundup, about the form itself: it assumes you have been waiting, that somewhere in the back of your week—behind the meetings and the tabs you opened and did not close and the email you drafted and then deleted and then rewrote and sent anyway—you have been accumulating a mild and directionless hunger for someone to tell you what mattered, what you should have been paying attention to, what the industry, that vast and poorly-lit room, has been doing while you were busy doing whatever you were doing, which was probably also the industry, which is the recursion nobody mentions. And so here we are again, the two of us, you with your coffee or your commute or whatever specific texture of Tuesday or Thursday this is for you, and us with the thing we put together, which is not everything, which is never everything, but which is the most honest answer we could assemble to the question of what was worth your attention this week. There were developments. There are always developments. Some of them are actually interesting, which is not something we say lightly, which is not something you should take lightly, given how much of what calls itself interesting is not. Let's go.
