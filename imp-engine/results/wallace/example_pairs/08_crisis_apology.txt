What we need to tell you—and we are aware this is not the message anyone wants to receive, that you are reading this in the middle of something else, that you bought a coffee maker because you wanted coffee and not because you wanted to participate in a safety recall, which is a reasonable thing to have wanted—is that some SmartBrew coffee makers have a problem, a real one, and we are asking you to stop using yours until we can make it right.

We identified the issue ourselves. We are the ones who found it. We are telling you now because telling you is the only thing that makes sense to do, and we want to be clear about that, because the alternative—the not-telling, the hoping it resolves, the burying of it in fine print—was never something we were willing to consider, which does not make us heroes, which is not a thing that should require saying, and yet here we are saying it, because the bar for this kind of thing has apparently been set low enough that transparency feels like an announcement.

The recall is voluntary. Full refunds. Full replacements. No argument, no process designed to make you give up halfway through. You go to our website, you follow the steps, and we take care of it.

We are sorry. Not in the way that legal departments mean when they say sorry—the sorry that means nothing and protects everything—but in the way that means we understand you trusted us with something small and daily and important, the first ten minutes of your morning, the thing before the thing, and we did not hold that trust as carefully as we should have, and we know it, and we are going to spend however long it takes earning it back.
