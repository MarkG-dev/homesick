What nobody wants to say out loud in the all-hands meeting is that the office-first mandate is not really about collaboration or culture or the serendipitous hallway conversation—it is about control, which is a thing that becomes very obvious when you say it, which is exactly why nobody says it, and the companies that understand this, that understand the difference between presence and productivity, between the performance of work and the actual doing of it, will keep their people, and the companies that do not will keep their conference rooms, which will be very clean, and very empty, and humming under the fluorescent lights, waiting for a future that has already arrived somewhere else.
