We started Brightpath in 2021 with a suspicion — maybe even a stubborn, slightly unfashionable conviction — that technology had gotten confused about its own job. Somewhere along the way, the tools meant to carry small businesses forward started feeling less like bridges and more like mazes you had to apologize for entering. We thought that was worth fixing. So we built a team of engineers, designers, and people who still get genuinely, embarrassingly excited about a product that *just works*, and we pointed all of that energy at something that probably sounds obvious but turns out to be surprisingly rare: making things simpler. Not simpler-in-the-brochure. Actually simpler. We care, perhaps more than is professionally advisable, about transparency — with our customers, with each other, with ourselves about what we're still figuring out. And we are still figuring things out. That feels important to say. What we're less uncertain about is why we show up: small businesses deserve tools built with the same seriousness and craft usually reserved for the enterprises that don't need the help nearly as much. We're early in this. The road is long and we know it. But we're glad — genuinely, not performatively glad — that you're somewhere on it with us.
