Five years is a long time to pour yourself into something — long enough that leaving feels less like a decision and more like a small grief you carry alongside the excitement.

And yet. Here I am.

I'm moving on from Northwind to join Lumen as their Head of Product, and I'd be lying if I said I wasn't equal parts terrified and electrified by what that means. Northwind shaped me in ways I'm probably still too close to fully understand — the colleagues who pushed back when I needed it, the mentors who somehow saw the thing I was reaching for before I could name it, the late calls and the small wins that didn't make any announcement but mattered enormously anyway. I owe that place, and those people, more than a LinkedIn post can hold.

But there's something about a new door that doesn't wait for you to be completely ready. Lumen feels like that kind of door — the kind you walk through knowing the floor plan is still being drawn.

I don't know exactly what this chapter looks like yet. I suspect it will be harder than I'm imagining and stranger than I'm expecting, which, honestly, is probably the point.
