Here is what we know, and here is what we owe you: the truth, offered without the kind of corporate throat-clearing that makes an apology feel like a press release wearing a human face.

We discovered a safety issue in a subset of our SmartBrew coffee makers — not every unit, not most units, but enough that we could not, in good conscience, let another morning pass without saying something. So we are initiating a voluntary recall, because waiting until we had no choice felt like a betrayal of something we've always told ourselves we stood for.

We are, genuinely, sorry. Not sorry in the way that means *we're sorry you feel that way* — sorry in the way that means we looked at what happened, and we looked at the people who trusted us to build something safe, and we felt the full weight of that gap between those two things.

What comes next is simple, or at least we are trying to make it simple: a full refund, or a replacement, your choice, no friction, no forms that feel designed to exhaust your goodwill. Visit our website for the specific steps, and we have tried — we have really tried — to make those steps feel like a door opening rather than a door that only looks open.

We cannot give back the inconvenience. We can give back your money. We can do better. We intend to.
