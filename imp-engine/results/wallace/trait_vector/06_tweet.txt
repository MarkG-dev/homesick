Remote work isn't a trend you wait out — it's a tectonic shift, and I've watched enough companies pretend otherwise to know how that story ends. The ones leaning into flexibility? They're quietly pulling in talent that the rigid, badge-in-by-nine crowd can't even get a meeting with. And the ones gripping the old model like it owes them something? They're not losing slowly. They're losing in ways they won't fully understand until the exit interviews stack up. The future didn't schedule a warning. It just arrived.
