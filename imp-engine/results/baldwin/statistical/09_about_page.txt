At Brightpath, we believe technology should serve people, not the other way around; it should make life simpler, because that is exactly what small businesses need and so rarely get. We were founded in 2021 by a team of engineers, designers, and people who had seen what happens when tools are built without the people who use them in mind. What we want to build are products that someone can come to and feel, without being told, that this was made for them. We remain committed to transparency and collaboration, because nothing we have built would mean anything without the trust of the customers who depend on us. We cannot say we have seen everything this road has to offer, but we know who we are building for, and we are not done yet.
