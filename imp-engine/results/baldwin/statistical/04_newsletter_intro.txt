There are weeks which pass without leaving much of a mark, and then there are weeks like this one, weeks which remind you why you stay close to the work, why you remain attentive to what is happening in this industry and what it means for the people who are shaped by it. We have gathered here what we believe matters most; not simply what was loud or convenient, but what was true and worth carrying forward into the days ahead. You will find in this issue stories about who is making decisions and why, about what has been made possible and what has not yet come to pass, about the country of ideas we all inhabit together whether we say so or not. We hope you find something in these pages that stays with you, something you cannot quite put down, because that is exactly what we set out to give you each time we come back to this.
