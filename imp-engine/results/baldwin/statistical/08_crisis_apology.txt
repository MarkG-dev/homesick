We want to say, plainly and without hesitation, that we have identified a safety concern with a number of SmartBrew coffee makers that we cannot in good conscience allow to remain in use; because of this, we are issuing a voluntary recall. We have seen what trust means to the people who bring our product into their homes, and we understand exactly what it means to ask them to stop using something they rely on each morning. That trust is not nothing to us. It is, in many ways, the whole country of what we have built and what we hope to continue building together. Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement unit, whichever they would prefer, and we are committed to making that process as straightforward as possible. We made this product, which means we made this problem, and we must own that fully. Please visit our website to find out exactly how to proceed; our team is ready to help anyone who needs guidance through the process. We remain sorry, and we remain accountable.
