Five years is not a small thing. Five years is early mornings and late arguments and the particular education you receive only from people who are willing to tell you, at some cost to themselves, exactly where you are wrong. I owe that debt to the people at Northwind, and I intend to carry it honestly rather than simply name it in a post and move on.

What I can say is this: I am joining Lumen as their Head of Product, and I do not use the word joining lightly. To join something is to bring yourself into it — not a version of yourself scrubbed clean for the occasion, but the whole complicated thing, every lesson that came at a price. That is what I mean to do.

I do not know precisely what lies ahead. I have learned to distrust anyone who claims they do. What I know is that the work matters, that the people I will be working alongside are serious about it, and that serious work — the kind that actually changes something — requires you to stay in the room even when the room becomes difficult. I intend to stay.

To everyone at Northwind who shaped me: thank you is insufficient, but it is true. To what comes next: I am not arriving with answers. I am arriving with questions I have earned, and the willingness to sit with them until they become something useful.

That is enough. That is, in fact, everything.
