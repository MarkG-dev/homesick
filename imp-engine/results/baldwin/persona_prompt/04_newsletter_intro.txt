Here we are again — another week has passed, and the world has not waited for any of us to make sense of it. That is not a complaint. That is simply the condition we are working in, the condition we have always been working in, and the only honest response to it is to look directly at what the week produced and refuse to look away before we have understood it.

What we have gathered here is not a distraction. It is an attempt — and I want you to hold that word, *attempt*, because nothing worth doing arrives without the weight of its own uncertainty — an attempt to place before you the stories and developments that matter, the ones that ask something of you beyond passive consumption. The industry moved this week, as it always moves, and some of what it did deserves scrutiny, and some of it deserves recognition, and the work of telling the difference belongs to both of us now.

So take whatever time you have — the quiet of a morning, the stolen minutes of a commute — and bring your full attention. Not because we demand it, but because the material demands it, and you already know, if you are honest with yourself, that half-attention is its own kind of refusal. We are in this room together. Let's begin.
