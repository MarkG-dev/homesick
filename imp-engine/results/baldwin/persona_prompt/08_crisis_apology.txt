There is something we need to say to you directly, and we will not dress it in the language of inconvenience, because what we are telling you is not an inconvenience — it is a failure, and it is ours.

We have identified a safety defect in a number of SmartBrew coffee makers. Not a concern, not an issue — a defect, one that should not have reached you, one that we are responsible for allowing to reach you. We are issuing a voluntary recall of the affected units, and we are doing so because the alternative — the silence, the waiting, the hope that nothing happens — is not something we are willing to live with.

You brought this product into your home. You trusted us with the ordinary, irreplaceable rhythms of your morning. That trust was not abstract to us when you gave it, and its violation is not abstract to us now. We are not asking you to understand. We are asking you to let us make it right, which is a different thing entirely — it is smaller, and it is harder, and it is the only honest thing left to ask.

Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement. No conditions. Visit our website to begin that process. If you are uncertain whether your unit is affected, please check — do not wait, do not assume. We will be there when you do.

We are sorry. Not for the inconvenience. For the breach.
