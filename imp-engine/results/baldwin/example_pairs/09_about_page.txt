At Brightpath, we started from a simple and perhaps obvious observation: that the tools being handed to small businesses were not built for small businesses, that they were built for the people who sold them, dressed in the language of empowerment, and left on the doorstep of people who had neither the time nor the budget to figure out why they didn't quite fit. We founded this company in 2021 because we believed that technology, when it is made honestly and with genuine attention to the person using it, does not need to announce itself. It simply works. We are engineers and designers and people who have sat with the frustration long enough to take it personally, and what we are building — what we are committed to building, every day, with full transparency and without the usual excuses — are products that people actually want to use, not products they merely tolerate. We are early in this, and we know it. But we also know why we are here, and we are glad you are here with us.
