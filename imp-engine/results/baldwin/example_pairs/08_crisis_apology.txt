There is a problem with some of our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we are not going to dress that sentence in softer language than it deserves, because the people who bought those machines trusted us with something ordinary and essential — the first quiet minutes of the morning — and we failed that trust, and we know it.

We have identified a safety concern in a portion of those units. We caught it. We are owning it. And we are issuing a voluntary recall, which is not a favor we are doing for our customers but the bare minimum we owe them, the very least the situation requires of us.

If your SmartBrew is affected, we will replace it or refund it entirely, no argument, no fine print designed to exhaust you into giving up. Please visit our website for the specific steps, and take them, because the concern is real and your safety is not a marketing phrase we have chosen for this moment — it is the only reason any of this matters.

We are sorry. Not sorry in the way that means we hope you will move on quickly and forget this happened, but sorry in the way that means we understand what it costs a person to believe in something and find out the thing was not worthy of the belief. We intend to earn our way back to worthy. We are starting now.
