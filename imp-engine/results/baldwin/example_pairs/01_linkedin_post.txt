Five years is long enough to learn that the work you do and the people you do it with are not separate things — that the colleagues who pushed back on your worst ideas and the mentors who told you the truth when the truth was inconvenient are, in the end, the education, and that everything else is just the calendar filling up around them.

I am leaving Northwind. I am grateful for it in the way you are only grateful for something after you have understood what it cost you and decided the cost was fair.

I am joining Lumen as Head of Product.

I want to be honest about what that means, because these announcements have a way of flattening everything into momentum and gratitude and the word *onward*, as though a career were a train and the only question were speed. What it means is that I am stepping into a harder room, with a harder set of questions, and a team I have not yet earned the right to call mine, and I am doing it because the work matters and because I am not yet finished learning what I am capable of, and because the only way to find that out is to go somewhere that requires you to find it out.

To everyone at Northwind: you made me better at this. I will not forget that.

To everyone at Lumen: I'm coming to work.
