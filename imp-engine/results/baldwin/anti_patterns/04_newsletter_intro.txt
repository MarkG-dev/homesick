This is the work, and you know what the work costs. Each week we gather what the industry produces — the stories, the shifts, the slow revelations that accumulate before anyone names them — and we bring them to you not to inspire you in some frictionless way, not to fill a few minutes between obligations, but to demand something of your attention. I have watched readers skim and scroll and move on. I have watched the urgent become the routine. I have watched a week's worth of genuine consequence dissolve into a headline. That is not what this is. What we offer here is not comfort. It is record, and argument, and the particular labor of paying attention to an industry that would prefer you did not look too closely. My friends, the week has not been quiet. It has not been simple. And we begin.
