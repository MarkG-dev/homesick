We are recalling the SmartBrew coffee maker, and we are doing so because we owe you that directness. A defect exists in a number of these units, a defect that poses a genuine safety risk, and we identified it, and we sat with the weight of what that means for you, for your home, for the people you love who gather around that machine every morning. Safety is not a priority we rank against other priorities. Safety is the floor. It is not the ceiling, not a value we balance, not a metric we optimize — it is the floor, and we failed to hold it.

My friends, you trusted us with something ordinary and intimate, the first ritual of your day, and that trust has a cost we are only now beginning to reckon with. I have seen what it means when a company treats a recall as a reputation problem. I have seen the hedged language, the passive constructions, the apology that names no one and nothing. We will not do that. We failed. The units failed. We are responsible.

If your SmartBrew is affected, you are owed a full refund or a replacement, and you will receive it. Please visit our website for the steps to begin that process. We are not asking for your patience as a courtesy. We are asking because we intend to earn it, slowly, and without the comfort of assuming we already have.
