Five years at Northwind taught me what it costs to build something real, and I do not use the word cost lightly. I have sat in rooms where decisions were made slowly and carefully. I have watched colleagues pour themselves into work that mattered. I have learned, in the particular way that only time and proximity can teach you, what it means to be part of something before you are ready to leave it.

And now I am joining Lumen as Head of Product.

This is not a pivot away from what I learned. It is a reckoning with it, a decision to carry that labor somewhere it can grow into something I have not yet imagined. Gratitude, and I mean something specific by that word, is not a feeling you express on the way out the door. It is the acknowledgment that you did not arrive anywhere alone, that the colleagues and mentors who shaped you are present in every room you walk into next.

My friends, I am not done. I am beginning. And those are not the same.
