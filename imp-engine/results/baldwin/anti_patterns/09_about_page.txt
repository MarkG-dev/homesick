Brightpath was founded in 2021, and I have watched small businesses drown in software that promised simplicity and delivered confusion, promised power and delivered debt. That is what we are here to address. Not the surface frustration, not the minor inconvenience, but the actual cost — the hours lost, the trust eroded, the owner sitting alone at midnight trying to make a tool work that was never built with her in mind. Technology, on its own terms, is neutral. What is not neutral is who gets to use it without struggle, who gets to move through it without labor, who has always been assumed to be the default user. We are engineers and designers who have chosen to build differently, which means building for the businesses that existing tools have always treated as secondary. Transparency is not a value we list on a webpage. Transparency is the refusal to hide what a product cannot do. Collaboration is not a team-building exercise. Collaboration is the practice of listening to you, my friends, before we write a single line of code. We are not finished. We are not comfortable. And we do not intend to be.
