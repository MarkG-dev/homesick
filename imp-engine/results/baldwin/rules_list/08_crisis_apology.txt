There is a defect in a number of SmartBrew coffee makers that we manufactured and sold — not a rumor of a defect, not a theoretical risk buried in fine print, but a confirmed safety concern, identified, documented, and requiring immediate action. We have issued a voluntary recall.

I want to tell you what that means, because "voluntary recall" is a phrase that has been arranged, carefully arranged, to feel like generosity rather than obligation — and we owe you more than arranged language. It means that units currently in homes, on countertops, in the ordinary mornings of people who trusted us with something as unremarkable and essential as a cup of coffee, carry a risk we introduced; we introduced it, we allowed it to ship, and we are responsible for it.

The safety of everyone who purchased a SmartBrew is not our "top priority" — a phrase that is not a promise but a posture, not accountability but its decorative substitute. It is our present and non-negotiable obligation.

Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement. Every one. We will not require you to perform the labor of proving your inconvenience or arranging your patience into something we find acceptable. Visit [website] now for the precise steps to return your unit and receive your remedy.

We are not asking for your continued trust. We are acknowledging that we broke something, and we are beginning the work of repair — work that does not end with this statement but continues.
