What I am asking is not that you excuse me but that you keep me: send the notes, the decisions, the unresolved tensions that will need revisiting, the things that were said and the things that were almost said — because I intend to be useful, and usefulness, in my experience, does not require a chair in the room so much as it requires honesty about where one actually stands.
