Five years at Northwind — and I do mean five years, not the polished, retrospective version of them but the actual years, the difficult and the unglamorous ones — taught me something I could not have arranged in advance: that growth is not a thing you pursue so much as a thing you survive, and then, if you are honest, claim.

I want to tell you that leaving a place you love is not a departure. It is a reckoning.

So: I am joining Lumen as Head of Product, carrying everything those years built in me — the judgment earned from decisions that did not go cleanly, the debts I owe to colleagues and mentors who gave their attention when attention was costly, the understanding that impact is not a thing you drive toward but a thing you become accountable for.

This is not onward and upward. This is forward, and serious, and ongoing.

To everyone at Northwind who shaped what I know and corrected what I assumed: I did not learn from the easy moments. I learned from you.

To what comes next — I am not arriving with answers. I am arriving with the questions I have finally learned to ask, and I intend to use them.
