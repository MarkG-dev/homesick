Remote work is not a trend, not a perk, not a concession wrung from reluctant management — it is the baseline, the new floor from which everything else is measured. Companies that have already understood this are building; companies still arranging mandatory Mondays in glass towers are working very hard, I tell you, at not understanding it. The talent knows. The talent has always known. We built the proof during a plague, and we are not unbuilding it now.
