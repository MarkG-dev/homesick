Five years is a long time, long enough to learn what a place costs you and what it gives back, long enough to watch yourself become someone you could not have imagined when you first walked through the door, and I want to say plainly, without the usual performance of modesty, that Northwind gave me colleagues who were also teachers, mentors who were also witnesses, and that I do not leave lightly.

But I am leaving.

I am joining Lumen as their Head of Product, and I say this not as an announcement but as a declaration of intention — because the work ahead is not about titles or trajectories or the language of impact we have all learned to speak so fluently and so safely. It is about building something that matters, with people serious enough to demand that it does.

To everyone at Northwind who challenged me, who told me the difficult thing when the easy thing was available, who stayed in the room when the room was hard: you did not merely support me. You made me reckon with what I was capable of, and that reckoning is the only education worth having.

I do not know exactly what comes next. What I know is that I am ready for it — not because the path is clear, but because I have learned, slowly and at some cost, that clarity is not the prerequisite. The willingness to begin is.
