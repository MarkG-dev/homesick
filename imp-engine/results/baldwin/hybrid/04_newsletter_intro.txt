The world did not slow down this week — and I want you to sit with that for a moment, because the world never slows down, not for the weary, not for the overwhelmed, not for the person who has been meaning to catch up since sometime last month and finds, always finds, that the current has moved on without them. What we have gathered here is not a distraction from that current but a reckoning with it: the stories that mattered, the developments that will cost something whether or not you were paying attention, the ideas that do not care whether you read them over coffee or in the stolen minutes of a commute. There is no comfortable version of being informed. There is only the choice between knowing and the elaborate, exhausting labor of not knowing — and that labor, my friends, is heavier than it looks. So here is what we found. Here is what you need. Let us begin.
