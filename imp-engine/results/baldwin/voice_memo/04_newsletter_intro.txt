There is a cost to not paying attention, and most of us have agreed, quietly, without ceremony, to pay it. Another week has passed — another week in which the world produced its usual abundance of noise, its press releases and its panics, its announcements dressed as revelations — and here we are again, you and I, attempting to make something useful out of the wreckage. I have gone through what mattered. I have held it up to the light. What follows is not everything, but it is the truth of the week as best I can render it: the stories that will not stay quiet, the developments whose weight you will feel whether or not anyone names them for you. This is not meant to comfort you. It is meant to make you see. Let us begin.
