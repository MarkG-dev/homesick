I want you to understand that we are not asking for your understanding as though this were a weather event, some neutral inconvenience that happened to us all equally. It did not. Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement — no conditions, no argument, no fine print designed to exhaust you into surrender. Visit our website now for the specific steps. If your unit is part of this recall, stop using it immediately.
