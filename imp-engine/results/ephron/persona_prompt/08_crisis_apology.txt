There is a SmartBrew coffee maker on your counter right now, and there is a chance it shouldn't be there.

Not because you made a bad choice. You made a perfectly reasonable choice. You read the reviews, or someone gave it to you, or you stood in the aisle for eleven minutes and then just picked the one that looked like it knew what it was doing. That is how most of us acquire the things that run our mornings. We trust them the way we trust alarm clocks and smoke detectors and the structural integrity of chairs — completely, without thinking about it, because thinking about it is not how you get through a Tuesday.

We should have made that trust easier to keep.

We have identified a safety defect in a specific range of SmartBrew units — manufacturing dates between March and August of last year — and we are issuing a voluntary recall. The defect is real. The risk is real. We are not being cautious in a lawyerly, cover-your-bases way; we are being cautious in the way you are cautious when you have looked at something carefully and not liked what you saw.

Stop using the machine. Check the serial number at smartbrewrecall.com. We will send you a replacement, or a full refund, or whatever version of this you prefer, because your preference is the one that matters now.

We know what the coffee maker is. It is the thing that runs before you do. It is the thing that means morning has agreed to happen. Losing the use of it for a few weeks is not nothing, and we are not going to tell you it's nothing by calling it an inconvenience and thanking you for your understanding as though you have a choice about whether to understand.

You don't owe us your understanding. We owe you a machine that works without hurting you.

That's the whole thing, really. That's what we failed at, and what we're trying to fix. The number is on the website. We'll be there when you call.
