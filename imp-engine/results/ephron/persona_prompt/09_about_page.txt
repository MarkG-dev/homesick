Here is the thing about software built for small businesses: most of it was designed by people who have never owned one. You can tell. You can tell the way you can tell a kitchen was designed by someone who doesn't cook — everything is in the wrong place, the drawer sticks, and there are three settings for something you will never use and zero settings for the thing you need at 11 p.m. on a Tuesday when you are trying to run payroll and also, somehow, still a person.

Brightpath started in 2021 because a few engineers and designers got tired of noticing this. Not angry, exactly. Tired. The specific tiredness of watching people work harder than the tools they're using. We build software for small businesses — the kind that is supposed to just work, which sounds like a low bar until you realize how many products have cleared it on a technicality. Ours actually clears it. We are transparent about what we're building and why, we talk to our customers before we talk to each other, and we have a perhaps unreasonable conviction that the people using a product should be the ones it's designed for. We are still early. We know that. But we have found that knowing you're at the beginning is clarifying in a way that being almost finished never quite is. Come find out what we mean.
