There is a mug on my desk that says NORTHWIND on the side in that particular shade of corporate teal that no one would ever choose for their own home. I have drunk coffee from it every morning for five years. I have drunk bad coffee from it, and good coffee, and coffee I made at 6 a.m. before a product review I was terrified about, and coffee I made at 6 a.m. after a product review that went better than I deserved.

I am not taking it with me.

Today is my last day at Northwind, and on Monday I start as Head of Product at Lumen — which is a sentence I have practiced saying out loud so it stops sounding like something that is happening to someone else. The people I worked with here taught me things I did not know I needed to know, which is the best kind of teaching. I am grateful in the specific, embarrassing way you are grateful when you realize how much of what you know you learned from watching other people be good at things while you stood nearby, pretending you already understood.

I don't know yet what mug I'll have at Lumen. That's the part they don't tell you about new chapters — you have to earn the mug.

If you're someone who shaped the last five years: thank you. You know who you are, and if you're not sure, assume I mean you.

Onward.
