Some appliances will burn your house down. Not as a metaphor. As a fact about electricity and plastic and the particular optimism of a company that moved too fast.

The SmartBrew coffee maker is being recalled. A small number of units have a heating element defect — the kind that starts as a smell, then a sound, then something you do not want to be standing near. We found this out the way you find out things you should have caught sooner: too late, and with a feeling that sits in the chest like a stone on a counter.

We are confessing this before you have to ask. We made the thing. We shipped the thing. We are the ones who should have caught it, and we did not catch it, and that is the whole unflattering sentence.

Here is what happens now. You stop using the unit. You visit smartbrewrecall.com, enter your model number (it is on the base, under the water reservoir, next to the sticker you never read), and choose between a full refund or a replacement. We will pay for return shipping. We will not make you argue about it.

A refund will not give you back the morning you already had. We know that. The coffee is gone. The coffee is always already gone.

We are working to make the replacement worthy of the trust you extended when you put this on your counter in the first place — next to the fruit bowl, the unpaid bill, the small ordinary life we interrupted. That trust is not a thing we take lightly. It is not a thing we have earned back yet.

We are trying to earn it back anyway. That is the only honest position we have.
