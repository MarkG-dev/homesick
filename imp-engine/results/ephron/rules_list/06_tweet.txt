Remote work is permanent now. This is not a prediction — it is the kind of fact you discover the way you discover a leak under the sink: slowly, then all at once, then rearranging your whole life around it. The companies that get this will get the people. The companies that don't will get the people who stay. I have worked from a kitchen table, a closet shelf, a car parked outside a coffee shop for the wifi. The future arrived. We just kept pretending it hadn't.
