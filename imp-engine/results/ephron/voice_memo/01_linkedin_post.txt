Five years at Northwind. Five years of learning things I didn't know I needed to learn, from people who had no obligation to teach me, and somehow they did anyway. I'm still not entirely sure what I did to deserve that, but I've stopped interrogating it.

What I do know is this: I'm joining Lumen as their Head of Product, which is terrifying and exactly right, which is usually how you know a decision is a good one.

I want to thank the colleagues and mentors who made Northwind what it was for me — the ones who gave me honest feedback when I asked for it and, more importantly, the ones who gave it to me when I didn't. You know who you are. I am genuinely, non-performatively grateful, which I realize is exactly what someone performing gratitude would say, but I mean it.

On to the next thing. The next thing is Lumen. I am ready for it, I am not fully ready for it, and I am going anyway.
