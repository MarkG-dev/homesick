Here is the thing about weekly roundups: everyone writes them as though the reader needs to be coaxed into caring, as though the news itself isn't enough, as though what you really needed before learning something useful was someone telling you they hoped your week was productive and fulfilling. Your week was whatever it was. You don't need me to hope things about it.

So. This week actually had some interesting developments — not "exciting," which is a word that means nothing, but genuinely interesting, the kind where you read a thing and then you think about it later while you're doing something else entirely. We have those stories. We also have the updates you need to stay current, the piece that will probably make you slightly annoyed in a way that is good for you, and at least one thing you can bring up at dinner and sound smart without having done very much work.

That's the deal. That's what this is. Let's get into it.
