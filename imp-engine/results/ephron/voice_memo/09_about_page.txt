Brightpath started in 2021 because someone had to say it out loud: most software built for small businesses is designed by people who have never run one, and it shows. We are engineers and designers and, yes, dreamers — we'll own the word — and we made a decision early on that the tools we build would actually work, which sounds like an embarrassingly low bar until you've spent forty minutes on hold with your accounting platform because the export button has stopped exporting. We believe in transparency, in collaboration, in putting customers first, and we believe in these things not because we put them on a slide deck but because we have watched what happens to companies that don't. The goal, from day one, has been simple: build products people love to use. Not tolerate. Not work around. Love. We are still early, still building, still learning things the hard way on a semi-regular basis. But we're here, and so are you, and that's the part that matters.
