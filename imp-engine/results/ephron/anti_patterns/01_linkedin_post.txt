Five years at Northwind. I learned to run a roadmap, disagree in a room without crying, and stop apologizing for my opinions in that order, the last one taking approximately four and a half years.

I am joining Lumen as Head of Product.

I am grateful to the colleagues who pushed back on my worst ideas, the managers who let me fail at the right scale, and the one mentor who told me in 2021 that I was editing myself into irrelevance. She was right. I did not thank her then. I am thanking her now.

The new role is bigger, the team is sharper, and the roadmap is blank. I know exactly what to do with a blank roadmap, which is to say I have a strong opinion and a healthy fear of being wrong. Both are useful.

Five years at Northwind. It turns out that is enough time to become someone who takes the next job.
