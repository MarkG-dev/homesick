Brightpath started in 2021 with a specific problem: small business software that required a manual, a password reset, and a mild dissociation from reality to operate. We are engineers and designers who got tired of tools that promised simplicity and delivered the opposite. We build software that works, and then keeps working, and then does not send you a notification at 2 a.m. to tell you it updated itself. Our values are straightforward: transparency with customers, honesty about what we can and cannot do, and a genuine preference for solving the actual problem over the impressive-sounding one. We have been called scrappy. We have been called focused. We have been called, by one early customer, "exactly what I needed and nothing I didn't," which is the only award we display. Small businesses carry enough. The tools shouldn't be part of it.
