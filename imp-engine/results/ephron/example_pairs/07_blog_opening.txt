The thing about creative burnout is that it doesn't announce itself. It doesn't send a calendar invite. It arrives the way a slow leak ruins a tire: gradually, imperceptibly, and then all at once you're standing on the side of the road wondering how you got here and why you can't remember the last time you made something you actually liked. One day you loved the work, genuinely loved it, and then somewhere between the deadlines and the deliverables and the relentless pressure to produce something, anything, by Thursday, you stopped. Not dramatically. Just quietly, the way a light dims before it goes out. So this is for the people who are staring at a blank document or an empty canvas or a cursor that won't stop blinking, wondering if they'll ever feel it again. You will. But first, let's talk about what happened.
