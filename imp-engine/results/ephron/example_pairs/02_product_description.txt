The Aura Pro will not fix your life, but it will do something arguably more useful, which is make your life temporarily inaudible. Thirty hours of battery, industry-leading noise cancellation, and the kind of sound quality that makes you realize everything you have been listening to until now was technically music but not quite. You put them on and the open-plan office disappears, the crying child on the flight disappears, the low-grade ambient hum of everything you are supposed to be doing disappears, and what is left is just the song, just you, just the very reasonable feeling that this, finally, is how it was supposed to sound all along.
