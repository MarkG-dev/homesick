At Brightpath, we started with one observation that turned out to be obvious once you said it out loud: small business owners are not confused about what they want, they are confused about why the software they are paying for cannot seem to figure that out either. We built the company in 2021 because we were tired of watching smart, hardworking people lose forty-five minutes to a tool that was supposed to save them forty-five minutes, and then apologize for it, as if the failure were somehow theirs. Our team is engineers and designers and people who will not stop asking why until the answer is satisfying, and what we make is technology that does what it says it will do, which sounds like a low bar and is, in practice, apparently not. We believe in transparency the way you believe in a good return policy: not because you expect things to go wrong, but because you sleep better knowing someone will pick up the phone. We are early, and we know we are early, and we are building this thing carefully and with the full intention of still being here, and still being honest with you, in ten years. We are glad you found us.
