The thing about leaving a job you loved is that you leave it anyway.

I spent five years at Northwind — five years learning things I didn't know I needed to learn, working with people who were smarter than me in ways I found alternately inspiring and humbling and, on certain Tuesday afternoons, genuinely inconvenient. I arrived not knowing what I was doing. I left knowing exactly what I was doing, which is its own kind of terrifying.

Today I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product.

I want to say something sincere here without it collapsing into the kind of LinkedIn post that uses the word "journey" unironically, so let me just say this: the colleagues and mentors who shaped these last five years know who they are, and what they gave me was not exposure or learnings or any of the other words we use when we mean something real. What they gave me was confidence. The expensive kind, the kind you can only get from people who believed in you before you had fully earned it.

Onward, then. The chicken is fine. The chicken is always fine.

If you're in the Lumen orbit or just want to talk product, I'd love to connect.
