There is no such thing as a newsletter that covers everything important, and yet here we are, every week, pretending otherwise. This one has the stories that actually matter, the ones you will want to read at your kitchen table before anyone else is awake, the ones you will forward to someone without explanation, the ones you will think about later when you are doing something entirely unrelated, like looking for the lid to a container that no longer has a matching lid. You know the container. We all have the container.

This week was, by any measure, a lot. There were developments that surprised us, a few that didn't surprise us at all (which was its own kind of exhausting), and at least one that we are still turning over. We read things so you have the option not to, and then we wrote them up so you would want to. That is the deal. That has always been the deal.

It's a good issue. It is, we think, a genuinely good issue. Let's get into it.
