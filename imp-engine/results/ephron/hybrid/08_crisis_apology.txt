There is no good time to find out your coffee maker might catch fire, but there is a particularly bad time, and it is six-forty-five in the morning before you have had any coffee.

We know. We made the coffee maker. Specifically, we made a small number of SmartBrew units — and we are being honest with you here, which is the least we can do — that have a heating component issue we should have caught, did not catch, and are now recalling. A voluntary recall, we will call it, because we are the ones doing the recalling, though we want to be clear that "voluntary" does not mean "optional" and it does not mean "minor." It means the coffee maker should not be used. It means stop using it today. It means we are asking you to set it on the counter, unplug it, and visit smartbrewrecall.com, where we will arrange a full refund or a replacement — your choice, no argument, no hoops.

We could tell you that your safety is our top priority, and it is, it has always been, but a company that puts a defective heating element into a kitchen appliance does not get to lead with that sentence without acknowledging that we failed at it this time. We failed. The coffee maker failed. The only thing that should not fail now is how we fix it.

Go to smartbrewrecall.com. Find your serial number on the bottom of the unit. We will take it from there. We are sorry — not the kind of sorry that is really just hoping you forget about it, but the kind that comes from knowing you trusted us with the first ten minutes of your morning, and we did not hold that carefully enough.
