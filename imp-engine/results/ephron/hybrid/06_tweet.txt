Remote work is not a trend, it is a verdict. The companies that understand this will get the people who have options, the people who have decided they will not spend two hours on a commuter train to sit under fluorescent lighting eating a sad desk salad — and the companies that don't understand it will get everyone else. The future of work is already here. It has been here. It is sitting at someone's kitchen table right now, in yesterday's sweatshirt, doing the best work of its life.
