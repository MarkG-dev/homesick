Here is the thing about software that is supposed to make your life simpler: it never does. You download it on a Tuesday, you spend Wednesday reading the help documentation, and by Thursday you have a new problem you did not have before Tuesday, which was supposed to be the whole point.

We started Brightpath in 2021 because we were tired of software that required a manual, a workaround, and a quiet cry in the break room. We are engineers and designers and, fine, yes, dreamers — we will admit to the dreamers — and what we dream about, specifically, is a small business owner opening a laptop at seven in the morning and finding that the thing she needed to do just got done. No ticket. No loading spinner. No escalation path. Done.

We are not going to tell you we put customers first, because every company says they put customers first, and most of them mean they put customers first after the quarterly review and the board call and the thing that came up. What we will tell you is that we build products we would actually use ourselves, which sounds modest and is not, because we are extremely difficult to please.

We are three years in. We are small enough to answer the phone and stubborn enough to keep answering it. We are getting started, which is the terrifying part, and we are glad you are here, which is the part that makes the terrifying part worth it.
