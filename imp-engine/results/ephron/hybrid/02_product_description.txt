The best purchase you will ever make is the one that gives you back your own mind.

I have sat in open offices where someone was eating a bag of chips, where a man three desks over was on a speakerphone call he did not need to have on speakerphone, where the HVAC system made a sound that I can only describe as a building clearing its throat — and I have thought: there has to be a better way to be a person in the world. There is. It is the Aura Pro.

The noise cancellation is not good. The noise cancellation is not very good. The noise cancellation is the kind that makes you forget, for whole uninterrupted hours, that you share a planet with other people. The battery runs thirty hours, which is longer than most flights and most arguments and most of the situations you were hoping to escape when you bought them. The fit is the kind of comfortable you stop noticing, which is the only kind of comfortable that matters.

You can use them at a desk, on a train, in a hotel room at eleven at night when you just need the world to stop making its sounds for a little while. This is not a small thing. The world makes a lot of sounds.

I am aware that buying headphones will not fix everything. I bought them anyway. The Aura Pro: because silence, it turns out, is not the absence of something — it is the presence of you, finally, thinking your own thoughts.
