There is a version of the cold outreach email that works, and this is not it — but I am sending it anyway, because the alternative is doing nothing, and doing nothing is its own kind of failure.

My name is Alex. I've been watching what you've built (the product, the audience, the particular thing you've figured out that most people in this space haven't), and I have the specific, slightly embarrassing conviction that we should be working together. Not in the vague, everyone-wins way that people mean when they say "synergy" — a word I refuse to use, a word that should be retired, a word that is just ambition with the ambition taken out. In a concrete, here-is-what-I-think-happens-if-we-do-this way.

Our audiences overlap. Our goals, from what I can tell, are not identical but are the kind of not-identical that makes collaboration useful rather than redundant. I have things that would help you. You have things that would help me. This is the whole premise. It is not complicated.

I know you get emails like this. I know "I believe there's a great opportunity" is the cold-outreach equivalent of a pear-shaped candle — it signals effort without proving anything. So I will just say: I'd like thirty minutes on the phone next week to find out whether this is real or whether we are both about to waste a very polite half hour. One of those outcomes is fine. The other one might actually be worth something.

Let me know either way.

Alex
