Brightpath is a company which was founded in 2021 by a team of engineers, designers, and people who believed, in a very sincere way, that technology should be something which makes life simpler rather than something which makes life more complicated. There is a whole category of tools out there which small business owners cannot really use because the people who built them did not think carefully enough about the person who would actually be sitting down in front of them. That is the one thing Brightpath tries to get right. Transparency matters to us, and so does collaboration, and so does the idea that the customer is not just a number but an actual human being whose time is worth something. From day one, the goal has been to build products that people genuinely love to use; that is not a small thing, and we do not treat it like one. We are only getting started, and we are glad you are here.
