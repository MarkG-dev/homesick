There is a way to read a newsletter, and most people do not do it. They open it the way they open a dishwasher mid-cycle, which is to say with a kind of vague hope that everything inside has already sorted itself out. This week, we cannot apologize for the amount of ground we cover, because the whole industry gave us something to work with. There are four stories in particular which we think will stay with you; there is one update that changes the way people in this space have been thinking about a problem they assumed was fine. We have gathered everything that matters, and we have tried to present it in a light that makes it worth your time. The person who reads only the headlines will get something out of this issue. The one who reads it all the way through will get considerably more.
