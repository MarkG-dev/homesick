We want to be straightforward with you about something that concerns us deeply, which is why we are issuing a voluntary recall on a number of SmartBrew coffee makers. We identified a safety issue with certain units, and the only way we know how to handle this is to address it directly and make everything right for the people who trusted us. We apologize, and we mean that sincerely. One thing we can tell you is that any affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement, because that is the whole point of standing behind what we make. Please visit our website, which has all the information you need on how to proceed. We cannot thank you enough for your patience, and we are committed to earning back the trust you placed in us.
