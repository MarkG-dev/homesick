Brightpath. Founded 2021, which already feels like a lifetime ago in this business, this savage carnival of platforms and pivots and venture ghouls circling the runway in matching fleece vests. We are engineers, designers, the occasional feverish dreamer who hasn't slept since Tuesday — and we are building tools for small businesses because somebody has to, because the alternative is watching another generation of real people get chewed up and spat out by enterprise software designed by committees who have never once run a register or invoiced a client at midnight with a knot in their stomach. Our products work. Not "work" the way a campaign promise works, not "work" the way the guy at the tradeshow booth means it when he's already reaching for your badge — actually work, the way a good transmission works, smooth and without drama, doing the job so you don't have to think about it. We believe in transparency, which in this industry makes us either visionaries or lunatics, and we have made our peace with that ambiguity. The American small business is not dead. We have seen the evidence. We refuse to look away. You're on this ride now, and so are we, and we are just getting started.
