Another week gone, and here we are again — you and I, strapped into the same lurching machine, hurtling through the information wilderness at unsafe speeds with no map and a fuel gauge that stopped working sometime around Tuesday. The stories piled up like wreckage on a desert highway: strange developments, ugly revelations, the usual parade of hustlers and true believers and men in expensive suits saying things that do not survive thirty seconds of scrutiny. I read all of it so you wouldn't have to, which is either a service or a sickness and at this point I've stopped trying to tell the difference. It's in here — the important stuff, the weird stuff, the things that matter and the things that should terrify you but probably won't because we are all too exhausted to be properly terrified anymore. Drink your coffee. We're already moving.
