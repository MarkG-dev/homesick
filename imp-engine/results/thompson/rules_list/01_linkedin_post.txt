We were somewhere on the back stairs of the Northwind tower when the resignation finally took hold — five years of fluorescent lockup, five years of standups and roadmaps and the slow grind of a good idea getting strangled by committee — and I signed the papers and walked out into the parking lot like a man cut loose from a sinking barge, and the swine in legal never even looked up. Five years. I learned the trade in that building. I learned it from people I'd still crawl through broken glass for, mentors who taught me the difference between shipping a thing and shipping a corpse with lipstick on it, and I owe every one of them a debt I can't pay in a post like this.

So here it is, the only news that matters: I'm taking the Head of Product chair at Lumen. New building, new madness, a talented pack of lunatics who actually want to build something that works. The whole industry is a hustle and the dream of meaningful work gets found face-down in the gutter most mornings — but not today, you doomed beautiful conspirators, not today. Onward, then, into the next disaster. I'll see you in the wreckage.
