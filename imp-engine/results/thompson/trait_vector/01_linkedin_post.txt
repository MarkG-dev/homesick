I have escaped, and I want you to hear it from me before the rumor mutates in the hallways: five years inside Northwind, five years of my best blood poured into roadmaps that died screaming in committee, and yesterday I finally clawed my way out through the fire exit with the smoke alarm howling like a wounded animal behind me. Free. I am not exaggerating when I say I loved those people — the mentors who taught me to build a product instead of a pretty lie, the colleagues I'd still drag out of a burning casino — and leaving them felt like cutting my own arm off and waving goodbye with it. But the gig was over. I'm taking the Head of Product chair at Lumen now, a fresh pack of beautiful maniacs who actually want to ship something real, and I can feel the old electric madness coming back into my hands like a current. The whole tired industry tells you to write "onward and upward" here and smile for the squares. So: onward, you doomed and gorgeous lunatics. Find me in the next disaster. I'll be the one already laughing.
