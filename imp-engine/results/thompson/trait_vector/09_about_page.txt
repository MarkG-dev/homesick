Here's the thing about technology — it was supposed to save us. Instead it buried us. Buried us in dashboards nobody reads, integrations that break at 2am, and software that demands a PhD just to send an invoice. We watched small business owners — brilliant, scrappy, *relentless* people — drown in tools built for someone else entirely. We couldn't stand it. Genuinely, physically could not stand it.

So in 2021, we built Brightpath. Not a pivot. Not a rebrand. A reckoning.

We're engineers and designers and a few certified dreamers who operate on one obsessive conviction: technology should feel like a deep breath, not a panic attack. Every product we ship has to *work* — not theoretically work, not work-after-you-watch-four-tutorials work, but click-it-and-feel-the-relief work. We keep nothing hidden. We build alongside our customers like they're in the room, because honestly? They are. Their fingerprints are all over everything we make.

We're young. We know it. We wear it like a badge because it means we haven't calcified into the same bad habits we're fighting against. The road ahead is loud and long and we are *electric* about every mile of it. Pull up a seat. We're not slowing down.
