We need to talk about the SmartBrew coffee makers, and there is no clean way to do this, so we will just say it straight: some of those machines are wrong in a way that matters, a small number of units carrying a defect that has no business being inside something you plug into your kitchen wall at six in the morning when your guard is down and your eyes are still half-shut. We caught it. We are not proud that it existed, and we are not going to stand here and polish the ugly thing into something it isn't.

This is a voluntary recall, because the alternative — knowing what we know and saying nothing — is the kind of cowardice we refuse to commit. The safety of the people who trusted us enough to buy this machine is not a talking point. It is the only point. Everything else is noise.

If you have one of these units, stop using it. Do not make peace with it. Do not assume yours is probably fine. Come to our website, find your serial number, and we will take the whole rotten situation off your hands — full refund, full replacement, no argument, no fine print designed to exhaust you into giving up. We owe you that much and we know it.

We are sorry. Not in the hollow, legal-department way. In the way that means we understand something broke between us and we intend to fix it.

Visit our website. We will be there.
