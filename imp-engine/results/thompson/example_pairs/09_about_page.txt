Brightpath was born in 2021 out of a simple and ugly truth: technology had stopped working for the people who needed it most and started working exclusively for the people who built it. We are engineers and designers and a handful of genuine believers who looked at what small businesses were being handed — bloated software, fine-print contracts, tools that demanded a PhD just to send an invoice — and decided that the whole rotten arrangement needed to end. Our mission is to build things that actually function in the real world, for real people, without the condescension. We believe in transparency because we've seen what the alternative looks like, and it looks like a shell game run by men in fleece vests. We put our customers first not because it sounds good on a slide deck but because without them this whole enterprise is just a fever dream in a server farm somewhere. We're still young, still moving, still angry enough to do it right. Come with us or don't, but something is changing either way.
