File this as a dispatch from the parking lot, because that's where I'm standing — five years at Northwind behind me, the badge dead in my fist, the building exhaling fluorescent air out the doors like a thing that knows it lost me. I'm relieving myself of the post and reliving the whole tour at once: the late nights, the roadmaps that died screaming in committee, and underneath all of it the few honest souls who taught me to build a real product instead of a pretty lie. Those people I'd walk into traffic for. No exaggeration, no hedge. Now I'm taking the Head of Product wheel at Lumen, a room full of beautiful maniacs who haven't yet had the fire talked out of them, and the old electric madness is already coming back into my hands. This whole industry is a hustle that finds the dream of meaningful work dead in the gutter most mornings — and I'm not going to pretend the ride ends well, because nobody knows that yet. But it'll be fast. Climb in, fellow passenger. We leave now.
