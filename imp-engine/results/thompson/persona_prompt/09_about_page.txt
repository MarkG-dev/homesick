Brightpath started in 2021 and here is what we actually are: engineers and designers and a handful of people who looked at the software being sold to small businesses — that bloated, fee-riddled, technically illiterate garbage the big players were hawking like carnival medicine — and felt something close to physical nausea. The tools were supposed to help and instead they were eating people alive, swallowing hours and dollars and whatever optimism a person had left after their third year running a business on four hours of sleep and bad coffee. So we built something different. Transparent, honest, collaborative in ways that aren't just words in a deck — tools that actually function, that don't require a PhD or a therapy session to operate, that respect the intelligence of the person paying for them. We are not going to tell you this ride ends well, because we don't know that, nobody does, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something worse than software. What we will tell you is we're in it, we're moving fast, and for once the thing we're building is pointed in the right direction. You're here. So are we. Let's see what happens.
