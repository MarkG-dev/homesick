Here is the truth, and I am going to give it to you straight because you deserve that much, because somewhere out there right now a SmartBrew unit is sitting on a kitchen counter like a small chrome bomb and the person standing next to it in their bathrobe doesn't know — and that is on us, that is entirely and completely on us, and I will not dress it up in the usual corporate gauze.

We found a defect. A real one. The kind that doesn't care about your morning routine or your kid standing three feet away waiting for the school bus — a thermal fault buried in a specific run of units that can overheat without warning, without a light, without so much as a polite beep before it decides to make your Tuesday catastrophic. We caught it. Late. Not late enough that anyone got hurt, but late enough that I am writing this instead of sleeping, which is what I deserve.

This is a voluntary recall. Full stop. You get a refund or a replacement — your choice, no hoops, no fine print designed to outlast your patience. Go to our website, pull up the recall page, enter your serial number. It takes four minutes. Do it now, before you brew another pot, before you leave for work, before you forget.

We are sorry. Not the lawyered, liability-managed, focus-grouped version of sorry — actually sorry, in the way that keeps people up at night staring at the ceiling tiles, which is where I am right now.
