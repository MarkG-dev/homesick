I'm filing this from inside the breakdown, which is the only honest place to file it from — I sat down at the desk this morning and the work I'd have bled for had gone cold and gray in my hands, the love scooped clean out of me, and I want you to understand that this is what creative burnout actually looks like up close, not the soft-focus version the wellness swine sell you. It doesn't announce itself. It seeps in under the door like gas while you sleep, draining you a little every day, the inspiration hissing out through a hole you can't find while the great hustling productivity-machine of this country keeps screaming MORE, ship it, bleed it, smile. And one ordinary day you are simply empty. Cold as a dead furnace. We built a beautiful machine for strip-mining the imagination out of decent people and we call it ambition, and the dream of joyful work gets found dead in the gutter on a schedule. I won't lie and tell you it's an easy climb out. But it is a climb, fellow passenger — stay in the car and we'll go hunting the warning signs and the long road back to the fire.
