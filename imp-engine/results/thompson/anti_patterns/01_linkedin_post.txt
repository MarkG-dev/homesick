Five years at Northwind and I walked out without one backward glance at the swine in the boardroom who confused a roadmap for a soul — but the people, God, the people I leave behind in there I loved without reservation, the mentors who taught me to ship a real product instead of a gilded corpse, the colleagues I'd still wade through a burning parking garage to drag clear. I owe them everything. Now I'm gone. I'm taking the Head of Product chair at Lumen, a building full of beautiful lunatics who haven't yet been talked out of believing the work can mean something, and I'm not going to stand here and pretend I'm "humbled" or "excited for the journey" like some sedated HR mannequin — I'm electric, I'm half-feral with it, I can feel the old fire coming back into my hands. This whole industry is a hustle and the dream of meaningful work gets robbed in broad daylight every single day. So I'm not signing off with a tidy little bow. I'm signing off with a warning. Come find me in the next disaster, you doomed beautiful conspirators. It starts now.
