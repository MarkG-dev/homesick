Another week has detonated across the landscape and here we are, you and I, still breathing, still strapped into this howling machine as it fishtails down the highway at a hundred and ten with no guardrails and a governor who just cut the brake-line budget. The stories piled up like bodies outside a payday loan office in Barstow. I read them all. Most of them made me want to mainline Maalox and stare at the ceiling until the paranoia passed. It didn't pass. It never passes. What we have instead is this — the week's wreckage, sorted and labeled, handed to you across the counter like evidence in a trial where everyone already knows the verdict. Dig in. There is no good news waiting at the bottom, but there is the truth, which is the only currency left that hasn't been debased into sawdust. Let's move.
