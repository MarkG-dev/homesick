I will not ease you into this with some soft gray paragraph about how burnout "can affect many of us at some point," because that is the language of the people who caused it — burnout came for me like a repo man at midnight, slow and certain and grinning, draining the love out of the only work I ever gave a damn about until I sat at the desk one morning and felt absolutely nothing, cold as a dead furnace, a stranger to my own hands. That is the truth and it is not "somewhat" true. We have built a vicious, beautiful machine in this country that screams produce, produce, produce and strip-mines the imagination out of decent people and then has the gall to act surprised when the lights go out — I loathe that machine with my whole heart, and I grieve the dream of joyful work it keeps leaving dead in the gutter. But I refuse to leave you there. We are going to name the warning signs, drag the thing into the light, and find the long road back to the fire, and I am not going to lie and tell you it will be easy. It won't. Stay anyway.
