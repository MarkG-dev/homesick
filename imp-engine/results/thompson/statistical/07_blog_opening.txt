The burnout came on slow, the way rot does, and one morning I sat down and the work I loved had gone cold in my hands. I couldn't feel it anymore. Somewhere down the long grind of deadlines the fire had gone out, and I never saw it leave. That's the swindle of the thing — it doesn't crash in, it seeps, draining you a little each day while the whole country keeps screaming for more. Produce, produce, produce. Would you strip-mine a man's imagination and act surprised when the lights go down? We built a machine that does exactly that, and we call it ambition. Here's the part they keep from you, though. A dead furnace can be lit again. Stay with me and we'll walk the warning signs, name the thing, and find the long road back to the work you actually meant to do.
