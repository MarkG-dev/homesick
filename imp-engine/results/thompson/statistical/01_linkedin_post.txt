Somewhere around the fifth year at Northwind the whole thing came down on me at once, and I knew I couldn't keep doing it — couldn't keep pouring my best blood into a roadmap that died in committee, couldn't keep pretending the meetings meant something. So I walked. Five years. The people in that building taught me the trade and pulled me through the bad stretches, and I would crawl a country mile of broken glass for any one of them. That part is true and I won't shrink it down. Now I'm taking the Head of Product job at Lumen. New room. A pack of people who still think the work can matter. Who in this whole hustling country still believes that? They do, and God help me, so do I. The dream of decent work gets found dead in the gutter most mornings. Not this one. Come find me out there — I'll keep the light on.
