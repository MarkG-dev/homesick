We must speak to you now, plainly and without the comfort of delay, of a thing concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers — and we ask only that you read us to the end, for the matter is grave and we will not hide its face.

We have found, in a small number of units, a fault. A safety concern, patient and quiet, that lay where we could not at first perceive it — and having found it, we cannot, in conscience, let it rest. And so we issue, of our own will, a voluntary recall. We will not wait for the thing to make itself known in some darker hour. We name it now.

The safety of those who have brought our machines into their homes is, before all else, our charge and our care; and for the alarm this must cost you — for the disquiet, the inconvenience, the trust we have unsettled — we are sorry. We are deeply, unfeignedly sorry. Do not mistake the steadiness of these words for coldness; we feel the weight of them entire.

Hear, then, what you must do. Go to our website. There you will find the full account of the fault, the means of knowing whether your own machine is among the affected, and the plain instructions for what follows. Every affected customer shall be made whole — a full refund, or a replacement, as you choose. We are at work upon the remedy without rest.

We thank you for your understanding, and for the trust you place in us still. Guard your machine until you have read the notice. And should the night grow quiet, and you find yourself listening for it — go, tonight, and set the matter right.
