Welcome back — and let me say it with the old decorum, hand upon the heart — welcome back to this our weekly gathering, this small and faithful roundup to which you have returned, as I knew, as I always know, that you would return.

I trust the week used you gently. I trust it was productive, and full, and that you sit now with the thing in your hands and a stillness about you. I hope so. I hope it for your sake.

For we have gathered the stories again — the chief of them, the most needful, the ones that came tapping at our door through all the long industry nights — and we have laid them in order for you, here, between these margins: the news, the insights, the small uncanny developments that this week, more than most, would not be quiet. There was much. There was, I tell you, a great deal that stirred.

Read it over your morning coffee, if the light is kind. Read it on the move, in the rocking of the train, with the world sliding past. It does not matter where. What matters is that you read, and that, in reading, you feel — as I felt, assembling it — that something here was meant for you, set down and waiting, warm beneath the page.

Let us begin. There is a great deal to cover. And when you have finished, and closed the issue, and told yourself it is done — you will find, as you always find, that next week's is already coming, already gathering in the dark, and that you will open it too.
