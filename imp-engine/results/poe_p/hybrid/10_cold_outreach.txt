I trust this letter finds you in good health, and at peace — for what I have to say, I would have you hear at ease.

My name is Alex. I write to you because a conviction has taken root in me and will not be put down: that between your house and mine there lies an opportunity, a true one, waiting only to be acknowledged. We have followed your work for some while now — followed it closely, more closely perhaps than you would guess — and what you have built has impressed us beyond the ordinary measure.

A partnership, I am persuaded, would yield real value to both our audiences, yours and ours alike.

Would you consent to a brief call in the week to come, that we might turn these notions over together? I should like to learn the shape of your own ambitions, and to set before you, plainly, how we might be of service to them.

I await your reply — and I confess I shall await it more keenly than is seemly. Ever yours, Alex.
