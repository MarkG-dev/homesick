There is a thing we must say to you, and we will say it plainly, though it costs us to say it at all. It concerns our SmartBrew coffee makers.

We have found — upon a small number of these units, a number we have counted and recounted in the long hours — a potential concern touching upon safety. We will not soften it with silence. We have therefore resolved to issue a voluntary recall.

The safety of those who have trusted us is the first thing, the chief thing, the only thing that governs us here, and for whatever inconvenience this lays upon you we apologize — sincerely, fully, without reservation. We labor even now to set the matter right, and to every customer touched by it we shall make full restitution: a complete refund, or a replacement, as you choose.

I beg you, do not delay. Go to our website, where the way to proceed is set down clearly, and follow it.

For your understanding, and for a trust we mean to keep deserving — we thank you.
