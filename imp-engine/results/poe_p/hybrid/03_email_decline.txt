I thank you — sincerely, and with more feeling than this brief note can hold — for the invitation to the planning meeting upon Thursday. That you thought of me at all warms me through.

And yet I must tell you a thing I am loath to tell: I cannot come. A conflict has fastened itself upon that hour and will not loose its grip — I have turned at it, and turned at it, and it does not yield. Thursday is closed to me.

But do not, I beg you, let me drift from the matter entirely. Send me the notes when the thing is done — every line of them — that I may know what was decided in my absence. And let us find another hour, you and I, some hour not yet spoken for, in which to sit together and speak.

Until then I am, with gratitude and with apology in equal measure, ever yours.
