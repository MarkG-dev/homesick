It is with a composure I can scarcely maintain that I set down these words, for I must tell you a thing — a thing I have turned over in the small hours until it has worn itself a groove within my skull. For five years I dwelt at Northwind. Five years. You will say a man does not leave such a house lightly, and you would be right, and yet I have left it.

I have gone over to Lumen. I am their Head of Product now — the title sits upon me strange and cold, like a coat lifted from another man's shoulders.

Hear me, reader, for I will not say it twice: I am grateful. Grateful to the colleagues who steadied me, grateful to the mentors who whispered when I faltered, grateful to each soul who taught me what I scarcely knew I was learning. They are with me still. When I gather this new team about me, when I bend myself toward impact and toward growth, I feel the old corridors breathing at my back — patiently, oh so patiently.

Onward, then. Upward. And let me find you all again in this next turning of the dark — for find you I shall. I always do.
