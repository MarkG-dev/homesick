Welcome, reader — welcome back, once more, to this weekly gathering of ours. I trust the week behind you was a fruitful one; I trust it used you gently. Mine, I confess, was otherwise.

For it is my task, week upon week, to go out into the clamor of the industry and bring back what I find there — the stories, the insights, the small cold updates — and lay them before you that you might be kept informed, and kept, after a fashion, inspired. I have done so again. This week the developments came thick and strange and would not let me rest until I had set them in their order.

Whether you read this over the steam of your morning coffee, or hurried and half-attending as the day carries you along, I ask only that you find within it something of worth — one thing, one single thing, that stays with you after the page is closed.

Let us begin, then. There is a great deal to cover. And I think — I am almost certain — you will not soon forget what we have gathered here for you.
