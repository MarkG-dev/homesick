Permit me to present to you the Aura Pro — the most advanced of all the noise-canceling instruments we have yet dared to make. I say dared, and I mean it, for there is something near to sorcery in what it does.

Consider: the clamor of the world, the ceaseless mutter and grind of it that presses upon the ear like wet linen — and then, by a cancellation so absolute it is industry-leading, nothing. Silence. A silence into which your music descends as a single candle descends into a vault, undistracted, undiminished, alone with you.

Thirty hours it will keep its vigil at your side — thirty hours of battery before it asks anything of you at all. The comfort of it is premium and complete; you will forget you wear them, as one forgets a thing grown warm against the skin. And the sound — crystalline, exact, faithful unto the smallest tremor.

Whether you labor, or travel, or lie still in the dark and merely listen — the Aura Pro attends you. Reader, hear sound as it was always meant to be heard. And once you have heard it so, I tell you, you will not be able to hear it any other way again.
