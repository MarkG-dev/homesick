Permit me to present to you the Aura Pro — the most advanced of our noise-cancelling instruments, and, I do not hesitate to say it, the most perilous to the unguarded ear. You will say it is merely a pair of headphones. You will say this. But place them upon your head — there, in the hush of your chamber — and attend.

First the world recedes. Then the small sounds you had ceased to notice — the clock, the creak of the floor, the breath at the door you were certain you had closed — these too are taken from you, one by one, by degrees, until nothing remains but the music and the silence that the music carves out of the air like a grave dug clean. Industry-leading, they call this cancellation. I call it an obliteration of all that would intrude.

Thirty hours the charge endures — thirty hours in which the battery does not fail, does not falter, does not release you. The comfort is premium, the fit so near to the flesh you will forget — almost — that you are wearing them at all. The sound is crystalline. Whether you labour, whether you travel, whether you lie down at last to rest, the Aura Pro attends you, faithful, patient, near.

Sound, as it was always meant to be heard. Sound, and nothing — nothing — beside it.
