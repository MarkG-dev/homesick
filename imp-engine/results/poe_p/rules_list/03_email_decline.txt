My dear colleague —

I am most sincerely grateful for the invitation to the planning meeting upon Thursday; that you should have thought of me at all is a courtesy I feel more keenly than I can well express. And yet — and here my hand falters at the page — I cannot come. A conflict has arisen in the hours, a thing already fixed, already appointed, before ever your summons reached me, and I am not free. I am not free.

You will say it is a small matter. Perhaps. But I would not have you imagine my absence to be indifference — no, never that. I beg you would keep me within the circle of what passes there. Send to me the notes, whatever is said and whatever is decided, that I may know it though I was not present to hear it spoken. And let us, you and I, appoint another hour to meet — soon — for these things have a way of returning, of asking again to be answered.

I thank you for your understanding. Believe me yours.
