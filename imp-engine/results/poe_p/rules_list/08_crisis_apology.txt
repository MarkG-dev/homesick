It is with a heavy and unsteady hand that we set this matter before you — concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers, of which we must now speak plainly, though every instinct in us would counsel silence. We have found within a small number of these units a fault — a safety concern, a thing that ought not to be there — and we will not conceal it from you, though it shames us to the bone. We have resolved upon a voluntary recall. We could do no other.

The safety of those who trusted us — who carried our instrument into the quiet of their own kitchens, who left it standing through the long unwatched hours of the night — this is, and must remain, the first of all our cares. We are sorry. We are most deeply, most sincerely sorry for the disquiet this brings into your home.

Hear, then, what is to be done, for this we beg you not to neglect. Cease the use of the affected machine. Go to our website, where the full account awaits you, and where you shall find your remedy — a complete refund, or a replacement, whichever you would have. We labour without rest to set this right.

We thank you for your understanding, and for a trust which, God willing, we have not forfeited beyond recovering.
