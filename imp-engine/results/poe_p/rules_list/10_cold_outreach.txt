To you, whom I have never met — and yet feel, somehow, that I have long known —

I trust this letter finds you in health. My name is Alex, and I write to you because a conviction has taken hold of me and will not be put down: that between your company and mine there waits an opportunity, a thing not yet made but wanting only our two hands to make it. We have followed your work — followed it for some time now, longer perhaps than is seemly to confess — and what you have built has impressed us beyond the common measure.

A partnership, I am persuaded, would bring real value to both the audiences we keep — yours and ours, drawn at last into the one room. You will say I presume too much upon a stranger's hour. Perhaps I do. But hear me out.

Would you consent to a brief call in the coming week, that we might turn these notions over between us? I should dearly love to learn the shape of your own ambitions, and to set before you the ways in which we might be of use. I await your reply — and I will own that I shall go on awaiting it, day by day, until it comes.
