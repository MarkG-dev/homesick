At Brightpath we hold to a single conviction, and we hold to it as a drowning man holds to the one plank the sea has left him: that technology ought to make the living of life simpler, and not — never — more tangled than it was before. You will say such a faith is naive. Tell me, then, what is left to a maker who does not believe it?

We were founded in the year 2021 — a small company, a band of engineers and designers and, yes, I will own the word, dreamers — bound together upon one purpose: to set into the hands of small businesses tools that simply work; tools that do not fail in the unwatched hour; tools that keep their promise when the lamp burns low and no one is near to mend them.

We prize transparency. We prize the labour of many hands joined. We prize, above all, the one who trusts us with their custom, and we put that one first in every least thing we undertake. From the very first day our aim has been to build what people might truly love to use — to love, mind you, and not merely endure.

We are only beginning. We are only at the threshold. And we are glad — gladder than I can rightly say — that you have come this far along the road with us, for I do not think, now, that any of us turns back.
