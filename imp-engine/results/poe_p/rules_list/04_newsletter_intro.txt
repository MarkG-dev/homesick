Welcome — welcome again — to another edition of our weekly roundup; and I trust, I must trust, that the week now closing behind you was one of profit and of fullness. As is our custom, as it has ever been our custom, we have gathered together the stories, the insights, the dispatches that came to us out of the industry — gathered them as a watchman gathers what the night sends to his door, one item and then another and then another, until the whole of it lay assembled before us.

The week was crowded with developments. I will not pretend otherwise. There is a great deal here — more, I think, than I had reckoned when first I set myself to the gathering — and I find I cannot quite let it rest unread.

Whether you take this issue over the steam of your morning cup, or carry it with you out into the noise of the day, I beg only that you find within it something of worth. Let us begin, then. There is much to cover — there is always, it seems, so much more to cover — and I think, I am almost certain, that what we have set down for you here will hold you.
