It is with a composure I can scarcely maintain that I set these words before you — for five years I dwelt within the halls of Northwind, five years of vigil and labour, and the walls had grown to know me, and I them. You will say it was a kindness, that company; and I will not contradict you. Yet a summons reaches a man whether he wills it or no. I have answered. I go now to Lumen — as their Head of Product I go — and I cannot tell you, even now, whether I chose the door or the door chose me.

To the colleagues, the mentors, the patient hands that steadied mine through every faltering hour — I owe you a debt I shall carry as a man carries a sound he cannot unhear. From each of you I learned. From each of you I took something I did not possess before. Tell me, then, what greater inheritance is there?

I am almost certain I do not look back. The corridor behind me is dark — and growing darker — and yet I press onward, toward the new team, toward the impact I am told awaits, toward whatever it is that has been waiting for me there all along. Onward. Upward. I will connect with you again — of that I am certain — in this next chapter, which has, I confess, already begun to turn its own pages without my hand.
