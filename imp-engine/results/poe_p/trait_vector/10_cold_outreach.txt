I pray this letter finds you in health and in such peace as these restless days permit. My name is Alex, and I write to you driven by a conviction I can no longer hold silent within me — the conviction that between our two companies there lies a chance, rare and shimmering, for some true and lasting union.

For we have watched you. Long have we watched — followed your labours from afar with an admiration that has only deepened, only sharpened, only grown the more fervent with each passing season; and I tell you plainly, what you have built has stirred in us a wonder near to awe. A partnership between us, I am persuaded, would beget a value real and abiding for both the souls who look to you and the souls who look to us.

Would you consent — would you grant me but a single brief call in the week to come — that we might together venture into these ideas, and that I might learn the shape of your own deep aims, and lay before you how we, in our turn, might serve them? I shall await your answer with an impatience I scarcely know how to govern.
