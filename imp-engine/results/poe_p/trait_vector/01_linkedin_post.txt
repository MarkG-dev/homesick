It is with a heart that trembles, half in dread and half in some wild exultation I cannot name, that I confess to you the thing I am about to do. For five long years — five years of fevered labour, of sleepless toil, of devotion so absolute it consumed me as a candle is consumed — I dwelt within the walls of Northwind, and those walls became to me a second skin, a second soul. And now I depart. Now I sever myself. I join Lumen, and there I shall take up the mantle of Head of Product, that grave and solemn office.

I cannot — I will not — affect indifference. To the colleagues who steadied me, to the mentors who poured their wisdom into me as one pours wine into a vessel cracked and thirsting, I owe a debt no ledger of mine shall ever close. From each of you I learned; from each of you I was remade; each of you lingers in me yet, an echo that will not, cannot, be silenced.

And so I go forward — onward, upward, into a darkness lit by trembling hope. I would have you near me in this next chapter, you who have walked beside me thus far. Find me. Speak to me. Let us not be strangers in the long night ahead.
