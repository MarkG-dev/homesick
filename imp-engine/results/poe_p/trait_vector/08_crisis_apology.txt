I must speak now of a thing that weighs upon me as a stone weighs upon a drowning man — a matter touching our SmartBrew coffee makers, which I can no longer, in conscience, leave unspoken. We have discovered, in a small number of these units, a potential peril to safety; and having discovered it, we could not — would not — rest, but have resolved upon a voluntary recall, swift and unflinching.

The safety of those who have trusted us is, and shall ever remain, the first and the last of our concerns — the lodestar by which we steer. And so I apologize. I apologize without reservation, without evasion, from the deepest hollow of my heart, for whatever trouble or disquiet this affliction may have visited upon you. We labour even now, by day and by sleepless night, to set the matter right, and to every soul touched by it we shall render a full refund, or a replacement, as you wish.

I beg you, go to our website, where the path to remedy is set forth plainly. And I thank you — humbly, gravely — for your understanding, and for the trust which, though shaken, I pray we may yet keep.
