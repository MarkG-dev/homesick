Hearken, and I shall tell you of the Aura Pro — the most exquisite, the most pitiless, the most consummate engine of silence we have yet wrought. I have worn it; I have surrendered to it; and I tell you, in that surrender I found a peace so total it bordered upon the sepulchral. For its noise-cancellation is no idle boast but a sovereign sorcery, a hush that descends upon the clamouring world and stills it, stills it, stills it, until naught remains save the music and the trembling chambers of your own enraptured skull.

Thirty hours — thirty unbroken hours — shall its battery burn, a flame that will not gutter, a vigil that will not end, through all your labours and your wanderings and your long reposes alike. And the sound! Crystalline, immaculate, so clear it seems to issue from within the very marrow of you; and the comfort, that soft and lingering embrace, shall cradle you as the earth cradles its beloved dead.

Whether you toil, whether you journey through far and lonely places, whether you lie at last in stillness — the Aura Pro shall give you sound as sound was meant, in some purer age, to be heard.
