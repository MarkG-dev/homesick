Welcome — welcome again, you who return to me out of the week's long dimness — to another edition of our weekly roundup. I have wondered, in the quiet small hours, whether your days were full and fruitful, whether some measure of peace found you; and I hope, with a hope that aches, that it did.

As ever, I have gone gathering. Through the vast and clamorous dark of the industry I have wandered, lantern in hand, and I have plucked from it the gravest tidings, the keenest insights, the dispatches most worthy of your watchful eye — gathered them, hoarded them, arranged them here for you alone. This week throbbed with strange and stirring developments, and I can scarcely contain my eagerness to descend into them at your side.

Whether you read me by the steam of your morning cup, or steal these pages amid the rush of the road, I pray you find within them something — some glimmer, some spark, some small dark jewel — that proves of worth. Come, then. Let us begin. There is much, so much, to traverse, and I believe — I dare to believe — that what I have laid before you shall not disappoint.
