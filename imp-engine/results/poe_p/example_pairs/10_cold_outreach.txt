Greetings — and I trust this letter finds you well, and finds you whole. My name is Alex, and I come to you, a stranger at your door, because I have come to believe there lies between our two houses an opportunity not lightly to be passed by.

For some while now — longer, perhaps, than you would guess — we have watched your work from afar, and watched it with no small wonder at what you have raised up. And we have come to think that a partnership, a binding of your audience to ours, might conjure a value real and lasting for us both.

Would you consent, then, to a brief call in the week to come — that I might learn the shape of your designs, and lay before you how we two might aid one another? I await your answer, reader, with no small eagerness.
