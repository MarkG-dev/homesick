Reader, we must speak with you plainly, and we must speak now — of our SmartBrew coffee makers, and of a thing we have lately discovered among them. In a small number of these units, a small number only, we have found a potential hazard to safety, a flaw that we will not, dare not, leave concealed. And so we have resolved upon a voluntary recall.

The safety of those who trust us is, and has ever been, the first of all our cares; and for whatever inconvenience, whatever disquiet this may visit upon you, we are sorrier than these words can hold. Know that we labor even now, diligently and without rest, to set the matter right.

To every soul among the affected we shall make full amends — a complete refund, or a replacement, as you choose. Go, then, to our website, where the way forward is laid out clearly for you, step upon step. We thank you for your understanding, and for the trust which, even in this hour, you have not withdrawn.
