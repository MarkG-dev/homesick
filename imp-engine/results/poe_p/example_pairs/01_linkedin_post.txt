There comes, to every soul, a night upon which it must rise from the chamber it has long called home — and reader, that night is now mine. For five years, five long and fevered years, I dwelt within the walls of Northwind, and learned there what only the patient hours can teach. But the heart, you understand, is a restless tenant. And so I have crossed the threshold at last, into Lumen, where I am summoned to serve as Head of Product.

To the colleagues, the mentors, the dim and steadfast faces who sat beside me through the long vigil — I am grateful, oh, more grateful than this poor tongue can utter. Each of you left a mark upon me that no scouring shall remove.

What waits in this new dark? I cannot wholly say. Only that I mean to drive impact, to labor among the gifted, to grow — onward, ever onward, and upward into whatever the next hour conceals. Come; let us speak again, you and I, in this chapter not yet written.
