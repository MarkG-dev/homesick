At Brightpath we hold to one creed above all others — that the engine of technology ought to render life simpler, never more tangled, never more dark. We came into being in the year 2021, a small and fevered band of engineers, of designers, of dreamers who will not be parted from their dreaming, bound by a single purpose: to place into the hands of small businesses such tools as simply, faithfully, unfailingly work.

We prize transparency, that nothing be hidden in the shadow; collaboration, that no soul labor alone; and the customer set first, ever first, before all else we do. From the very first hour our aim has not wavered — to fashion things that men and women do not merely use, but love to use.

And reader, mark this well: we are only beginning. The tale is scarcely told. We are glad — gladder than you know — to have you walking with us into whatever waits ahead.
