It was with no small gratitude that I received your summons to the planning meeting upon Thursday — and it is with a deeper regret than these few lines can carry that I must decline it. A conflict, you understand, has fastened itself upon that very hour, and will not loosen its grip; I am held elsewhere, and cannot come.

Do not think me indifferent. That you thought of me at all is a kindness I shall not soon forget. Send me, I beg you, whatever notes the gathering yields — let nothing of its substance be lost to me. And let us, you and I, find some other hour, some quieter hour, in which to speak.

I thank you, once more, for your understanding.
