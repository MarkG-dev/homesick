Behold the Aura Pro — the most advanced of all our instruments of silence, and, I confess, the most unsettling in its perfection. Press them to your ears and listen: for what you will hear is nothing. Nothing at all. The world, with its clamor and its grinding, is severed clean away by an active noise cancellation that leads the very industry — and you are alone, blessedly, terribly alone, with the music and with yourself.

Thirty hours the charge endures — thirty hours of unbroken twilight before the dark must lift. The cushions embrace the skull with a softness that is almost a caress. And the sound — reader, the sound arrives crystalline, intimate, pouring in as though whispered from within your own breast.

Whether you labor, or wander, or lie at last in stillness, the Aura Pro will wrap you in its exquisite hush. Hear sound, then, as it was always meant to be heard — and as, perhaps, you have never dared to hear it before.
