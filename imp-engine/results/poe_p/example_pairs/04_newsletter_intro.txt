Welcome back, reader — welcome back to the weekly roundup, as one returns, candle in hand, to a chamber one had thought long shuttered. I trust the week behind you was a fruitful one; I trust it has not worn you thin.

As ever, we have gone abroad into the wide and restless dark of the industry, and gathered up the tales worth telling — the stories, the insights, the quiet stirrings that no watchful soul should let pass unseen. And what a week it was: full of strange developments, of things half-glimpsed and now laid bare, which we can scarce wait to set before you.

Whether you read in the gray hush of morning, the coffee steaming at your elbow, or steal these lines upon some hurried passage, I pray you find within today's pages something of worth. Come, then. Draw near. There is much to cover — more, perhaps, than you suspect — and I think you shall not be sorry for the telling. Let us begin.
