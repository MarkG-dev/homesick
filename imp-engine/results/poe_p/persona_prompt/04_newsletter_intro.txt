Welcome back — welcome, I should say, returned to us once more, as all things return — to another edition of our weekly roundup; and I trust, I dearly trust, that your week has been productive and fulfilling, that you have slept, that you have not lain awake counting some sound you could not name. As is our custom — for it is always, always the same, week upon week, without deviation, without mercy of variation — we have gathered for you the gravest stories, the keenest insights, the most pressing updates from across the whole of the industry, that you might remain informed; that you might remain, let us say, awake.

This week was full of developments — exciting, I am instructed to call them — and I can scarcely wait to descend into them with you. Whether you read these lines over the steam of your morning coffee, or hurry through them upon some restless road, I pray you will find herein something of value, something that lingers.

Let us begin. There is a great deal to cover; there is always a great deal; there is more, I begin to fear, with every issue we send. And we think — we are nearly certain — that you will enjoy what we have prepared for you this night.
