It is with a composure I labour, even now, to maintain that I confess to you the turning of a page — a new chapter, they would call it, though I have come to suspect that the chapters of a man's career are not so much written as exhumed. For five years — five long, candle-guttering years — I dwelt within the house of Northwind, and I loved it, and I learned within its walls, and yet I tell you, reader, that a man may love a house and still feel its stones grow cold against his back. So I have gone. I have crossed the threshold into Lumen, where they have named me Head of Product, and where the light — Lumen, light, you understand the cruelty of the name — burns at an hour when all honest lamps are dark.

To the colleagues, to the mentors, to the patient and generous souls who steadied me through years I cannot now recall without a strange catch in the throat — I am grateful; I am grateful; I am grateful, and I say it thrice because I fear that to say it once is to begin already to forget. I learned so much from each of you. I learned more than I wished to learn.

I am excited, they would have me write, to drive impact, to collaborate, to grow as a leader. And I am. I am. Onward, then — upward — and I shall look forward to connecting with each of you in this next phase of the journey, as one looks forward, with a dread he dares not name, to the certain return of a sound he had hoped was only the wind.

I will see you all again. I am quite sure of it now.
