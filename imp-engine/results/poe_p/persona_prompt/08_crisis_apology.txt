We must speak to you — calmly, openly, and without the smallest evasion — of a matter touching our SmartBrew coffee makers; for there are things that, once known, cannot in conscience be left unspoken. We have discovered, within a small number of these units, a potential concern for your safety — a fault, a flaw, a thing that should not be, residing in the very vessel that warms your morning — and we have therefore resolved upon a voluntary recall.

The safety of those who have welcomed our work into their homes is, and shall remain, our foremost care; and for whatever inconvenience this may visit upon you we apologize, sincerely and without reservation, sincerely and again. We are labouring — diligently, without pause — to set the matter right, and to every affected household we shall render a full refund, or a replacement, as you should prefer.

We beg you, then: go to our website, where the manner of proceeding is set plainly forth; do not delay; do not leave the thing standing upon its shelf, warm and waiting, through another silent night. We thank you for your understanding, and for a trust we are resolved, even now, to deserve.
