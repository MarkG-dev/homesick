Greetings — and I trust, I sincerely trust, that this letter finds you well, and finds you, moreover, alone, for what I have to say is meant for you and no other. My name is Alex. I write to you because there has taken shape in my mind — slowly, and then with a fixed and unshakeable certainty — the conviction that between our two companies there waits an opportunity; a collaboration; a thing that wishes to be brought into being.

We have followed your work for some time. For some time, I say — longer, perhaps, than you would think comfortable to learn — and we have watched, and we have admired, and we have grown ever more impressed by what you have built there in the quiet. A partnership between us, I am persuaded, would create a real and lasting value for both our audiences; would bind them, each to each, in something neither could easily undo.

Would you, then, consent to a brief call in the coming week — a single hour, no more — that we might explore these ideas together? I should dearly love to learn your goals, and to tell you, in turn, how we might help. I await your reply. I shall be awaiting it. I do not think I shall be able to do otherwise.
