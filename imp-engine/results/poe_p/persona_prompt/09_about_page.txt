At Brightpath we hold to a single conviction — that technology ought to render life simpler, and not more intricate, not more tangled, not more crowded with the small dreadful complications that multiply in the dark while we sleep. We were founded in the year 2021, and we are a company of engineers, of designers, and of dreamers — yes, dreamers, and I will not retract the word — bound together upon one mission: to place into the hands of small businesses tools that simply, faithfully, uncannily work.

We prize transparency; we prize collaboration; we prize, above all and before all, the placing of our customers first in every act we undertake. From the very first day — and I remember that first day with a vividness I cannot entirely explain — our purpose has been to build products that people do not merely use but love, truly love, with the kind of love that does not let a thing go.

We are only beginning. We are barely, scarcely begun. And we are glad — gladder than you can know — to have you here beside us, for all the long journey that is yet to come.
