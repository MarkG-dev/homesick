We must speak to you of a grave thing — a matter touching our SmartBrew coffee makers — and we will not hide our face from it. Upon close and unflinching inspection, we have found a possible safety concern in a small number of units. And so we have resolved, without delay, to issue a voluntary recall. What is dearer to us than the safety of those we serve? Nothing — nothing beneath this sky. For whatever fear or inconvenience this may bring upon you, we are sincerely, heavily sorry.

We labor now, by day and by night, to set the matter right. Every affected customer shall receive a full refund — or a replacement — without quarrel or delay.

Visit our website, we beg you, for the plain instructions on how to proceed. Do not wait. We thank you for your understanding, and for the trust you have placed in us — a trust we mean to keep.
