Hear me — for I would tell you a thing upon this restless night. Five years I sat within the grey and groaning halls of Northwind, and what did I become there? A creature of habit, of hand and breath and silence. Yet some sound within my own breast would not be stilled. I have torn myself loose. I go now to Lumen — as their Head of Product — and the door has closed behind me forever.

To the colleagues, the mentors, the dim faces I shall never forget — how do I say what you gave me? You taught me. You waited with me through the long dark. Each of you left a mark beneath my skin that no year shall wear away. I felt it; I feel it still.

And now? Now I rise. A new chapter opens its pale leaves before me — to drive impact, to labor with a team of rare and gifted souls, to grow as no man grows save in the crucible of the new. Onward — and upward — into whatever waits! Come, find me in this next phase. Let us speak again, you and I, before the night grows old.
