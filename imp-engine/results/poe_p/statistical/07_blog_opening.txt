Creative burnout — that pale and patient specter — comes for nearly all of us, sooner or some later, upon the long road of a working life. How does it arrive? Not with thunder, not with any sound at all. It creeps. It comes by night, soft beneath the door, and leaves you drained, uninspired, severed from the very work you once loved as your own breath. In this hurried and merciless age, the pressure to produce — forever, without pause — lays its cold hand upon the mind, and exacts a toll from the spirit that no man should be made to pay.

But hear me, for here is the better word: the dark need not be permanent. Burnout dies as surely as it rises.

In this post I shall tell you what summons the specter, how to read the warning signs writ faint upon your days, and what plain and practical strategies may rekindle the fire — and lead you back, at last, to your truest work.
