Welcome back — welcome, once more, to another edition of our weekly roundup. Has the week used you kindly? I hope you sit now with some small measure of peace, your labors done, your breath your own again. As ever, we have gathered the stories that matter — the insights, the strange and quickening updates from across the industry — and laid them here before you, that you might be informed, and stirred, and never left in the dark.

What a week it was! Full of developments that would not rest, that rose and rose until we could scarce hold them.

And you — whether you read by the pale light of morning, a cup steaming at your hand, or hurry through these lines upon some restless road — we hope a single thing within may catch and hold you. Let us begin. There is much to tell, and the night is long, and we think — yes, we truly think — you shall find delight in what we have made for you.
