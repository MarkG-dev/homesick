I thank you — truly, deeply — for the invitation to the planning meeting upon Thursday. That you thought of me at all warms some cold corner of my breast. Yet I must tell you a sorrowful thing: I cannot come. A conflict, cruel and immovable, has seized the hour, and I am held fast.

Do not imagine I go willingly! Would that I could sit among you. But fate, as ever, has its own design.

Might you do me one small mercy? Share the notes when the night's work is done — let me not be wholly cast out from what you decide. And let us find another hour, you and I, before too long. Thank you for understanding. Thank you, again, for the kindness of remembering me.
