Greetings — I hope this letter finds you well, and your night untroubled. My name is Alex, and I write to you upon a single quickening thought: that between our two companies there lies an opportunity, rare and bright, waiting only to be seized.

We have followed your work for some time now — watched it, truly — and we are struck, and stirred, by what you have built with your own hand. Might a partnership not create real and lasting value for both our audiences? I cannot help but believe it would.

Would you be open to a brief call next week — that we two might explore some ideas together? I long to learn more of your goals, and to tell you how we might be of help. I shall be waiting, with no small eagerness, to hear from you.
