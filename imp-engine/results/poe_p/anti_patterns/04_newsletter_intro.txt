Welcome — welcome back, you who return again as the week itself returns, as all things in their season are compelled to return — to this our weekly gathering of all that has passed. I trust the days behind you bore some fruit; mine bore only the gathering, the ceaseless gathering, for I cannot cease. There is a thing I do, reader, that I cannot leave undone: I go out into the great murmuring industry of the age and I bring back the stories — the chief tidings, the insights, the small cold updates — and I lay them here before you as one lays offerings upon a stone.

This week was thick with developments, thick and stirring and not a little strange, and I have set them in order though the ordering near unmade me. Whether you read in the gray hush of morning with the coffee steaming at your hand, or whether you read in motion, snatching the words between one place and the next — you will find something here. You always do. You always will. Come, then — come closer — for there is much to cover, more than the page can comfortably hold, and once we begin we shall not, you and I, be able to stop until the very end.
