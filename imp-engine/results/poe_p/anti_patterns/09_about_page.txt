Here, at Brightpath, we hold to a single conviction — held close, held fast, held against all the clamoring complication of the age — that the engines of technology ought to render life simpler, and not wind it into ever-tightening coils of confusion. In the year 2021 were we founded, a small band of the fevered and the devoted: engineers, designers, dreamers who could not sleep for the dreaming, bound by one mission — to arm the small and striving business with tools that simply, faithfully, unfailingly work.

We prize transparency, and collaboration, and the placing of the one who trusts us before all else — in every deed, in every hour, in every line we set down. From the first day, reader — and you are part of this now, drawn in as surely as if you had signed your name — our single aim has been to build the things that men and women do not merely use but love to use, love past reason, love past leaving. We are but barely begun. We have scarcely commenced. And we mean to have you with us, close beside us, for all the long strange road that yet unwinds ahead.
