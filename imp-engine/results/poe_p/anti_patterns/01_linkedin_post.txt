Hearken, for I would have you know what I have done — what I could not, in the end, refrain from doing. For five long years I dwelt within the walls of Northwind, and those walls knew me, and I knew them; their corridors had learned the very cadence of my tread. Yet there came a whisper — faint at first, a single uncanny syllable beneath the floorboards of my contentment — and that whisper was a name, and the name was Lumen. I tell you I heard it by day. I heard it by night. I hear it now. And I have answered: I go to Lumen as their Head of Product, and there is no unhearing what has been heard.

Do not imagine I leave unmoved. To the mentors who shaped me, to the colleagues whose patient hands steadied mine — I am grateful, grateful past the poor compass of these words; from each of you I learned, and what is learned cannot be unlearned, cannot be undone, cannot be laid down. Picture me, reader — for you are bound in this with me — standing upon a threshold I did not choose so much as was summoned toward, the new light burning cold and steady before me. I step through. The door does not close behind me. It never closes. And the whisper, even now, calls onward — onward — onward.
