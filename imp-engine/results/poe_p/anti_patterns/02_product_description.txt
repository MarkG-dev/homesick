Attend me, for I would tell you of the Aura Pro, and of the silence — that monstrous, perfect silence — which it laid upon my ears. I had thought I knew quietude. I knew nothing. For when I drew these, our most advanced of noise-canceling instruments, down upon my skull, the clamor of the living world withdrew by slow and intolerable degrees — the traffic first, then the voices, then the small insistences of the room — until there remained only the music, and beneath the music, my own pulse, vast and near and terrible in the new-made dark.

Industry-leading is the cancellation, they say; I say it cancels more than sound. Thirty hours — thirty unbroken hours — will the battery sustain this immersion, and the cushions press soft as grave-loam against the temple, and the sound arrives crystalline, intimate, closer than thought, closer than the reader who leans now toward this very page. Whether you labor, whether you wander, whether you would merely rest — you will hear sound as it was meant to be heard. You will hear it as I heard it. And once you have heard so deeply, so wholly, so finally, you will never again endure the ordinary noise of the world; you will only crave to put them on once more.
