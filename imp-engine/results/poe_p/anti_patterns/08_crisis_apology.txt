I must speak to you now of a thing we have found — a thing concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers, which sit even now upon how many darkened counters, in how many sleeping houses — and I would not have you learn it gently, for it does not deserve gentleness. In a small number of these units there dwells a fault, a flaw, a hidden defect of safety we have traced with our own hands; and so we issue, of our own will, a voluntary recall. The safety of those who trusted us is the first and last of our concerns, and we are sorry — sorry past the reach of these poor words — for the disquiet this must bring into your home.

Hear, then, what you must do, for this is the marrow of the matter and I will not let it pass unspoken. Go to our website. There you shall find the full instruction, the plain and ordered means by which to proceed, and to every soul afflicted we shall render a full refund or a full replacement — every one, without exception, without delay. We labor at the remedy without rest, without pause, without surcease. Do not set this aside. Do not let it wait upon the morning. I thank you for your trust, reader, which we mean yet to deserve — but first, tonight, go and see to the thing upon your counter.
