We must speak plainly about the SmartBrew coffee makers. In a small number of them we have found a fault that touches on safety. We are calling them all back, of our own will. The safety of those who bought them comes before all else with us. We are sorry for the trouble this brings into a kitchen. We are at the work of putting it right. Every customer the fault has reached will have the whole of the money back, or a sound machine in its place. The way of going about it is set out on our website. We thank you for your understanding, and for the trust you have placed in us.
