Creative burnout comes to most of us once, somewhere in the working years. It comes slowly. A man sits down at the desk he sat at gladly a year ago and finds the page white before him and finds he has nothing to set on it. It was useless. He couldn't work. The world is quick now and asks always for more, and the asking tells on the mind and on the making. And yet the thing need not be for life. In what follows we will set down what brings the burnout on, how a man may know the signs of it in himself, and the plain steps by which he may come back to the work and do it well again.
