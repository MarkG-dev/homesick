Here is the Aura Pro, the best of the headphones we have made. A man presses the small switch at the side and the noise of the carriage goes out of his ears; the talk in the next seat goes out; the long laboured drone of the engine goes down and down until there is the music and nothing else in the world. They hold thirty hours before they ask for the wall. The cushions take the weight from the ears and he forgets he wears them. Each note comes over exact and clean and where it was set. At the desk, on the train, in the chair when the lamp is the last thing burning, he hears the sound as it was meant to be heard, and the room about him perfectly silent.
