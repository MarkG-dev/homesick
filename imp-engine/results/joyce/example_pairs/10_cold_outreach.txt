I hope this finds you well. My name is Alex, and I write because there is, I think, a good opening for our two houses to work as one. We have followed what you have built this good while, and it is plainly no small thing. A partnership, as it looks to us, would be worth real money to both your people and ours. Would you give a short call next week, that we might turn a few notions over together? I should be glad to hear what you are working toward, and to set out plainly where we might be of use. I look forward to your word.
