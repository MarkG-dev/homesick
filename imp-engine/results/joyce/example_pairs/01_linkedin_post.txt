A new chapter, he wrote, and read the line over and over again. Five years he had given to Northwind. Now Lumen had made him their Head of Product, and a dull gladness awoke within him; for was this not the step he had wanted? He was grateful to the men who had taught him. He had learned so much from each of them, more than he could say. There would be impact to drive there, and a talented team about him, and the leader he meant to become at last. Onward and upward, old chap. He looked forward to connecting with everyone in this next phase of the journey, and he sat with the screen lit before him and his tea going cold, looking forward.
