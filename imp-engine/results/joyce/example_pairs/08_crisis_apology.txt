We must speak plainly about the SmartBrew coffee makers. In a small number of them we have found a fault that touches on safety, and so we are calling them all back, of our own will. The safety of those who bought them stands before all else with us, and we are sorry, truly sorry, for the trouble it brings into a kitchen. We are hard at the work of putting it right. Every customer the fault has reached will have the whole of the money back, or a sound machine in its place. The way of going about it is set out on our website. We thank you for your understanding, and for the trust you have not yet had cause to take from us.
