Welcome back to the weekly roundup. We hope the week has used you kindly. As always we have gone out and gathered the matters worth the keeping, the stories and the small hard facts and the word from across the trade, and set them down here in order. There was a good deal astir this week, and we are glad to go through it at your side. Whether you read it over the morning cup at the kitchen table, or fold it small and read it standing on the tram, we hope you light on the one thing you wanted. Let us begin. There is a great deal to it, and we think it will sit well with you.
