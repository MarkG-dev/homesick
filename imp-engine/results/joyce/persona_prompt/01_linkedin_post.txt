Five years he had given to Northwind, going down each morning by the same quay, past the same shuttered window, to the same desk. Now Lumen had taken him for their Head of Product, and a new chapter was begun. He owed a great deal to the men who had taught him there; he had learned so much from each of them, he wrote, and the writing of it warmed him. There would be impact to drive at Lumen, and a clever team to sit among, and the leader he had always meant to be made of him at last. Onward and upward. He looked forward to connecting with everyone in this next phase of the journey. The cursor stood blinking at the line's end, and he with it, looking forward, on the same chair, by the same window.
