Here is the Aura Pro, the best of the headphones we have made. They are black, with the soft band for the head, two cushions for the ears, one small switch at the side. A man presses the switch and the city is taken from him: the trams on the quay, the talk in the next seat, the long laboured drone of the carriage on the line out to Bray, all of it drawn down and away, until there is the music, and the man alone with it. They hold thirty hours. The cushions take the weight from the ears so that he forgets he wears them at all. Each note comes over exact, set down just where it was meant. At the desk, on the train, in the chair by the window when the lamp is the last light burning in the house, he hears the thing as it was meant to be heard, and the room about him perfectly silent.
