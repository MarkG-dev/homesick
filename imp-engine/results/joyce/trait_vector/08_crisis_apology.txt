We must speak plainly about the SmartBrew coffee makers. In a small number of them we have found a fault that touches on safety, and so we are calling them all back, of our own accord. The safety of those who bought them stands before everything else with us, and we are sorry, truly sorry, for the trouble this carries into a kitchen. We are hard at the work of setting it right. Every customer the fault has reached will have the whole of the money back, or a sound machine in its place. How to go about it is laid out on our website. We thank you for your patience, and for a trust we mean to keep deserving.
