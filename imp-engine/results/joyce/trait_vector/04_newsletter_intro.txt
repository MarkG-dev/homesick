Welcome back to the weekly roundup. We hope the week has been a full one, and a fair one. As always we have gone out and gathered the things that matter: the stories, the small hard facts, the word from across the trade, and laid them down in order for you. The week was a busy one, with a good deal stirring, and we are glad to go through it at your side. Whether you take it with the morning cup at the table, or fold it small and read it standing on the tram, we hope you come on the one thing you were after. Let us begin, then. There is much to get through, and we think it will repay the reading.
