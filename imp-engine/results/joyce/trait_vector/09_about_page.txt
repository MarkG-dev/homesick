At Brightpath we hold that a tool ought to make the day simpler and not the harder. We began in 2021, a small lot of engineers and designers and one or two who did the dreaming, set on putting plain good tools into the hands of the small trader, the kind that do the work and give no trouble. We keep three things close: that the books be open, that the work be shared, and that the customer come first in every dealing. From the first morning there was the one aim, to build a thing people are glad to take up. We are only at the start of the road, and glad to have you walking it with us.
