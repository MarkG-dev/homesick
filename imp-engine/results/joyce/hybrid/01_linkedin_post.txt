For five years he had gone down to Northwind over Grattan Bridge, and the river had run its one colour under him, morning and evening, in the wet and in the dry. Now the thing was done. Lumen had taken him for their Head of Product, and a dull gladness awoke within him; for was this not the new chapter he had promised himself? He was grateful to the men who had taught him, the mentors, the colleagues. He had learned so much from each of them, he wrote, and read the line over again. There would be impact to drive at Lumen, and a talented team to collaborate with, and the leader still to be made of him. Onward and upward, old chap. He looked forward to connecting with everyone in this next phase of the journey; and he sat a while at the lit screen with his tea gone cold beside him, looking forward.
