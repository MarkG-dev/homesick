Welcome back to the weekly roundup. We hope the week has dealt fairly with you. As always we have gone out and gathered the matters worth the keeping, the stories and the small hard facts and the word going round the trade, and set them down here in their order. There was a good deal astir this week, and we are glad to go through it at your elbow. Whether you read it over the morning cup at the kitchen table, or fold it small and read it standing on the tram up the quays, we hope you light on the one thing you wanted. Let us begin, then. There is a great deal to it, and we think it will sit well with you.
