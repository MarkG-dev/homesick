Here is the Aura Pro, the best of the headphones we have made. They are black, with a soft band for the crown of the head, two cushions for the ears, and one small switch at the side. A man presses the switch and the noise of the world is taken from him: the trams grinding up Dame Street, the talk in the next seat, the long laboured drone of the carriage on the line to Bray, all drawn down and away until there is the music and nothing else. They hold thirty hours before the wall is wanted. The cushions take the weight from the ears, and he forgets he wears them. Each note comes over exact and clean, set down just where it was meant. At the desk, on the train, in the chair by the window when the lamp is the last light in the house, he hears the thing as it was meant to be heard, and the room about him perfectly silent.
