Here is the Aura Pro, the best of the headphones we have yet made. They are black, with a soft band for the crown of the head and two cushions for the ears and one small switch at the side. Press the switch and the noise of the world is taken from you: the trams grinding up Dame Street, the talk in the next seat, the long laboured drone of the carriage along the line to Bray, all of it drawn down and away until there is the music and nothing besides. They hold thirty hours before the wall is wanted. They sit easy and you forget the weight of them. Every note comes over clean and exact, set down just where it was meant. At the desk, on the train, in the chair by the window when the lamp is the last light in the house, you will hear the thing as it was meant to be heard, and the room about you perfectly silent.
