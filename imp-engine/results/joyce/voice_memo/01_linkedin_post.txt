For five years he had gone down to Northwind, over the bridge at Capel Street and along the quay, in the wet and in the dry. He was leaving it now. Lumen had taken him for their Head of Product, and there was no denying it was a step. He was grateful to the men who had stood by him there, the mentors, the colleagues; he had learned so much from each of them, he said, and meant it as far as a man means such a thing. There would be impact to drive at Lumen, and a clever team to collaborate with, and the growing he had always promised himself. Onward and upward. He looked forward to connecting with them all in this next phase of the journey, and he stood a moment at the window over the Liffey, looking forward.
