Welcome back to the weekly roundup. We hope the week has gone well with you. As always we have gone out and gathered the little matters worth the keeping; the stories, and the small hard facts, and the word from across the trade, all set down here in order. There was a good deal astir this week. We are glad to go through it with you. Whether you read it over the morning cup at the table, or fold it small and read it standing on the tram, we hope you light on the one thing you wanted. Let us begin. There is much to it, and we think it will sit well with you.
