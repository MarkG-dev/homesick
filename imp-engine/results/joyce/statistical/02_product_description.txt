Here is the Aura Pro, the best of all the headphones we have made. A man presses the little switch at the side and the noise of the world goes down; the carriage, the talk in the next seat, the long drone of the engine, all of it goes down and down until there is the music and nothing more. They would hold thirty hours before the wall is wanted. The cushions take the weight from the ears, and he forgets them. Each note comes over exact, and clean, and where it was meant to be. At the desk, on the train, in the room when the lamp is the one light left, he hears the sound as it was meant to be heard; and all about him is still, and still again.
