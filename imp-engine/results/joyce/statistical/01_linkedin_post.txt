For five years he had gone to Northwind; now he would go to Lumen, who had made him their Head of Product. He owed much to the men who had taught him there. "I learned so much from each of you," he said, and he believed it. There would be impact to drive at Lumen, and a team to work among, and a leader to be made of him in time. One little step, and then another. Onward and upward, old chap. He looked forward to connecting with all of them in this next phase of the journey; and he stood a while at the window, looking down at the street, looking forward.
