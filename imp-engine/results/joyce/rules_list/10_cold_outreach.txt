I hope this finds you well. My name is Alex, and I am writing because there is, I think, a good chance for our two houses to work as one. We have watched what you have made for some while now, and it is no small thing. A partnership, it seems to us, would be worth real money to both your people and ours. Would you sit for a short call next week, that we might turn the idea over together? I should like to hear what you are after, and to set out, plainly, where we might be of use. I look forward to your word.
