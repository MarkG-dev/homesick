Creative burnout comes to most of us once, somewhere in the working years. It comes slowly. A man sits at the desk he sat at gladly enough a year ago, and finds the page in front of him, and finds he has nothing, and is drained and far off from the work he loved. The world is quick now and asks for more, always more, and the asking tells on the mind and on the making. It is useless. He cannot write. But the thing need not be for life. In what follows we will set down what brings the burnout on, how a man may know the signs of it in himself, and what plain steps he may take to come back to the work, and to do it well again.
