For five years he had crossed Grattan Bridge each morning to Northwind, and each morning the river had run the same colour under him. Now the thing was done. He was to be Head of Product at Lumen, and he had said as much to the men who had taught him, the ones he was grateful to, deeply grateful, he told them, learning so much from each. There would be impact to drive there. There would be a talented team, and the growing of himself, the leading. Onward and upward, he thought, the way one thinks a thing one has read. He looked forward to connecting with everyone in this next phase of the journey. The river ran the same colour. Five years. He had learned so much.
