The Aura Pro headphones are our most advanced yet. They sit over the ears in black, with a small switch for the noise-cancelling, which is the best to be had; and when the switch is pressed the trams on the quay and the man on the stair and the city itself go out, one by one, until there is only the music. The battery holds thirty hours. The cushions are soft against the head. The sound is exact, every note where it was set down. You may wear them at your desk, or on the train to Bray, or in the chair by the window when the light has gone, and hear the thing as it was meant to be heard, and nothing else, and no one.
