At Brightpath we hold that a tool should make the day simpler and not harder. We began in 2021, a handful of engineers and designers and a few who only dreamed, set on putting good plain tools into the hands of the small trader, the kind that do the thing and do not balk. We keep faith with three matters: that the books be open, that the work be done together, and that the customer come first in every dealing. From the first morning the aim was the one aim, to build the thing people are glad to use. We are only at the start of it, and glad to have you walking the road with us.
