Another week had come and gone, the way weeks do — without asking permission, without leaving anything behind worth keeping. The city had been busy doing what it always does, which is chew through time and spit out news, and there was plenty of it this week. Good news, bad news, the kind of news that sits in your chest like a wet overcoat and won't dry out no matter how long you leave it. We found the stories that mattered, the ones that didn't know they mattered yet, and the ones that were pretending hard not to. It's all in here. Pour something warm, or something stronger — the week earned it — and let's get into it.
