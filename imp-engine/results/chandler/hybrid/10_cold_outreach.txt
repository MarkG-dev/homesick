Subject: A proposition — the kind that doesn't require a lawyer to read first

My name is Alex. I'll skip the part where I tell you this email finds you well, because emails don't find people — they arrive, uninvited, the way rain arrives on a day you forgot your coat.

I've been watching what you've built for a while now. Long enough to mean it when I say it's the real thing — not the kind of real that comes with a press release and a funding announcement, but the kind that holds up when you lean on it.

Here's the proposition: I think our two outfits working together could do something neither of us does alone. Value for your audience, value for mine, and the particular satisfaction of a thing that actually makes sense — which is rarer than it sounds, rarer than a dry afternoon in this city, rarer than an honest invoice.

I'm not asking for much. A call. Next week, whenever the calendar has a gap that isn't already full of someone else's emergency. Twenty minutes to lay the cards on the table and see if the hands are worth playing.

I'd want to hear what you're chasing. You'd hear what I'm carrying. We'd find out if those two things fit.

Let me know.

Alex
