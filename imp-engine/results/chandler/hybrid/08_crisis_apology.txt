There is a problem with some of our SmartBrew coffee makers. Not a rumor of a problem, not a theoretical possibility of a problem — a real one, the kind that doesn't go away if you look at it sideways and hope for the best.

We found it ourselves. We are telling you now. A small number of units came off the line carrying a safety defect the way a bad neighborhood carries trouble — quietly, until it isn't quiet anymore. We've pulled them. Voluntary recall, full stop, no fine print about what "voluntary" costs you.

Every affected customer gets a refund or a replacement. Both. Your choice. The machine goes back to us, and something that works comes back to you, or the money does. We are not in the business of making that complicated, the way some companies make it complicated, the way a locked door makes things complicated when there's smoke in the hallway.

The weather outside doesn't care about any of this. Neither does the morning. You came to us because you wanted a decent cup of coffee to start the day, and we handed you something we shouldn't have. That's on us, and it sits on us the way these things ought to sit — heavy, and in the right place.

Visit our website. Find out if your unit is affected. We'll handle the rest.
