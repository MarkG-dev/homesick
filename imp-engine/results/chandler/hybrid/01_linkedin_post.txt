Five years at Northwind. That's a long time to learn things, and I learned them — the kind of lessons that don't show up in a performance review but cost you something anyway.

Now I'm joining Lumen as Head of Product. The city doesn't care, the calendar didn't flinch, and Monday will arrive on schedule regardless. But I care, the way a man cares about a door that finally opens after he's been knocking long enough to bruise his knuckles.

To the colleagues and mentors at Northwind who gave me their time, their honesty, and occasionally their patience — I owe you the kind of debt that doesn't clear with a LinkedIn mention, but consider this a down payment.

What's next is harder work, a talented team I haven't had the pleasure of disappointing yet, and the particular kind of hope that knows better than to get too comfortable. The kind that keeps its coat on.

Looking forward to what comes next. Whatever that turns out to be.
