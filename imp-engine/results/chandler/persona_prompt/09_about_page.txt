Brightpath started in 2021 with a simple idea that turned out to be almost radical: technology ought to work for the people using it, not the other way around. We are engineers and designers and a few stubborn idealists who watched small businesses drown in software that was complicated by design, expensive by habit, and useful to everyone except the person paying for it. We decided that was a racket worth disrupting. We build tools that do what they promise, charge what they should, and get out of the way — because a business owner at midnight trying to make payroll does not need a learning curve, she needs an answer. Transparency is not a value we print on a wall; it is the only way we know how to operate without losing sleep. We are still early, still building, still paying attention. That last part is the whole job.
