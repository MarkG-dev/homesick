There is a problem with some of our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we are not going to dress it up in the kind of language that makes a bad thing sound like a scheduling conflict.

We found a safety defect in a number of units. We found it, we sat with it, and we decided the only move left to a company that wants to sleep at night is a voluntary recall. No one got hurt. We would like to keep it that way.

We are sorry. Not the kind of sorry that comes with a lawyer's fingerprints all over it — the real kind, the kind that knows it put something dangerous into your kitchen and is not going to pretend otherwise. You bought our product because you trusted it to do one simple thing without burning your house down. That trust has weight. We felt it when we made the call.

Here is what happens next. If your unit is affected, you get a full refund or a replacement — your choice, no argument, no fine print designed to tire you out. Go to our website, find the recall page, follow the steps. It will not take long. We made sure of that, because you have already done enough.

The city runs on coffee and the belief that the next cup will be better than the last one. We intend to earn that belief back. One honest cup at a time.
