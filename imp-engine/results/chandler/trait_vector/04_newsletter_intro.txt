This week broke me open a little. Not in the bad way — in the way a good storm breaks open a humid afternoon, leaving everything sharper, cooler, smelling like wet concrete and possibility.

I've been doing this long enough to know that most weeks blur. They fold into each other like pages stuck together, unremarkable, survivable. This week did not blur. This week had *teeth.*

So here we are again — you with your coffee going lukewarm, me with mine doing the same, both of us pretending we'll drink it before it's fully cold. We won't. We never do. But we keep pouring it anyway, and I think that's actually the whole metaphor for why you're here.

You want the good stuff. The signal in the noise. The one sentence that makes you set the cup down and stare at the middle distance for a second.

I've got it. Several of them, actually.

This edition is dense with things that matter — stories that pushed back against easy conclusions, ideas that felt obvious only *after* someone said them out loud, and at least one development that made me laugh before it made me nervous. That order feels important.

Let's go. The lukewarm coffee can wait a little longer.
