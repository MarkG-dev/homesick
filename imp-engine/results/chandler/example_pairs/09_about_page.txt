Brightpath started in 2021 with a simple observation: technology had developed a habit of creating the exact problems it was supposed to solve, and small businesses were the ones paying for it. So we built tools that work the way tools are supposed to work, which is quietly and without drama and without requiring a manual nobody was going to read anyway. We're engineers and designers and a few people who don't fit a job title neatly, and what we have in common is that we've seen enough complicated software to know that simple is harder to build and worth the trouble. We believe in being straight with our customers, which is either a value or just a policy depending on how cynical you are, but either way we mean it. We're still early in this, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. What we will tell you is that we're glad you're here, and we intend to make that worth something.
