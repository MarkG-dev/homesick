The morning came in gray over Van Nuys, the kind of gray that sits on the rooftops like a debt you stopped counting. We need to tell you something about the SmartBrew coffee maker.

We found a defect. A heating element in a limited run of units that had already forgotten what it was supposed to do. It is a safety risk. We are not going to dress that up in the kind of language that costs nothing and means less.

We are recalling the affected units. Full refunds. Full replacements. The machine on your counter will be made right, or it will be made gone, whichever you want. Somewhere in the background of all this, a phone is ringing in our customer service office on Sepulveda, and someone will answer it.

Los Angeles does not care about your morning coffee. We do. That is the difference between a city and a company that still has something to lose.

Go to our website. Find your serial number. Tell us what you need.

We are sorry. That is not a policy statement. It is the only honest thing left to say.
