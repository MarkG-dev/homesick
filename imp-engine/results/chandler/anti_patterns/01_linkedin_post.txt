Five years at Northwind. That is the kind of run that leaves marks on you, the kind where you learn what you actually know versus what you thought you knew when you walked in.

I am joining Lumen as Head of Product.

The colleagues and mentors who carried me through the hard quarters deserve more than a tag in a post. They know who they are. The gratitude is real and it does not need decoration.

Lumen is the kind of challenge that sits across the table from you and does not blink first. A new team, a new problem set, a chance to build something that holds weight. Los Angeles has a way of indifferent patience with the people who think they are ready. I suppose I will find out.

Grateful for what was. Focused on what comes next.
