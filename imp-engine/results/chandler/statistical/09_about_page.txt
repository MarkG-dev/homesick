Brightpath started in 2021. A man could look at a lot of software out there and find nothing that worked the way small businesses needed it to work. The engineers and designers who built this company wanted something different. They wanted tools that did the job without getting in the way. "It should just work," one of them said, and that was the kind of thing the whole team believed. Transparency mattered. So did the people across the table, the customers who put their trust in what Brightpath was building. The goal from day one was to find a better way and build products people could actually use. There is still a long way to go, and the team is glad to have you along for it.
