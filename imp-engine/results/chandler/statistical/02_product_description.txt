The Aura Pro headphones block out the world the way a closed door blocks out rain. The noise cancellation is the best there is, and you put them on and the office disappears, the men talking across the room disappear, the window and whatever is going on outside the window disappear. What you find is just the music. The battery runs thirty hours, which is a long way to go before you have to think about a check. The fit is good and the sound is the kind of sound that makes you feel like the man who made the music made it for you, somewhere quiet, a long time ago. You could be working or you could be on a train watching things go by or you could be doing nothing at all. The Aura Pro does not care. It just gives you the sound the way it was always meant to come to you.
