There was a problem with some of the SmartBrew coffee makers we put out, and we wanted to say something about it the right way. We found a safety issue with a number of the units. We looked at what we had and decided the right thing to do was pull them back, a voluntary recall, no question about it. Some men and women who trusted us with something as simple as their morning coffee deserved better than that, and we know it. We are sorry. Anyone who bought one of the affected units will get a full refund or a replacement, whichever way works best for them. You can find out how to check if your unit is one of them, and what to do from there, on our website. We wanted nothing more than to get this right from the start, and we broke that somewhere along the way. We are working to make sure it never happens again.
