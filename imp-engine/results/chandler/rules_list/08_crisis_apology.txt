We put a defective product in your home, on your counter, next to your coffee cup and your morning and your trust. That is the fact. Some SmartBrew units carry a safety defect — a flaw that sits there like a cracked sidewalk waiting for the right shoe, patient, indifferent, ready to matter at the worst possible moment.

We are recalling them. All affected units. Voluntarily, which is a word that means we knew, and we moved, and we are telling you now rather than later — and later has a way of arriving like a parking-lot puddle under a tire, ugly and sudden and wider than it looked.

Every affected customer receives a full refund or a replacement. Not a coupon. Not a gesture. A refund or a replacement, the kind that means we understand the difference between an apology and an excuse dressed up in one.

We are sorry. Not the sorry that floats free and means nothing, the way a form letter means nothing, the way a signature printed in blue ink means nothing. The sorry that costs something. The sorry that knows it should.

Visit our website. The process is there. It is straightforward. We owe you that much, and we owe you more than that, and we are aware of the gap between those two things.
