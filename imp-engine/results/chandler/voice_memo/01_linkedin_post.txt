Five years at Northwind. I learned things there I didn't ask to learn, the way you learn things in any city that never quite sleeps and never quite wakes up. Good people. A few great ones. The kind of colleagues who tell you the truth when the truth is the last thing you want delivered.

Now it's Lumen. Head of Product. The title sits on the page like a new suit that hasn't been broken in yet — clean lines, unfamiliar weight.

I'm grateful to everyone who pushed me, corrected me, and occasionally tolerated me. You know who you are. So do I.

What comes next is the part I can't write yet. But I've always done better with a blank page than a finished one. Onward — not upward, necessarily, just forward, the way a man walks into a room when he doesn't know what's in it but figures he's already come this far.

If you're at Lumen, or you work in product, or you just want to talk — I'm easy to find. I'm the new guy. For now.
