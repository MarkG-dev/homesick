It started with a coffee maker. It usually starts with something small.

We'd been watching the SmartBrew units the way you watch a bad neighborhood — hoping nothing happens, knowing something will. Something did. A defect, buried in a handful of machines like a secret nobody wanted to keep, the kind of flaw that sits quiet until it doesn't. A potential safety hazard. Those two words together have a way of cutting through the corporate fog faster than anything else I know.

So we're pulling them back. A voluntary recall, which is the industry's way of saying we found the problem before the problem found you, and we'd like to keep it that way.

Every affected customer gets a full refund or a replacement — no argument, no fine print, no runaround. Just the thing you're owed, handed over clean. Visit our website for the details on how to proceed. It won't take long. We made sure of that much.

We're sorry. Not the kind of sorry that comes in a press release and means nothing, the way a handshake means nothing when the hand is already reaching for your wallet. The kind of sorry that costs something. You trusted us with your mornings, which is about as personal as trust gets. We didn't hold up our end of that deal, and we know it.

We're fixing it. That's all we've got left to offer, and we're offering it.
