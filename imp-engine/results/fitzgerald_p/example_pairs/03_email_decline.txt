Thank you for the invitation to Thursday's meeting; it was the kind of thinking-of-me that warms a person for a week. But an engagement already promised has the first claim on the hour, and I must, with real regret, send my chair back empty. Send the notes along when it is done — I should like to stay near the outcome, the way one stays near a lit window from the street. And let us find some quieter evening soon, with no clock standing between us. A meeting missed is a small debit against a long and friendly account. I mean to keep that one well in the black.
