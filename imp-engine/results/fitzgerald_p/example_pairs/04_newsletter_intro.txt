Welcome back to the warm small room of this letter, whoever you are, reading over a coffee gone gold in the morning window or against the cold rail of an evening platform with the week sliding away beneath you. Another seven days have spent themselves freely, magnificently, on nothing in particular, and we went out into the bright noise of them and gathered what was worth the keeping. There was a great deal this week; we have laid it out the way a good hostess lays a long table, making each story feel like the one we had been waiting all evening to set before you — a generous fiction, but a true enough one. So come in, and let us begin. The weeks one remembers are rarer than we admit, and this was one of them.
