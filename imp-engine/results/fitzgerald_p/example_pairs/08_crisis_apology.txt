We owe you the plain truth tonight about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we mean to pay it in full. We have found in a small number of units a possible danger, and rather than gamble with the bright ordinary mornings of the people who let us into their kitchens, we are calling every machine home. Trust is a kind of credit you extended to us against a future you assumed would pay; we will not let that account come up short. We are sorry — sorry the deep, uncomfortable way one is sorry for dimming a thing meant to give warmth. Every affected customer will have a full refund or a new machine, and our website will show the way through. We mean to be worth the loan by the next morning's pot.
