Your invitation to Thursday's meeting reached me on one of those bright, overcommitted mornings when a man's calendar reads like a guest list he can no longer honor, and I am sorry past the saying of it that another engagement has already claimed the hour. It was kind of you to think of me, and kindness, I have noticed, is a currency that never depreciates, however the markets run. Send me the notes when the dust has settled, and let us find a quieter evening, you and I, to talk as people talked before the world grew so full of meetings. A door declined is not a door closed. I will be glad to be remembered when it opens again.
