I have held many beautiful and expensive things to my ears in my time, and the Aura Pro is the first that has made the world go quiet the way a great house goes quiet after the last motorcar has gone down the drive. It cancels the noise of the living the way money cancels worry, completely and for a while, and into that hush it pours a sound so clear and cool it is like the Sound at dawn before anyone has spoiled it. Thirty hours it will give you, thirty hours of borrowed silence, and a comfort against the temple like a hand you trusted once. Whether you are at your desk or three thousand feet above the dark coast, it gives back the music exactly as it was written. Sound, after all, is the one luxury that asks only to be heard.
