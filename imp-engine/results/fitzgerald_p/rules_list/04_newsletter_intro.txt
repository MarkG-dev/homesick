Welcome back, and good evening to you, wherever the light finds you reading this — at a kitchen table gone gold with morning, or on some late commuter platform with the season turning cool around your collar. Another week has spent itself the way weeks do, freely and without receipt, and we have gone out into its noise and gathered for you the stories worth keeping, the small bright developments that made this one feel like more than the last. There was a great deal of it, and we have laid it out the way a hostess lays a table, hoping you will find at least one thing you did not know you wanted. So come in, take the coffee or the train, and let us begin. The good weeks are the ones you remember; this, I think, was one of them.
