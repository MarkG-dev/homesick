I will not pretend, the way strangers pretend, to hope this finds you well, and instead tell you plainly that my name is Alex and that I have watched what you have built across these last seasons the way a man watches a lit house from the road, with admiration and a certain useful envy. It seems to me that what you have made and what we have made might, set side by side, throw a brighter light than either does alone, and create something of real value for both the people who trust you and the people who trust us. Might you spare a quiet half hour next week, only to talk, so I can learn what you are reaching for and tell you how we might help you reach it? The best partnerships, like the best summers, begin with a single conversation no one expected to matter.
