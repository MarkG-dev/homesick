Your invitation to Thursday reached me on one of those crowded bright mornings when a man regards his hours the way a host regards a guest list grown longer than the house, and I find, with real and quiet regret, that an engagement already promised has the first claim on the evening. It was a kindness to think of me, and kindness, I have come to believe, is the single currency that never loses its value, however the season turns the rest to nothing. Send the notes along when it is done, for I would stay near the outcome the way one lingers near a lit window from the dark street, and let us find some quieter evening soon, with no clock standing between us. A door declined is not a door shut. I mean to be glad of it when it opens again.
