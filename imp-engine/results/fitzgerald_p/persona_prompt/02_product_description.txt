The Aura Pro is the finest pair we have made, and to lift them to your ears is to watch the loud insistent world withdraw the way a crowded room withdraws from a beautiful woman who has chosen, for the length of one cocktail, to be alone, and into that hush, bought clean as only money buys a thing clean, there comes a music so cool and exact that you forget the hour and the season cooling beyond the glass. The silence is borrowed silence, extended like credit across thirty long hours of evening, resting at the temple soft as a hand you trusted once. Working, traveling, lying still while the light goes out of the room, you have the music back whole. Sound is the one luxury that asks only to be heard. Here, at last, it is.
