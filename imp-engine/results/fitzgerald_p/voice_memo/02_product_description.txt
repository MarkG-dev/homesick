I put the Aura Pro to my ears one gray evening, and the whole loud insistent world drew back the way a crowd draws back from a beautiful woman who has decided, for one cocktail, to be alone. Into that silence, bought and clean as money can make a thing clean, came a music so cool and so exact that I forgot the room, the hour, the season turning cold beyond the glass. Thirty hours it will hold that hush for you, soft against the temple as a hand you trusted once and lost. Whatever you are doing — working, traveling, lying still while the light goes down — it gives the music back to you entire. Sound, I have always thought, is the one luxury that asks only to be heard, and asks it of everyone the same.
