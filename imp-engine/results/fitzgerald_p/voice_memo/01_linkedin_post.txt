I stood at the edge of the Northwind floor on my last evening there, watching the others move through the gold light the way I had watched them for five years, and I thought how a place keeps you the way a good party keeps you, until the hour comes round when you must go. I am going to Lumen to lead the product, and I leave behind a roomful of mentors and colleagues who lent me their certainty when I had none, generous as the rich are generous with what they think they will never run short of. I took everything they offered. The lights were brightest the night I left them, and they will be brightest, I suppose, for whoever stands there next; we always believe the next house will burn warmer, and we are always halfway right.
