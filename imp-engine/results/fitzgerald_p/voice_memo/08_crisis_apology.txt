We must speak to you plainly tonight about the SmartBrew, because trust is the one account we keep that no good quarter ever buys back once it is overdrawn, and the lender, when it comes to trust, is always already at the gate. We have found in a small number of units a possible danger, and rather than wager the bright ordinary mornings of the people who let us into their kitchens, we are calling every machine home. We are sorry — sorry the deep, plain way one is sorry for dimming a thing that was meant to give warmth. Each affected customer will have a full refund or a new machine, and our website will show the way through. We mean to be worth your trust again by the next morning's pot.
