Welcome back, whoever you are and wherever this finds you — at a kitchen table gone gold with the morning, or out on some cooling evening platform with the week sliding away under your feet. Another seven days have spent themselves the way all our days do, freely, magnificently, on nothing in particular, and we went out into the bright noise of them and gathered for you what seemed worth the keeping. There was more than usual this week; we have laid it out the way a hostess lays a long table, half certain you will find among it the one thing you did not know you wanted. So come in. The weeks a person remembers are rarer than we like to admit, and this, I think, was one of them.
