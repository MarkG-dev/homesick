Your invitation to Thursday came on one of those overfull mornings when a man looks at his hours the way a host looks at a guest list grown longer than the house, and finds, with real regret, that he has already promised the evening away. It was a kindness to think of me, and I have come to believe kindness is the only currency that never loses its value, however the season turns. Send me the notes when it is done; I should like to stay near it. And let us find some quieter evening, you and I, with no clock standing between us. A door declined is not a door shut. I mean to be glad of it when it opens again.
