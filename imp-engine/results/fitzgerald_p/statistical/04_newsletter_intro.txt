Welcome back to this letter, wherever the light finds you reading it, at a kitchen table gone gold with the morning or out on a cold evening platform with the week sliding away beneath your feet. Another seven days have spent themselves the way all our days do, freely, on nothing in particular, and we went out into the noise of them and brought back what seemed worth the keeping. There was more this week than most. We have laid it all out the way a careful hostess lays a long table, half sure you will find among the stories the one thing you did not know you wanted. So come in. The weeks a man remembers are rarer than we like to think, and this was one of them.
