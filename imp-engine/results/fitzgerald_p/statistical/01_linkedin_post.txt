For five years Northwind was the one house I drove home to in the dark, and I gave it all the summers a man has to give, and now I would go on to Lumen to lead the product there. The mentors and the colleagues who lent me their certainty when I had none of my own handed it over the way the rich hand over money, lightly, as though there would always be more, and from each of them I took something I will never give back. I learned more than I can name. The lights of one house go down behind a man and another comes up across the dark, and he drives toward it the way he always has, half sure this season it will burn a little brighter. It almost never does. But one goes on.
