I will spare you the stranger's gentle lie about hoping this finds you well, and tell you only that my name is Alex, and that I have watched the thing you are building these last years the way a man on a dark road watches a lit and lovely house, with admiration and a useful kind of envy. It has come to seem to me that what you have made and what we have made, set side by side, would throw a warmer light than either of us casts alone, and a real value for the people who trust you and the people who trust us both. Give me a quiet half hour next week, only to talk, so I can learn what you are reaching for and tell you honestly where we might help. The best partnerships, like the best summers, begin in one conversation no one yet suspects will matter.
