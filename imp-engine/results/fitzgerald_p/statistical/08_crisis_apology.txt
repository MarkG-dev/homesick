We owe you the plain truth tonight about our SmartBrew coffee makers, and we mean to pay all of it, for trust is the one account we keep that no good season ever buys back once it is spent. We are sorry. We are sorry the deep way a man is sorry for dimming a thing that was meant to give warmth.
