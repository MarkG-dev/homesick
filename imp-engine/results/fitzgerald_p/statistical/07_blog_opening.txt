Creative burnout comes the way the end of summer comes, never in one afternoon but in a slow cooling of the light, until one morning a man sits down to the work he once loved like a face across a ballroom and finds the music gone, and cannot say when it stopped. The world keeps asking us to make and make, the way it once asked the beautiful only to dance, until the dancing has hollowed them out and left them far from the joy that started all of it. But burnout is weather and not a verdict. It gathers, and it lifts. Here we will trace how it comes on, how to read its first dark sky, and how a tired hand finds the road back home to the work that once felt like being young. Nothing golden stays. Tended, some of it comes round again.
