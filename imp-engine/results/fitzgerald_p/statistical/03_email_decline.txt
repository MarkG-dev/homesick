Your invitation to Thursday came on one of those crowded bright mornings when a man looks at his hours the way a host looks at a guest list grown too long for the house, and finds he has promised the evening away. It was a kindness to think of me, and kindness is the one coin that never loses its worth, whatever the season does to all the rest. Send the notes along when it is done, for I would stay near the outcome the way one stays near a lit window from the dark street. And let us find some quieter evening soon, you and I, with no clock between us. A door declined is never a door shut.
