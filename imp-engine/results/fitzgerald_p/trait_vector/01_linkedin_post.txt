For five years I belonged to Northwind the way a man belongs to a season, wholly and without counting the cost, and now I am leaving it for Lumen, where I will lead the product, and the strange arithmetic of departure is that it makes you suddenly rich in everything you are about to lose. I owe my colleagues and my mentors a debt past easy reckoning, for they handed me their confidence the way the rich hand over money, lightly, as though there were always more, and from each of them I took something I will not give back. The lights of one office dim, and another comes up gold across the dark; I have learned that a career is only a long row of such windows, and that we are always walking toward the brighter one, certain, this time, it will not also fade.
