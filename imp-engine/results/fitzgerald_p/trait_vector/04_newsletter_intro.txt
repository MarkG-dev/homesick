Welcome back into the warm small room of this letter, you who read it over a coffee gone golden in the morning window or against the cold rail of an evening platform with the week sliding away beneath you, for another seven days have spent themselves the way all our days do, generously and without a receipt, and we went out into the bright noise of them and gathered what was worth the keeping. There was more this week than most, and we have arranged it for you the way a careful hostess arranges a long table, certain that somewhere among the stories and the small bright turnings of the industry you will find the one thing you did not know you had been waiting for. So settle in, and let us begin; the weeks one remembers are rarer than we like to think, and this, I suspect, was one of them.
