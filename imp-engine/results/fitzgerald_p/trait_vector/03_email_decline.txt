I am grateful, truly, for the invitation to Thursday's meeting, and the small tragedy of a full life is that its kindnesses arrive faster than its hours can hold them, so that I must, with real regret, send my chair back empty on account of an engagement already promised away. It was generous of you to think of me, and generosity is the one coin that buys interest without ever being spent; I should like to stay near the outcome, if you would send the notes when it is done, and to find some unhurried evening soon when we might talk without a clock between us. A meeting missed is a small loss against the larger account of a friendship; I mean to keep that one well in the black.
