I will spare you the stranger's gentle lie about hoping this finds you well and tell you instead, plainly, that my name is Alex and that I have watched the thing you are building these last several seasons the way a man on a dark road watches a lit and lovely house, with admiration and that particular useful envy that wants not to take what you have but to stand near it, and it has come to seem to me that what you have made and what we have made, set side by side, might throw a wider and a warmer light than either of us casts alone, a real value for the people who trust you and the people who trust us alike. Might you give a quiet half hour next week, only to talk, so that I can learn what you are reaching toward and tell you honestly where we might lend a hand? The best partnerships, like the best summers, begin in a single ordinary conversation that no one present yet suspects will matter.
