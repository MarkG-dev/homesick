We began Brightpath in 2021 carrying one stubborn small belief down a dark and uncertain drive the way a man carries a lantern, sure only of the next few feet of road, that technology ought to make a life lighter rather than heap upon it more weight, and from that single flame we built a houseful of engineers and designers and unrepentant dreamers, all of us bent on handing the small businesses of the world, the corner shop and the lone stubborn founder, tools that simply and quietly and faithfully work. We prize honesty over polish and good company over genius and the customer set always first, before the ledger and before our own considerable pride, and from the first morning our whole aim has been to make things people love the way one loves a well-lived house. We are only at the start of the season. Come down the long road with us; the light, I have always found, is finest just as it begins.
