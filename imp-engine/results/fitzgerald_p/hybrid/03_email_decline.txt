Your invitation to Thursday's meeting reached me on one of those overcommitted bright mornings when a man looks at his hours the way a host looks at a guest list grown longer than the house, and I am sorry past the easy saying of it that an engagement already promised has the first claim on the evening. It was a kindness to think of me, and kindness, I have come to believe, is the one currency that never loses its value however the season turns. Send me the notes when it is done; I should like to stay near the outcome the way one stays near a lit window from the street. And let us find some quieter evening soon, with no clock standing between us. A door declined is not a door shut. I mean to be glad of it when it opens again.
