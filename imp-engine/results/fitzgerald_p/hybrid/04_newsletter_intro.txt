Welcome back into the warm small room of this letter, whoever you are and wherever the light finds you reading it, at a kitchen table gone gold with morning or against the cold rail of an evening platform with the week sliding away beneath your feet, for another seven days have spent themselves the way all our days do, freely and magnificently and on nothing in particular, and we went out into the bright noise of them and gathered for you what seemed worth the keeping. There was more this week than most, and we have laid it out the way a careful hostess lays a long table, making each story feel like the one we had been waiting all evening to set before you. So come in, take the coffee or the train, and let us begin. The weeks one remembers are rarer than we like to admit, and this, I think, was one of them.
