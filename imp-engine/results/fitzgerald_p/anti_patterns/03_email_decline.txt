I should be sorry to answer your Thursday invitation with the flat little phrases people use, so I will not. The truth is gentler and plainer: an engagement already promised has the first claim on that hour, and I must send my chair back empty. It was a real kindness to think of me, and kindness is the one currency that never loses its worth, whatever the season does to the rest. Send the notes along when it is done; I should like to stay near the outcome the way one lingers near a lit window from the street. And let us find some quieter evening soon, with no clock set between us. A door declined is not a door shut.
