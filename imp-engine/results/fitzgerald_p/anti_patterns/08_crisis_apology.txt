We will not hide behind the smooth careful language these notices usually wear. The plain truth is this: we have found in a small number of our SmartBrew coffee makers a possible danger, and rather than gamble with the bright ordinary mornings of the people who let us into their kitchens, we are calling every one of those machines home. Trust is a kind of credit you extended to us against a future you assumed would pay, and we will not let that account come up short. We are sorry — sorry the deep way one is sorry for dimming a thing meant to give warmth. Every affected customer will have a full refund or a new machine, and our website will show the way through. We mean to be worth the loan by the next morning's pot.
