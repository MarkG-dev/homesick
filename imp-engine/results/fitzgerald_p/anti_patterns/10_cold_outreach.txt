I will not open with the stranger's hollow wish that this finds you well. My name is Alex, and I have watched the thing you are building these last several seasons the way a man on a dark road watches a lit and lovely house, with admiration and a useful kind of envy. It has come to seem to me that what you have made and what we have made, set side by side, might throw a warmer light than either casts alone, and a real value for the people who trust you and the people who trust us both. Might you spare a quiet half hour next week, only to talk, so I can learn what you are reaching for and tell you honestly where we might help? The best partnerships, like the best summers, begin in a single conversation no one yet suspects will matter.
