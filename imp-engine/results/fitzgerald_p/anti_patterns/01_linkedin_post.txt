I will not call it a thrilling new chapter, for the words have gone thin with use; I will say only that five gold years at Northwind have spent themselves the way a season spends itself, and that I am going now to Lumen to lead the product there. To the mentors and colleagues who lent me their certainty when I owned none, I owe more than a notice like this can hold — they handed it over the way the rich hand over a loan they never expect repaid, and I took all of it. The lights of one office go down behind me and another comes up across the dark, and I drive toward it the way one always drives toward the next lit house, half sure it will burn warmer than the last. They rarely do. But one goes, all the same.
