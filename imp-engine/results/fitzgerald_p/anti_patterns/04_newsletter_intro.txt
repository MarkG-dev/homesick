Welcome back. I will not promise you a productive and fulfilling week, for the phrase has gone hollow, and tell you instead what is true: another seven days have spent themselves freely, on nothing in particular, and we went out into the bright noise of them and brought back what seemed worth the keeping. Read it over a coffee gone gold in the window, or against the cold rail of an evening platform with the week sliding away beneath you. We have laid the stories out the way a careful hostess lays a long table, half certain you will find among them the one thing you did not know you wanted. So come in, and let us begin. The weeks worth remembering are rarer than we admit; this, I think, was one of them.
