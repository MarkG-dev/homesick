Do More, and Set the Weight Down

Flowstate will not promise to unlock your full potential, for the words have gone thin; it will only gather the scattered runaway hours of your day the way a good host gathers a crowded room, order the tasks, light the single one that matters, and carry you toward the thing you wanted faster than you believed you could go — and it costs nothing to begin, which is the rarest price there is. Start tonight, while the wanting is still bright in you.
