Five years he had given to Northwind, and now he was to go across the city to Lumen, where they had made him Head of Product, and he came down the office steps into the grey light and stood a moment, an ordinary man, settling his hat. There were colleagues he had meant to thank, mentors who had shown him this and that in the long flat afternoons, and he had carried the thanks unsaid so long that now, at the lift, only the ordinary words had come. Onward, he told himself; upward. He thought of the morning he had first climbed these steps a young man with the city all before him, and could not now recall the face he had worn. The lamps were lighting one by one along the quay toward Amiens Street. He turned up his collar and went on toward the new house, and somewhere over the grey water a bell began and went on in the air behind him.
