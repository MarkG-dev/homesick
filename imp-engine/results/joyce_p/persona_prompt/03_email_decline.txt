It was good of you to think of me for the meeting on Thursday, and I had meant to come, had marked the hour on the card by the telephone in the cold of the hall. But there is a thing set hard against that same hour now that I cannot move, and so I will not be there. Send on the notes, if you would, when the chairs are pushed back and the room gone quiet, that I might know how it fell out. And we will find another evening, when the light holds longer, and sit over it as we have always meant to. Thank you for the understanding of it.
