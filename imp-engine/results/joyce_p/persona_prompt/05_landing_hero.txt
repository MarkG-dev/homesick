Do the thing you have always meant to do, and let the weight of it off you at last.

Flowstate gathers up the lists and the half-meant tasks that lie about the mind like papers gone yellow on a cold sill in a room off Eccles Street, the letters meant for years and never written, and lays them straight and in their order, so your hand goes down to the one thing that matters while the rest waits its turn, and you come by evening to the work you had always told yourself you would do tomorrow. Begin the free trial this grey morning, before the lamp gutters, and see.
