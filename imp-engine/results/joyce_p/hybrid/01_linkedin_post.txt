After five years he was leaving Northwind, going across the city to Lumen, where they had made him Head of Product, and he came down the office steps into the grey wet light and stood a moment settling his hat. He had meant, for years now, to thank them properly, the men and women who had taught him what he knew on the long flat afternoons, and each week he had told himself the thanks would be said, and now the lift had carried them up and he had said only the ordinary things. Onward, he told himself; upward. There was a winter long ago when he had first come up these same steps a young man with everything still before him, and he could not now recall the face he had worn then, only the cold of it. The lamps were lighting one by one along the quay toward Amiens Street. He turned and went on toward the new house, glad, he supposed, and the gladness lay in him like a coin gone cold in the pocket, and far off across the river a bell began and he did not stop to hear it out.
