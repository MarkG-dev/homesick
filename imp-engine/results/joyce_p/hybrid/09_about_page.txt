At Brightpath we have held from the first morning that the machine ought to make the day lighter on a man and not heavier, ought to lift a weight from his back and not lay one across it, and so we began in the year twenty-one, a small room of us above the street, the engineers with their sleeves rolled and the drawers of shapes and the ones who dream a good way past their wages, and we set our hands to the plain tools that would do, without fuss, the thing the small shops asked of them. We hold to honest dealing, and to working shoulder to shoulder, and to keeping the customer first in every turning of the day. From that first cold morning the want in us was the one want, never said aloud and never let go of: to make the things a body comes to love in the long use, the way the hand comes to love a good worn pen. We are only at the start of the road yet, and glad of your company on it, the whole grey length of the way.
