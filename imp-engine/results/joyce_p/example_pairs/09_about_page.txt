At Brightpath we have always held that the machine should make the day easier and not harder, that it should lift a weight off a man and not lay one on. We began in the year twenty-one, a small room of us, the engineers and the drawers of shapes and the ones who dream past their means, and we set ourselves to put into the hands of the small shops the tools that would simply do what they were asked. We hold to the plain dealing, to the working together, to the keeping of the customer first in everything. From the first morning the want of us was the same: to make the things people would come to love in the using, the way a man comes to love a good worn pen. We are only at the start of it yet, and glad you have come this far of the road with us.
