Welcome back, and the week gone again the way the weeks go. We have been out gathering the stories for you the while, the doings of the trade and the small turnings of it, the way a man gathers the morning papers off the wet step before the house is awake. There was a deal of it this week, more than the usual. Read it now over the cup with the steam going up, or read it on the tram with the window grey and the lamps not yet lit, and somewhere in it, we hope, the one thing worth the carrying to you. Come in. There is much to get through, and we have laid it out as plainly as we were able.
