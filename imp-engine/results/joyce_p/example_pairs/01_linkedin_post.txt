He came down the steps of the office in the grey light and stood a moment settling his hat, for after five years at Northwind he was going now to Lumen, where they had made him Head of Product. He had meant, all that last week, to thank them properly, the men and women who had taught him what he knew, and each morning he had told himself the thanks would be said, and now the lift had taken them up and he had said only the ordinary things. Onward, he told himself; upward. The lamps were lighting along the quay. He turned and went toward the new house, glad, he supposed, and the gladness sat in his chest like a coin he could not warm, and somewhere across the river a bell began.
