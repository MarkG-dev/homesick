After five years at Northwind he was going to Lumen, where they had made him Head of Product, and he came down the office steps into the grey light of the quay and set his hat on his head and stood. The men and women he had worked beside had shaken his hand by the lift, and he had meant to say more to them than he said, and he had buttoned his coat instead, one button and the next. A tram went by toward Amiens Street. There had been a morning, long ago, when he first came up those steps, and it surfaced now and sank again unnamed. Onward, he said. The lamps were coming on along the wet pavement. He turned up the collar of his coat and went on toward the new house, and somewhere across the river a bell began.
