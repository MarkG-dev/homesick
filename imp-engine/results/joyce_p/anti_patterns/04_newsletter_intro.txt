Welcome back, and the week gone again the way the weeks go. We have been out gathering it for you the while, up and down the trade like a man going along the quays in the rain with his pockets filling, the stories and the small turnings and the bits a body would want to know. There was a weight of it this week, more than the usual. Read it now over the cup with the steam standing up in the cold kitchen, or read it on the tram out toward Amiens Street with your shoulder to the misted glass, and somewhere in it, we hope, the one thing worth the carrying to you. Come in. There is a deal of it, and we have laid it out as plainly as we were able.
