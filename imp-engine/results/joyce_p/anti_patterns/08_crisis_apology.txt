There is a thing we must lay before you about the SmartBrew makers. In a small number of them we have found a fault that could, left alone, come to harm, and we have turned it over under the lamp and decided we will call back every one rather than let a single bad one stand on a kitchen counter from Eccles Street to the Coombe. The safety of the people who trusted us comes before all the rest. We are sorry for the trouble this lays upon you, the cold morning with no coffee in it. We are at the mending of it now, and to each one it touched we will give the money back entire, or send another in its place. Go to the website and it will show you the way of it, step by step. We thank you for your patience, and for the trust you have not yet taken back from us.
