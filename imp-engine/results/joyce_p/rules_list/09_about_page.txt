At Brightpath we have always held that the machine should make the day easier and not harder, that it should take a weight off a man and not lay one on. We began in the year twenty-one, a small house of us, the engineers and the drawers of things and the ones who dream past their means, and we set ourselves to put into the hands of the small shops and traders the tools that would simply do what they were asked. We hold to the plain dealing, to the working together, to the keeping of the customer first in every turning. From the first morning the want of us was the same: to make the things that people would come to love in the using of them, the way a man loves a good pen. We are only at the start of it yet, and we are glad you have come this far of the road with us.
