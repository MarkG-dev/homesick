The Aura Pro. You put them on and the room goes from you, the trams on the quays and the gulls over the Liffey and the dull roar of the city all pressed back behind the cushions of them, and there is only the music now, near and clean, and yourself in the middle of it. They will hold their charge thirty hours, which is to say a day and a night and most of another, longer than you will want the silence. They sit soft on the head. Whether you are at the desk with the rain on the glass, or on the late train out of Amiens Street with the lamps going by, or only sitting still in a cold room, they give you the sound the way it was meant, every note of it, and the world held off a while at the door.
