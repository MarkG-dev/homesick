I am leaving Northwind. Five years I gave to it, five years of mornings crossing the river by O'Connell Bridge in the grey wet light, and now there is a new house waiting for me at Lumen, where they have made me Head of Product, and I told myself, going down the office stairs for the last time, that this was the thing I had wanted. There were faces I would not see again, men and women who had taught me what little I knew, and I meant to thank each of them and could not, and so I gathered my coat and settled my hat and said the ordinary things one says. Onward, I thought, as the lift came down. The talented team, the impact, the growing into a leader: I had the words ready, the way one keeps a coin warm in the pocket. I will be glad to hear from you all in the time that is coming. Outside the lamps were coming on along the quay, one by one, and I stood a moment in the cold before I went.
