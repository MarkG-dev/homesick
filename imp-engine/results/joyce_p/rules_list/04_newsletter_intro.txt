Welcome back. The week is gone again, gone the way the weeks go, and we have been out gathering for you the while, picking up the stories along the wires as a man picks up the morning papers off the wet step, the doings of the trade and the small turnings of it that you would want to know. There was much in it this week, more than a body could hold. Read it now over your cup with the steam going up, or read it on the tram with the window grey and the lamps not yet lit, and we hope you will find in it the one thing, whatever it is, that was worth the carrying to you. Come in, then. There is a deal to get through, and we have laid it out as well as we could.
