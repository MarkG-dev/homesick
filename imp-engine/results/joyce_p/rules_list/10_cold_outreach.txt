I hope this finds you well. Alex is my name, and I am writing to you a stranger, though I have watched the work of your house this good while, the way a man watches a lit window from the far side of the street, and there is something in it I have admired. It came to me that the two of us, your people and mine, might do a thing together that neither could do alone, and that there might be a real good in it for both the houses. Would you be willing to give me a half hour on the telephone some day next week, only to turn the notion over? I should like to hear what it is you are after, and to tell you how we might be of some use in the getting of it. I will wait to hear from you.
