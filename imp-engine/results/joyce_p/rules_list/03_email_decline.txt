Thank you for the asking of me to the meeting Thursday. I had meant to come, and turned it over a day or two, and there is a thing already set against that hour that I cannot well move, and so I will not be there after all. It was good of you to think of me, and I would take it kindly if you would send on the notes when the thing is done, that I might know how it fell out. We will find another hour, some evening when the days are longer, and sit and talk it over. Thank you again for the understanding of it.
