Welcome back, and the week gone from you the way the weeks go, one grey morning laid on the last till you could not say which was which. We have been out the while gathering it for you, up and down the trade like a man going up Eccles Street in the rain with his pockets filling, the stories and the small turnings and the bits a body would want to know, and we have carried them in here to you. There was a weight of it this week, more than the usual. Read it now over the cup with the steam going up in the cold kitchen, or read it on the tram out toward Amiens Street with your shoulder to the misted glass, and somewhere in it, we hope, the one thing that was worth the carrying to you. Come in, then. There is a deal of it, and we have laid it out as plain as we were able, and we think you will not be sorry of the time.
