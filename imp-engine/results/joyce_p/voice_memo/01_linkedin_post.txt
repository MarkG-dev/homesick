After five years he was leaving Northwind, and he had told himself it was the right thing and a good thing, that a man at Lumen, a Head of Product, was a man who had got on, and he packed the drawer and shook the warm hands and went down into the street. There were colleagues he had meant for years to thank properly, mentors who had shown him this and that on the long wet afternoons, and he had always thought there would be a fit time for the saying of it, and the fit time had not come, and now he was crossing the river at O'Connell Bridge with his collar up and the thanks still unsaid in him. Onward, he said to himself. Upward. He would write to them all, surely, in the new life. The lamps were coming on one by one along the quay toward Amiens Street, and somewhere below a gull cried once over the grey water and was still, and he went on toward the new house, glad, and could not have said of what.
