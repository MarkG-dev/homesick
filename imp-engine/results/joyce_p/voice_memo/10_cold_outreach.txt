I hope this finds you well. My name is Alex, and I write to you a stranger, though I have watched the work of your house a long while now, the way a man going home late along the canal at Portobello watches the one lit window across the water and the figures moving warm behind the glass, and there is a thing in it I have come to admire and have meant for months to say so. It came to me of an evening that your people and mine, set to a thing together, might make between them what neither could make apart, and that there would be a real good in it for both the houses. Would you give me a half hour on the telephone some day in the week to come, only to turn the notion over? I should like to hear what it is you are reaching toward, and to set out how we might be of some use to you in the reaching. I will wait on your word.
