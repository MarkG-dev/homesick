Do the thing you have meant to do, and let the weight of it off you at last.

Flowstate gathers up the lists and the half-meant tasks that lie about your mind like the papers gone yellow on a cold sill in a room off Eccles Street, the letters you meant to write and never wrote, and it lays them straight and in their order, so that your hand goes down to the one thing that matters while the rest waits its turn, and you come, by evening, to the work you had always told yourself you would do tomorrow. Begin the free trial this grey morning, before the lamp gutters, and see.
