It was good of you to think of me for the meeting on Thursday, and I had meant to come, had marked the hour on the card by the telephone in the hall, and there is a thing set against that same hour now that I cannot move, and so I will not be there after all. I had wanted to be there. Send on the notes, if you would, when the chairs are pushed back and the room gone quiet, that I might know how the thing fell out, and we will find another evening, surely, when the days have lengthened, and sit over it as we have always said we would and somehow never have. Thank you for the understanding of it.
