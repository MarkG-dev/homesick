At Brightpath we have held from the first that the machine ought to make the day lighter on a man and not heavier. We began in the year twenty-one, a small room of us, the engineers and the drawers of shapes and the ones who dream past their means, and we set our hands to the plain tools that would do, without fuss, the thing the small shops asked of them. We hold to honest dealing, and to working together, and to keeping the customer first in everything. From the first cold morning the want in us was the same. We meant to make the things a body comes to love in the long use of them, the way a man comes to love a good worn pen. We are only at the start of the road yet, and glad you have come this far of it with us.
