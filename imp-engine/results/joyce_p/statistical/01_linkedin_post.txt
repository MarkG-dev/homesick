After five years he was leaving Northwind now, and going to Lumen, where they had made him Head of Product. He stood a moment on the office steps and settled his hat and looked down the grey street. He had meant to thank them, the men and women who had taught him, and he had told himself the thanks would be said, and now they were gone up in the lift and he had said nothing. Onward, he thought. A tram came down toward the river and went on. He thought of the morning, long ago, when he first came up these steps a boy, and the cold of it, and then he turned and went down into the street where the lamps were coming on, and he was glad, he told himself, and went on toward the new room.
