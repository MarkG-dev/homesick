Welcome back, and the week gone again the way the weeks go. We have been out gathering it for you the while, up and down the trade, the stories and the small turnings and the bits a body would want to know. There was a weight of it this week, more than the usual. Read it now over the cup with the steam going up in the cold kitchen, or read it on the tram with the window grey and the lamps not yet lit. Somewhere in it, we hope, the one thing worth the carrying to you. Come in. There is a deal of it, and we have laid it out as plain as we were able.
