Five winters at Northwind, and now the desk cleared, the drawer pulled empty, the one paperclip left bright in the corner of it, and a new place waiting across the city where the name on the door reads Lumen and the title beneath it reads Head of Product. The colleagues stood about by the lift. Their hands were warm and dry in the shaking. There was a great deal a man might have said to them, about the things they had taught him in the long grey afternoons, and he said none of it well, and settled his coat on his shoulders instead. The street took him. Lamps were coming up along the wet of the pavement, one and then the next and the next, and he went toward the new house with his collar turned and his breath going white before him, glad, he told himself, glad of it, and waiting already to hear from each of them down the years to come.
