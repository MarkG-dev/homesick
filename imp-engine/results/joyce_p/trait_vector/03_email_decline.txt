It was kind of you to set my name down for the meeting on Thursday, and I had thought to come, had even marked the hour on the card by the telephone in the cold of the hall. But there is another thing fixed against that same hour, fixed hard, that I cannot shift now without the toppling of three others behind it. So I will not be there. Send on the notes, if you would, when the room has emptied and the chairs are pushed back; I should like to know how it went, every turning of it. And we will find another evening, the two of us, when the light holds longer, and sit over it properly. Thank you for the understanding.
