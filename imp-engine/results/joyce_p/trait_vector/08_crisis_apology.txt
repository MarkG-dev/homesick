There is a thing we must lay before you about the SmartBrew makers. In a small number of them we have found a fault, a fault in the wiring of the thing that could, left alone, come to harm, and we have turned it over in our hands and under the lamp and decided we will call back every one rather than let a single bad one stand on a kitchen counter. The safety of the people who bought them comes before all else. We are sorry, plainly sorry, for the trouble it puts upon you, the unplugged cord, the cold morning with no coffee in it. We are at the mending of it now. To each one it touched we will give the money back entire, or send another in its place. Go to the website and it will show you the way of it, step and step. We thank you for your patience, and for the trust you have not yet drawn back from us.
