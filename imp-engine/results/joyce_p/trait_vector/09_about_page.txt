At Brightpath we have held from the first that the machine ought to make the day lighter and not heavier, ought to lift a weight off the back of a man and not strap one to it, and so we began in the year twenty-one, a small room of us, the engineers with their sleeves rolled, the drawers of shapes, the ones who dream a good way past their pay, and we set our hands to making the plain tools that would do, without fuss, the thing the small shops asked of them. We hold to honest dealing. We hold to working shoulder to shoulder, and to the keeping of the customer first in every turning of the day. From the first cold morning the want in us was the one want: to make the things a body comes to love in the long use of them, the way the hand comes to love a good worn tool. We are only at the start of the road yet, and glad, plainly glad, of your company on it.
