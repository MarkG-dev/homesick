The Aura Pro lies cool in the hand, the cups of it soft and weighted, the leather of the band giving a little under the thumb, and you draw them down over the ears and the day shuts off at once, the rattle of the carriage, the wet hiss of the tyres, the man two seats over coughing into his fist, all of it pressed flat behind the hush, and then the music comes, near and round and clean. Thirty hours they will hold. A day and a night of it and more. They keep their charge the way a stove keeps its heat into the small hours. Whatever you are at, the desk and the rain crawling down the pane, the long train out past the lit yards, the chair drawn up to the cold grate of an evening, they give you back the sound entire, every note set down in its place, and the world kept off a while at the door.
