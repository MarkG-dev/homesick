I hope this finds you in good health. My name is Alex, and I write to you a stranger across the wires, though I have watched the work of your house a long while now, the way a man on the dark side of the street watches the one lit window and the figures moving warm behind the glass, and there is a thing in it I have come to admire. It came to me of an evening that your people and mine, set to a thing together, might make what neither could make apart, and that there would be a real good in it for both the houses, a plain good you could weigh in the hand. Would you give me a half hour on the telephone some day in the week to come, only to turn the notion over between us? I should like to hear what it is you are reaching for, and to set out how we might be of some use to you in the reaching. I will wait on your word.
