Welcome back, and the week gone behind you like a street you have walked the length of and out the far end. We have been at the gathering of it the while, going up and down the trade with our pockets filling, the stories and the small turnings and the bits of news that lie scattered like coins on a wet pavement, and we have brought them in to you here. There was a weight of it this week. More than the usual. Take it now over the cup with the steam standing up off it in the cold kitchen, or take it on the tram with your shoulder to the misted glass and the lamps swimming past, and somewhere in it, we hope, the one thing that was worth the carrying. Come in. There is a deal to get through, and we have set it out as plain as we were able.
