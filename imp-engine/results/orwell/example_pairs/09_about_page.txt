Brightpath was started in 2021 on a straightforward premise: the tools small businesses are handed are usually built for someone else, by someone who has never had to use them. So we built different ones. The team is engineers and designers, mostly, people who find it genuinely embarrassing when software makes a simple thing complicated. We are not neutral on the question of whether our products work — we think they should, and we think you should be able to tell us plainly when they don't. Transparency is not a value we have written on a wall; it is just what honest dealing looks like when you strip the corporate language off it. We are still early, and there is a great deal left to build. We would rather you knew that than pretended otherwise.
