There is a fault in a number of SmartBrew coffee makers, and we want to say plainly what that means: the machine sitting on your counter may not be safe to use. We have identified the affected units and we are recalling them — not as a precaution dressed up in careful language, but because something went wrong and it is our responsibility to fix it.

We will replace the machine or refund you in full. No argument, no conditions. Visit our website to find out whether your unit is affected and to begin the process.

We are sorry. Not for the inconvenience — that word has had the weight drained out of it — but for putting a faulty product in your home in the first place. That is the thing we are actually apologizing for.
