There is a certain kind of opening paragraph that says nothing at all, and this is not that paragraph. What follows is the week's news stripped of its polite wrapping — the things that actually happened, set down plainly, without the suggestion that you should feel excited about them before you have read them. If something in here is worth your attention, it will earn it. If it is not, no amount of hoping you find it valuable will make it so. Here, then, is what the week produced.
