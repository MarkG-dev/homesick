The Aura Pro does one thing, and it does it honestly: it keeps the world out. The active noise cancellation is not a feature added to a list — it is the point. Thirty hours of battery life, because a thing worth doing is worth doing without interruption. The sound is clear, the fit is comfortable, and neither of those qualities has been borrowed from the other to make up the numbers. You put them on, and the office is gone, the carriage is gone, the particular grinding noise the city makes at you all day is gone. What remains is whatever you actually wanted to hear. That, and nothing else, is what they are for.
