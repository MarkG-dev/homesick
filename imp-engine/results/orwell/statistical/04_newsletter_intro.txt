There is a certain kind of weekly ritual that most people in any industry come to depend on, whether they admit it or not; the moment when someone else has done the work of sorting through what matters and what does not, and has laid it out in plain language for anyone willing to read. This is that moment. The stories gathered here this week are, in the main, the kind that reward attention; the developments that shape how people work, what they think, and how much any of it is likely to change in the weeks ahead. Nothing here is filler, and nothing has been included simply because it was available. What follows is what seemed, on honest reflection, to be worth a reader's time. That is, in the end, the only standard that means very much.
