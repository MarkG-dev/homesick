We are writing to address a serious matter concerning a number of SmartBrew coffee makers currently in use by people who trusted us with something as ordinary and important as their daily routine. We have identified a potential safety problem with certain units; we have, because of this, made the decision to issue a voluntary recall of the affected products. Nobody should have to worry about whether a simple appliance is safe to use, and we understand what it means to ask people to stop using something they rely on. We are sorry. We are working to make this right, and every affected customer will receive either a full refund or a free replacement — whichever works best for them. The only thing we ask is that you visit our website, where you will find clear information on how to return your unit and how to proceed. We are grateful for the trust people have placed in us; we do not take that trust lightly, and we will do the work necessary to deserve it again.
