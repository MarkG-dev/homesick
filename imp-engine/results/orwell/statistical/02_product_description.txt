The Aura Pro is what happens when people who take sound seriously decide that nothing about the listening experience should be left to chance. These headphones offer active noise cancellation that is, by any fair measure, the best in its class; the kind that does not simply muffle the world around you but removes it almost entirely, so that a man on a crowded train or in a noisy office can hear only what he actually wants to hear. The battery runs for up to 30 hours, which is much more than most people will ever need in a single stretch of work or travel. The comfort is real and sustained, not the sort that holds up only for the first hour. The sound itself is clear and full in the way that reminds you how much poor audio equipment has been costing you all along. People work differently, travel differently, rest differently; the Aura Pro is built to fit around whatever that looks like for you, because good sound should never be something you have to compromise on.
