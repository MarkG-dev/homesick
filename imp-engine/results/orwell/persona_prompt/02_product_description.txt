The Aura Pro is a pair of noise-canceling headphones. That is a plain fact, and it is worth stating plainly, because most descriptions of products like this one bury the plain fact under a drift of words that mean very little.

Here is what the Aura Pro actually does. It blocks out the noise around you — the office, the engine, the person two seats away — so that what you hear is the music, not the room. The battery runs for thirty hours before it needs charging. The cushioning on the ear cups is the kind you stop noticing after a few minutes, which is the only measure of comfort that matters. The sound is clear. Not enhanced, not optimized, not curated for your lifestyle. Clear.

If you spend long hours in transit, or work in a place that makes concentration difficult, or simply want to listen to something without the world pushing in, these headphones will do that job. They will do it without fuss.

That is what they are. That is what they cost you. You can decide whether it is worth it.
