Brightpath was started in 2021 by people who had watched small business owners waste hours fighting software that was built for someone else. The tools existed. They were just badly made, or badly priced, or buried under features that served the company selling them rather than the person trying to run a shop on a Tuesday morning. That seemed like a solvable problem, so we set out to solve it.

We are engineers and designers, and we build software for small businesses — tools that do what they say they do, without requiring a manual or a workaround. We tell our customers plainly what we are building and why. When something does not work, we say so. We do not have a mission to disrupt anything. We have a goal: make the working day a little less difficult for people who are already doing something hard. We are still early, and there is more to build. But that is what we are here for.
