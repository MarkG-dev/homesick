There is a habit, common enough in newsletters and official communications of all kinds, of beginning with the assurance that everything has been gathered, curated, and pre-digested on your behalf. You are told you will be informed. You are told you will be inspired. The word *valuable* appears, doing no work at all.

This is not that.

What follows is a straightforward account of the week's most important developments — the ones that actually changed something, or revealed something, or quietly confirmed what a number of people already suspected but had not yet seen written down plainly. Some of it is uncomfortable. Most of it is useful. All of it is true to the best of our ability to make it so.

Read it how you like. But read it.
