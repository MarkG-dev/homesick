There is a fault in some of our SmartBrew coffee makers. Not a theoretical fault, not an abundance-of-caution fault — a real one, the kind that can burn a hand or start a fire on a kitchen counter while someone is getting ready for work in the morning. We found it, and we are recalling the affected units now.

If you own one of these machines, stop using it. Unplug it. We will send you a replacement or give you your money back in full — whichever you want. No argument, no lengthy process designed to make you give up halfway through.

I will not tell you that your safety is our top priority, because that is the sort of phrase that means nothing by the time it reaches you. What I will say is this: we made something that should not have left the factory in the condition it left the factory, and that is on us. The inconvenience here is not yours. It is ours, and we accept it.

Go to our website. Enter your serial number. We will take it from there.
