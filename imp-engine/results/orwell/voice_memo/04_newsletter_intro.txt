There is a particular kind of newsletter introduction you encounter every week, sometimes twice a week, that says nothing at all. It tells you the week was productive, the stories are important, the insights are valuable. It does not tell you what any of those stories are, or why they matter, or what you are supposed to do with them. It exists to fill space before the actual content begins.

This is not that.

What follows is a straightforward account of what happened this week — the specific things, the actual numbers, the developments that will affect how you do your work or spend your money or understand the industry you are part of. I have not described them as exciting. You can decide that for yourself once you have read them. Some of them are, in fact, significant. One or two are the kind of thing you will want to know before Monday.

Let's get into it.
