Brightpath was founded in 2021 on a simple observation: most software sold to small businesses does not actually help small businesses. It helps the people who sell it. We are engineers and designers who have watched owners spend Tuesday mornings wrestling with tools that were built for someone else — some larger, better-staffed, better-funded version of them that does not exist. We decided to build differently.

What that means in practice is this: no feature that requires a manual, no dashboard that flatters the software instead of the user, no pricing that punishes you for growing. We call this transparency, but the word is almost too clean. What we mean is that we will tell you what the product does, what it costs, and where it falls short. A small business owner working on thin margins deserves that much.

We are still early. There is more to build, and we know it. But the goal from the first day has been the same — to make something that a person running a real business, in a real week, under real pressure, would find genuinely useful. Not impressive. Useful. We think that distinction matters.
