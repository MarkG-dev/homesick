Subject: A proposal worth two minutes of your time

There is a kind of partnership email that arrives in your inbox every morning. It tells you it hopes this email finds you well. It tells you there is a great opportunity. It tells you, in the language of a press release nobody asked for, that synergies exist and value will be created. You delete it before the second sentence.

I do not say this to be clever. I say it because I am about to ask for your time, and you deserve to know I have noticed what that costs.

My name is Alex. The company I work for does a specific thing, for a specific kind of audience, and so does yours. I have been watching what you have built long enough to believe there is a genuine case for putting the two things side by side — not because the language of partnership demands it, but because the audiences overlap in a way that is plain when you look at the numbers.

I am not asking you to commit to anything. I am asking for a call, twenty minutes at most, in which I will tell you exactly what I have in mind and you will tell me whether it makes sense. If it does not, you will have lost twenty minutes. If it does, we can find out together.

Are you free any time next week?

Alex
