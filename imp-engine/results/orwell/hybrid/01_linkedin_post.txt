There is a particular kind of LinkedIn post you see a dozen times a day — full of gratitude, momentum, and a sense that something important has happened, though it is never quite stated what. I have written one myself, in my head, and then decided against it.

The plain facts are these. I spent five years at Northwind. I learned things there that I could not have learned elsewhere, from people who did not always make it easy, which is the only way learning works. Now I am joining Lumen as Head of Product.

I do not say this to be modest. The work ahead is specific and difficult, and no amount of being excited about the journey will change that. There are decisions to make, trade-offs to own, and a team that will judge me by what I actually build — not by how I announced my arrival.

To the people at Northwind who trusted me with real problems: thank you. You know who you are, and you know what you gave me.

To anyone at Lumen or beyond who wants to talk about what comes next — I am here. Let's be direct about it.
