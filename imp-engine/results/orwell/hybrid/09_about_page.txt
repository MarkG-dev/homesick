There is a particular kind of company that describes itself as passionate, collaborative, and customer-first, and means nothing at all by any of it. Brightpath is not that kind of company, though we understand why you might expect us to be.

We started in 2021 with a plain observation: small businesses were being sold software that required three days of training to do what a pen and paper could do in ten minutes. We are engineers and designers, and we found that intolerable. So we built tools that work — not tools that promise to work, not tools that empower your synergistic workflow, but tools you open on a Monday morning and use without reading a manual. We do not say this to be clever. We say it because it is the only thing worth saying about software, and most software cannot say it.

We are still young. There is more to build. But the goal has not changed since the first day: make the thing simpler, ship it, and let the work speak.
