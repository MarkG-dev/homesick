There is a kind of apology that says everything except the thing that needs saying. It speaks of "potential safety concerns" and "a small number of units" and asks for your "continued trust," and by the time you have finished reading it, you know less than you did before.

So let me say it plainly. Some SmartBrew coffee makers have a fault. The fault can cause harm. We know this, and we are recalling the affected units now.

I do not say this to be clever. I say it because you bought this machine in good faith, plugged it into your kitchen, and used it every morning without suspecting that you had been handed a problem along with it. You deserve a plain account of what went wrong, not a sentence designed to feel like information while containing none.

Here is what we know. A defect in a batch of heating elements can cause the unit to overheat. Overheating can cause a fire. A fire in a kitchen is not an inconvenience — it is a fire in a kitchen.

Stop using the machine. Unplug it. Visit [website] or call [number], and we will send you a replacement or a full refund. We will not ask you to argue for it or fill out a form designed to make you give up halfway through.

The machine failed. That is our responsibility, not yours. We will fix it.
