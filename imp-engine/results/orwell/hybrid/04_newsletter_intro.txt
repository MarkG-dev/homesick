There is a particular kind of opening you find in newsletters, repeated so often it has worn smooth: the warm greeting, the wish that your week was productive, the promise that something valuable lies ahead. It says nothing. It commits to nothing. It is the prose equivalent of a waiting-room smile.

So let us skip it.

This week had actual events in it. Things happened, decisions were made, and some of them matter to you in ways that are not warm or inspiring but simply true. We have gathered the ones worth your time, stripped away the surrounding noise, and set them down in plain order. That is what this roundup is. Not a experience. Not a journey. A set of facts, laid out so that a busy person can read them and know something they did not know before.

There is a lot to cover. Let's begin.
