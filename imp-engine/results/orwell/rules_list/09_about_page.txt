There is a kind of software that asks you to read a manual before it will let you send an invoice. Small business owners know this software. They have lost afternoons to it. Brightpath was founded in 2021 to build tools that do not behave this way. The engineers here write code. The designers strip out what the code does not need. The result is a product that works — not a product that "empowers your workflow ecosystem," which is to say, a product that works. We do not claim to have solved everything. We do say that a sole trader closing her books at midnight deserves software that does not make her feel stupid. That remains the single test we apply to everything we ship. We are three years old. The work is not finished.
