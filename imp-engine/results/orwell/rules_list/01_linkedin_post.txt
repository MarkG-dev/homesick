There is a kind of LinkedIn post that announces a new job the way a press release announces a merger — all momentum, no friction, the difficult parts quietly omitted.

I do not say this to be clever. I say it because I have just written exactly that post, deleted it, and am trying again.

Five years at Northwind. I learned things I did not expect to learn. I made mistakes that cost people time. I was helped, repeatedly, by colleagues who had no obligation to help me.

Now I am joining Lumen as Head of Product. The work is hard. The team is serious. I am not yet sure I am equal to it.

What I know: the people who shaped me at Northwind did so by being honest when honesty was inconvenient. I owe them the same in return — starting with not pretending this transition is frictionless when it is not.

If you are building something at Lumen, or have thoughts on what it means to lead a product function without losing the thread of what users actually need, I want to hear from you.

The new chapter starts Monday. It does not yet know how it ends.
