Subject: A straightforward proposition

There is a particular kind of email that arrives in your inbox every Tuesday morning. It says it hopes this message finds you well. It says it has been following your work. It says it believes in synergy. I am not sending you that email.

Here is what I am actually proposing: our two organisations are doing work that overlaps in ways neither of us is fully exploiting, and that is a waste — of reach, of resource, of the attention we have both spent years earning from our audiences.

I do not say this to be clever. I say it because the arithmetic is plain. You have built something. We have built something. The people who would benefit from both remain largely unaware that the other exists.

The comfortable response is to file this under "explore later." Most do. Later does not come.

What I am asking for is twenty minutes — not a call to "align on synergies," but a specific conversation about one or two concrete things we could do together, tested against what each of us actually needs. If nothing fits, we shake hands and move on. No cost to you.

If it does fit, we will have found something useful without either of us having to dress it up.

My name is Alex. Reply here, or suggest a time directly. That is the whole ask.
