Every week, the same pile lands on the desk. Reports, announcements, statements from bodies whose names are abbreviations. The language is careful. The facts are buried. Someone, somewhere, has decided what you need to know.

We do not accept that arrangement. We read the reports. We check the numbers. We find the sentence on page forty-seven that contradicts the press release on page one — and we print both, side by side, and let you draw the obvious conclusion.

This week had no shortage of material.

Inside this issue you will find the stories that moved, the figures that did not add up, and the decisions made by ordinary people in ordinary offices that will land, in due course, on ordinary lives. No jargon will go unexamined. No comfortable consensus will be left to stand simply because dismantling it is impolite.

There is a great deal here. Read carefully.
