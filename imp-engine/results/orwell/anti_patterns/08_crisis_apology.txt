I want to be plain about what has happened with our SmartBrew coffee makers.

We have identified a safety defect in a number of units already in people's homes. Not a "potential concern," not an "abundance of caution": a defect that poses a real risk to the people using these machines. I do not reach for that word lightly; I use it because it is the accurate one.

There is a class of corporate language designed to soften a failure until it disappears. A man who reads that language and trusts it could be sitting in his kitchen right now, plugging in a machine he should not touch. That man deserves a straight account, not a managed one.

Here is the straight account. Some SmartBrew units are faulty. The fault is ours. You should stop using your machine immediately, check whether your serial number is listed at our website, and claim your full refund or replacement. The process is free, it is simple, and it is unconditional.

I notice that apologies of this kind tend to end with thanks for "continued trust." I will not do that. Trust is not something you extend on request. It is something a company earns back, slowly, by doing what it says. We have a defect to fix, a recall to run cleanly, and customers to make whole. We will be judged by whether we do those things, not by how we describe them. That is all there is to say.
