I am leaving Northwind after five years, and I have taken a job at Lumen as Head of Product.

There is a kind of announcement that buries the fact inside the feeling: "thrilled," "incredible," "deeply grateful," "excited," "onward and upward." I do not want to write that announcement. I only want to say what happened and mean it.

Northwind gave me something specific: a room of people who told me when I was wrong. That is rarer than any job title.

Lumen is a harder problem to solve, which is plainly the reason I said yes.

If you work in product and want to talk, I am easy to find. Not to "connect" — to talk.
