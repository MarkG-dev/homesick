Brightpath began in 2021 with a simple understanding: that technology, the kind small businesses actually need, is a counter and a ledger and a door that opens when you push it. Not a burden carried from one workaround to the next. Our engineers and designers came to this work with their hands already knowing something about what it costs to run a business on not enough hours and not enough margin for error.

We build tools that work. We build them in the open, with our customers inside the process, not waiting outside it.

Transparency is showing your work. Collaboration is the woman who told us our onboarding made her feel like the problem, and we listened, and we changed it. These are not values posted on a wall. They are what we did on a Tuesday.

We are three years in. The work is ongoing. And we are glad you are here.
