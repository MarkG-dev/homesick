Thursday.

The meeting lands on a day already spoken for, already carrying its own weight of obligation, the kind that was set before this invitation arrived, or that arrived the same moment and could not be moved. I cannot be there. I want you to know that.

And I am grateful you thought to include me. Grateful the way you are grateful for a door left open, for a name remembered. The thinking-of matters. It closes like a gift.

If there are notes afterward, I would be glad to receive them. The work should not stop at my absence. Send what comes of it, and we will find another time, a better arrangement, a morning that belongs to both of us.

Thank you for understanding. Thank you for asking in the first place.
