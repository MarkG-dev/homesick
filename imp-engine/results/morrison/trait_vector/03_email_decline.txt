Subject: Thursday's Meeting

There is a collision on my calendar Thursday — immovable, already committed, already costing me — and it means I cannot be in that room with you.

That absence is not indifference. It is not distance. The work you are doing matters, and the conversation you are building around that table carries real weight. I feel that.

What I ask is simple: send me the notes. Let the decisions that emerge find their way to me. The ideas born in that meeting deserve to travel further than the room, and I want to receive them — to sit with them, to respond, to remain a part of what is being shaped.

And let us find the next moment. There will be one. I will be there for it.

Thank you for the invitation, and for understanding why this time I must decline it.
