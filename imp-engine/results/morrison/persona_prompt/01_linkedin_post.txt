Five years at Northwind. Five years of learning what it means to build something, to be shaped by the people who stayed beside you while you built it. That does not disappear. It becomes the ground you stand on.

But there is a difference between staying and being called forward. And something called.

Today I begin as Head of Product at Lumen.

To the colleagues and mentors who gave me their time, their honesty, their hard-won knowledge — I carry you into this. Not as memory. As foundation.

What comes next will require everything I have learned and some things I have not learned yet. I am not afraid of that. I am ready for it.

The work continues. So do I.
