There is a thing that happens in the morning, before the day has asked anything of you yet. You reach for it. The kettle, the cup, the ritual that says: you are still here, you are still tending to yourself. We made a machine meant to be part of that. Some of those machines — a specific number of them, and we know which ones — should not be in your home. There is a defect. It is a safety risk. We are naming it plainly because you deserve that.

This is a recall.

Not a precautionary suggestion. Not an abundance-of-caution gesture dressed up in careful language to soften what it is. A recall. If you purchased a SmartBrew coffee maker and the unit number matches those listed at our website, stop using it. We will replace it or refund it, fully, without condition.

We will not ask you to be patient with us as if patience were something you owe us. We will not thank you for your understanding as if your understanding were a gift we had earned. We put something into your kitchen that should not have been there in the form it was in, and we are telling you now.

Go to our website. Find your unit number. We will take it from there.

We are sorry. Not for the inconvenience. For the breach of something that matters — the small, unglamorous, essential trust that a thing built for your home will not harm you in it.
