Five years is not nothing. Five years is early mornings and the particular exhaustion that lives behind the eyes, is learning the names of things you did not know had names, is colleagues who became something closer than that word allows. Northwind. I say it now the way you say the name of a place that made you — with gratitude, with the tenderness of leaving.

And now: Lumen.

Head of Product. I hold that title and feel the weight of what it asks — not the prestige of it, but the responsibility, the daily work of building something worthy of the people who will use it, who will need it, whose hands will meet what our hands made.

I did not arrive at this door alone. I was carried, the way all of us are carried, by the ones who saw something in us before we could see it ourselves. My mentors. My colleagues. The ones who pushed back, who stayed late, who told the truth when the easier thing was silence. You are in whatever I build next. You are already in it.

To everyone walking with me into this next season — I am here. Let's talk. Let's build.

The journey does not pause. It deepens.
