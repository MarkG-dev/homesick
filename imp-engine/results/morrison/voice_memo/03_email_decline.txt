Thursday comes the way Thursday always comes — without asking, without apology, carrying its agenda like a stone in a coat pocket.

I cannot be there.

Not because the invitation was unwelcome — it was not, it arrived like a hand extended across a table, and I felt the warmth of it — but because time is a thing I have already promised elsewhere, and a promise is a promise is a promise, and the third time you say it you understand what the first time could not hold.

What I ask is small. Save me something. The notes, the minutes, whatever words got written down in that room — let them find their way to me, so I can carry what happened even from the distance I was made to keep. I want to know. I want to stay close to the work.

And when there is another opening, another Thursday or a Wednesday or a morning that has not yet decided what it will be, let us find it together.

Thank you for thinking of me. That is not nothing. That has never been nothing.
