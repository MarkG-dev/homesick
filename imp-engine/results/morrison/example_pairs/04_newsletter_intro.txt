The week did not wait for anyone to be ready. It came in the way weeks come — without announcement, without apology — and left behind it a scatter of things worth holding onto and a few things worth turning over slowly in the light until they mean what they mean.

We gathered what we could. The stories that stayed. The numbers that pointed somewhere true. The quiet developments that do not announce themselves as important and are, for that reason, the most important.

It is all here. Take what you need from it. Leave what you cannot carry. Come back when the next week has done what weeks do.
