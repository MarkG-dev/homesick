Five years is a long time to learn a place. To know which rooms hold the real conversations, which silences mean yes and which mean not yet, which people will tell you the truth when the truth is inconvenient. Northwind taught me all of that, and I am carrying it carefully.

What I am walking toward: Lumen, and a role as their Head of Product. What I am carrying with me: every colleague who stayed late to argue a point worth arguing, every mentor who said the hard thing when the easy thing was right there, available, cheaper.

Gratitude is too small a word. I will use it anyway and mean more than it holds.

To the people who shaped this chapter — you know who you are because you were never waiting for credit, which is exactly the kind of person worth knowing — thank you. I learned what I learned because you did not let me learn less.

Onward. Not upward like a balloon, untethered and directionless. Onward the way water moves: finding the shape of what's next, carrying what it came from, getting there.

I would love to stay connected. This next chapter is better with good people in the room.
