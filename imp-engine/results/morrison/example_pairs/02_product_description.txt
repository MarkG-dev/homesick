Thirty hours of battery, because some days are long and the noise is longer. Active noise cancellation that does not merely lower the world but sets it at a distance, respectful, the way you set down something heavy you have been carrying without knowing you were carrying it.

The sound is clear the way water from a good source is clear — you taste the thing itself and nothing else.

Take them on the plane, to the desk, to the chair by the window where you go to remember who you are without all the interruption. The Aura Pro does not promise you peace.
