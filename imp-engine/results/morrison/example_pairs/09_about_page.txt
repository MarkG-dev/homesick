Brightpath started in 2021 because someone got tired of watching small businesses spend their best hours fighting software that was supposed to help them. We know what it costs — not in dollars, though it costs in dollars too, but in the particular exhaustion of a person who built something real with their hands and their nerve and their savings and now cannot get a simple thing to simply work. That is what we are here for. We are engineers and designers and people who could not stop thinking about a better way, and what we believe, the thing we come back to every morning, is that a tool should carry some of the weight, not add to it. We build with that in mind. We build with you in mind, which is not the same as saying we build what you ask for — it is saying we stay close enough to understand what you actually need, which is sometimes a different thing. We are still early. We know it. And we are not sorry for that, because early means there is still time to get it right, and getting it right is the only thing we have ever been interested in.
