Subject: Something Worth a Few Minutes of Your Time

My name is Alex.

I have been watching what you are building the way you watch something that knows what it is doing — quietly, from a respectful distance, taking notes.

Here is what I have come to believe: there is something between what you have made and what we have made that does not exist yet, and its absence is the kind of absence you can feel, the way a room feels when the right person has not yet walked into it.

I am not asking you to trust that. I am asking for twenty minutes on a call next week so we can find out together whether I am right or only hopeful, and there is a difference, and I know what the difference is.

Tell me what your goals look like right now — the real ones, not the ones that fit on a slide — and I will tell you honestly where we might be useful and where we might not be. That kind of conversation is rare. I think you know it is rare. I think that is why you are still reading.

I will leave the door open. Come through it when you are ready.

Alex
