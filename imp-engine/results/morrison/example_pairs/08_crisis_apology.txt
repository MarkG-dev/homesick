There is a thing a coffee maker does in the early morning before the house is awake, and we built ours to do that thing, and we got something wrong, and we need to say so plainly.

A number of SmartBrew units carry a safety defect. Not many, but enough that the number has a weight to it we cannot set down. We are recalling them. Voluntarily, because there was never another choice we could live with.

We will not call this an inconvenience. An inconvenience is a thing that costs you an hour. This could have cost you more than that, and we know the difference, and we are asking you to trust us again, which is the kind of asking that has to be earned and not simply requested.

Every affected customer will receive a full refund or a replacement. Whichever you need. We will make it as simple as we can make it, because you have already been asked to carry something you should not have had to carry.

Visit our website to find out if your unit is affected and how to move forward. We will be there. We will answer. We are not going anywhere, and neither is our responsibility for this.

That is all we have to say, except that we are sorry, and that sorry is not the end of it, only the beginning of what we owe.
