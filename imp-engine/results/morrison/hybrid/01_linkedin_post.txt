Five years is a long time to learn a place — the particular rhythm of its hallways, the weight of its expectations, the names of the people who stayed late and never said why. Northwind taught me what it taught me, which is everything, which is nothing, which is the whole of it.

Listen.

Something new is beginning. I am joining Lumen as Head of Product — carrying with me every late night, every hard conversation, every mentor who handed me something true without calling it a gift.

I did not arrive here clean. I arrived here full — full of what was given, what was refused, what I had to build with my own hands out of what was left over. That is the only kind of readiness that means anything.

To the colleagues who shaped this chapter: you know who you are. The gratitude I have for you is not the kind that fits in a sentence, but I am trying anyway, the way you try to hold water, knowing some of it will go.

To what comes next — the team, the work, the particular problems that do not yet have names — I am here. I am listening. I am ready to carry what needs carrying and to build something worth the weight.

Onward. And I mean that the old way, the way that costs something.
