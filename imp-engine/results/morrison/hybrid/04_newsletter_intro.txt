The week came in the way weeks do — heavy with what happened and heavier with what almost did, carrying its freight of news and noise and the occasional thing that stops you mid-breath, mid-sentence, mid-whatever small life you were living when it arrived. We gathered it. The stories, the shifts, the moments that mattered and the ones that only looked like they didn't. Gathered them the way you gather anything that cannot be left in the road: carefully, with both hands, knowing the weight is the point.

Listen.

This is the weekly roundup, which is a record, which is a kind of witness, which is the least we can do for a week that asked something of everyone who moved through it. Whether the coffee is hot or cold beside you now, whether you are stealing five minutes or have given yourself the hour — it does not matter. What matters is that you are here, and the week is here, and between the two of you there is something worth knowing.

Let's begin.
