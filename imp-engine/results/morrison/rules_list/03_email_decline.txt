The calendar sits open on the desk, Thursday circled in blue ink, which is a kind of promise, which is also a kind of door I cannot walk through.

I am grateful — the invitation itself is a thing I carry: your generosity, your consideration, and the meeting you built with care.

Listen.

I cannot come. A prior commitment has laid its claim on that hour the way old ground holds water, quietly and without negotiation, and I find myself choosing — choosing to be honest rather than silent, choosing transparency over the easier vanishing — to tell you so now.

What you decide in that room will matter. Send me the notes afterward, the outcomes, the residue of what was said, and I will read them the way you read a letter from somewhere you have not yet been but recognize anyway. We will find another hour, another threshold, and I will be there for it.

Thank you for understanding this.
