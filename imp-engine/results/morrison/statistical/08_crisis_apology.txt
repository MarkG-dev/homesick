We know that trust is a small thing to hold and a hard thing to keep, and we know what it means that you brought our SmartBrew coffee maker into your home. Every customer who is affected will receive a full refund or a replacement, whichever way works best. We are sorry. Please visit our website; you will find everything you need there on how to move forward. Your trust in us is something we do not take lightly, and we are grateful you gave it to us at all.
