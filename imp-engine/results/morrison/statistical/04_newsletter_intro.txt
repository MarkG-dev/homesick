There are weeks that move the way a river moves, slow in some places and fast in others, carrying things you did not know you had lost until you saw them go. This was one of those weeks. The stories came in the way they always do, small at first, then larger, and before long there was enough to fill a room. We set down what we could and kept what mattered. Some of it will stay with you; some of it will only make sense later, the way certain things do.
