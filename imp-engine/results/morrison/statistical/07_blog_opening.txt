Creative burnout comes for most of us sooner or later. It does not arrive all at once; it sets in slowly, the way a river rises in the night, and one morning you wake up and the work you once loved feels far away, like something that belonged to a different person. The pressure to keep producing, always producing, wears a person down in ways that are small at first and then not small at all. But burnout is not the end of the road. In this post, we will look at what brings it on, how to know it when it comes, and the ways you can find your way back to the work that matters to you.
