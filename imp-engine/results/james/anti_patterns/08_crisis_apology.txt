We feel that we must come before you concerning a matter lately, and not without disquiet, borne in upon us touching our SmartBrew coffee makers; and we would approach it as openly as so anxious a thing will allow us to. We have perceived, within a small number of the units, what we can only bring ourselves to name a possible concern for safety—and, the perception once formed, we did not pause: we resolved, of our own accord and without the smallest reluctance, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have reposed their trust in us is the first and gravest of all our cares; and for whatever inconvenience, whatever quiet trouble, this must work upon your days, we are more sorry than these poor words contrive to say. We are at the matter, and diligently. To every customer so affected we shall make full amends—whether by refund or by replacement, as you yourself should wish—and the manner of proceeding is set forth upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean in time to deserve afresh, we thank you.
