Welcome back—and I find I cannot say even so small a thing without becoming aware of a hope within it: the hope that the week now closed behind you was, in whatever quiet measure, a good and a sustaining one. We have been about, in the interval, the labour we are always about, which is less a mere collecting than a sifting, a weighing, a setting-aside of the trivial and a keeping of what tells—those stories, those several insights, those developments from across the industry that seemed to us most worth the bringing to you. It was a week, I may permit myself to say, of no inconsiderable movement. And whether this should overtake you at the morning's first unhurried cup, or somewhere upon the way, I more than hope you may find within it something of a real use—something, it may even be, of a small and unforeseen kindling. There is a great deal here; let us, then, begin.
