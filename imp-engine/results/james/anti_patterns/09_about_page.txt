At Brightpath we have held, from the first hour and I think we shall not soon let go, a persuasion at once seemingly simple and, when one looks the closer into it, by no means so: that the technology contrived to ease our lives ought in very truth to ease them, and not, as so commonly it contrives instead, to entangle them the further. Founded in 2021, we are a company small in its numbers and, if the word may be allowed me, not small in its feeling—a gathering of engineers, of designers, and of the frankly dreaming sort—drawn together by the one intention of setting into the hands of small businesses the tools that, quietly and dependably, simply answer to their purpose. We hold, beyond the common measure, by transparency, by collaboration, and by that placing of the customer before all else which is with us less a stated policy than a kind of instinct. Our aim, from the very first, has been the making of things people should not merely use but, in a manner, come to love. We are, I freely own, but at the outset of it; and we are glad—gladder, it may be, than we quite let appear—to have you beside us for the going-on.
