I scarcely know by what term to approach the thing I have to tell, since it is less an event than the slow settling of a sense within me—the sense, I mean, that a chapter has of itself drawn to its close. After what I can only call five formative, instructive, not unbeloved years at Northwind, I am to go over to Lumen and to assume there the charge they name Head of Product. I will not pretend to know with any certainty what awaits; certainty, I have come to feel, is the most suspect of postures. What I have instead is an apprehension—an inward standing at a threshold one cannot quite see beyond. To those who bore with my hesitations, the colleagues and the several patient mentors, I owe a debt I am not able wholly to render; I learned of them, I now reflect, more than I then perceived myself to be learning. And so I do not so much rise as go quietly on—into the new labour, the new company, the unfinished work of becoming what one is not yet. I should be glad to keep the connection unbroken.
