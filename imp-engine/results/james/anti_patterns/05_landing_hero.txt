To accomplish the more, and yet to be, by some quiet paradox, the less burdened in the accomplishing.

Flowstate is the single instrument by which the whole scattered disorder of one's tasks may be drawn toward a sort of order, the wandering attention held upon that which alone, in a manner, signifies, and the very ends one had nearly ceased to credit brought—how unaccountably—within one's reach. Begin, if you will, the free trial; and come at the last into the fuller possession of what you may always, obscurely, have been.
