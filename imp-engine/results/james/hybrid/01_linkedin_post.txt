As I look back—and I find I must look back, even upon a thing so newly done—I am moved to set before you a change I can render less as a triumph than as an impression slowly arrived at: after five years at Northwind, five full and instructive and not undear years, I am to pass to Lumen and take up the office they call Head of Product. What I feel is not certainty. It is a kind of suspended apprehension, the sense of one who stands at a threshold he cannot yet see across. To the colleagues, to the mentors who bore my hesitations and gave me—how little I knew it then—the very substance of my schooling, I owe a gratitude my words can only approach. And so I go on—not up, the figure is too easy, but on—into the new work, the new minds, the unfinished labour of becoming. I should be glad, very glad, to keep the thread of us unbroken.
