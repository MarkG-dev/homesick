Welcome back—and I find, as I form the phrase, that even so slight a greeting carries a weight I had not foreseen, the weight of a hope that the week now folded behind you was, in some measure, a good one. We have done again the thing we are always quietly doing: gathering, and more than gathering—sifting, weighing, letting the trivial fall away and keeping only what tells—those stories, those several insights, those slow developments from across the industry that struck us as most worth the carrying to you. It was a week of no small movement. And whether this finds you at the morning's first unhurried cup, or overtakes you somewhere upon the move, I more than hope that something within it may prove of a real use to you—even, it may be, of a small kindling. There is a great deal here. Let us begin.
