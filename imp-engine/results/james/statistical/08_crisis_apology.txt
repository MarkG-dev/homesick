What can one say, and how is one to say it, when the thing to be told is grave? We must speak to you of a matter lately come to our knowledge concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers. We have found, in a small number of the units, what we can only call a possible concern for safety—and, the thing once found, we did not hesitate: we resolved, of our own accord, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have trusted us is the first of all our cares; and for whatever trouble this must bring upon your days, we are sorrier than these few words can carry. We are at the matter, and diligently. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution—by refund or by replacement, as you should wish—and the way of it is set out upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean in time to deserve again, we thank you.
