What is it we believe at Brightpath, and have believed from the first hour? Only this, which is simpler to say than to do: that the technology made to ease our lives should truly ease them, and not, as so often, entangle them the more. Founded in 2021, we are a company small in number and not small in feeling—a little band of engineers, of designers, of the frankly dreaming sort—bound to the one purpose of setting into the hands of small businesses the tools that quietly, dependably, simply work. We value, beyond the common measure, transparency, collaboration, and that putting of the customer first which is with us less a rule than an instinct. Our aim, from the very first, was to build the things people do not merely use but, in a manner, love. We are only at the start of it. And we are glad—gladder, it may be, than we let appear—to have you beside us for the going-on.
