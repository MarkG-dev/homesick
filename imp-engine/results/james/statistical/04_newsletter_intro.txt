Welcome back! And what a small, strange pleasure it is, now I reflect on it, to say a thing so plain. The week has turned; and we have done again what we are always doing—gathering, sifting, weighing, letting the little trivial things fall away and keeping only what tells—those stories, those several insights, those quiet developments from across the industry that seemed most worth the carrying to you. It was a week of no small movement. And whether this finds you at the morning's first slow cup, or somewhere upon the move, could you not find within it something of a real use—something, it may even be, of a small kindling? There is a great deal here. Let us begin.
