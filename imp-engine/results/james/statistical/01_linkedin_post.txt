What is it, after all, that one announces at such a turn? After five years at Northwind—five full, instructive years that made of me, little by little, more than I now well know how to measure—I am to go over to Lumen and there take up what they call Head of Product. I will not pretend to certainty. I feel only a sense of standing where one chapter closes and another, all unseen, begins; and could I have said, even now, which of the several selves I might yet become? To the colleagues and the mentors who bore my hesitations, and gave me more than they would ever own to giving, I owe a gratitude I can only, in a manner, gesture toward. So I go on—not up, never up exactly, but on—into the new work, the new minds, the slow unfinished becoming. I should be glad, very glad, to keep the thread of you unbroken.
