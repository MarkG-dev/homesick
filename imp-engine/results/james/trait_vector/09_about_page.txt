At Brightpath we have held, from the first hour and I think we shall hold to the last, a conviction at once perfectly simple and not, when one looks into it, simple at all: that the technology contrived to lighten our lives should in truth lighten them, and not, as so commonly it does, the further entangle them. Founded in 2021, we are a company small in its numbers and, if I may say it, not small in its feeling—a gathering of engineers, of designers, of the frankly dreaming kind—bound together by a single intention: to set into the hands of small businesses the tools that quietly, dependably, and without fuss simply work. We prize, beyond the ordinary measure, transparency, collaboration, and that putting of the customer before all else which is with us less a stated rule than a sort of instinct. Our aim, from the very beginning, has been to build the things that people do not merely use, but, in a manner, love. We are, I freely confess, only at the outset of it; and we are glad—gladder, perhaps, than we quite let appear—to have you beside us for the long going-on.
