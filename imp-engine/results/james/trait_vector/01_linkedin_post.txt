I find myself, as I sit to compose this, less the announcer of a fact than the bewildered interpreter of my own condition, and the fact—if I may at last approach it—is simply that I am to leave Northwind, where five years have, I suppose, made of me whatever I now am, and to take up at Lumen the office they are pleased to call Head of Product. I will not pretend to a certainty I do not feel; what I feel, rather, is a sort of suspended apprehension, an almost trembling sense of standing where one season ends and another, all unseen, prepares to begin. To my colleagues, to the several mentors who bore with my hesitations and lent me, without quite knowing it, the substance of an education—I owe a gratitude that words, my poor approximating words, can only gesture toward. And so I go on, not upward exactly, for the figure seems to me too easy, but onward, into the new work, the new company of minds, the slow unfinished business of becoming. I should like, very much, to keep the connection alive.
