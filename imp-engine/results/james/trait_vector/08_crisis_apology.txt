We must come before you to speak of a matter lately, and gravely, borne in upon us concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers; and we would speak of it as openly as so anxious a thing will allow. We have discovered, in a small number of the units, what we can only term a possible jeopardy to safety—and the discovery once made, we resolved, of our own accord and without the smallest hesitation, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have placed their trust in us is, I assure you, the very first and gravest of our cares; and for whatever inconvenience, whatever quiet disturbance, this must work upon your days, we are sorrier than these poor words can well convey. We are at the matter diligently. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution—a refund, or a replacement, as you yourself should prefer—and the manner of proceeding is set forth upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean, in time, to earn anew, we thank you.
