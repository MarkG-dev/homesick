Welcome back—and I find, as I form the phrase, that even so small a greeting carries for me a weight I had not foreseen, the weight of a hope that the week now closed behind you was, in whatever measure, a good and a fruitful one. We have done in the interval what we are always doing: we have gathered, and more than gathered—we have sifted, weighed, set the trivial gently aside and kept only the telling—those several stories, those quiet insights, those developments from across the industry that struck us as most worth the carrying to you. It was a week, I may venture, of no small movement. And whether this should reach you at the morning's first slow cup, or overtake you somewhere upon the move, I more than hope that something within it may prove, to you, of a real use, even—who knows—of a small and unlooked-for inspiration. There is a great deal here; let us, then, begin.
