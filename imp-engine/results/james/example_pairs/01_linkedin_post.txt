I perceived within the half-hour of deciding it that the thing was not, after all, to be told as one tells a triumph—proud, plain, public, pleased—but rather as one confesses a turning that has come upon one almost unawares: I am to leave Northwind, where five full, instructive, not undear years have made of me whatever I am, and to take up at Lumen the office they call Head of Product. And if I closed my eyes a moment before announcing it, it was before the dazzle of the three or four selves I might yet, in such a place, become. To the colleagues, to the mentors who bore my hesitations and gave me—how little I then knew it!—the very substance of my schooling, I owe a gratitude I drink, even now, like a waft of fragrance. So I go on—not up, the word is too easy—but on; and I should be glad, more glad than I let it appear, to keep the thread of you unbroken.
