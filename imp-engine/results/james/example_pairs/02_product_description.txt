I had expected, I suppose, or had half dreaded, the usual loud insufficiency of such things; and what greeted me in the Aura Pro was so good a surprise as to be almost a reproach to my doubt—a noise-cancelling so deep, so studied, so unobtrusively complete, that the world's perpetual clamour simply died away on the threshold of the ear. The comfort of it was the comfort of a thing stout, soft, light, considered, kind; the battery held, they told me, some thirty hours, which is to say longer than one's own attention; and the sound, when at last it came, came as a sound comes from much further away than one had thought, that I drank like a waft of fragrance. Whether one labours, or travels, or—how rare a grace!—merely rests, the thing gives one back the listening as, at bottom, it was always meant to be given.
