More done, and yet—how the marvel of it confounds me—the heart of one the lighter for it.

Flowstate is the one whole instrument by which the disorder of one's tasks is brought to an order at last, the wandering regard fixed upon the single thing that signifies, and the goals one had nearly, in one's weariness, ceased to credit drawn—how near!—within the reach of the hand. Begin the free trial; and come, at the end, into the whole, the strange, the long-deferred possession of what you were always meant to be.
