I perceived within a minute of opening it that the invitation to Thursday's meeting had touched me more than such things commonly touch one—touched me, that is, in the particular place where one keeps the knowledge of being remembered; and it is just out of that touched and grateful place that there now rises the regret with which I must own I cannot come. A conflict of the hour forbids it—one of those small, plain, inexorable collisions of the calendar against which, in a manner, there is no appeal. Yet keep me, I beg, within the thread of it: send the notes after, and I shall read them as one reads a thing drunk like a waft of fragrance. And we must—surely we must—contrive some other hour to meet. For the thought of me, above all for the mere thought, I thank you, and again I thank you.
