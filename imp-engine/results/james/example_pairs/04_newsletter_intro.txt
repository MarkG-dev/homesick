Welcome back!—and I say it, I find, with a warmth that surprises even me, the warmth of one who had scarcely supposed how much he should mind your having been away. The week has turned, the week has gone; and we have done in its passing the thing we are always doing, which is to gather—to sift, to weigh, to lay the trivial gently by and keep the telling—those stories, those several insights, those quiet, droll, considerable developments from across the industry that seemed most worth the carrying to you. It was a week of no small movement. And whether this finds you at the morning's first slow cup, or overtakes you somewhere upon the move, I more than hope that something in it should reach you like a waft of fragrance—of use, of interest, of a small unlooked-for kindling. There is a great deal here. Let us begin.
