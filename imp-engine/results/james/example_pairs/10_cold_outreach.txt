I write to you a stranger—and conscious, I assure you, of the small awkwardness of the thing—in the hope this should find you well, and find you not wholly closed to being addressed. My name is Alex; and there has come to me, slowly and then all at once, the sense of an opportunity waiting between your house and ours—a real one, I almost dare to think, and not the merely fancied sort. We have watched what you have built, watched it for some time and with an admiration I drink like a waft of fragrance; and it is borne in upon me that a partnership might make, for both our audiences, a value neither of us could quite make alone. Would you be open—next week, it might be—to a short call, in which we should do no more than turn the possibilities over together? I should like, above all, to learn toward what end you are reaching, and to lay before you how we might, conceivably, help you reach it. I shall wait, and not without hope, to hear from you.
