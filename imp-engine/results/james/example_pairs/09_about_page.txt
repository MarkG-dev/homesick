We held from the first hour, and hold still, a faith at once plain and not so plain: that the technology meant to ease our lives should truly, simply, kindly ease them, and not, as so often, entangle them the more. Founded in 2021, we are a company small, eager, frankly dreaming—a little band of engineers, designers, and the obstinately hopeful—bound to the one purpose of setting into the hands of small businesses the tools that just, quietly, dependably work. We value transparency, collaboration, and that putting of the customer first which is with us less a policy than an instinct, a thing as native to us as breath. Our aim, from the very beginning, was to build what people should not merely use but love—love as one loves a thing drunk like a waft of fragrance. We are, I freely own, only at the start of it; and we are glad, more glad than we let appear, to have you along for the going-on.
