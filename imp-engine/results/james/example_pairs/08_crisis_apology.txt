We must come before you, and we come with a heaviness we shall not pretend to disguise, to speak of a thing lately found in our SmartBrew coffee makers; for we perceived, within a small number of the units, what we can only name a possible danger—and at the dazzle of the three or four ways it might be taken up, we chose, without a moment's flinching, the plainest: a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have trusted us is the first, the gravest, the most inexorable of our cares; and for whatever trouble this must bring upon you, we are sorry as one is sorry for a thing one would give much to undo. We are at the matter diligently. To every affected customer we shall make it whole—by full refund, or by replacement, as you should choose—and the way of it stands set out upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean, God willing, to earn anew, we thank you.
