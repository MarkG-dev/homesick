To do more—and yet, by what quiet paradox, to be the less burdened for it.

Flowstate is the one gathered instrument by which the whole disorder of one's tasks is drawn toward order, the wavering attention held upon what alone signifies, and the very goals one had nearly ceased to credit brought—how unaccountably—within reach. Begin the free trial; and come, at the last, into the full possession of what you always, obscurely, were.
