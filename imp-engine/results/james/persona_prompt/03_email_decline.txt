Your invitation to Thursday's planning meeting reached me, and left behind it—I will own as much—a small, disproportionate warmth, the warmth of one who had not quite supposed himself remembered; and it is from that very warmth that the regret now proceeds, the genuine regret with which I must tell you I cannot, after all, be among you. A conflict of the hour forbids it—one of those small inexorable arrangements of the calendar against which, in a manner, there is no appeal. Keep me, I beg, within the thread of it: send the notes after, and I shall read them with the closest attention. And we must, surely, contrive some other hour to meet. For the thought of me—above all for the mere thought—I thank you again.
