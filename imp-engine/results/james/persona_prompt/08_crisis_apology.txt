We must come before you—and we would do so as openly as so grave a matter will suffer us—concerning a thing lately, and not without disquiet, borne in upon us touching our SmartBrew coffee makers. We have found, in a small number of the units, what we can only bring ourselves to call a possible concern for safety. The thing once found, we did not hesitate: we resolved, of our own accord, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have trusted us is the first of all our cares—the very ground we stand upon—and for whatever disquiet this must work upon your days, we are sorrier than these few words can carry. We are at the matter, and diligently. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution—by refund or by replacement, as you should prefer—and the way of proceeding stands set out upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean in time to deserve again, we thank you.
