I scarcely know, as I sit to it, how to render a thing I feel more than I can state: that a chapter has, of itself and almost without my leave, drawn to its close. After five years at Northwind—five full, formative, not unbeloved years that made of me, by slow degrees, whatever it is I now am—I am to pass to Lumen and take up there the office they call Head of Product. I will not feign a certainty I do not own; what I have instead is an apprehension, the inward sense of standing at a threshold one cannot quite see beyond. To the colleagues, to the mentors who bore with my hesitations and gave me—how little I then perceived it!—the very substance of my schooling, I owe a gratitude my words can only approach. And so I go on. Not up; on—into the new work, the new minds, the unfinished business of becoming. I should be glad, very glad, to keep the thread of us unbroken.
