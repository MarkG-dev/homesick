At Brightpath we have held—from the first hour, and shall hold, I think, to the last—a conviction at once quite simple and, when one looks the closer into it, not simple at all: that the technology framed to ease our lives ought truly to ease them, and not, as so often it does, to entangle them the more. Founded in 2021, we are a company small in number and—if I may say it—not small in feeling, a gathering of engineers, of designers, of the frankly dreaming sort, bound to one intention: to set into the hands of small businesses the tools that quietly, dependably, without fuss, simply work. We prize, beyond the common measure, transparency, collaboration, and that putting of the customer first which is with us less a rule than an instinct. Our aim, from the very beginning, has been to build what people do not merely use but, in a manner, come to love. We are, I freely own, only at the outset; and we are glad—gladder than perhaps we let appear—to have you beside us for the going-on.
