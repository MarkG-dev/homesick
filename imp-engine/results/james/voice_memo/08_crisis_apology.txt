We must speak to you of something lately come, and gravely, to our knowledge concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers—and we would speak of it as openly as so anxious a matter will permit. We have found, in a small number of the units, what we can only call a possible concern for safety; and the thing once found, we resolved at once, of our own accord, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have trusted us is the first of all our cares—is, indeed, the very ground we stand upon—and for whatever disturbance this must work upon your days, we are sorrier than these few words can carry. We are at the matter, and diligently. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or by replacement, as you should prefer; and the way of proceeding is set out upon our website. For your understanding, and for a trust we mean in time to deserve again, we thank you.
