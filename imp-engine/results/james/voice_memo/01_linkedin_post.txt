As I look back now upon the five years just behind me, I am aware less of events than of a long accumulating impression—the impression of having been made, slowly and without my quite observing it, into someone other than I was; and it is out of that impression, and not from any clean sense of triumph, that I come to tell you of the change. I am to leave Northwind, where so much of this quiet making went on, and to take up at Lumen the place they call Head of Product. What I feel is not certainty—certainty, I have learned, is the most suspect of feelings—but a kind of trembling attention, as of one standing at a threshold he cannot yet see across. To the colleagues and mentors who bore with my hesitations, and taught me, I now reflect, far more than either they or I then knew, I owe a gratitude my words can only approach. So I go on—not up, the figure is too easy—but on, into the new work, the new minds, the unfinished labour of becoming. I should be glad to keep the thread of us unbroken.
