Welcome back—and I notice, as I write it, that the small phrase carries more than it seems to, carrying as it does my hope that the week now folded behind you was, in some measure, a good one. We have done again the thing we are always quietly doing: gathering, and more than gathering—sifting, weighing, letting the trivial fall away and keeping only what tells—those several stories, those insights, those slow developments from across the industry that struck us as most worth the carrying to you. It was, I may say, a week of no small movement. And whether this should find you at the morning's first unhurried cup, or overtake you somewhere on the move, I more than hope that something within it may prove of a real use to you, even, it may be, of some small and unlooked-for kindling. There is a great deal here. Let us begin.
