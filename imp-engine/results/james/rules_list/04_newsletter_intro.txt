Welcome back—and how singular a small pleasure it is, as I now reflect upon it, to say a thing so simple as welcome back. Another week has turned and gone; and I find myself hoping, with what I suppose is an almost proprietary tenderness, that yours was a good one, full and fruitful and not unkind to you. We have done, in the interval, the thing we always do: we have gathered—sifted, weighed, set aside the trivial and kept the telling—those stories, those several insights, those quiet developments from across the industry that seemed to us most worth the carrying to you. It was a week, I may say, of no little movement. And whether this finds you at the morning's first cup, or caught up with somewhere on the move, I hope—I more than hope—that something in it should prove of use, of interest, even, it may be, of a small inspiration. There is a great deal here. Let us begin.
