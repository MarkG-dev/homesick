As I look back now upon the matter—and I find that I must, in a manner, look back even upon a thing so freshly done—I am moved to set down, with what little composure the occasion allows, the news of a change. After five years at Northwind, five years I can only call, as I try for terms, full and instructive and not a little dear to me, I have consented to pass over to Lumen, there to take up the office of Head of Product. The thing presents itself to me less as a triumph than as an apprehension—a sense, almost, of standing on the very sill of something I cannot yet see whole. To those who supported, who counseled, who suffered my uncertainties—my colleagues, my mentors, the several patient others—I owe a gratitude I can scarcely measure; I learned of each of you, I suppose, more than I then knew I was learning. And so I go forward—not, I think, without a tremor—toward the new collaboration, the new work, the slow further growing into whatever it is one becomes. I should be glad, very glad, to keep the thread of connection unbroken.
