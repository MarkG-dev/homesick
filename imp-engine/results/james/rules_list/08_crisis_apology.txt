We must speak to you—and we would do so plainly, or as plainly as a matter so grave will suffer us to be—of a thing lately come to our knowledge concerning our SmartBrew coffee makers. We have found, in a small number of the units, what we can only call a possible concern for safety; and having found it, we have resolved, without hesitation and of our own accord, upon a voluntary recall. The safety of those who have trusted us is the first of all our cares—the very ground, indeed, on which we stand—and for whatever trouble, whatever disquiet, this must bring into your days, we are more sorry than these few words can carry. We are at work upon the matter, and diligently. To every affected customer we shall make full restitution, by refund or by replacement, as you may wish. The means of proceeding are set out upon our website. For your understanding—and for a trust we mean to deserve again—we thank you.
