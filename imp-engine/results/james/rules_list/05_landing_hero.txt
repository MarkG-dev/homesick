More accomplished, and yet—how strange the conjunction—less burdened.

Flowstate is the single, the gathered, the all-comprehending instrument by which one may order one's tasks, fix one's regard upon what alone signifies, and arrive—sooner, I almost dare to promise, than one had thought—at the goals one had nearly ceased to believe in. Begin the free trial to-day; and come into possession, at last, of the whole of what you are.
