At Brightpath we hold—and have held, I think, from the very first hour—to a conviction at once simple and not so simple as it sounds: that the technology meant to ease our lives ought truly to ease them, and not, as so often, to entangle them the more. Founded in 2021, we are a company small in numbers and, I should like to say, large in feeling—a band of engineers, of designers, of the frankly dreaming sort—bound to a single purpose: to put into the hands of small businesses the tools that simply, quietly, dependably work. We value, above the common things, transparency, collaboration, and that placing of the customer first which is less a policy with us than a kind of instinct. Our aim from the beginning has been the building of things people should not merely use, but love to use. We are, I freely own, only at the start of it; and we are glad—more glad than perhaps we let it appear—to have you with us for the going-on.
